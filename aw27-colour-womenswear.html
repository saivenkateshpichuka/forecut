The palette

Deep violet and tannic bordeaux lead, softened by dusty mauve and silicone pink, with waxy ochre as the light.

SeasonalDeep violetPantone 19-3638 TCX Tillandsia Purple#563474The key colour of the season, from coats to knits.
SeasonalTannic bordeauxPantone 19-1724 TCX Cabernet#64242EA deep red-brown alternative to black.
SeasonalDusty mauvePantone 16-2107 TCX Orchid Haze#B0879BSoft, smoky pink-purple, now gender-neutral.
AccentSilicone pinkPantone 14-1909 TCX Coral Blush#E6B2B8A soft, slightly artificial pink for knits and accessories.
AccentWaxy ochrePantone 16-0953 TCX Tawny Olive#C4962CA painterly yellow-ochre as the light in dark ranges.
CoreMushroomPantone 13-1105 TCX Brown Rice#C7BBA4A living beige for knits, coats and leather.
