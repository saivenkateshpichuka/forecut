The story

Neutrals with colour inside: off-black, mushroom, greyed blue and copper khaki.

Violet, plum and tannic bordeaux replace black, over neutrals that are no longer neutral: tinted off-blacks, mushroom beige and greyed blue.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

CoreTinted blackPantone 19-3713 TCX Deep Well#2C2A33An off-black with a violet cast, instead of pure black.
CoreMushroomPantone 13-1105 TCX Brown Rice#C7BBA4A living beige for knits, coats and leather.
CoreGreyed bluePantone 17-4020 TCX Blue Shadow#66829AGrey turned blue: tailoring, denim, outerwear.
CoreCopper khakiPantone 17-0935 TCX Dull Gold#8A6F48Khaki leaning to taupe and copper for utility and menswear.

How to use it

Use for: Tailoring, outerwear, menswear, denim.

Proportions

Lead with tinted black as the main colour, mushroom as the second, and keep copper khaki to trims, linings or one piece.

Departments

Womenswear, Menswear, Kidswear, Footwear carry colours from this story.
