Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cardigans worn as shirts

Collared cardigans buttoned up as shirts. It changes the whole front view, which is what customers see first online.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Check balance at fit so the front hangs straight.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Crochet-panel knits

Crochet panels set into knits. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Back crochet panels or use them as overlays to keep shape.
  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Fabric and trims

Cotton crochet panels or crochet-look knit.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Elbow-seam jumpers

Reinforced elbow seams. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Knitted bomber jackets

Zip bombers in knitted fabric. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
