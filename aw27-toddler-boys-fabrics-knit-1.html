Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Kidswear, Tops, rising

Painterly animal prints

Hand-painted looking animals rather than cartoon characters. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Tops, peaking

Brushed-back fleece

Soft brushed inside for sweatshirts and joggers. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Kidswear, Knitwear, peaking

Chunky cable cardigan

Oversized cables in a soft wool blend; wooden-look buttons sewn on with shanks. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Kidswear, Knitwear, peaking

Colour-block knits

Big blocks of colour in two or three yarns; easy to update each season. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • For under-3s, avoid small parts: no beads, sequins or loose pom-poms. Use snaps, sewn-down appliqué and embroidery instead.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
