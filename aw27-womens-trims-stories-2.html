Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Button-shoulder knits

Buttoned tabs along the shoulder. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Contrast-tipped knits

Contrast stripes at necks and cuffs. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fine-gauge zip polos

Collared knits with a short zip. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Half-zip knits

Test small

  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Smocked knit details

Sell through

  • Cricket cables with tipping
  • Embroidered denim
  • Gilded embroidered shirting
  • Knitted polo collars
  • Lace-embroidered poplin shirts

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage
