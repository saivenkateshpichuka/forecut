AW28 Kids' Season Colour Analysis

AW28 kidswear colour is projected around bottle green, mulberry and vermilion with antique gold and oat.

Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 kidswear fairs take place in January 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fairs, kidswear brands' long-term practices and market drivers, and will be updated as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Forecut's AW28 projection: ink, mulberry and bottle green over oat and slate, with petrol teal and vermilion as accents[1]
  • Future Snoops' AW28/29 "Blocked brights": "Bold colour becomes an act of defiance, a refusal to disappear into the algorithmic mass"; blue leads, with red and purple rising, and cornflower as a new "anchor colour"[2]
  • AW27 fairs put violet, plum and deep red forward as alternatives to black, and pink and mauve became gender-neutral[3]

Colour groups

  • Core: oat, petrol teal.
  • Seasonal: bottle green, mulberry, antique gold.
  • Accent: vermilion.

By product

  • Bottle green: coats.
  • Mulberry: knits.
  • Vermilion: accessories.
  • Oat: basics.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip key colours on each main fabric before committing.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Keep the core deep and the accents shallow until the fairs confirm them.
