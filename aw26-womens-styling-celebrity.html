Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses with double front pleats, sharp creases, bermuda suit shorts and mary jane flats. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Airport off-duty

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In plum with mustard.

Cropped boxy shirts with denim waistcoats, waistcoat and trouser sets and mary jane sneakers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.
