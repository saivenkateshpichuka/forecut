The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

  • Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe
  • DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts
  • Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing; fringed hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Barrel leg with curved outseam

Curved seams shaping the leg. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Bubble hems

Puffed hems on short skirts. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bubble-hem dresses

Short dresses with a puffed, gathered hem. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corset-seamed dresses

Visible corset-style seams on day dresses. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Cuffed wide trousers

Wide legs finished with deep turn-ups. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Deep turn-ups

Deep cuffed hems. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Bubble hems
  • Bubble-hem dresses
  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Cuffed wide trousers
  • Deep turn-ups

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Scale-beaded mermaid hems
  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Single-pleat suit trousers
  • Unfinished raw hems

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Fringed hems
  • Ruched side seams
  • Thick contrast topstitching

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.
