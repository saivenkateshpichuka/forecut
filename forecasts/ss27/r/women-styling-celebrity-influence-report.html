SS27 Women's Celebrity Influence

The 2026 red carpets previewed SS27: sheer gowns, strong colour, dark romance, '90s white minimalism and feathers. This is how those looks reach the SS27 customer.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women

Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

What the red carpet showed

At Cannes in May 2026, NET-A-PORTER's editors identified five red-carpet trends:

  • Sheer beauty. Despite the festival's dress code, Natasha Poly wore a see-through georgette and pleated satin Ferragamo gown, and Chloé Zhao a pointelle-knit gown from Gabriela Hearst.
  • Colour confidence. Demi Moore wore custom red and lilac Gucci, and Simone Ashley and Isabelle Huppert wore vintage red McQueen and Gucci.
  • Dark romance. Black gowns with ruffles, velvet and lace on Ruth Negga, Cate Blanchett and Cara and Poppy Delevingne; Negga's custom Saint Laurent combined a guipure lace bodice with a tulle peplum.
  • '90s minimalism. Pristine white gowns with clean, minimal shapes.
  • Feathers. Some of the most talked-about looks of the festival.

At Paris Haute Couture Week in July, Demi Moore and Cynthia Erivo attended Pierpaolo Piccioli's Balenciaga debut, which closed with Gigi Hadid.

How it connects to SS27

Each red-carpet trend has a direct SS27 runway counterpart: sheer gowns and the sheer suits of Dior and Louis Vuitton; red and lilac and the season's purple and primary blocks; dark romance and the dark lace of Première Vision's decoration report; white minimalism and the rise of white to 28.4% of SS27 looks. Red-carpet exposure months before delivery is why these ideas are likely to sell when they reach stores in spring 2027 (Forecut assessment).

Looks to build

  • The sheer column: a sheer knit or georgette column dress over a slip, with flat sandals for day.
  • Red head to toe: a red satin skirt with a red knit or shirt.
  • Lilac evening: a lilac satin slip dress with a cropped jacket.
  • White minimal: a white bias-cut dress or a white suit with no visible trims.
  • Dark romance for day: a black lace-trimmed slip skirt with a white shirt.
Design

Design implications

  • Translate red-carpet ideas into day weights: georgette and fine knit instead of beaded tulle.
  • Keep the celebrity idea in one element (colour, sheerness, lace) and simplify the rest.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Time marketing to the 2027 awards and festival calendar, when the same ideas return to the red carpet.
  • Sheer and occasion pieces sell close to events; plan smaller, later deliveries for them.
  • Avoid licensing or naming celebrities in product marketing without agreements.
