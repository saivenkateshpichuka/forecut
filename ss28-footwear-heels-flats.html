Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Bow-trim flats

Flats finished with a small bow. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Cut ties on the bias for a soft drape.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Fluid georgette, crepe de chine or satin.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Comfort block heels

Lower, wider block heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Low block sandals

Strappy sandals on low block heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Mary Jane flats

Flats with a strap across the instep. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Place the strap at the instep and use a buckle or snap.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Patent ballet flats

High-shine patent flats. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Allow for less give in coated fabrics; adjust ease and avoid tight seams.
  • Add a cushioned insole and a flexible sole for comfort.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Polyurethane-coated cotton or recycled polyester with a glossy finish.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bow-trim flats
  • Comfort block heels
  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Low block sandals
  • Mary Jane flats
  • Patent ballet flats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Cap-toe kitten slingbacks
  • Horsebit mules
  • Kitten-heel pumps
  • Satin evening pumps

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos
  • Curved sculptural heels
  • Embellished flats
  • Horsebit vamp hardware
  • Patent leather
  • Square-toe ballet flats

Actions

  • Develop one sport sole and use it across three uppers.
  • Market comfort features with clear claims.
  • Keep uppers refined to stay smart.
