Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Cargo pockets

Utility cargos cooling in womenswear. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Elastic-back tailored waists

Flat fronts with elastic at the back waist. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Leather-look trousers

Straight trousers in faux leather. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Satin slip skirts

Fluid satin skirts cut on the bias. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, peaking

Ankle-strap balloon trousers

Loose balloon-leg trousers gathered at the ankle by a cinchable strap; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027, 6 Nov 2026. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, peaking

Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers

Very full trousers with a low crotch gathered into a narrow ankle, shown at ALUF, Lilly Sarti and Uó at São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Elastic-back tailored waists
  • Leather-look trousers
  • Satin slip skirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Quilted panel skirts

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Button-front midi skirts
  • Clean straight-leg trousers
  • Cropped ankle trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops
  • Shirred-waist fleece skirts
  • Barrel-leg trousers
  • Bubble hems
  • Cargo midi skirts

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
