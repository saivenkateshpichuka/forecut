SS27 Men's Short Story

Proportions shrink: micro jackets, cropped blousons, shorts and boxer shorts worn as outerwear replace the long, oversized line.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear
Trend status
Rising

The story

Hypebeast named "the micro jacket" one of the defining SS27 menswear trends: shrunken, cropped and body-hugging micro peacoats, collarless trucker jackets with raw edges, bombers and blousons at Celine, Junya Watanabe, Prada and Willy Chavarria. Prada paired leather with slim five-pocket trousers and cropped jackets with flat pockets that worked as shirts. Below the waist, Dolce & Gabbana showed microshorts and boxer shorts left the bedroom: worn bare, above the waistline or as outerwear at JW Anderson, Willy Chavarria and Dries Van Noten, in gingham and stripes at GmbH and Charles Jeffrey Loverboy.

Heat was the driver. Hypebeast's verdict: "the beating sun is the true main character of SS27".

Colours

Sand, tobacco and ice blue from the SS27 menswear palette, with rust and salmon pink as accents. See SS27 Men's Season Colours.

Silhouettes

  • Cropped blousons and micro trucker jackets ending at the waist.
  • Slim five-pocket trousers and closer-cut long trousers.
  • Tailored shorts and boxer-style shorts in shirting.

Fabrics

Leather, including woven and perforated leather for breathability; gingham and striped shirting cottons; denim.

Trims and details

Flat patch pockets, raw edges, elastic and button-fly waistbands on boxer shorts, minimal hardware.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. Cropped jackets are rising; boxer shorts as outerwear are an early, younger-customer idea.

Products and customers

  • Cropped leather and suede blousons; micro trucker jackets; tailored and boxer-style shorts; slim five-pocket trousers.
  • Customers: fashion-forward men under 35 first; the cropped blouson reaches a broad audience.

Design implications

  • Re-cut jacket blocks shorter: hem at or just above the waist, with a slimmer sleeve.
  • Design boxer shorts with real outerwear construction: fly, pockets, a stable waistband.
  • Balance short jackets with full or straight trousers for wider customers.

Commercial implications

  • Add depth to cropped blousons; keep boxer shorts as a test.
  • Shorts carry the high-summer drop; plan them as a coordinated category with shirts.

Evidence and sources

  • The SS27 trends: what comes after fashion's hottest season ever?, Hypebeast, July 2026
  • Milan menswear finds ease on the fashion runways, even as the world feels uneasy, The Star (Associated Press), 23 June 2026
