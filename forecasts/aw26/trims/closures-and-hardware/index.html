The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Power tailoring

Sharp, slick suiting in pinstripes and slate grey, with pointed shoulders, peak lapels and double-breasted fronts. Oxblood accents and polished heels finish the look.

Why now

Return-to-office dressing and a move away from very oversized blazers toward sharper fits.

Seen at

  • Gucci: Horsebit pumps with sharp suiting
  • Classic-fit suits (market): Up 44% in market adoption
  • Double-breasted suits (market): Up 4% year on year; pointed shoulders up 12%

Key pieces

Pinstripe, Peak lapels, Pointed shoulders; sharp pointed shoulders.

Source: Who What Wear (AW26 trends); Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Harrods, AW26 menswear trends; Who What Wear

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Belted wrap fronts

Self-fabric tie belts replacing buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Buckle side adjusters

Adjusters replacing belt loops. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Buckled ankle boots

Ankle boots with a buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Button-shoulder knits

Buttoned tabs along the shoulder. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless double-breasted jackets

Double-breasted fronts without lapels. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Angrakha-overlap tunics
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Button-front midi skirts
  • Half-zip knits
  • Harness buckle straps

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Long exposed button fly

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Offer the pinstripe suit as separates so customers can mix sizes.
  • Use pointed shoulders on jackets, but keep trousers relaxed.
  • Add a waistcoat that works alone as a top.
  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
