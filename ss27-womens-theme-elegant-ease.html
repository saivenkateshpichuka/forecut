The story

SS27 Women's Themes.

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Menswear three-piece suits
  • Pleated bib fronts
  • Pointed kitten mules
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Backless apron tops
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Basque waist bodices
  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos
  • Halter-neck dresses
  • Pointed flats
  • Sheer lattice capes

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Denim waistcoats
  • Sharp pointed shoulders

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Drop waist with pleated skirt

Actions

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
