The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Belted shirt dresses

Classic shirt dresses with a self-belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bubble-hem dresses

Short dresses with a puffed, gathered hem. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Clean column dresses

Straight, unadorned column shapes. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Column knit dresses

Long ribbed knit dresses in column shapes. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corset-seamed dresses

Visible corset-style seams on day dresses. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Floral print midis

Small and painterly florals on midi dresses. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Belted shirt dresses
  • Bubble-hem dresses
  • Clean column dresses
  • Column knit dresses
  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Floral print midis

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Quilted and padded dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Scale-beaded mermaid hems
  • Striped patchwork shirt dresses
  • Basque waist bodices

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Contrast sash waists
  • Gathered waist maxis
  • Lace-edged bias slips

Actions

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
