The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops

Sweatshirts, tees and dresses printed with the image of a shirt layered on top, as at À La Garçonne, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts

Pinstripe shirts with one exaggerated collar point, tucked into soft skirts, as at Dior SS27. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Band-collar shirts with long placket

Collarless shirts with an extended placket. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Cropped boxy shirts

Short camp-collar shirts with straight hems. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Double-pocket camp shirts

Camp-collar shirts with two chest pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Henley plackets

Short button plackets on jersey tops. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops
  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Cropped boxy shirts
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Henley plackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Technical stretch shirting
  • Utility shirt-jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Velvet cape tops
  • Epaulette shirts
  • Gilded embroidered shirting
  • Lace yoke insets
  • Lace-embroidered poplin shirts
  • Pussy-bow and scarf necks

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Backless apron tops
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Ruched centre fronts
  • Rugby stripes, short plackets
  • Sheer mesh layering tops

Actions

  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
