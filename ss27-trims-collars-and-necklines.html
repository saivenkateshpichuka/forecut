Collars and necklines

Build around Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses, Backless apron tops and Band-collar shirts with long placket. The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and colour comes back in full: purple, true red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites and sand.

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

  • Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe
  • DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts
  • Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing; fringed hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses

Fitted midi sheaths in metallic tweed with an off-shoulder, sculpted neckline; Rebecca Vallance's custom tweed dress for Helena Christensen at Victoria Derby Day, Melbourne (31 Oct 2026), in line with the VRC's sleeveless midi sheath forecast for the carnival. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Backless apron tops

Halter tops that tie at the neck and leave the back open. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Band-collar shirts with long placket

Collarless shirts with an extended placket. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Collarless cropped jackets

Round-neck cropped jackets with clean edges. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Cuban camp collars

Camp collars fading for winter. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Detachable hoods

Hoods that zip or button off for two looks. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Backless apron tops
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Cuban camp collars
  • Detachable hoods

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses
  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Band-collar shirts
  • Collarless tailored jackets
  • Contrast-collar polos
  • Funnel-neck puffers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Oxford button-downs
  • Popover shirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.
