Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, fading

Belted high waists

Worn belted and high. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, fading

Clean leather bombers

Brown, navy and burgundy leather bombers. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, fading

Denim trucker jackets

Classic trucker jackets with chest flaps. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, fading

Geometric panel piecing

Triangular and angled panels. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
