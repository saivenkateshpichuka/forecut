The short version.

Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Beads and sequins on knits

Embellishment moving onto everyday jumpers. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Ombré sequin gowns

Long gowns with sequins graded from dense to sparse or light to dark; Elie Saab's dégradé sequins reached the high street in the H&M collaboration launched 22 Oct 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, peaking

Starry crystal velvet gowns

Black velvet bustier gowns scattered with crystals laid out like a night sky; Louis Vuitton on HoYeon Jung at the Governors Awards, Los Angeles, 15 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, emerging

Sequinned and beaded jeans

Embellishment on everyday denim. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
