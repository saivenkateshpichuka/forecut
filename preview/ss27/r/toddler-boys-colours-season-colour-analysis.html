SS27 Toddler Boys' Season Colour Analysis

Aqua, navy and sand with green and red accents for SS27 toddler boys.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Category
Colour
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • At Playtime Paris, red "emerged as one of the defining colors of the season"; stripes ran horizontal, vertical and diagonal[1]
  • Sailor collars, navy blues and nautical details appeared throughout Playtime Paris[1]
  • Pitti Bimbo 103 took "The Pitti Pool" as its theme and called it "the most colorful summer edition yet"[2]

Colour groups

  • Core: sand, white.
  • Seasonal: aqua, navy, sand.
  • Playful colour remains important for this age; keep brights as accents.

Design implications

  • Low-impact, skin-friendly dyes; check colour fastness to saliva and sweat where required.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Depth in sand and white basics.
