Design notes

How to use the trims.

Menswear, Tailoring, emerging

Angrakha-overlap tunics

Tunics with an overlapping angrakha-style front worn under a bandhgala, as at Tasva by Tarun Tahiliani, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026); a festive idea for India and the Gulf. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, emerging

Braided officer coats

Officer-style coats and jackets with exaggerated proportions trimmed in braids and cords, reworked from French military dress uniforms; Songzio x Egonlab 'The Art of Dark Poetry', Seoul (Korea Herald, 15 Nov 2026). Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, emerging

Contrast-collar polos

Polos with contrast collars. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Piqué, fine-gauge knit or jersey.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, emerging

Convertible backpack puffers

Oversized reversible puffers with underarm slits that fold and strap into a backpack; Melitta Baumeister x Nike, New York, 5 Nov 2026. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
