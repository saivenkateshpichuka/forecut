Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Jewel-tone cashmere

Fine knits in aubergine, mustard and emerald. A colour-led detail that is easy to roll across many styles.

Design notes

  • Lab-dip early and approve under daylight and store lighting.
  • Roll the colour across tops, bottoms and accessories.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Knitted bomber jackets

Zip bombers in knitted fabric. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Knitted ties

Slim knitted ties. An accessory-led detail that adds styling value at low cost.

Design notes

  • Make accessories detachable where possible.
  • Match finishes with other trims.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Leather and wool varsity

Varsity jackets with rib trims. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
