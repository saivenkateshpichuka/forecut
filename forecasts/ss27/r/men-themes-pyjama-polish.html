SS27 Men's Pyjama Polish

Sleepwear turns into city wear: long tunic shirts, piped two-piece sets and check sets with matching coats, made luxurious.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading
Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2027. Rising; suits resort and holiday drops.

On this pageThe storyColoursSilhouettesFabricsTrims and detailsImagesWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The story

Dior lengthened sleepwear into knee-length tunics and showed two-piece sets in khaki, blue and beige with black collars and piping; Sarah Burton's first menswear collection for Givenchy included a buttoned beige and blue check two-piece with a matching coat. Alice Feillard, director of menswear buying at Galeries Lafayette, described "a pyjama effect, but a rather sophisticated, luxurious one". Hypebeast also suggested pajama-style dressing in satin, and monochrome sets of long-sleeve tops with wide trousers appeared at IM Men and Celine.

Colours

Khaki, blue and beige with black piping; sand and ice blue from the SS27 menswear palette.

Silhouettes

  • Two-piece sets: camp-collar or tunic shirt with drawstring or wide trousers.
  • Knee-length tunic shirts.
  • Set with matching coat.

Fabrics

Poplin, satin, check shirting, seersucker, fine cotton and silk blends.

Trims and details

Contrast collars and piping, covered buttons, drawstrings, patch pockets.

Images

Make

Products and customers

  • Two-piece sets, tunic shirts, set-plus-coat looks; sold as separates and as sets.
Design

Design implications

  • Piping and contrast collars carry the idea: specify them carefully.
  • Cut set trousers with a fly and proper waistband so they work as city trousers.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Price sets to reward buying both pieces.
  • Good for resort and holiday markets (Gulf, India, Southeast Asia).
