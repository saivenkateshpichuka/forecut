Season direction

The collection framework.

Translate the season into material, shape and construction choices for your own customer.

Handloom quiet luxury

Forecut proposes a restrained festive capsule in which cloth, weave and fit carry the value. Treat maker attribution and reliable finishing as part of the product story.

Product opportunity

Chanderi, Maheshwari, khadi and tussar in calm colours, with little embellishment and beautiful cloth.

Development action

Build a calm-colour handloom capsule: kurta sets, sarees and men's kurtas. Swap heavy embroidery for tone-on-tone work at the neckline and cuffs. Tell the weave and cluster story on the label and product page.

Indigo, blue and white

Use an indigo-to-ivory story as an original Forecut range-building direction. Compare tonal surface work with a single metallic accent; validate customer response before extending the palette.

Product opportunity

Indigo kurta sets, blue-and-ivory sarees, a restrained Nehru jacket and a coordinated occasion accessory.

Development action

Add indigo to every category: kurtas, denim, sarees and footwear. Pair indigo with ivory and champagne gold for festive. Try one denim-meets-handloom fusion piece.

Festive made easy

Translate occasion dressing into coordinated separates and lighter layers. Prioritise secure drapes, comfortable linings and practical movement, with every closure tested on the intended wearer.

Product opportunity

Pre-draped sarees, embroidered co-ords, kaftans and light lehengas that look festive without the effort.

Development action

Offer the hero festive look in a pre-draped or co-ord version. Add capes and organza dupattas as add-ons to sell up. Show three ways to wear each piece across wedding functions.

Indian craft, contemporary placement

Place craft where it is visible and comfortable: a border, cuff, yoke or accessory. Develop this as an original Forecut styling direction with maker credit, cost transparency and aftercare.

Product opportunity

Artisan-made footwear, small-scale mirror or kantha accents and carefully placed bandhani or block-printed panels.

Development action

Source one GI-tagged hero product and credit the craft cluster. Add mirror or kantha accents to everyday basics. Pair Kolhapuris and juttis with western looks in imagery.

Development notes, materials & pricesSeason development

For the winter wedding and festive edit, develop pre-draped saree gowns in the emerald and sapphire palette. Compare one restrained everyday version with one occasion sample; validate weight, fit and cost before extending the range.

Construction & validation

Merchandising offers

Give customers a reason to buy.

Offer ideas below are proposed merchandising tests for your brand. They are not active Forecut or designer discounts.

Occasion complete

Offer garment, footwear and potli as transparent separately priced choices; compare bundle attachment with full-price singles.

Fit appointment

Test an included alteration appointment on selected premium pieces; price the labour before making the offer.

Wedding-function edit

Style one hero piece for two functions and offer the second layer as an add-on instead of discounting the whole look.

Kunal Rawal

India Couture Week 2026

Documented observation

Functional linings, contemporary drapes and tonal craft appear in the designer’s account of occasion dressing.

Collection story

Comfort is part of the luxury proposition: an occasion garment must work through movement, sitting and a long celebration.

Forecut translation

Develop an original lightweight layer, fit it in motion and make the construction benefit visible in product information. Do not copy a proprietary fastening.

Arpita Mehta

India Couture Week 2026

Documented observation

The FDCI interview describes nuanced Bandhani, fluid fabric bases and reinterpretations of familiar textile techniques.

Collection story

A familiar craft can feel contemporary through scale, placement and restrained colour rather than more surface coverage.

Forecut translation

Test a single craft on one hero piece and simpler matching coordinates. Build the artisan schedule into the delivery plan.

Jayanti Reddy

India Couture Week 2026

Documented observation

The FDCI interview discusses lighter base fabrics, strategic embroidery and the value of tailoring.

Collection story

An heirloom proposition can depend on balance and wearability as much as ornament.

Place craft where it matters visually, retain alteration allowance and document care so the piece can be worn again.
