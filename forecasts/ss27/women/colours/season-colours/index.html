SS27 Women's Season Colours

Lilac and violet lead the womenswear palette, with butter for softness and true red and Klein blue for statement pieces. White is the neutral that is rising; warm browns and greys give way.

The palette

Colour Pantone TCX (nearest) HEX Role
Lilac 15-3720 TCX #B3A0C9 Seasonal: lead colour
Violet 19-3642 TCX #603F83 Seasonal: depth and evening
Butter 12-0711 TCX #F6E199 Seasonal: soft neutral
True red 18-1763 TCX #C71F2D Seasonal: statement
Klein blue 18-3949 TCX #3850A0 Accent: statement
Optic white 11-0602 TCX #F2F0EB Core

These colours come from Forecut's SS27 colour forecast, built from runway colour shares, editorial colour reports and Première Vision's key colours[1]. Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match: confirm against a physical Pantone book before dyeing.

The evidence

Purple is the season's new colour. Chanel showed lilac, Miu Miu royal purple, and violet ran through Milan[2].

Soft, calming pastels. Pascal K. Douglas described "colours that soothe and lower cortisol levels: butter, lavender, sage"[3]. Victoria Beckham and Icicle in Paris and Ashley Williams in London showed shoulder-padded shirts in light blue, yellow and baby pink; Bottega Veneta paired light-yellow pleated trousers with a bright pink jabot blouse[3]. Dior used a ballet-slipper pink across blazers, skirts and dresses[4].

Strong blocks of primary colour. Chanel, Balenciaga and Celine paired canary yellow, true red and cobalt in strong blocks[2].

White up, browns down. White rose from 23.7% to 28.4% of looks and ecru gained 1.4 points, while chocolate, almond and grey each lost between 1.5 and 1.8 points[5]. Black remains the most worn colour at 35.2%[5].

Gold as a full look. At Saint Laurent, 49 of 59 looks were gold, from brass to rose[4]. Treat gold as a finish (lamé, metallic thread, hardware) rather than a dyed colour.

Colour groups and how much to use

  • Core (about half the range): optic white and black, with sand and midnight from the season's shared key colours for depth.
  • Seasonal (about a third): lilac, violet, butter and true red. Lilac and butter carry day pieces; violet and red carry evening and statement pieces.
  • Accent (the rest): Klein blue, and gold as a finish. One piece or one detail per look.

Combinations and applications

  • Lilac with butter and white. The softest combination: shirts, knitwear and skirts for day.
  • Violet with true red. An evening and occasion pairing; use satin or duchesse to carry the depth.
  • Head-to-toe single colour. Céline, Akris and Stella McCartney showed one shade from head to toe [2]; lilac, red or white work best.
  • Primary blocks. True red, Klein blue and butter in large, flat blocks on tailoring and knitwear.
  • White as the base. Optic white suits, skirts and shirts take any of the seasonal colours as an accent.

Timing

Lilac and violet are rising into SS27 and continue into winter: Première Vision's AW27-28 trend forum opened with deep violet, plums, dusty pinks and mauves [6]. Butter and pastels are a summer story. White is rising for SS27; warm browns are fading.

Products and customers

  • Lilac and butter: shirts, knitwear, skirts, summer tailoring.
  • Violet and true red: occasion dresses, satin skirts, statement tailoring.
  • Klein blue: knitwear, shirts, accessories.
  • Optic white: suiting, shirting, layered summer dresses.

Design implications

  • Approve one standard per colour. Head-to-toe and colour-blocked looks need every fabric and trim to match one approved shade; each white needs its own standard.
  • Test lilac across fibres. Lilac shifts strongly between silk, cotton and polyester; dye lab dips on every base cloth.
  • Use gold as a finish. Lamé, metallic yarns and hardware, not a dyed colour.

Commercial implications

  • Lead with lilac in visual merchandising and keep it in depth across categories, so a customer can build a full look.
  • Buy red and Klein blue lighter. Strong colours sell as statements: fewer units, more styles.
  • Reduce warm browns compared with AW26 and SS26 buys; move the neutral budget towards white and ecru.
