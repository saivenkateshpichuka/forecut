By market

Regional direction for the season, with the trims that fit each market.

Asia

  • Waisted outerwear for East Asia; lightweight layering pieces for tropical markets
  • Craft on knits and festive wear for India and the Gulf winter season
  • Structured tailoring in soft wools for Japan, Korea and northern China

Fits: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Open Johnny-collar knit polos

Europe

  • Waisted and belted outerwear, soft-focus brushed surfaces
  • Irregular, "living" textures from Première Vision AW27-28
  • Quiet tailoring in technical wools

Fits: Utility trench details, Double-face wool overcoats, Raglan-sleeve overcoats

North America

  • Technical outerwear and waisted coats
  • Elevated denim and leather
  • Sneakers step back for loafers and refined shoes

Fits: Utility trench details, Technical parkas, Chore and work jackets

South America

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028, in lighter weights for the tropical north
  • Leather and knitwear for Argentina and southern Brazil

Fits: Waxed cotton jackets, Printed resort shirts, Grandad collar linen shirts

Africa

  • West Africa: occasion and festive (Detty December) rather than cold-weather wear
  • South Africa: AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • South Africa AW27 runway (SAFW, Oct 2026): kaftans, capes and blanket wraps, A-line shapes, handcrafted and unisex denim, ochre, azure and orange-blue palettes

Fits: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Striped rugby shirts

Oceania

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • Merino knitwear and practical outerwear

Fits: Utility trench details, Utility overshirts with snaps, Printed resort shirts

Design notes

How to use the trims.

Menswear, Trousers, peaking

Buckle side adjusters

Adjusters replacing belt loops. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Cricket jumpers

Cables with striped tipping. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, peaking

Double-breasted two-piece

DB suits with wide lapels. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, peaking

Fur and shearling collars

Fur accents on bombers and coats. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Use faux fur or recycled shearling-look pile; make fur parts detachable where you can so the garment is easier to care for.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Faux fur, recycled shearling-look pile or bonded borg, backed with a stable knit.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
