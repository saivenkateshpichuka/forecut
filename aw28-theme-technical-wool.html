Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Tailored wool shorts

Pleated shorts in wool. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.
  • Check length on petite and tall fits.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Technical wool suiting

Wool suiting with stretch and water resistance. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Technical wool travel suits

Crease-resistant wool with hidden closures. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Brushed wool-cashmere coats

Soft, brushed blends with a hazy surface. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Flannel overshirts

Checked flannel overshirts. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, emerging

Quilted suit jackets

Tailored jackets in quilted wool. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Tailored wool shorts
  • Technical wool suiting
  • Technical wool travel suits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Quilted suit jackets
  • Technical merino layers
  • Technical wool cargo trousers
  • Wool-felt flats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Flannel overshirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Double-face wool overcoats
  • Grey melange flannel
  • Leather and wool varsity
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Merino crewnecks
  • Toggle and horn-button closures

Actions

  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
