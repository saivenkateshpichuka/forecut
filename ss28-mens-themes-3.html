The story

What is behind it.

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Taped-seam commuter jackets
  • Technical stretch shirts
  • Unstructured commuter blazers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Technical wool travel suits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Linen travel suits
  • Patch pockets on soft jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Soft suede moccasins

Actions

  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.
