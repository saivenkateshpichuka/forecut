How this forecast was built

Pre-fair forecast. The AW27 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime Paris) take place in January 2027. This report applies the 2026 adult yarn, fabric and leather fair evidence and the AW26 kidswear fair record to kidswear, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • Première Vision's colour preview opens with "grayed mid-tones, as if patinated by time", with purples "taking center stage"[1]
  • Première Vision's AW27 theme "Living Matter" puts materials "at the center", with materials "infused with memory"[2]

Stable

Oat, greyed blue and waxy ochre for equestrian pieces.

Plum Play

Plum and dusty mauve for knits.

Field

Sage and oat for basics.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • One capsule per story.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • The calmest story carries volume.

Evidence and sources

  1. AW 27-28 colour preview: a colour shaped by time, Première Vision, 2026
  2. Autumn-Winter 27-28: "Living Matter", Première Vision, 21 May 2026
