Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Brushed alpaca

Fluffy, brushed surfaces. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, peaking

Double-face wool overcoats

Unlined coats with hand-finished edges. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Rib-knit sweatshirts

Ribbed knits cut like sweatshirts. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, peaking

Tech fleece jackets

Brushed fleece zip jackets. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
