Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Hiker styles

Hiking boots worn with tailoring. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Hiking boots with tailoring

Rugged soles under suits. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Kitten-heel knee boots

Knee boots on low kitten heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Rubber-soled Chelseas

Chelseas on lugged rubber soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Keep the sole light with an EVA or lightweight rubber unit.
  • Use strong elastic gussets and a pull tab for easy wear.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.

Fabric and trims

Lightweight rubber or EVA lug soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Western knee boots

Knee-high boots with western stitching. Stitching is a subtle detail that reads up close and in zoom imagery.

Design notes

  • Offer calf widths or stretch panels for fit.
  • Add stitching on the shaft and a stacked heel.
  • Specify stitch length and thread weight on the tech pack.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, peaking

Belted knee-high boots

Knee-highs with a single buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Hiker styles
  • Hiking boots with tailoring
  • Kitten-heel knee boots
  • Rubber-soled Chelseas
  • Western knee boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Insulated hybrid hikers
  • Weatherproof derbies

Sell through

  • Chelsea boots for tailoring

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Piped satin track jackets

  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Crepe-sole desert boots

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
