AW27 Men's Knit Fabric Direction

Men's AW27 knits: extra-fine merino, brushed and hairy yarns, Shetland-type textures and technical knits.

Season
Autumn Winter 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

On this pageHow this forecast was builtThe evidenceFabric by fabricWhat to do
Research details

Research period Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Woolmark presented merino wool as "the technical fibre" for AW27 sourcing at Pitti Filati
  • Brushed woollens, fur effects, mosses and wool sponges add touch; tapestries, jacquards and nomadic influences form the pattern repertoire
  • Pitti Filati's AW27 research theme "Discipline" draws on equestrian codes: dressage (braided manes, saddle pads), Western reining (Quarter Horse spots, quilt geometry, denim, suede, flannel) and show jumping (stripes, colour blocking, technical knits)
