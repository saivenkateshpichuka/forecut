SS27 Men's Colour Stories

Three colour stories for SS27 menswear: Sun-Faded for tailoring and linen, Aquatic for summer shirts and shorts, and Orange Energy for statements.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Colour
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

Sun-Faded

Optic white, sand, tobacco and ice blue[1]. Tailoring, shirting, linen. Brunello Cucinelli's cable knits with a sun-faded finish, achieved with resin in the dyeing process, show the look in knitwear[2].

Aquatic

Turquoise, sky blue and electric blue on leather jackets, shorts, linen shirts, socks and boat shoes[3]. Pair with white.

Orange Energy

Blood orange, rust, tangerine and true orange, on a sunset-gradient overcoat, a cropped blouson or a V-neck sweater, or as a single accessory such as a bag or cap[3].

Design implications

  • Give each story its own delivery or capsule.
  • Sun-Faded mixes with both other stories; Aquatic and Orange are best kept apart.

Commercial implications

  • Sun-Faded carries volume; Aquatic and Orange Energy carry newness.
