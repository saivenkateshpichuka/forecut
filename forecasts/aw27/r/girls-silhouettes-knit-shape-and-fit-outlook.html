AW27 / Silhouettes / Girls

AW27 Girls' Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Girls' AW27 knits: chunky cardigans, pointelle, kidult intarsia, knit dresses.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Silhouettes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-fair forecast. The AW27 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime Paris) take place in January 2027. This report applies the 2026 adult yarn, fabric and leather fair evidence and the AW26 kidswear fair record to kidswear, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • FashionUnited notes "kidult" knitwear with whimsical faces and regressive animal motifs and a customer raised on Y2K who buys playful charms[1]
  • Pink and mauve are becoming gender-neutral, reaching menswear and even junior sweatshirts[2]
  • Pitti Filati's AW27 research theme "Discipline" draws on equestrian codes: dressage (braided manes, saddle pads), Western reining (Quarter Horse spots, quilt geometry, denim, suede, flannel) and show jumping (stripes, colour blocking, technical knits)[3]

Shapes

  • Chunky cardigan.
  • Pointelle top.
  • Intarsia jumper.
  • Knit dress.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Neck openings wide enough for easy dressing.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Sweatshirts and knits are reorder lines.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  2. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited (Florence Julienne), 4 September 2026
  3. Pitti Filati 99 Research Area: Discipline (Fall/Winter 2027/2028), Pitti Immagine Filati, June 2026
