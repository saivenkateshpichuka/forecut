Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Check tailoring

Prince of Wales and windowpane checks. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Dark rinse, clean finish

Deep indigo with minimal fading. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Floral print midis

Small and painterly florals on midi dresses. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Fluid satin wide legs

Satin trousers for day and evening. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
