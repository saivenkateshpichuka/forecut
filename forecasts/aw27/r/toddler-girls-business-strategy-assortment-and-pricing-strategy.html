AW27 Toddler Girls' Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Assortment and pricing for AW27 toddler girls.

Season

Audience
Toddler Girls
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-fair forecast. The AW27 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime Paris) take place in January 2027. This report applies the 2026 adult yarn, fabric and leather fair evidence and the AW26 kidswear fair record to kidswear, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • Fashion is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027, with markdown risk from excess stock[1]
  • Resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion through 2027[1]

Assortment

Tier What
Core Basics, jeans, sweatshirts
Seasonal Outerwear, knits, cords
Statement Occasion, novelty

Pricing

  • Full price on outerwear and merino.
  • Value on basics and multipacks.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Durable for hand-me-downs.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Weekly sell-through tracking.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
