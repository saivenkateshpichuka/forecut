Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, emerging

Quilted panel skirts

Quilted midi skirts for warmth. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, emerging

Quilted suit jackets

Tailored jackets in quilted wool. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, fading

Dark rinse, clean finish

Deep indigo with minimal fading. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, fading

Double-layer sheer skirts

Two sheer layers, sometimes beaded, worn over another skirt or trousers for SS27. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
