Design notes

How to use the trims.

Kidswear, Tops, peaking

Covered snap fastenings

Snaps instead of buttons for under-3s; tested for pull strength. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Tops, peaking

Ribbed jersey bodysuit

Envelope neck and snap crotch for easy changes. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Occasion, rising

Embroidered anarkali

Flared anarkali with neckline embroidery, cotton lined. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on occasion. Works in kidswear occasion as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Dresses, rising

Hand-smocking

Real or hand-look smocking on yokes and cuffs for occasion wear. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use elastic thread or shirring with enough stretch to pull on easily.
  • Use the yoke to add shape and structure.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • For under-3s, avoid small parts: no beads, sequins or loose pom-poms. Use snaps, sewn-down appliqué and embroidery instead.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
