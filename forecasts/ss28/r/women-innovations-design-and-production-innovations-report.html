SS28 Women's Design and Production Innovations

Design and production for SS28: modular construction, product data under the EU passport timeline, development booked around the January 2027 fairs.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience

Innovations

Market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams[1]
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027, so SS28 production is the first to be planned under it[2]
  • Première Vision Paris presents SS28 collections on 26-28 January 2027[3]

Actions

  • Modular and articulated construction.
  • Fibre and origin data per style.
  • Sample at the fairs; confirm after the shows.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Record fibre, origin, care and test results for every style.

Commercial implications

  • Plan production around Lunar New Year and EU product-data rules.

Evidence and sources

  1. SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
  2. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
  3. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited, 4 September 2026
