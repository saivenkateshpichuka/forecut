SS27 Boys' Harbour Club

Nautical classics for boys: Breton stripes, navy and red, sailor-collar shirts for occasions and stripe sets for every day.

The story

Sailor collars, navy blues and nautical details ran through Playtime Paris, with red and stripes as season-defining [1]. Cozmo showed striped tees and coordinated sets [2]; Loir Paris's sailor top was in MilK's selection [3]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to boyswear, roughly ages 4 to 14.

Colours

Navy, red, white and sand.

Silhouettes

  • Breton tee.
  • Sailor-collar shirt for occasions.
  • Bermuda shorts.
  • Stripe set.

Fabrics

  • Cotton jersey and interlock.
  • Poplin and seersucker for shirts.
  • Linen-cotton for shorts.

Trims and details

  • Contrast tape on collars.
  • Anchor-look buttons.
  • Engineered stripes.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027.

Products and customers

  • Breton tees, sailor shirts, shorts, stripe sets, canvas shoes.

Design implications

  • Match stripes at side seams.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Stripes are reorder lines; sailor shirts are occasion buys.

  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
