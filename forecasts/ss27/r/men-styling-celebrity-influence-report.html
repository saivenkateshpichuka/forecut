SS27 Men's Celebrity Influence

Front rows and designer-celebrities shaped SS27 menswear: Pharrell Williams's surf-themed Louis Vuitton and music stars at couture point to soft leather, satin and statement accessories.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

What was seen

  • Pharrell Williams built a sandy beach and an artificial wave for Louis Vuitton's surf-themed show[1], and Louis Vuitton showed Speedy bags in many colours and beaded monogram versions[2].
  • At Paris couture week, Bad Bunny, Michelle Yeoh and Emma Corrin were among the stars at Schiaparelli[3].
  • Menswear's crossover with womenswear, noted by GQ France's Patrick Clark[1], is visible in what male stars wear: satin, sheer shirts and jewellery.

Looks to build

  • Soft leather blouson with a white tee and straight trousers.
  • Satin shirt open over a rib tank.
  • Beaded or coloured bag as the single statement.

Design implications

  • Translate red-carpet ideas into wearable fabrics and one statement piece.

Commercial implications

  • Time campaigns to festival and award seasons; avoid using celebrity names without agreements.
