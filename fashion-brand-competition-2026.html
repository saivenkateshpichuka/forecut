What this tells you

  • Value fashion is winning in India: Zudio added nearly 200 stores in a year.
  • Globally, Uniqlo is outgrowing Zara and Shein in percentage terms, while Shein's growth and profits have slowed.
  • Speed is the new battleground: fashion delivered in under an hour is spreading from metros to tier-II cities.
  • Scale is not profit: ABFRL grew revenue 11% but still reported a net loss.
