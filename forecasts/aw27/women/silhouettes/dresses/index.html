The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Beaded-fringe canvas dresses

Structured canvas dresses finished with long beaded fringe; Hertunba opened Lagos Fashion Week 2026 Day 2 (29 Oct) with a black canvas dress and red beaded fringe. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Fishtail gowns with attached drapes

Strapless fishtail gowns with a drape attached at the bodice and worn like a pallu, as in Suneet Varma's Kashi, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). A construction change that affects both look and make.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Confirm the factory has the right machinery.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Laser-cut coral-relief dresses

Dresses laser-cut into branching, coral-like openwork and finished with bead embroidery; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 in Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Off-shoulder ruffle dresses

Dresses and corsets cut off the shoulder, with ruffles and gathers along the neckline, at Amir Slama, Ammi and Hisha, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Cut ruffles on the bias or curve for a soft drape; keep volume controlled.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses

Fitted midi sheaths in metallic tweed with an off-shoulder, sculpted neckline; Rebecca Vallance's custom tweed dress for Helena Christensen at Victoria Derby Day, Melbourne (31 Oct 2026), in line with the VRC's sleeveless midi sheath forecast for the carnival. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Fishtail gowns with attached drapes
  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Clean column dresses
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Bias-cut velvet
  • Contrast sash waists

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fringed hems
  • Ruched side seams

Actions

  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
