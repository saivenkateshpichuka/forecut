SS27 Kids' Partywear Styling

Party SS27 kidswear: bold looks and sparkling details from the pool-party runway, with bright colour and reversible statement dresses.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

Pitti Bimbo opened with the Pirouette Disco Pool Party Runway, "bold looks, sparkling details, and positive vibes"[1]. Poco Blush showed bold hues and a reversible printed dress[2].

Formulas

  • Bright dress + sparkle hair accessory + sandals.
  • Sequin or metallic top + shorts.
  • Printed shirt + bright shorts for boys.
  • Reversible dress for two looks.

Design implications

  • Line sequin and metallic pieces; check for sharp edges.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Party buys are seasonal; deliver late spring before summer events.

Evidence and sources

  1. Pitti Bimbo 103: the energy of summer ignites kidswear, Pitti Immagine, 13 May 2026
  2. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
