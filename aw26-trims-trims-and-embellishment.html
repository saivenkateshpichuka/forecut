Trims and embellishment

Build around Ombré sequin gowns, Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos and Beads and sequins on knits. Rich, maximal and tactile. Big outerwear, sharp tailoring and heavy embellishment, with 1920s glamour running through evening and footwear.

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Fully loaded

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Why now

Social media rewards texture and shine, and embellishment makes basics feel special.

Key pieces

Sequins, Beadwork, Sheer layers; sequin and chainmail minis.

Source: Who What Wear, AW26 trends (the source describes the trend without naming brands)

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Why now

Party-season demand meets nostalgia for glamour. Drop waists are easier to wear than bodycon, which helps them sell beyond evening.

Seen at

  • Chanel: Fringed silks and beaded sequins
  • Fendi: Drop-waist dresses and 1920s evening codes
  • Rabanne: Chainmail and sequin minis

Key pieces

Drop waist, Fringe, T-bar heels; drop waist with pleated skirt.

Source: Who What Wear, Autumn/Winter 2026 trends

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Ombré sequin gowns

Long gowns with sequins graded from dense to sparse or light to dark; Elie Saab's dégradé sequins reached the high street in the H&M collaboration launched 22 Oct 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos

Black smoking suits embroidered all over in black, worn as the alternative to a gown; Marian Saud for Débora Plager at the Martín Fierro de Cable awards, Buenos Aires, 12 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Beads and sequins on knits

Embellishment moving onto everyday jumpers. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Brogue perforations

Broguing returns. Perforation adds breathability and a crafted look.

Design notes

  • Use perforations as decoration and for breathability.
  • Back perforations with a breathable lining.
  • Keep perforation away from flex points.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Contrast-tipped knits

Contrast stripes at necks and cuffs. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Cricket cables with tipping

Cable jumpers with striped V-neck tipping. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brogue perforations
  • Contrast-tipped knits
  • Cricket cables with tipping

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Braided officer coats
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Check-trimmed loafers
  • Feathers on satin
  • Gilded embroidered shirting

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Carpenter hammer loops
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Lace-edged bias slips

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Embellish one area per garment (pocket, cuff or yoke).
  • Use sheer layers to restyle existing knits.
  • Keep embellished pieces as limited drops.
  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
