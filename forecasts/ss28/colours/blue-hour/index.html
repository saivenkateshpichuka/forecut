The story

Royal blue, sonar turquoise and inky blue: summer blues with real pigment.

Blues come back strong, royal and a new turquoise, beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge of inky blue and murky green.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

SeasonalRoyal bluePantone 18-3949 TCX Dazzling Blue#3850A0Back and brighter, slightly softened.
SeasonalSonar turquoisePantone 16-5123 TCX Baltic#279D9FA softened turquoise between ocean and digital.
CoreInky bluePantone 19-3933 TCX Medieval Blue#29304EA blue so dark it reads as black: summer's new dark.
CoreBleached linenPantone 11-0701 TCX Whisper White#EDE6DBA warm off-white base for summer.

How to use it

Use for: Shirting, denim, swim, tailoring.

Proportions

Lead with royal blue as the main colour, sonar turquoise as the second, and keep bleached linen to trims, linings or one piece.
