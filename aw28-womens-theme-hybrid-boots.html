Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Hiker styles
  • Kitten-heel knee boots
  • Western knee boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Insulated hybrid hikers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Hybrid sport-smart boots
  • Slim-sole Chelseas
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Flat suede knee boots
  • Lug-sole combat boots
  • Mid-calf western boots

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Embroidered and silk boots
  • Knee-high block-heel boots
  • Low ankle sock boots
  • Low-rise bootcut jeans

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
