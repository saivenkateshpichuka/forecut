Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, emerging

Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts

Pinstripe shirts with one exaggerated collar point, tucked into soft skirts, as at Dior SS27. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, emerging

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, emerging

Beaded-fringe canvas dresses

Structured canvas dresses finished with long beaded fringe; Hertunba opened Lagos Fashion Week 2026 Day 2 (29 Oct) with a black canvas dress and red beaded fringe. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, emerging

Collarless tailored jackets

Jackets with clean V-necklines and no lapels. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
