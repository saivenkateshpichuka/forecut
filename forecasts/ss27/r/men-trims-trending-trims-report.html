SS27 Men's Trending Trims Report

Studs and stones on denim, embroidered birds and florals, contrast piping, flat pockets and coral-like beading lead SS27 menswear trims.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Studded denim: oversized stones on jeans and sweaters, seam outlines in studs and rhinestones, at Dolce & Gabbana, Louis Vuitton, Acne Studios and others; Philipp Plein's crystal-encrusted denim takes days of handwork.
  • Birds: doves and cranes, hand-illustrated, embroidered, printed or silhouetted, at Yohji Yamamoto, Wooyoungmi and Shinyakozuka.
  • Beading: coral-like beaded accents at Dolce & Gabbana.
  • Piping and contrast collars: on Dior's sleepwear sets.
  • Embroidery and colour gradients on shirts: Egonlab, Dries Van Noten.
  • Flat pockets: cropped jackets with flat pockets that work as shirts at Prada.
  • Punk hardware: safety pins and chains at DSM Kei Ninomiya.

Products and customers

  • Studded jeans, embroidered shirts and tees, piped sets, flat-pocket jackets.

Design implications

  • Stud and stone placement should follow seams for a cleaner look.
  • Test bead and stud attachment through washing.

Commercial implications

  • Studded denim at premium; piping and embroidery work at mid price.
