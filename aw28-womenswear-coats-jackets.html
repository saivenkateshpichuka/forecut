The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Bonded, raw-cut seams

Bonded panels with taped or raw-cut edges. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Stop fraying with a stitch line set in from the edge so it stays controlled after washing.
  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Hidden magnetic closures

Fronts that close with concealed magnets instead of buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Stand-collar field coats

Short stand collars on zip-front field coats. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Two-tone panelled coats

Contrasting panels pieced across the body. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets

Cropped zip fleeces with stand collars and gathered drawcord hems, embroidered with tiny flowers; Uniqlo x Cecilie Bahnsen FW26 (announced 5 Nov 2026, Japan launch 20 Nov). The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Short, boxy down puffers covered in floral embroidery; The North Face x Cecilie Bahnsen 'Lucia Nuptse Short' in black and navy, launched in Copenhagen 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Stand-collar field coats
  • Two-tone panelled coats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Packable down liners
  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Sheer lattice capes
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Detachable hoods
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Raglan sleeve coats
  • Toggle and horn-button closures

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
