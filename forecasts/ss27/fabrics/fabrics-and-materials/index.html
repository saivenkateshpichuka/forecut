Fabrics

Build around Piped satin track jackets, Tonal suede low-profile sneakers and Broderie anglaise dresses. The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and colour comes back in full: purple, true red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites and sand.

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

  • Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets
  • Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship
  • Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Piped satin track jackets

Zip-up satin track jackets with contrast piping, as in Born x Raised's Pre-Holiday 2026 drop; sporty polish for evening. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Tonal suede low-profile sneakers

Slim, low-profile sneakers in a single tone of leather or suede; Jil Sander x Puma reworked the Speedcat in all-black, all-white and white suede (on sale Oct 2026). Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Broderie anglaise dresses

White eyelet-embroidered cotton dresses with puff sleeves and tiers, as at Chloé SS27. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Brushed wool-cashmere coats

Soft, brushed blends with a hazy surface. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Chambray work shirts

Chambray shirts with two flap pockets. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Broderie anglaise dresses
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Chambray work shirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Sheer-panel check shirts
  • Mesh and fabric ankle boots
  • Mesh running sneakers
  • Packable technical shells

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Mesh midi skirts
  • Rubber clogs
  • Satin evening pumps
  • Sheer fine-gauge layers
  • Sheer mesh layering tops
  • Suede skate shoes

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.
  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
