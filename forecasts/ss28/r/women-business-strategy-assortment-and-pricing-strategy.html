SS28 Women's Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Assortment and pricing for SS28 womenswear.

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Fashion is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027; resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion[1]
  • C2 Fashion Studio calls SS28 "Structured Clarity": consumers "are no longer asking for more. They're asking for better"[2]

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Linen, shirts, tailoring in bleached linen, straw, inky blue
Seasonal (about a third) Royal blue, mauve, after-dusk dresses
Statement (the rest) Lace, corsetry, crochet

Pricing

  • Full price on technical tailoring and craft.
  • Value on linen basics.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Fewer styles, more combinations.

Commercial implications

  • Track sales-to-stock weekly; plan resale and rework routes.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
  2. Spring Summer 2028 trend intelligence: the rise of structured clarity, FashionUnited (partner content, C2 Fashion Studio), June 2026
