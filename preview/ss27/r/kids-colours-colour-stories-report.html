SS27 Kids' Colour Stories

Three colour stories for SS27 kidswear: Pool Brights, Seaside Calm and Red and Navy.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Colour
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

Pool Brights

Aqua, lemon, coral and grass green for swim, play and summer camp. It reflects Pitti Bimbo's pool theme and colourful summer edition[1].

Seaside Calm

Sand, white, soft navy, vintage pink and pastel green for basics, baby and nightwear. It follows Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" palette[2] and Future Snoops' Lull[3].

Red and Navy

Red, navy and white for stripes, sailor pieces and denim, from Playtime Paris[4].

Design implications

  • One delivery or capsule per story.
  • Seaside Calm mixes with both other stories.

Commercial implications

  • Seaside Calm and Red and Navy carry volume; Pool Brights peaks before holidays.

Evidence and sources

  3. Kids spring/summer 2027 trends outlined by Future Snoops, Playtime, June 2026
  4. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
