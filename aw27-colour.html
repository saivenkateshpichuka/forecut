Overview

Trade-fair signals; runways in February and March 2027.

Violet, plum and tannic bordeaux replace black, over neutrals that are no longer neutral: tinted off-blacks, mushroom beige and greyed blue.

Core

The base of the range: carry these across most styles.

Tinted black, Mushroom, Greyed blue, Copper khaki

Seasonal

What makes the season new: give these real depth.

Deep violet, Tannic bordeaux, Dusty mauve

Accent

Small, high-impact doses: one piece, one trim.

Waxy ochre, Sage, Silicone pink

Colour stories

How the colours combine, story by story.

Painted by time

Plums, bordeaux and waxy ochre, softened as if by an old varnish. Velvet, brushed wool and patinated leather.

Use for: Coats, eveningwear, leather, knits

Deep violet, Tannic bordeaux, Waxy ochre, Tinted black

New neutrals

Neutrals with colour inside: off-black, mushroom, greyed blue and copper khaki.

Use for: Tailoring, outerwear, menswear, denim

Tinted black, Mushroom, Greyed blue, Copper khaki

Pink for all

Dusty mauve and silicone pink move into menswear and tailoring, not only knits.

Use for: Knits, shirting, tailoring, accessories

Dusty mauve, Silicone pink, Mushroom, Deep violet

Living green

Sage and moss from regenerative materials and the outdoors.

Use for: Outerwear, knits, kidswear

Sage, Moss, Copper khaki, Mushroom

By department

Separate palettes for womenswear, menswear, kidswear and footwear.

Womenswear

Deep violet and tannic bordeaux lead, softened by dusty mauve and silicone pink, with waxy ochre as the light.

Menswear

How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the September 2026 fabric and leather fairs (Première Vision, Lineapelle, Milano Unica) and carried forward from the SS27 runways. The fairs put deep violet, plums, dusty pinks and mauves at the centre, with tinted off-blacks, greyish blues, sage and waxy ochre.
