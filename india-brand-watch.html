What this tells you

  • Craft-led festive is mainstream: tissue, gota, bandhani and block print run from value retailers to designer labels.
  • Corsets and ready-to-wear drapes are moving from designer capsules into D2C labels.
  • Pastels with sage and ivory lead day festive; jewel tones lead evening.
  • Indian footwear startups are building on juttis, Kolhapuri shapes and hand-lasted sneakers.
