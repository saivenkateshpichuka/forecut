Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, with radiant earth deepening into rust and brick.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Argyle knits, Cargo pockets, Corset-seamed dresses and Cuffed jean hems are rising.

Test in small quantities

Bonded denim outerwear, Knitted hoods and snoods, Knitted pinafore dresses and Packable down liners are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Asymmetric wrap closures, Band-collar shirts with long placket and Barrel-leg cargo jeans are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Barrel-leg trousers, Basque waist bodices and Beads and sequins on knits are moving out.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Cargo pockets
  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Half-zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded denim outerwear
  • Knitted hoods and snoods
  • Knitted pinafore dresses
  • Packable down liners
  • Quilted panel skirts
  • Quilted suit jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted safari jackets
  • Belted shirt dresses
  • Bishop-sleeve knits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Barrel-leg trousers
  • Basque waist bodices
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Bias-cut velvet

Actions for the range

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Raglan sleeve coats, Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Single-breasted car coats

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Cinched peplum jackets, Lightweight technical trench, Utility vest layers, Quilted liner jackets

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Floral print midis, Matching denim suits, Stretch performance denim, Denim shirt-jackets

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Drop-shoulder shirt dresses, Belted shirt dresses

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Sheer lattice capes, Sheer-sleeve dresses, Floral print midis
