SS27 Toddler Boys' Knit Fabric Direction

Knits for toddler boys: organic cotton jersey and interlock, terry, rib and UPF swim.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceFabric by fabricWhat to do
Research details

Research period Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Salt and Pepper describes its SS27 fabrics as sustainable organic cotton, "exceptionally soft and gentle on the skin"
  • Cadhan showed soft organic cotton pyjamas, with a "Sogni d'oro" line for sensitive skin
  • Swimwear, T-shirts and accessories with built-in UV filters were prominent and are becoming common, linked to family concern for health and to changing climate

Fabric by fabric

Knit Uses
Organic interlock Rompers, sleepwear
Jersey Tees
Terry Sets, towels
UPF swim Swim
Design

Design implications

  • Test for skin-safety and colour fastness.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Basics drive repeat purchase.
