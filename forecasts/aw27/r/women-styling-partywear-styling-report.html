AW27 Women's Partywear Styling

Party AW27 womenswear: velvet, satin, lace and lurex; dressing up without an occasion.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • The Première Vision selection came out "silkier and more influenced by eveningwear codes" than expected: satins, crushed velvets and wool blends[1]
  • Milano Unica: double-faced wool and cashmere, flannels and cashmere fleece ("Classic"); ripstops and ultralight nylon ("Freestyle"); lurex jacquards, sequin tulle, metallic velvets and lamé tweeds ("Romance")[2]
  • Boland's "Dramatic Antidote" and "Unapologetic Presence" seek beauty and seduction; she quotes Dries Van Noten: "The only answer to these times is beauty"[2]

Formulas

  • Velvet blazer + satin slip.
  • Lace dress + boots.
  • Lurex knit + tailored trouser.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Comfortable linings.

Commercial implications

  • Deliver November and December.

Evidence and sources

  1. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited (Florence Julienne), 4 September 2026
  2. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
