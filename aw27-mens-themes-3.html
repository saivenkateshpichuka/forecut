The story

What is behind it.

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Flat-front slim trousers
  • Full straight legs
  • Funnel-neck puffers
  • Funnel-neck zip knits
  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Leather court sneakers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Fly-front placket shirts

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Clean leather bombers
  • Pocketless clean shirts
  • Single and double pleats
  • Utility cargo trousers
  • Wool flannel trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
