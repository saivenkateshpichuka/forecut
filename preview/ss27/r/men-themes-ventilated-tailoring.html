SS27 Men's Ventilated Tailoring

The suit adapts to heat: lighter, open-necked and in places transparent, in silk, gauze, seersucker and perforated leather.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear
Trend status
Rising

The story

Milan designers "went lighter" in silhouette and broadly agreed that a well-dressed man still wears a suit, but the suit is adapting[1]: lighter, less oversized tailoring with open necklines and more airflow, dress shirts left unbuttoned or rendered transparent[1]. Thom Browne showed layered seersucker suiting[1]. In Paris, Jonathan Anderson's Dior collection had fluid suits in very light, almost sheer silk and gauze[2], and Hypebeast tracked sheer double-breasted suits at Dior and sheer trousers at Hed Mayner[3]. At Pitti Uomo, Herno launched crease-proof travel pieces in stretch wool and technical fabrics[4].

Colours

Ice blue, sand and white, with tobacco for depth. See SS27 Men's Season Colours.

Silhouettes

  • Unlined, soft-shoulder jackets with open-necked shirts.
  • Lighter double-breasted suits.
  • Long trousers cut closer to the body.

Fabrics

Silk and gauze[2], seersucker[1], perforated and woven leather[1], fine lightweight wools blended with silk or linen[5], crease-proof stretch wool[4].

Trims and details

Minimal canvas and interlining, unlined backs, mother-of-pearl buttons, open-weave and perforated panels.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027; strongest for wedding season and summer events. Forecut expects lighter tailoring to carry into SS28.

Products and customers

  • Unlined summer suits; seersucker suits; linen travel suits; sheer or semi-sheer shirts.
  • Customers: working men who still wear tailoring in hot climates; wedding guests.

Design implications

  • Remove weight: half-canvas or unconstructed fronts, quarter lining.
  • Test semi-sheer shirting for modesty and comfort before committing.
  • Use crease-recovery and stretch wool for travel pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Lead tailoring with summer weights rather than four-season cloth.
  • Price sheer and silk pieces at premium; seersucker and linen at mid.
