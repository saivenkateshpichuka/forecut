SS28 Men's Couture Materials and Details

Couture materials for SS28 men: technical silk, fine linen, satin.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Men
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams[1]
  • Milano Unica's SS28 theme "Metamorphosis" rests on Lightness, Enrichment ("the rediscovery of handcrafted techniques") and Adaptability, with LIGHTWEAR, HANDWEAR and TECHWEAR themes[2]

Materials

  • Technical silk.
  • Fine linen.
  • Satin.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Check certifications and animal-material rules per market.

Commercial implications

  • Premium materials on few hero pieces.

Evidence and sources

  1. SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
  2. MU Metamorphosis: Spring-Summer 2028 collections, Milano Unica, 2026
