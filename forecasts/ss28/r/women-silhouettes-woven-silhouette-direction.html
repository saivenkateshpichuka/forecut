Live report, updated 26 November 2026

SS28 Women's Woven Silhouette Direction

SS28 women's wovens are projected to be lighter and more adaptable: technical blazers, adaptable skirts, fluid trousers and corset-line evening dresses.

Season
Spring Summer 2028
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2028.

On this pageHow this forecast was builtThe evidenceShapes to lead withWhat to do
Research details

Research period SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams
  • Performance, everyday and occasion wear merge: "technical blazers, adaptable skirts, and tailored baselayers", in sun-warmed neutrals and soft gradients
  • Lace, corsetry and dramatic silhouettes return, reinterpreted with lighter materials
  • SS27 was shaped by heat: Hypebeast called the sun "the true main character of SS27"

Shapes to lead with

  • Technical blazer.
  • Adaptable skirt (wrap, convertible length).
  • Fluid trouser.
  • Corset-line slip dress.
  • Sheer shirt.
Design

Design implications

  • Develop blocks early; confirm proportions after the shows.
