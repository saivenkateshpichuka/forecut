Trim stories

One story per theme.

Alpine Club

Ski-lodge dressing comes down the mountain: patterned knits, fleece and puffers layered over flannel, as seen across the Milan men's shows in the Winter Olympics season.

Workwear Refined

The work coat stays the key outerwear piece, now cleaned up and worn with a tie, tailored trousers or dress shoes.

Slim Line

After seasons of volume, tailoring narrows: streamlined car coats, sharp double-breasted suits and 1930s drape in grey flannel.

Varsity Heritage

Preppy sport in winter weight: rugby stripes, cricket jumpers, varsity jackets and button-downs.
