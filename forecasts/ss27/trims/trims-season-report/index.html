Overview

The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and colour comes back in full: purple, true red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites and sand.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses, Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos, Asymmetric wrap closures and Backless apron tops are rising and keep growing into AW27.

Test in small quantities

Ankle-strap balloon trousers, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Beaded-fringe canvas dresses and Floral-lined showerproof jackets are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Cargo midi skirts, Chunky rib turtlenecks and Contrast collars and cuffs are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Carpenter hammer loops, Large flap patch pockets and Overshirt chest flap pockets are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

Loewe: The return of the tie; Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"; SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats

17-6333 TCX and 12-5404 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

Altuzarra: Deshabille looks; Christian Cowan: Undone layers; Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks; LII: Double-layer minis

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin

17-0610 TCX and 11-0608 TCX

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe; DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts; Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing

16-1448 TCX and 12-1009 TCX

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Backless apron tops
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Barrel leg with curved outseam

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Contrast cuffs
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Floppy oversized collars

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Carpenter hammer loops
  • Large flap patch pockets
  • Overshirt chest flap pockets

Actions for the range

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.

Markets

How each region reads SS27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Best fit: Lace-trimmed slips over knits, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Gilded embroidered shirting, Lace-embroidered poplin shirts

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Best fit: Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Stand-collar field coats, Hidden-placket overcoats, Lace-trimmed slips over knits

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Best fit: Collarless cropped jackets, Lace-trimmed jacket edges, Belted safari jackets, Floral-lined showerproof jackets

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Best fit: Denim with chain and patch trims, Patch-pocket denim jackets, Embroidered denim, Printed resort shirts

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Best fit: Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Gilded embroidered shirting, Lace-embroidered poplin shirts, Band-collar shirts with long placket

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Best fit: Denim with chain and patch trims, Patch-pocket denim jackets, Embroidered denim, Utility trench details
