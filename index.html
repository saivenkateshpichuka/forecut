How it works

From market signal to sample-ready insight.

Signal collection

We monitor runway coverage, street style, search interest and retail launches across India and global markets every week.

Detail-level analysis

AI-assisted analysis identifies construction details, trims and materials, and classifies each as emerging, rising, peaking or fading.

Actionable reports

Monthly detail cards with design notes, fabric guidance and pairing recommendations help your team sketch, specify and sample faster.
