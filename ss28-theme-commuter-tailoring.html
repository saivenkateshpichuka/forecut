Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Articulated sleeves

Shaped elbows for easier movement in outerwear. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Concealed-placket shirts

Fly-front plackets that hide the buttons. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Soft unstructured tailoring

Unlined jackets with soft shoulders. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Remove or reduce shoulder pads and canvassing for a relaxed fit.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Stretch commuter suiting

Suits with stretch and articulated sleeves. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Taped-seam commuter jackets

City jackets with bonded, taped seams. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Technical stretch shirts

Stretch shirts with articulated sleeves. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Articulated sleeves
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Soft unstructured tailoring
  • Stretch commuter suiting
  • Taped-seam commuter jackets
  • Technical stretch shirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Hidden-zip blouses
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Linen travel suits
  • Patch pockets on soft jackets
  • Soft drape jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Soft suede moccasins

Actions

  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.
