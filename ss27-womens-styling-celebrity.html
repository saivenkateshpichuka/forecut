Red-carpet arrival

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In lilac with klein blue.

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses with double front pleats, sharp creases, bermuda suit shorts and lace-up court shoes. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Airport off-duty

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In violet with butter.

Cropped boxy shirts with high-waisted pleated trousers, menswear three-piece suits and mary jane flats. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Press-day tailoring

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In true red with optic white.

Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns with lace-trimmed hems, waistcoat and trouser sets and mary jane sneakers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Front-row statement

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In klein blue with lilac.

Pleated bib fronts with denim waistcoats, black tailoring with fuchsia accents and slip-on canvas sneakers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.
