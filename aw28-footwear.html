Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, with radiant earth deepening into rust and brick.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Bow-trim flats, Flat espadrille sneakers, Hiker styles and Hiking boots with tailoring are rising.

Test in small quantities

Insulated hybrid hikers, Insulated winter sneakers, Weatherproof derbies and Wool-felt flats are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Raffia wedge sandals, Sculpted rocker-sole trainers and Belgian loafers are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs, Sun-bleached distressed sneakers and Tonal suede low-profile sneakers are moving out.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bow-trim flats
  • Flat espadrille sneakers
  • Hiker styles
  • Hiking boots with tailoring
  • Kitten-heel knee boots
  • Rubber-soled Chelseas

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Insulated hybrid hikers
  • Insulated winter sneakers
  • Weatherproof derbies
  • Wool-felt flats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Sculpted rocker-sole trainers
  • Belgian loafers
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Chelsea boots for tailoring
  • Comfort block heels

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Black oxfords
  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Brogue perforations

Actions for the range

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Embroidered and silk boots, Hiking boots with tailoring, Chelsea boots for tailoring, Slip-on knit sneakers

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Horsebit loafers, Check-trimmed loafers, Hiking boots with tailoring, Slim-sole loafers

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Sneaker-sole loafers, Slim, low-profile sneakers, Mary Jane sneakers, Mesh running sneakers

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Flat strappy sandals, Low block sandals, Raffia wedge sandals

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Flat strappy sandals, Low block sandals, Raffia wedge sandals, Embroidered and silk boots

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Flat strappy sandals, Low block sandals, Raffia wedge sandals
