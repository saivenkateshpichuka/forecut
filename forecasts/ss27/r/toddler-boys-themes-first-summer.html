SS27 Toddler Boys' First Summer

Soft first summers for toddler boys: organic cotton sets, first-walker sandals and sun hats.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The story

Pisamonas showed first-summer shoes for babies and barefoot models [1]; Cozmo dresses newborns upward in striped tees and sets [1]; Salt and Pepper uses soft organic cotton [2]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to toddler boys, roughly 1 to 4 years.

Colours

Sand, aqua, navy and white.

Silhouettes

  • Short rompers and two-piece sets.
  • Pull-on shorts.
  • Sun hats.

Fabrics

  • Organic cotton jersey and interlock, muslin.

Trims and details

  • Snap closures, flat seams, printed labels.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Rompers, sets, sun hats, sandals.

Design implications

  • Easy dressing: snaps at the crotch, wide neck openings.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Gift buyers matter; package sets.

Evidence and sources

  1. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  2. Salt and Pepper: Spring/Summer 2027 brand profile, Pitti Immagine Bimbo, June 2026
