The story

Greyish mauve and magnetic violet, soft and hazy against bleached linen.

Blues come back strong, royal and a new turquoise, beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge of inky blue and murky green.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

SeasonalGreyish mauvePantone 15-3507 TCX Lavender Frost#BDABBEA light, atmospheric mauve.
AccentMagnetic violetPantone 19-3847 TCX Deep Blue#44377DA dense, bluish violet for contrast.
CoreBleached linenPantone 11-0701 TCX Whisper White#EDE6DBA warm off-white base for summer.
AccentPeachPantone 13-1125 TCX Peach Quartz#F5B895A soft warm accent for knits and dresses.

How to use it

Use for: Dresses, knits, evening.

Proportions

Lead with greyish mauve as the main colour, magnetic violet as the second, and keep peach to trims, linings or one piece.
