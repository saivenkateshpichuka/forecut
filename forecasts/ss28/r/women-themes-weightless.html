SS28 Women's Weightless

Lightness as luxury: hyperlight wovens, ultralight wool, translucent layers and soft, adaptable tailoring.

Season
Spring Summer 2028
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2028.

On this pageHow this forecast was builtThe storyColoursSilhouettesFabricsTrims and detailsWhat to do
Research details

Research period SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The story

Milano Unica's SS28 "Metamorphosis" starts from Lightness , and wool moves toward ultra-fine, airy and ultralight constructions . Future Snoops expects hyperlight wovens and technical silks .

Colours

Bleached linen, greyish mauve and royal blue.

Silhouettes

  • Unlined blazers.
  • Fluid trousers.
  • Sheer shirts.

Fabrics

Ultralight wool, voile, technical silk.

Trims and details

Minimal, tonal.

Make

Products and customers

  • Tailoring, shirts, layering pieces.
