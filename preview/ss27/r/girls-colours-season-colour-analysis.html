SS27 Girls' Season Colour Analysis

Girls' SS27 colour: soft pastels for dresses and swim, bold brights for play, red as the new accent.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Colour
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The evidence

  • Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" swimwear runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy, in technical fabrics including sustainable lycra, with UV protection[1]
  • Poco Blush, designed "almost exclusively for girls", showed bold hues and a loose, printed, reversible dress, and a T-shirt that releases a minty scent when crumpled[2]
  • At Playtime Paris, red "emerged as one of the defining colors of the season"; stripes ran horizontal, vertical and diagonal[3]

Colour groups

  • Core: sand, white, navy.
  • Soft: lavender, vintage pink, pastel green.
  • Bright: coral, lemon, aqua.
  • Accent: red.

Timing

Pastels stay strong; brights grow.

Products and customers

  • Lavender dresses, coral swim, lemon tees.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip across voile, jersey and swim.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Depth in lavender and sand.
