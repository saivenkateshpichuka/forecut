Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Technical wool cargo trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted field coats
  • Chambray work shirts
  • Cropped work jackets
  • Embroidered pocket shirts
  • Field jackets with bellows pockets
  • Multi-pocket gilets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Chore and work jackets
  • Overshirt-style blazers
  • Patch pockets on soft jackets
  • Pocket squares
  • Utility cargo trousers

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
