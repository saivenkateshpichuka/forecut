Overview

The short version.

Build Asymmetric wrap closures and Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts; sell through Barrel leg with curved outseam and Beads and sequins on knits.

Build

Rising and emerging trims to add.

Sell through and exit

Peaking and fading trims.

By market

Regional direction for the season, with the pieces that fit each market.

Asia

  • Waisted outerwear for East Asia; lightweight layering pieces for tropical markets
  • Craft on knits and festive wear for India and the Gulf winter season
  • Structured tailoring in soft wools for Japan, Korea and northern China

Fits: Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Europe

  • Waisted and belted outerwear, soft-focus brushed surfaces
  • Irregular, "living" textures from Première Vision AW27-28
  • Quiet tailoring in technical wools

Fits: Check-printed quilted puffers, Patent trench with storm flaps, Lightweight technical trench

North America

  • Technical outerwear and waisted coats
  • Elevated denim and leather
  • Sneakers step back for loafers and refined shoes

Fits: Cinched peplum jackets, Collarless cropped jackets, Lightweight technical trench

South America

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028, in lighter weights for the tropical north
  • Leather and knitwear for Argentina and southern Brazil

Fits: Check-printed quilted puffers, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis

Africa

  • West Africa: occasion and festive (Detty December) rather than cold-weather wear
  • South Africa: AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • South Africa AW27 runway (SAFW, Oct 2026): kaftans, capes and blanket wraps, A-line shapes, handcrafted and unisex denim, ochre, azure and orange-blue palettes

Fits: Check-printed quilted puffers, Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Photographic and art prints

Oceania

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • Merino knitwear and practical outerwear

Fits: Check-printed quilted puffers, Sheer lattice capes, Sheer organza overlays

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Smocked knit details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Chunky shawl-collar cardigans

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Backless apron tops
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Long exposed button fly
  • Ruched side seams
  • Rugby stripes, short plackets
