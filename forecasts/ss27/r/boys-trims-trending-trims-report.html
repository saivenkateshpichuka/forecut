SS27 Boys' Trending Trims Report

Boys' trims: side stripes and piping, rubberised badges, sailor tape, bar-tacked utility pockets and reflective details.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Sport influence was presented "through a more refined, creative lens"; denim felt "stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons"[1]
  • Sailor collars, navy blues and nautical details appeared throughout Playtime Paris[1]
  • Junior Style saw a "growing whimsical trend", layered styling and character-driven accessories; Tinycottons showed a "wonderfully chic bellboy character"[1]

Trims table

Trim Application Status
Side stripes and piping Track sets, shorts Rising
Rubberised badges Tees, caps Rising
Sailor collar tape Shirts Rising
Utility pockets Shorts, overalls Core
Character appliqué Tees Core

Design implications

  • Avoid sharp metal hardware; prefer rubber and fabric trims.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Trims refresh core tees and shorts.

Evidence and sources

  1. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
