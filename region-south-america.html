Climate & influences

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

  • European runway trends, adapted a season later for local summer
  • Brazilian beach and swim culture
  • Indigenous and regional craft, sustainable cotton

Runway & news from South America

The latest fashion weeks and collections, summarised with links to the original coverage.

Oct 2026
Barbour x FARM Rio: 'Tropical Greenhouse'

The Rio de Janeiro label's third collaboration with Barbour went on sale on 30 October: Barbour tartan reworked into a brown-and-blue sett and a tropical floral with banana motifs, on showerproof and quilted jackets, reversible liners, overshirts, knitwear, skirts and dresses, some with floral-printed linings.

Source: FashionUnited

Oct 2026
Premios Ídolo, Buenos Aires: corsetry and off-white

At Usina del Arte on 28 October, Argentine guests favoured off-white and bridal-adjacent looks: Lola Latorre in a laced-corset dress with an embroidered skirt by Valentina Schuchner, Cami Mayán in custom Vero de la Canal and host Sofía Gonet in a structured Es Gone dress.

Source: La Nación, Revista Gente

Oct 2026
Colombian designers take Colombia Day at Dubai Fashion Week SS27

On 25 October Laura Aparicio (NANDA) showed oversized, sculptural, fluid looks in yellow, red and blue; Adriana Capasso (TRACE) worked architectural volumes and textile manipulation inspired by Quindío coffee farms; ODA Store (Convergencia) mixed structured tailoring with fluid layers; Mónica Schuster by Sermetex showed couture eveningwear with sculptural capes, gold detail, hand embroidery and crystals. More than 20 FAD Institute emerging designers closed the day, marking its 10th anniversary.

Source: A&E Magazine

Oct 2026
SPFW N61 trend read: Aladdin trousers, satin, halter necks and red

Twelve trends from the 13–18 October São Paulo shows: Aladdin trousers (ALUF, Lilly Sarti, Uó), graphic tees, satin, halter necks, hip volume, off-the-shoulder ruffles, balloon shapes, feathers and fur textures, macramé and crochet with fringe, patchwork, printed sets and intense red. Other highlights: À La Garçonne's trompe-l'oeil shirt prints, Catarina Mina's droplet beadwork, João Pimenta's gender-fluid tailoring with sheer panels, and the debut of crochet couture label Ammi.

Source: Fashion Bubbles, CNN Brasil

Oct 2026
São Paulo Fashion Week N61

Ran 13–18 October with 26 shows, mainly at Pacubra in Ibirapuera Park, as part of SPFW's 30th-anniversary celebrations, with established labels, independents and new voices.

Source: FashionUnited Brasil, O Tempo

Apr 2026
Rio de Janeiro Fashion Week SS27: black minimalism to opulent maximalism

The revived Rio week (14–18 April 2026) showed five directions: black, architectural minimalism (Dendezeiro, Handred, Normando, Karoline Vitto); reworked structured suits (Aluf, Apartamento 03, Helo Rocha, Lucas Leao); crochet, lattice knits and open weaves for day-to-evening cover-ups (Angela Brito, Blue Man, Patricia Viera); one-shoulder swimsuits for more body types (Karoline Vitto, Lenny Niemeyer, Salinas); and heavily decorated maximalism built on Brazilian craft (Isabel Capeto, MISCI).

Source: FashionUnited
