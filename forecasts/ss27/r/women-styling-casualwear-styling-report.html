SS27 Women's Casualwear Styling

Casual SS27 is built from separates and contrast: jeans with a sheer jacket, joggers with a blazer, a rugby shirt with a brooch, and matching denim sets.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe runway looksFormulasWhy it worksWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

The runway looks

Designers styled everyday pieces against something unexpected. Proenza Schouler's Rachel Scott put a ribbed vest and jeans under a ballooning sheer floral jacket; Dries Van Noten pinned a brooch on a rugby shirt; Miu Miu paired joggers with blazers and sarong skirts with silk shirts. Givenchy's Sarah Burton built her collection on well-cut jeans and roomy poplin shirts. A denim jacket or shirt with matching jeans appeared in all four fashion capitals, and Copenhagen added capri trousers.

Rachel Scott summed up the approach: "We're not following rules, like: eveningwear must be worn with this kind of shoe, or bag".

Formulas

  • Jeans + ribbed vest + sheer jacket. Straight-leg jeans, a close rib tank and a light, sheer overshirt.
  • Joggers + blazer. Soft joggers with a cropped, waisted blazer and flat shoes.
  • Rugby shirt + skirt + brooch. A striped rugby shirt tucked into a midi skirt, finished with one brooch.
  • Denim set. Jacket and jeans cut from the same denim and washed together.
  • Capris + knit polo. Cropped trousers with a fine knit polo and ballet flats.

Why it works

Separates are the season's main idea: buyers at Galeries Lafayette Paris describe a fashion "that encourages self-creation rather than adopting a uniform". Casual pieces that combine with the season's skirts and jackets extend each purchase.

Design

Design implications

  • Design casual pieces in the season palette so they mix with skirts and tailoring.
  • Cut denim sets from the same fabric lot and wash to one recipe.
  • Keep casual knits and jerseys clean in finish so they can be dressed up.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Merchandise casual pieces with tailoring, not in a separate casual area.
  • Denim sets sell as two pieces: price the set to encourage buying both.
