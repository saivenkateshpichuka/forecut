Season direction

The collection framework.

Translate the season into material, shape and construction choices for your own customer.

The occasion wardrobe, recomposed

Scenario: an existing saree, skirt or kurta gains new life through a fresh layer. Current modular and lighter-occasion inputs support prototyping, not a prediction of guaranteed uptake.

Product opportunity

Detachable draped panels; separate capes; reversible occasion jackets; mixable saree blouses.

Development action

Make attachment points discreet and stress-test them. Check fit when parts are used independently.

Heirloom surfaces, modern placement

Scenario: restrained craft placement makes premium pieces easier to wear again. Heritage should be credited rather than reduced to a generic luxury claim.

Product opportunity

Narrow woven borders; tonal Bandhani; placed Kantha-inspired handwork through the appropriate maker partnership.

Development action

Approve stitch density and motif placement on actual size sets. Build traceability and artisan payment into sourcing briefs.

Repairable occasion shine

Scenario: aftercare becomes part of the premium proposition. Reflective couture offers a visual reference but creates particular durability and storage challenges.

Product opportunity

Replaceable decorative panels; removable brooches; controlled metallic accents; spare embellished patches.

Development action

Test abrasion, humidity exposure, trim replacement and packing. Supply a realistic care card and repair route.

Local making, contemporary shape

Scenario: recognisable local techniques pair with contemporary separates. The opportunity depends on supply reliability and demonstrated customer interest.

Product opportunity

Light craft jackets; textured straight kurtas; draped skirts with pockets; simple occasion footwear.

Development action

Obtain material and process records. Fit-test for movement and avoid unsupported recycled, regenerative or biodegradable claims.
