AW28 / Styling / Women

AW28 Women's Celebrity Influence

AW28 influence is projected toward confident colour and personal styling rather than single celebrities.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' AW28/29 "Blocked brights": "Bold colour becomes an act of defiance, a refusal to disappear into the algorithmic mass"; blue leads, with red and purple rising, and cornflower as a new "anchor colour"[1]

What to take from it

  • Bold colour as self-expression.
  • Personal combinations.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Design pieces that style several ways.

Commercial implications

  • Avoid real names and likenesses in marketing without agreements.

Evidence and sources

  1. Spotted on the catwalk: Future Snoops' colour trends for AW28/29, FashionUnited (Esmee Blaazer), 20 October 2026
