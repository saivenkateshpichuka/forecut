The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Hidden closures

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Why now

Supports the minimal, structured look and is easier to fasten.

Key pieces

Magnets, Concealed plackets, Hidden zips; hidden magnetic closures.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Key pieces

Stretch wool, Water-resistant, Travel suits; technical wool suiting.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Elbow-seam jumpers

Reinforced elbow seams. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Seamless technical knits

Seam-free quarter-zips with stretch. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Use a matte zip and a stand collar that sits flat.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Textured stripe knits

Striped knits with texture. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Cardigans worn as shirts

Collared cardigans buttoned up as shirts. It changes the whole front view, which is what customers see first online.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Check balance at fit so the front hangs straight.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Funnel-neck zip knits

Full zips on funnel-neck knits. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Elbow-seam jumpers
  • Seamless technical knits
  • Textured stripe knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Technical merino layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Cardigans worn as shirts
  • Funnel-neck zip knits
  • Half-button polo jumpers
  • Henley knits
  • Knitted bomber jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brushed alpaca
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Colour-block knits
  • Fair Isle yokes
  • Heavy shawl-collar cardigans
  • Knitted tank vests

Actions

  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
