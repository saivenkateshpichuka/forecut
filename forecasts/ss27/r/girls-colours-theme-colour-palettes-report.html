SS27 Girls' Theme Colour Palettes

Palettes for SS27 girls' themes: Beach Couture, Wildflower and Playground Pop.

Audience
Girls

Market

The evidence

  • Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" swimwear runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy, in technical fabrics including sustainable lycra, with UV protection[1]
  • Fatamadrina lists oversized florals, checks with fruit prints, paisley, vintage geometrics, nautical themes and gingham, and "Wildflowers Bloom" with delicate handcrafted embroidery[2]
  • Poco Blush, designed "almost exclusively for girls", showed bold hues and a loose, printed, reversible dress, and a T-shirt that releases a minty scent when crumpled[3]

Design implications

  • Share sand and white.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Accents shallow.
