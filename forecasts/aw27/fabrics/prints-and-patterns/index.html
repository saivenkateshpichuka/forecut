The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops

Sweatshirts, tees and dresses printed with the image of a shirt layered on top, as at À La Garçonne, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Argyle knits

Diamond argyle patterns on knits. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable fisherman jumpers

Heavy cables on chunky jumpers. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Keep the knit light enough to avoid sagging.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable-front cardigans

Cables running down cardigan fronts. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fair Isle yoke cardigans

Patterned yokes on button cardigans. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Fair Isle yokes

Patterned yokes on crewnecks. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Use the yoke to add shape and structure.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Lambswool or merino blends in 7–12 gauge jacquard.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops
  • Argyle knits
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Fair Isle yoke cardigans
  • Fair Isle yokes

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Photographic and art prints
  • Slogan T-shirts
  • Striped Breton tops
  • Tartan and check jacquards

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
