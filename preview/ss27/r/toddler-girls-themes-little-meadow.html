SS27 Toddler Girls' Little Meadow

Gentle florals and embroidery for toddler girls: muslin dresses, bloomers and soft knits.

The story

Fatamadrina's "Wildflowers Bloom" uses airy fabrics and delicate handcrafted embroidery [1]; Cadhan uses soft organic cotton for sleepwear [2]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to toddler girls, roughly 1 to 4 years.

Colours

Lavender, pastel green, sand.

Silhouettes

  • Muslin dresses with bloomers.
  • Rompers.
  • Cardigans.

Fabrics

  • Muslin, voile, organic jersey.

Trims and details

  • Embroidery with soft backing, covered buttons.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Rompers, sets, sun hats, sandals.

Design implications

  • Easy dressing: snaps at the crotch, wide neck openings.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Gift buyers matter; package sets.

Evidence and sources

  1. Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026. Design studio forecast; used for season-wide fabric, silhouette and print notes
  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
