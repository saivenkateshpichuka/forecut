The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Why now

Party-season demand meets nostalgia for glamour. Drop waists are easier to wear than bodycon, which helps them sell beyond evening.

Seen at

  • Chanel: Fringed silks and beaded sequins
  • Fendi: Drop-waist dresses and 1920s evening codes
  • Rabanne: Chainmail and sequin minis

Key pieces

Drop waist, Fringe, T-bar heels; drop waist with pleated skirt.

Source: Who What Wear, Autumn/Winter 2026 trends

Fully loaded

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Why now

Social media rewards texture and shine, and embellishment makes basics feel special.

Key pieces

Sequins, Beadwork, Sheer layers; sequin and chainmail minis.

Source: Who What Wear, AW26 trends (the source describes the trend without naming brands)

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Block-heel slingbacks

Slingbacks on mid block heels. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Cap-toe kitten slingbacks

Contrast cap toes on kitten slingbacks. Toe shape is the first thing that dates a shoe, so it signals newness fast.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Develop a new last for the toe shape; check fit across widths.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Embellished flats

Beaded and studded flats. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Horsebit mules

Mules with horsebit hardware. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Shape the footbed so the foot stays secure without a back.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Kitten-heel pumps

Low kitten heels on pointed pumps. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Cap-toe kitten slingbacks
  • Embellished flats
  • Horsebit mules
  • Horsebit vamp hardware
  • Kitten-heel pumps

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Clear PVC heels
  • Curved sculptural heels
  • Feathers on satin
  • Flat strappy sandals

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Ballet flats
  • Satin evening pumps
  • Square-toe ballet flats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Lead evening with one drop-waist dress in two lengths.
  • Add fringe to hems and sleeves rather than the whole garment.
  • Pair with T-bar heels and long pearls in visual merchandising.
  • Embellish one area per garment (pocket, cuff or yoke).
  • Use sheer layers to restyle existing knits.
  • Keep embellished pieces as limited drops.
