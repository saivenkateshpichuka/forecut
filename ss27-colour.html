Colour stories

How the colours combine, story by story.

Sun-faded

Whites, sand, tobacco and ice blue, as if bleached by summer. Linen, cotton and soft tailoring.

Use for: Tailoring, shirting, linen, menswear

Optic white, Sand, Tobacco, Ice blue

Purple reign

From lilac to violet, the season's most talked-about colour, paired with butter for an unexpected summer mix.

Use for: Dresses, knits, leather, evening

Lilac, Violet, Butter, Optic white

Full colour

Red, Klein blue and lemon worn head to toe. Primary colour without prints.

Use for: Statement dresses, outerwear, shoes

True red, Klein blue, Lemon, Midnight

Living green

Grass green and teal from nature and the pool: summer knits, swim and prints.

Use for: Swim, knits, prints, kidswear

Grass green, Teal, Sand, Butter

How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the spring/summer 2027 shows (June menswear and Pitti Uomo, September womenswear) and the Première Vision February fabric fair. White rose the most on the runways; purple was the most reported colour in New York; red returned head to toe; menswear moved to butter yellow, lavender, pink and ice blue over earth tones.
