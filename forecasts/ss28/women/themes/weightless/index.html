How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The story

Milano Unica's SS28 "Metamorphosis" starts from Lightness [1], and wool moves toward ultra-fine, airy and ultralight constructions [1]. Future Snoops expects hyperlight wovens and technical silks [2].

Colours

Bleached linen, greyish mauve and royal blue.

Silhouettes

  • Unlined blazers.
  • Fluid trousers.
  • Sheer shirts.

Fabrics

Ultralight wool, voile, technical silk.

Trims and details

Minimal, tonal.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Products and customers

  • Tailoring, shirts, layering pieces.
