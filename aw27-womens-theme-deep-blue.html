Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Dark rinse, clean finish
  • Brushed mohair
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Funnel neck with concealed zip
  • Half-zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Lab-dip cobalt and ink shades across knit, woven and leather.
  • Use deep blue as a core neutral, not just an accent.
  • Pair with cognac and cream.
