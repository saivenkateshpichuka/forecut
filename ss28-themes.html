Overview

Long-range outlook. Structured clarity and easy movement: fewer, better pieces with performance comfort for commuting, anchored by a warm earth orange.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts, Taffeta jogger evening trousers, Articulated sleeves and Clean column dresses are rising and keep growing into AW28.

Test in small quantities

Articulated-knee trousers, Bonded, raw-cut seams, Concealed-button blazers and Denim with technical details are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Barefoot sole-less sandals, Reworked racing sneakers and Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Cup-bodice gold column gowns, Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns and Ballet-sneaker hybrids are moving out.

The forecast

Select any piece to see its full detail.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Actions

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Actions

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.

Commuter tailoring

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders; stretch commuter suiting.

Actions

  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Actions

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.

Sport meets smart

Cushioned sport soles under refined flats, loafers and derbies: elegant shoes with sneaker comfort.

Key pieces

Sport soles, Hybrid shoes, Comfort heels; sport-sole smart flats.

Actions

  • Develop one sport sole and use it across three uppers.
  • Market comfort features with clear claims.
  • Keep uppers refined to stay smart.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Articulated sleeves
  • Clean column dresses
  • Clean minimal T-shirts
  • Clean straight-leg trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Denim with technical details
  • Elastic-back tailored waists
  • Hidden magnetic closures

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Reworked racing sneakers
  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Broderie anglaise dresses

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Flat-front straight trousers

Actions for the range

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.

Markets

How each region reads SS28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Column knit dresses, Seersucker and gingham shirting, Concealed-placket shirts

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Hidden-placket overcoats, Column knit dresses

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Collarless cropped jackets, Lightweight technical trench, Seersucker and linen jackets, Performance-jersey shirt dresses

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Seersucker sundresses, Seersucker and gingham shirting

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Seersucker and gingham shirting

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Stretch performance denim, Denim with technical details
