Season direction

The collection framework.

Translate the season into material, shape and construction choices for your own customer.

Adaptable summer sets

Scenario: versatility becomes a stronger reason to buy if shoppers want fewer occasion-specific pieces. Airy and adjustable 2027 runway ideas are an early design input only.

Product opportunity

Shirt-and-trouser sets; adjustable wrap kurtas; removable sleeve layers; compact travel capsules.

Development action

Prototype two ways to wear each piece without excess hardware. Fit-test adjustability at both ends of the size range.

Repair is part of the product

Scenario: dependable aftercare helps distinguish premium craft. It depends on service economics and customer adoption, neither of which is established for 2028.

Product opportunity

Replaceable buttons and ties; spare trim kits; accessible seams; detachable occasion panels.

Development action

Document repair steps and stock compatible components. Test that removable trims still survive normal wear.

Craft with a clear origin

Scenario: customers reward specific provenance over broad craft language. Local making is an identity cue, not automatic proof of lower impact.

Product opportunity

Cluster-attributed handloom shirts; small-batch indigo sets; restrained crochet and embroidery accents.

Development action

Record fibre composition, supplier and process; check batch consistency and substantiate any environmental claim.

Soft structure for daily dressing

Scenario: summer tailoring stays relaxed while becoming more purposeful. Build recognisable shapes with better finishing instead of adding novelty to every style.

Product opportunity

Unstructured overshirts; fluid sarees; straight-leg trousers; breathable waistcoats.

Development action

Check drape, collar recovery, seam stability and opacity. Build wear trials into development before range expansion.
