SS27 Girls' Beach Couture

Girls' summer dressing with a couture touch: romantic swimwear, sarongs and skirts, mother-daughter matching, bold reversible dresses and luxury "princess" pieces.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen
Trend status
Rising

The story

Marie Raxevisky's "Beach Couture" line offers UPF 50+ swimwear, sarongs and skirts in fine Italian fabrics and recycled materials, with coordinated adult and child pieces including a mother-daughter match[1]. Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" swimwear runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy[1]. Poco Blush, designed "almost exclusively for girls", showed bold hues and a loose, printed, reversible dress[2]. Speranza and Junona show a luxury "princess" style[2].

Colours

Vintage pink, pastel green and soft navy; coral and lavender; red as an accent. See SS27 Kids' Season Colours.

Silhouettes

  • Swimsuits and two-pieces with cover-up sarongs and skirts.
  • Loose and reversible printed dresses.
  • Occasion dresses with full skirts.

Fabrics

UPF swim fabrics, voile, organic cotton poplin, recycled fine fabrics.

Trims and details

Ruffles, smocking, embroidered flowers, bows.

Timing

In stores March to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Swim and cover-up sets, sarongs, printed and reversible dresses, mother-daughter capsules.
  • Customers: girls roughly 4 to 12 and their families.

Design implications

  • Keep ruffles and bows securely attached and away from the mouth area for younger girls.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Mother-daughter capsules extend the basket; price as a set.

Evidence and sources

  1. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  2. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
