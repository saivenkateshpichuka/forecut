The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

The waist returns

After years of oversized shapes the waist is back. Peplums, basque waists, balloon and bubble shapes and belted or wrapped fronts define the body again.

Why now

Forecasters see a return to elegance and shape. A defined waist refreshes existing silhouettes without new categories.

Seen at

  • Li Edelkoort, "Craft of Black": Wraps and belts the waist tightly
  • SS27 bridal and evening: Corseted bodices, basque waists and bubble skirts
  • Double belt: Returns as a waist accessory

Key pieces

Peplum, Basque waist, Bubble hem; basque waist bodices.

Source: FashionUnited, Spring/Summer 2027 trend report

Reworked denim

Denim reimagined: white cut-off shorts, cobalt-painted jeans, wax-coated bootcuts, embroidered and beaded denim, upcycled pieces and matching denim suits.

Why now

Denim is a core volume category, and new finishes refresh it without new fits.

Seen at

  • Area: Cut-off white shorts and trousers
  • Simkhai: Wax-coated bootcut jeans
  • PatBo: Embroidered and beaded denim
  • 7 For All Mankind: Skinny jeans
  • Miu Miu × Wrangler: Upcycled denim edition

Key pieces

Cut-offs, Coated denim, Denim suits; cut-off denim shorts.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Generous 1980s double-breasted suits

Roomy double-breasted suits with pleated, higher-waisted trousers in a late-80s/early-90s line; the VRC's Melbourne Cup Carnival 2026 forecast names them for race-day menswear, alongside the return of the tie. A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Low-rise bootcut jeans

Low-slung jeans, slim through the thigh with a slight flare below the knee, as in Madhappy x Lucky Brand (Oct 2026). This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Balloon trousers

Volume gathered into the ankle. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Barrel-leg cargo jeans

Curved legs with cargo pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Balloon trousers
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted field coats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops
  • Sheer lattice capes
  • Basque waist bodices
  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted trench blazers
  • Cropped blazers
  • Denim trucker jackets
  • Denim waistcoats
  • Long denim coats
  • Mid-rise relaxed jeans

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Drop waist with pleated skirt
  • Low-slung belted waists

Actions

  • Add a peplum version of your best-selling blouse.
  • Cut a basque-waist seam into a core dress block.
  • Sell double belts as an add-on accessory.
  • Add a coated finish to one core fit.
  • Sell denim suits as matching separates.
  • Shade-band jacket and jeans together to avoid mismatches.
