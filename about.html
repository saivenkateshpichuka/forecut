About

Forecasting fashion, one detail at a time.

Forecut is a detail-level fashion trend forecasting platform built for the Indian market. We help designers, brands and manufacturers decide which details to develop next, and when.

Why Forecut exists

Most trend reports describe a mood. The people who make clothes need specifics: which collar, which trim, which fabric, and when it will peak. Indian businesses also plan around their own calendar of festivals, weddings and summer, which global reports rarely reflect.

Forecut brings both together. We track garments, footwear, fabrics, embroidery, trims and colours down to individual construction details, and mark each one as emerging, rising, peaking or fading across India's festive windows and the global seasons through 2028.

What we offer

  • India forecast: ethnic and western wear, footwear, handloom weaves, embroidery and trims, planned around the Indian calendar.
  • Global seasons: detail-level reports from AW26 to AW28, with themes, colours of the year and price guides in rupees.
  • Practical detail cards: design notes, fabric and trim guidance, and downloadable cards for teams.
  • Monthly updates: forecasts are reviewed and refreshed every month.

How we work

We review public runway coverage, trade and retail reporting, street style and search interest, then use AI-assisted analysis to identify and track specific details. Every forecast is curated and written in our own words. Brands and designers are named only where public sources report them.

Where we are

Forecut is an independent platform made in India. It is currently in an early-access testing phase, with full access available at no cost. Your feedback directly shapes the launch.
