Trim stories

One story per theme.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Earth deepens

Warm earth orange deepens into rust and brick, often on quilted and padded pieces.

Hybrid boots

Smart boots on cushioned sport soles: Chelseas, hikers and weatherproof derbies.

Hidden closures

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Denim with technical details
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Stand-collar field coats
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Cargo pockets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Utility vest layers
  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted safari jackets
  • Carpenter jeans
  • Flap-pocket blazers
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Large flap patch pockets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Welt-pocket cocoon coats
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Toggle and horn-button closures
  • Velvet lapel facings
