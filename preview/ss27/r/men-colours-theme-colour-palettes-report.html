Why these palettes

They follow the runway colour: monochrome sets at Celine and IM Men[1], khaki, blue and beige sleepwear sets with black piping at Dior[2], nude and aquamarine at Dries Van Noten[2], and Forecut's SS27 menswear palette[3].

Design implications

  • Lead colour on most of the look; accent on one piece or the piping.
  • Share sand across themes so pieces cross-sell.

Commercial implications

  • Build depth in sand and ice blue; accents as small buys.
