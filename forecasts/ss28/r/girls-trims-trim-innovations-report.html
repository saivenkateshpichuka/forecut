SS28 Girls' Trim Innovations

Kidswear innovations: UPF labelling, cooling finishes, easy closures.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Girls
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime) take place in June 2027. This report projects from SS28 adult previews, the SS27 kidswear fair record and long-term drivers such as heat and UV protection, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams[1]
  • Everyday basics gain "integrated wellness properties" and materials that "intuitively adjust for temperature and activity"[1]

Readiness

Innovation Readiness (Forecut assessment)
UPF labelling Ready
Cooling finishes Early; test skin safety
Easy closures Ready

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Design for repair and disassembly.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Function supports full price.

Evidence and sources

  1. SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
