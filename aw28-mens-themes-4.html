Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Hiking boots with tailoring
  • Rubber-soled Chelseas

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Weatherproof derbies

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Check sports jackets
  • Chelsea boots for tailoring
  • Hybrid sport-sole derbies
  • Sport slides
  • Trail-sole hybrids

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Crepe-sole desert boots
  • Derby boots
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Quarter-zip tops
  • Suede chukkas
  • Windbreaker as a blazer

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
