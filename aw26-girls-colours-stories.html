Colour stories

How the colours combine.

Dark matter

Browns, charcoal and midnight replace head-to-toe black. Tone on tone, with texture doing the work: brushed wool, suede, velvet.

Use for: Coats, tailoring, knitwear, leather goods

Chocolate 19-1218 TCX, Charcoal 19-0201 TCX, Midnight 19-3923 TCX, Cloud white 11-4202 TCX

Old masters

Rich, painterly plum and moss with mustard as the light. Grown-up colour for velvet, satin and heavy knits.

Use for: Eveningwear, knits, tailoring

Plum 19-3218 TCX, Moss 18-0220 TCX, Mustard 15-0751 TCX, Chocolate 19-1218 TCX

Hot spots

Single saturated pieces against dark neutrals: a tomato-red leather coat, a cobalt shoe, a bubblegum blazer.

Use for: Outerwear, shoes, bags, statement pieces

Tomato red 18-1564 TCX, Cobalt 18-3949 TCX, Bubblegum 14-2311 TCX, Charcoal 19-0201 TCX

How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the autumn/winter 2026 shows (January menswear, February and March womenswear) and in-store sell-in. Chocolate was the second most-worn colour on the womenswear runways after black, charcoal and pine green grew the most, and plum, moss, tomato red and mustard were the colours reviewers singled out.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
