SS27 / Silhouettes / Girls

SS27 Girls' Woven Silhouette Direction

Girls' woven shapes: pleated, tiered and reversible dresses, puff-sleeve blouses, wide shorts and skirts, sailor-collar tops.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Silhouettes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The evidence

  • Fatamadrina lists relaxed shapes, dungarees, oversized T-shirts, pleated dresses and lightweight jackets as SS27 silhouettes[1]
  • Poco Blush, designed "almost exclusively for girls", showed bold hues and a loose, printed, reversible dress, and a T-shirt that releases a minty scent when crumpled[2]
  • Sailor collars, navy blues and nautical details appeared throughout Playtime Paris[3]

Shapes to lead with

  • Pleated and tiered dresses.
  • Loose reversible dress.
  • Puff-sleeve blouse.
  • Wide shorts and skorts.
  • Sailor-collar dress.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Dresses, blouses, skirts.

Design implications

  • Easy openings; back buttons only from older sizes.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Dresses carry the woven buy.

Evidence and sources

  1. Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026. Design studio forecast; used for season-wide fabric, silhouette and print notes
  2. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
  3. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
