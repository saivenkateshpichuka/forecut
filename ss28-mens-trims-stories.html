Trim stories

One story per theme.

Commuter Tailoring

Suits built for the day: stretch, packable, machine-washable and soft-shouldered for travel and the office.

Performance Ease

Sportswear function moves into everyday clothes: articulation, breathable mesh and quick-dry fabrics.

Natural Tech

Natural fibres that perform: linen, merino and cotton finished for easy care.

Sport Meets Smart

Hybrid shoes and sport soles under tailoring set the tone for the whole look.
