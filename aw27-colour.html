Overview

Early outlook. SS27 signals carried into winter: waisted outerwear, handcraft on knits, quiet structure and warmer earth tones, with luminous blue deepening for the cold season.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets, Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers and Elongated evening jumpsuits are rising and keep growing into SS28.

Test in small quantities

Ankle-strap balloon trousers, Lacquered funnel-neck trenches, Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts and Belted knee-high boots are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Cup-bodice gold column gowns, Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns and Low-rise bootcut jeans are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Long denim coats and Sheer fine-gauge layers are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers
  • Elongated evening jumpsuits
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Fringed macramé tops

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted suit jackets
  • Clean column dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Balloon trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long denim coats
  • Sheer fine-gauge layers

Actions for the range

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.

Markets

How each region reads AW27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Waisted outerwear for East Asia; lightweight layering pieces for tropical markets
  • Craft on knits and festive wear for India and the Gulf winter season
  • Structured tailoring in soft wools for Japan, Korea and northern China

Best fit: Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Lace-trimmed slips over knits

Europe

  • Waisted and belted outerwear, soft-focus brushed surfaces
  • Irregular, "living" textures from Première Vision AW27-28
  • Quiet tailoring in technical wools

Best fit: Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Hidden-placket overcoats, Brushed wool-cashmere coats, Sheer lattice capes

North America

  • Technical outerwear and waisted coats
  • Elevated denim and leather
  • Sneakers step back for loafers and refined shoes

Best fit: Cinched peplum jackets, Collarless cropped jackets, Belted safari jackets, Suede equestrian jackets

South America

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028, in lighter weights for the tropical north
  • Leather and knitwear for Argentina and southern Brazil

Best fit: Photographic and art prints, Long denim coats, Denim waistcoats, Embroidered denim

Africa

  • West Africa: occasion and festive (Detty December) rather than cold-weather wear
  • South Africa: AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • South Africa AW27 runway (SAFW, Oct 2026): kaftans, capes and blanket wraps, A-line shapes, handcrafted and unisex denim, ochre, azure and orange-blue palettes

Best fit: Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Photographic and art prints, Belted shirt dresses, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Oceania

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • Merino knitwear and practical outerwear

Best fit: Sheer lattice capes, Photographic and art prints, Sheer fine-gauge layers, Double-layer sheer skirts
