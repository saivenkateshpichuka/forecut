The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Dark rinse, clean finish

Deep indigo with minimal fading. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, emerging

Sun-bleached distressed sneakers

Trainers made in deliberately distressed, sun-bleached fabrics on a cushioned retro sole; Puma x JiyongKim Cell Geo, Seoul (Korea Herald, 15 Nov 2026). The finish changes the surface and handle without new patterns.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Test the finish after repeated washing.
  • Keep finish standards on file for repeat orders.

Fabric and trims

Leather, suede, mesh or knit uppers, rubber cupsoles or EVA midsoles, recycled laces.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Dark rinse, clean finish

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Black and grey denim

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Heavy rips and distressing
  • High-shine puffers

Actions

  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
