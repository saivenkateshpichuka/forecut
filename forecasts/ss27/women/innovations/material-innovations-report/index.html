SS27 Women's Material Innovations

Materials to watch for SS27 womenswear: lab-grown silk, stretch satins and taffetas, water-repellent linens, technical cottons, softshell and compact knits, and coated surfaces.

The evidence

  • Lab-grown silk: Balenciaga used AMSilk silk in its July 2026 couture collection[1].
  • Stretch satins: Première Vision reports satins, double satins and taffetas moving towards stretch[2].
  • Technical cottons and water-repellent linens: part of the "Robust Elegance" direction, with cottons gaining strength through yarn and weave[2].
  • Compact milanos and softshells: in "Powerful Flexibility"[2].
  • Membrane-backed draperies, spacers and rubberised meshes: structural fabrics borrowed from corsetry[2].
  • Coated surfaces: vinyl and translucent PVC, latex-effect rubber and shiny nylon on the SS27 runways[3].

Readiness

Material Readiness (Forecut assessment) Use in SS27
Stretch satin and taffeta Ready, widely available Skirts, fitted jackets
Water-repellent linen Ready from specialist mills Trench coats, jackets
Technical cotton Ready Workwear-inspired jackets and trousers
Compact milano, softshell Ready Structured knit jackets and skirts
Corsetry meshes and spacers Ready from lingerie suppliers Bodices, bra tops
Lab-grown silk Early; limited supply and high cost Showpieces only
PVC and coated fabrics Ready; check environmental claims and care Accent pieces

Further reading

Forecut's fabric innovation reports cover regenerated fibres, recycled polyester, bio-based materials and finishing in more depth; see the Innovations articles linked from this category.

Design implications

  • Use stretch satins to make structured skirts and jackets easier to wear.
  • Test coated fabrics for cracking, peeling and cleaning; label care clearly.
  • Consider water-repellent linen for unlined summer outerwear.

Commercial implications

  • Ready materials can go into SS27 volume; early ones belong in a few hero pieces.
  • Check every sustainability claim (bio-based, recycled, lab-grown) against supplier certificates before using it in marketing.
