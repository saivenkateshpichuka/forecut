Overview

The short version.

Borrow one couture idea per piece and keep the rest simple, so the cost stays under control.

Translations

From the atelier to your price point.

Hand technique in one zone

Work embroidery or beading into a yoke, cuff, neckline or hem border instead of the whole garment.

Structure without weight

Use princess seams, light interfacing and corset seaming in place of boning and heavy canvas.

Volume from light fabrics

Pleated organza, gathered tulle and bias-cut satin give drama at a lower weight and cost.

Finish as the story

Hand-felled hems, bound seams and covered buttons tell the customer where the value is.
