The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Commuter tailoring

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders; stretch commuter suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Floral-lined showerproof jackets

Showerproof field and quilted jackets with printed floral linings and reversible liners; Barbour x FARM Rio 'Tropical Greenhouse', on sale 30 Oct 2026. Lining details add a quality signal when the garment is open.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Choose a lining that is breathable and anti-static.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Lacquered funnel-neck trenches

Trench coats in a lacquered, high-shine finish with a tall funnel neck instead of a notch collar; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027 (seafoam), New York, 6 Nov 2026. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.
  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Sheer lattice capes

Open lattice or sheer capes layered over co-ords and bustiers; shown by Hertunba at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct) over a black-and-white woven peplum set. A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Make the cape detachable or attached at the yoke so the base garment sells on its own.
  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Shell-paillette jacket edging

Tweed and boxy jackets finished with shell-shaped paillettes along the fronts and hems; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 re-staged at Barangaroo, Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Woven-stripe longline overlayers

Ankle-length open overlayers in hand-woven stripes running in several directions; DARRA reworked Javanese lurik, ikat and songket this way at Jakarta Fashion Week 2027 (27 Oct 2026). A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.

Fabric and trims

Engineered mesh or knit uppers.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Articulated sleeves

Shaped elbows for easier movement in outerwear. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Sheer lattice capes
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Woven-stripe longline overlayers
  • Articulated sleeves

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Hidden magnetic closures

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Detachable hoods
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Raglan sleeve coats
  • Seersucker and linen jackets
  • Tie-cuff sleeves

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cropped parkas over evening gowns
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Lace-trimmed jacket edges
  • Patent trench with storm flaps
  • Shaped elbow seams

Actions

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
