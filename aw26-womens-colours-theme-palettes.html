How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the autumn/winter 2026 shows (January menswear, February and March womenswear) and in-store sell-in. Chocolate was the second most-worn colour on the womenswear runways after black, charcoal and pine green grew the most, and plum, moss, tomato red and mustard were the colours reviewers singled out.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
