AW28 / Couture and craft / Women

AW28 Women's Couture Materials and Details

Couture materials for AW28 women: suede, velvet, lace, double-faced cashmere.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Deep classic brown in leather and leather-look pieces, sporty pieces and tailoring, suited to suede and canvas-like materials[1]
  • AW27 fairs made wool lighter and softer, brought back double-faced woollens and hid performance inside classic suitings[2]

Materials

  • Suede.
  • Velvet.
  • Lace.
  • Double-faced cashmere.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Check certifications and animal-material rules per market.

Commercial implications

  • Premium materials on few hero pieces.

Evidence and sources

  1. Spotted on the catwalk: Future Snoops' colour trends for AW28/29, FashionUnited (Esmee Blaazer), 20 October 2026
  2. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited, 4 September 2026
