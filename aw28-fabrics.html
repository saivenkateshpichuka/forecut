Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, with radiant earth deepening into rust and brick.

The headline

A mix of fresh ideas and pieces carried on from last season.

Build into the range

Argyle knits, Leather-look trousers, Satin slip skirts and Sock-knit trainers are rising.

Test in small quantities

Hybrid quilted bombers, Insulated overshirts, Insulated winter sneakers and Quilted dinner jackets are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Black and grey denim, Brushed cardigan coats and Brushed wool-cashmere coats are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Brushed alpaca, Cable fisherman jumpers and Cable-front cardigans are moving out.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Leather-look trousers
  • Satin slip skirts
  • Sock-knit trainers
  • Textured stripe knits
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated overshirts
  • Insulated winter sneakers
  • Quilted dinner jackets
  • Quilted panel skirts
  • Quilted suit jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Black and grey denim
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Chambray work shirts
  • Check sports jackets
  • Check tailoring

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brushed alpaca
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Corduroy suits
  • Dark rinse, clean finish
  • Double-face wool overcoats

Actions for the range

  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Column knit dresses, Technical stretch shirting, Thermal-knit base layers

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Brushed wool-cashmere coats, Column knit dresses, Thermal-knit base layers

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Quilted liner jackets, Suede equestrian jackets, Performance-jersey shirt dresses

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Floral print midis, Stretch performance denim, Raw selvedge denim, Black and grey denim

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Floral print midis, Technical stretch shirting, Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Floral print midis, Sheer organza blouses, Double-layer sheer skirts, Stretch performance denim
