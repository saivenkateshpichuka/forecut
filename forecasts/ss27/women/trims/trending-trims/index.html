Spring/Summer 2027, Women, Trims, Trending Trims

SS27 Women's Trending Trims

Decoration returns in force: three-dimensional petals and corsages, ribbons used as structure, lingerie lace, crystal embroidery and fringe. Belts and visible fastenings carry the season's waist and separates story.

The evidence

Première Vision called the season's ornamentation a "decorative overload", drawing on historical ceremonial dress and on insects and microscopic forms, with caterpillars, fringes, ribbons, beads and fractals as motifs[1]. It singled out ribbon as material, ornament and structure: finishing ribbons, highly decorative ribbons and ribbons built into the fabric itself, inlaid into jacquards or applied onto tulle and lace[1].

The runways confirmed it. Dior used organza petals and hand-cut petals[2][3]; Chanel used corsages in the manner Coco Chanel favoured[2]; Prada placed crystal flowers on jewellery-like skirts and embroidered florals on Velcro fastenings[2]. Dries Van Noten showed sequinned flowers peeking from a hem and a brooch on a rugby shirt[2]. Fringe ran through Roksanda's tasselled evening dresses and Ahluwalia's fringed skirts, and was a fast-rising detail in Copenhagen[4].

Fastenings became decoration. Prada used coloured Velcro strips[2]; Miu Miu laced houndstooth skirts with cross-lace fastenings, left undone[2]; Ferragamo enlarged belts and covered snap buttons in sequins[3]. Belts cinched fluid pieces at Anteprima, Ferragamo, Alberta Ferretti and Ermanno Scervino, and Bottega Veneta and Max Mara used leather belts[3].

Lingerie trims moved outside: Fendi showed lace-trimmed bras with a fur stole[2] and a black embroidered satin triangle bra[5]; Dreaming Eli used old-fashioned lace and satin[5].

Why it matters

After seasons of minimal finishing, trims are the fastest way to make a simple skirt, shirt or knit read as SS27. They also travel well across price points: a corsage, a ribbon tie or a belt can carry the trend at entry price where a fully embellished garment cannot.

Timing

Petals, corsages and ribbons are rising for SS27 and suit the spring occasion calendar. Lingerie lace is at its height. Fringe and visible fastenings are early ideas to test. Forecut expects belts and ribbons to continue into AW27.

Products and customers

  • Corsages and petal trims: occasion dresses, satin tops, jackets (also as separate accessories).
  • Ribbon: bow-tie blouses, ribbon-laced skirts, ribbon-bound edges on jackets.
  • Lace: slip skirts and dresses, camisoles, bra tops.
  • Belts: sold with jackets and dresses, and as accessories.

Design implications

  • Keep trims removable where possible. Detachable corsages and belts reduce returns and allow restyling.
  • Specify attachment. Petals and beads need secure stitching and wash testing; state hand-wash or dry-clean care.
  • Match colour exactly. Trims must be lab-dipped to the garment colour, especially in head-to-toe looks.
  • Source ribbon early. Jacquard and ribbon-inlaid fabrics need longer lead times[1].

Commercial implications

  • Use trims to update core shapes rather than buying new styles: the same skirt with a corsage, a ribbon or a belt.
  • Sell trims as accessories too. Corsages, brooches and belts add units per transaction at low cost.
  • Cost carefully. Hand-applied petals and beads add labour; use them at premium price points and keep entry pieces to one trim.
