Season direction

The collection framework.

Translate the season into material, shape and construction choices for your own customer.

Cloth before decoration

Summer comfort is the starting point. Develop tactile cotton, linen and fine woven sets whose value comes from handle, proportion and finish.

Product opportunity

Straight kurta with relaxed trousers; linen overshirt; handloom shirt dress; lightweight everyday saree.

Development action

Compare unlined and half-lined samples. Test shrinkage, opacity, seam slippage and hand feel after laundering. Avoid heavy fusible panels that defeat airflow.

Light, with coverage

Sheer runway layers become wearable when opacity is designed into the garment. The Indian opportunity is a light overlay with an intentional inner, not an exposed look copied from a show.

Product opportunity

Organza over cotton-silk; airy cape over a day lehenga; fluid dupatta; sleeveless inner with a loose shirt.

Development action

Specify inner length, armhole coverage and attachment points. Check snagging, perspiration marks and movement in daylight.

Playful craft, controlled scale

Use handwork as a recognisable small detail, with clear maker attribution. Current designer and brand references support experimentation without establishing a universal trend.

Product opportunity

Contrast hand-stitched pockets; crochet-edge shirts; indigo printed co-ords; small patchwork panels.

Development action

Keep high-labour detailing away from seams and alteration points. Approve print registration, wash fastness and repeatable stitch quality.

Occasionwear that moves

Bring festive dressing into summer through less bulk and more flexible styling. Practical menswear construction is a relevant Indian lead signal.

Product opportunity

Light embroidered kurta sets; draped skirt separates; a soft Nehru layer; rewearable wedding-guest lehenga.

Development action

Use lighter linings, practical pockets and secure drape fastenings. Fit seated, walking and with raised arms; check trim abrasion.

Plan the work, then validate it.

    Set customer, occasion and price architecture; source fabric and make first silhouettes.

  2. December 2026

    Approve washed fit samples, colour fastness, opacity and costing; book craft capacity.

    Review actual full-price sales, returns and replenishment weekly; do not confuse social engagement with purchase.
