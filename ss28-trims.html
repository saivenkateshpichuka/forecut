Overview

Long-range outlook. Structured clarity and easy movement: fewer, better pieces with performance comfort for commuting, anchored by a warm earth orange.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Floral-lined showerproof jackets, Lacquered funnel-neck trenches, Shell-paillette jacket edging and Asymmetric wrap closures are rising and keep growing into AW28.

Test in small quantities

Bonded, raw-cut seams, Cargo pockets, Hidden magnetic closures and Articulated-knee trousers are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses, Belted wrap fronts and Block-heel slingbacks are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Brooch-fastened cardigans, Buckle side adjusters and Collarless cropped jackets are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Band-collar shirts
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Cargo pockets
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Denim with technical details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Bubble hems
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Button-shoulder knits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Denim with chain and patch trims

Actions for the range

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.

Markets

How each region reads SS28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Gilded embroidered shirting, Lace-embroidered poplin shirts, Band-collar shirts with long placket, Concealed-placket shirts

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Stand-collar field coats, Hidden-placket overcoats, Lacquered funnel-neck trenches

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Collarless cropped jackets, Lace-trimmed jacket edges, Belted safari jackets, Floral-lined showerproof jackets

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Denim with chain and patch trims, Denim with technical details, Patch-pocket denim jackets, Embroidered denim

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Gilded embroidered shirting, Lace-embroidered poplin shirts, Band-collar shirts with long placket, Concealed-placket shirts

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility shirt-jackets, Denim with chain and patch trims, Denim with technical details, Patch-pocket denim jackets
