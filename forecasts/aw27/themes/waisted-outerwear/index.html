Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

Key pieces

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Generous 1980s double-breasted suits

Roomy double-breasted suits with pleated, higher-waisted trousers in a late-80s/early-90s line; the VRC's Melbourne Cup Carnival 2026 forecast names them for race-day menswear, alongside the return of the tie. A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops

A satin dress worn pulled down into a floor-length low-rise skirt with lace at waist and hem and a matching bra top; Simone Rocha SS27 on Camila Cabello at the Latin Grammys, Las Vegas, 12 Nov 2026. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Sheer lattice capes

Open lattice or sheer capes layered over co-ords and bustiers; shown by Hertunba at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct) over a black-and-white woven peplum set. A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Make the cape detachable or attached at the yoke so the base garment sells on its own.
  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Shirred-waist fleece skirts

Soft fleece skirts with a shirred, adjustable waist and frills in a contrast material; Uniqlo x Cecilie Bahnsen AW26 'winter walk', on sale in Japan from 20 Nov 2026. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Cut ruffles on the bias or curve for a soft drape; keep volume controlled.
  • Use elastic thread or shirring with enough stretch to pull on easily.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lightweight cotton, seersucker or viscose.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Wired peacoats

Double-breasted peacoats with wired edges that hold a curved, stand-away shape; OPEN YY Fall/Winter 2026, Seoul (2 Nov 2026). A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops
  • Sheer lattice capes
  • Shirred-waist fleece skirts
  • Wired peacoats
  • Asymmetric wrap closures

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted suit jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted high waists
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Contrast sash waists
  • Cropped blazers
  • Cummerbunds over suits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long denim coats

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
