Design notes

How to use the trims.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Taped-seam commuter jackets

City jackets with bonded, taped seams. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, emerging

Articulated-knee trousers

Shaped knees for movement. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, fading

Wide-lapel suits

Broader lapels on two-piece suits. Lapel shape sets the attitude of a jacket before anything else is noticed.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Keep the roll line soft; pad-stitch or use a light canvas on premium styles.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, fading

Utility trench details

Gun flaps, epaulettes and belts. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
