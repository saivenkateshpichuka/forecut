How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

A long-range Forecut forecast from the first SS28 previews: Première Vision's October 2026 colour preview (royal blue, deeper sky blue, a new turquoise, greyish mauve and a dense violet), fabric-fair womenswear previews (straw, khaki green, warm beige, peach) and a darker after-dusk summer mood. It will be updated after the February 2027 fairs.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
