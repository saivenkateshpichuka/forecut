SS27 Kids' Trending Trims Report

Sailor collars and nautical tape, handcrafted embroidery, character appliqués, shells and sparkling details lead SS27 kidswear trims, under strict safety rules.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceTrims tableWhat to do
Research details

Research period Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • Nautical details: sailor collars and navy at Playtime Paris.
  • Character elements and layered accessories.
  • Handcrafted embroidery: Fatamadrina's "Wildflowers Bloom" uses delicate handcrafted embroidery.
  • Shells, pebbles, seaweed and embroideries: on Selini Action's handmade costumes.
  • Sparkle: Pitti Bimbo's Pirouette runway promised "bold looks, sparkling details, and positive vibes".

Trims table

Trim Application Status Safety note
Sailor collar and tape Tops, dresses Rising None specific
Embroidery Dresses, shirts Rising Soft backing on skin side
Character appliqué Tees, sets Core Securely stitched, no loose edges
Shell and bead decoration Swim, costumes Emerging Pull-test; avoid for under-3s
Sequins and sparkle Party Seasonal Line the inside; check edges
Design

Design implications

  • Prefer printed or embroidered motifs to attached parts for baby and toddler.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Embroidery and appliqué add value to core tees and dresses without new blocks.
