Design notes

How to put the leading shapes into a range.

Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

Cropped boxy knit

Short, wide sweater over high-waisted trousers and skirts. A focused detail update for knitwear.

Design notes

  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on knitwear. Works in kidswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

Lounge knit set

Soft knit top and wide trousers worn as a set for travel and home. A focused detail update for knitwear.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

Ribbed knit dress

Long-sleeved rib dress worn with tights and boots. A focused detail update for knitwear.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on knitwear. Works in kidswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Outerwear, rising

Track jacket

Retro zip track top with contrast piping; the sporty adult trend for kids. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • Sequins, beads and small hardware suit ages 5 and up only; sew them securely and test them.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
