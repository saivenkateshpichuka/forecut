The story

AW26 Women's Themes.

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Why now

Social media rewards texture and shine, and embellishment makes basics feel special.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Sequin and chainmail minis
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Mesh-panel tops
  • Sheer organza blouses
  • Embellished flats
  • Embroidered denim

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Gilded embroidered shirting
  • Sequinned and beaded jeans
  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Cropped parkas over evening gowns
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Elongated evening jumpsuits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Mesh midi skirts
  • Sheer mesh layering tops
  • Sheer organza overlays
  • Fringed hems
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Sequin-flecked Fair Isle jumpers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Embellish one area per garment (pocket, cuff or yoke).
  • Use sheer layers to restyle existing knits.
  • Keep embellished pieces as limited drops.
