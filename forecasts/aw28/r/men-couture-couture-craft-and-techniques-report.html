AW28 Men's Couture Craft and Techniques

Craft for AW28 men: suede finishing, natural dyeing, double-faced coats.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Men
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period

Market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • AW27 fairs made wool lighter and softer, brought back double-faced woollens and hid performance inside classic suitings[1]

Translating to ready-to-wear

  • Hand-finished suede.
  • Naturally dyed knits.
  • Double-faced coats.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Hand work on hero pieces only.

Commercial implications

  • Tell the craft story; price as limited.

Evidence and sources

  1. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited, 4 September 2026
