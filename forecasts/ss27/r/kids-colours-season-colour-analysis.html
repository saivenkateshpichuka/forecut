SS27 Kids' Season Colour Analysis

Bright, saturated summer colour returns to kidswear, led by red, aqua and lemon, balanced by soft pastels and calm neutrals.

When it lands

Red is rising for SS27; brights return after several pastel-led seasons. Soft pastels stay core for baby.

Research details

Research period Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • Red: "one of the defining colors of the season" at Playtime Paris, from bright statement shades to deeper tones.
  • Navy and nautical: sailor collars and navy blues throughout Playtime Paris.
  • Colour overall: Pitti Bimbo called its June edition "the most colorful summer edition yet"; Pisamonas' SS27 shoes arrive "full of color"; Poco Blush showed bold hues for girls.
  • Soft pastels: Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy.
  • Neutrals: Fatamadrina notes "neutrals enhanced with terracotta and sage green" alongside bright, saturated colours.

Colour groups

  • Core: navy, sand, white.
  • Seasonal brights: lemon, aqua, coral, grass green.
  • Seasonal soft: lavender, vintage pink, pastel green.
  • Accent: red.

By age

  • Baby and toddler: soft pastels and sand, with one bright accent.
  • Kids: brights with navy and white.
  • Older kids and teens: red, navy and stripes; lavender for girls who want softer colour.
Make

Products and customers

  • Red: tees, sandals, accessories. Aqua and lemon: swim and play. Lavender: dresses and knits. Navy and sand: bottoms and basics.
Design

Design implications

  • Lab-dip brights on both jersey and swim fabric; they shift between substrates.
  • Check colour fastness to chlorinated and salt water for swim.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Navy and sand carry volume; brights and red carry newness.
