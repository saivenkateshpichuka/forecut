AW27 Women's Woven Silhouette Direction

Wide shoulders over narrow lines: broad-shouldered and reversible coats, skirt suits, sculptural pleats, round-cut waists and tapered trousers.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Boland sees the upper body widening (80s shoulders, broad chests) over narrower trousers or skirts; key items include the skirt suit, broad-shouldered and reversible double-faced coats, camouflage parka and marl jumper
  • Cited looks include sculptural pleats, funnel necks, round-cut waists (Jil Sander), padded shoulders (Carven), oversized fur coats (Saint Laurent), teddy-bear coats (Loewe) and all-over lace (Saint Laurent)
  • Christine Boland's "Individual Familiarity": blazer, skirt, trousers and suit in personal combinations; quiet luxury stays, shown with new linings, piping or colours

Shapes to lead with

  • Broad-shoulder coat, including reversible double-faced.
  • Skirt suit with a court or pencil skirt.
  • Blazer with padded or rounded shoulder.
  • Tapered trouser.
  • Funnel-neck jacket.

Shapes to watch

Teddy and oversized faux-fur coats; camouflage parkas.

Design

Design implications

  • Rework shoulder blocks; keep sleeves narrow.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Coats and blazers lead the woven buy.
