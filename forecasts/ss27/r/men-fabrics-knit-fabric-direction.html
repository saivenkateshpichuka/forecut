SS27 Men's Knit Fabric Direction

Cool wools and extra-fine merino, elastic ribs, retro-sport piqués and sun-faded cotton cables for SS27 menswear knits.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2027.

On this pageThe evidenceFabric by fabricWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

Première Vision highlighted flat, Derby and Richelieu ribs, more elastic and elongated than jersey, and retro-sport bindings, honeycomb piqués, plated knits, diamond and wheat stitches in slightly yellowed, sepia-like tones; cool wools and extra-fine merino. Brunello Cucinelli's cable knits were dyed with resin for a sun-faded, one-of-a-kind finish.
