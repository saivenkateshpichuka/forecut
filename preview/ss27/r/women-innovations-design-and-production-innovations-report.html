Body scanning and 3D development, product data ready for the EU's Digital Product Passport, and production planned around new EU rules on unsold stock and low-value imports.

Design: scanning and 3D

Balenciaga used 3D body scans to build new mannequins for its July 2026 couture collection[1]. The same approach is available to ready-to-wear: scanning fit models and developing first samples in 3D reduces sampling rounds. Forecut's Garment Studio supports drafting and 3D checks before a physical sample.

Product data and regulation

  • Digital Product Passport: it will not apply to all fashion from the start of 2027; the European Commission plans to adopt the textiles delegated act in the fourth quarter of 2027[2]. Brands that collect fibre, origin and care data now will be ready.
  • Unsold goods: since 19 July 2026, large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel, accessories and footwear in the EU[2].
  • Low-value imports: since 1 July 2026, the EU applies a temporary €3 customs duty per item on consignments worth up to €150[2].
  • Ultra-fast fashion: a French law adopted on 8 July 2026 allows penalties of up to 50% of a product's price[2].

Production planning

  • Sourcing: brands are diversifying towards Vietnam, Bangladesh, India, Turkey and Morocco while keeping China relationships[2].
  • Calendar: Lunar New Year falls on 6 February 2027; Vietnamese and Chinese factories slow from late January and usually return to full capacity in early March[3]. Plan bulk for later spring drops after the holiday.
  • Climate risk: the World Meteorological Organization warns that El Niño could bring drought, flooding and extreme heat to key sourcing regions through early 2027[2].

Design implications

  • Develop first samples in 3D and keep physical sampling for final fit and fabric approval.
  • Record fibre composition, origin and care for every style from the start.

Commercial implications

  • Buy shallower in the EU and plan rework, resale or donation routes for unsold stock.
  • Build buffer time around Lunar New Year and climate disruption into the SS27 critical path.
