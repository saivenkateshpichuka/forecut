Core Item Update: Nehru jackets and bandhgalas 2026–27

Tailored ethnic menswear is the wedding-guest uniform.

Forecut Editorial · 11 October 2026

Overview

Tailored ethnic menswear is the wedding-guest uniform. Brocade and Chanderi Nehru jackets are a steady peak, contrast piping is the emerging detail, and tone-on-tone and velvet bandhgalas rise into the winter wedding season.

How to update your nehru jackets and bandhgalas range

  • Keep brocade and Chanderi Nehru jackets as a core wedding offer.
  • Test contrast piping on collars and front edges.
  • Add velvet bandhgalas for winter weddings, with a collar brooch as an add-on.
