SS27 Women's Trim Innovations

Ribbon built into the cloth, coated and pearlescent lace, rubberised corsetry meshes and fastenings used as decoration: the newest trim ideas for SS27.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Women

Trims

Market
Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

Ribbon as structure

Première Vision describes ribbon acting as material, ornament and structure: finishing ribbons, highly decorative ribbons and ribbons integrated into fabric construction, inlaid into jacquards, embroidered as backgrounds or applied onto tulle and lace[1]. For production, this means trims ordered as part of the fabric rather than added at the end.

Coated and pearlescent lace

The "Wet Sheen" direction brings plastic-coated laces and pearlescent satins[1]. Première Vision's decoration report adds Leavers lace with a melted-film coating for a latex-like sheen[2]. On the runway, Saint Laurent coated latex in gold leaf[3].

Corsetry components

Membrane-backed draperies, spacer fabrics and rubberised meshes borrowed from corsetry appear among Première Vision's structural fabrics[1]. They let a bodice hold its shape without boning.

Fastenings as decoration

Prada used coloured Velcro strips with embroidered florals on the fastenings[4]; Ferragamo covered snap buttons in sequins[3]; Miu Miu laced skirts with cross-lace fastenings[4]; Givenchy made belt buckles that double as powder compacts[4].

Readiness

Innovation Readiness for production (Forecut assessment)
Ribbon-inlaid jacquards Available from specialist mills; long lead times
Coated and pearlescent lace Available; test cleaning and abrasion
Rubberised and spacer meshes Available from lingerie and sportswear suppliers
Decorative fastenings Ready; mainly a design and sourcing decision

Design implications

  • Bring trim suppliers into fabric development early when ribbon is part of the cloth.
  • Test coated laces for cracking, peeling and dry-cleaning.
  • Use corsetry meshes to shape bodices and bra tops without rigid boning.

Commercial implications

  • Innovative trims justify a premium on a few hero pieces; do not spread them across the range.
  • Long lead times for jacquard ribbons mean early commitment: order SS27 quantities now and limit reorders.
