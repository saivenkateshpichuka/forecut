The story

What is behind it.

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Grandad collar linen shirts
  • Mesh running sneakers
  • Running-inspired sneakers
  • Seersucker suits
  • Seersucker trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Linen and seersucker shirts
  • Linen travel suits
  • Linen waistcoats
  • Striped cotton crewnecks
  • Waxed cotton jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids

Actions

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
