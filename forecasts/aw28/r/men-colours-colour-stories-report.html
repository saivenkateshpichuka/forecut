How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • "Marinated midtones": honey brown, mustard yellow, turquoise, lime, retro orange and a brown-burgundy, with "pickled fennel" as an anchor colour[1]
  • Vibrant cobalt with a violet undertone, paired with white; deep wine red with cream and black; bright matcha green with chartreuse and ecru, in menswear and womenswear[1]
  • Forecut's AW28 projection: ink, mulberry and bottle green over oat and slate, with petrol teal and vermilion as accents[2]
