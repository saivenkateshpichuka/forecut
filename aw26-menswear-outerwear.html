The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Harrods, AW26 menswear trends; Who What Wear

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Power tailoring

Sharp, slick suiting in pinstripes and slate grey, with pointed shoulders, peak lapels and double-breasted fronts. Oxblood accents and polished heels finish the look.

Why now

Return-to-office dressing and a move away from very oversized blazers toward sharper fits.

Seen at

  • Gucci: Horsebit pumps with sharp suiting
  • Classic-fit suits (market): Up 44% in market adoption
  • Double-breasted suits (market): Up 4% year on year; pointed shoulders up 12%

Key pieces

Pinstripe, Peak lapels, Pointed shoulders; sharp pointed shoulders.

Source: Who What Wear (AW26 trends); Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons

Nylon twill blousons in black, navy or khaki where thick contrast topstitching is the design accent; sacai Holiday 2026, Tokyo, from 6 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Piped satin track jackets

Zip-up satin track jackets with contrast piping, as in Born x Raised's Pre-Holiday 2026 drop; sporty polish for evening. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Clean leather bombers

Brown, navy and burgundy leather bombers. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Cropped work jackets

Short workwear jackets with chest pockets. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Double-face wool overcoats

  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Belted field coats
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Cropped work jackets
  • Double-face wool overcoats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Braided officer coats
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Leather-yoke nylon puffers
  • Funnel-neck puffers
  • Overshirt jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Shearling-collar aviators
  • Tech fleece jackets
  • Technical parkas

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • High-shine puffers

Actions

  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
