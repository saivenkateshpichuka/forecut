Spring/Summer 2027, Women, Themes

SS27 Women's Lingerie as Outerwear

Bras, bandeaus and slips move to the outside: worn under suits, with skirts and alone as tops. Satin, lace and boudoir dressing become day wear.

The story

Lingerie was one of the most visible ideas of SS27. Bralettes were worn alone or under jackets, and the bra returned as a simple top[1]. Prada, Fendi, Tod's, The Attico, Marni, MM6 and Blumarine all showed it: bras under suits and dresses, with skirts, and as tiny tops between swimwear and lingerie[2]. Fendi paired lace-trimmed bras with a fur stole[3]; Miu Miu put jewelled bras over sporty rash vests[3]; Dior placed a black bra top beneath an organza skirt suit[3].

The boudoir mood went further. Celine used creased, pressed satin and nightie- and negligee-inspired dresses; Julie Kegels layered deconstructed bras over T-shirts; Ester Manas, with Primadonna, showed sheer, lacy slips and negligees[3]. In London, Dreaming Eli used "slightly old-fashioned fabrics like lace and satin"[1].

Colours

Black, optic white and butter for lingerie bases; violet and true red for evening. See SS27 Women's Season Colour Analysis for codes.

Silhouettes

  • Bra and bandeau tops worn under unbuttoned jackets.
  • Slip dresses and slip skirts with lace edges.
  • Negligee-style dresses, unbuttoned at the shoulder.
  • Camisoles layered over knits and T-shirts.

Fabrics

Fluid and creased satin, lace, sheer tulle and mesh; dark Leavers lace and lace with a melted-film sheen[4].

Trims and details

Lace trims, satin bows and ribbons, adjustable straps, hook-and-eye fastenings as visible details, jewelled or embroidered cups.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. Slips and lace camisoles are at their height; bra tops as outerwear are rising and suit a younger customer first.

Products and customers

  • Bra tops and bandeaus in satin, lace and knit; slip dresses and skirts; camisoles; negligee dresses.
  • Styled with jackets, cardigans and shirts for wider audiences.
  • Customers: younger fashion customers for bra tops; a broad audience for slips and camisoles worn under tailoring.

Design implications

  • Bra tops need lingerie construction: cup sizing, firm top edges and adjustable straps.
  • Consider a partnership with a lingerie maker for structured pieces.
  • Offer modest styling: camisoles and slips under jackets and shirts work in every market.

Commercial implications

  • Size bra tops by bust and cup, not S to XL, or limit to soft, stretch styles.
  • Keep bra-top buys small; put depth into slips and camisoles, which reach a wider customer.
  • Style the look with jackets and knitwear in campaigns so the idea reads as day wear.
