Drafting method

Blocks are drafted with established industry block methods, as taught in fashion schools and set out in standard references such as H. Joseph-Armstrong, Patternmaking for Fashion Design. Forecut's own code calculates each piece from your measurements.

  • Bodice: front wider than back at the bust, bust dart sized by cup with the side seam lengthened to match, back waist and shoulder darts.
  • Sleeve: cap height set from the armhole so the cap carries the right ease for the sleeve type; elbow dart on fitted long sleeves.
  • Skirt: back hip wider than front; darts from the hip-to-waist difference.
  • Trousers: crotch depth plus ease, extensions by fit, crotch width checked against the thigh, back inseam walked 0.5 cm shorter.
Size charts

Forecut base charts: average body measurements for each category (women XS–XXL, men S–3XL, kids 2–12 years). For the best fit, enter your own measurements.

Accuracy

Pieces are drawn as vectors at true scale. After printing, the 5 cm square on the sheet must measure exactly 5 cm. If it does not, set the printer to 100% / actual size.

Before production
  • Sew a toile in calico and correct the fit.
  • Sizes are graded from your base size using the Forecut chart steps. Check the graded set against your own grade rules before bulk.
  • Add fabric shrinkage before cutting bulk (enter your wash-test result under Fabric).

How the pattern maker works

How it works

Load a Forecut detail, such as a band-collar kurta, Cuban camp collar shirt, barrel-leg jeans, peplum top or fit-and-flare Anarkali, and the matching garment and options load automatically. Or pick a category and a garment: fitted bodice, kurta, Anarkali, angrakha, shirt, T-shirt, hoodie, saree blouse, top, dress or frock, Nehru jacket, bandhgala, sherwani, skirt, lehenga, trousers, salwar, patiala, churidar and gharara.

Measurements start from a size chart and can be changed to your own in centimetres, millimetres or inches; the body diagram shows where to take each one.

Checked as you draft

Every piece is drafted from the same measurements, so pieces that are sewn together fit together: sleeve heads to the armhole, collars and bands to the neckline, cuffs and waistbands to the wrist and waist. The checks panel tests matching seams, sleeve cap ease and crotch width every time you change something.

Costing from the pattern

The pattern maker lays your actual pieces, with seam allowances, on the fabric width you choose and works out the fabric per garment, marker length and efficiency. Buttons, thread, zips and elastic are counted from the pattern. Add your rates for fabric, trims and making (SMV × cost per minute) to get the cost per garment, your price, the retail price and MRP with GST, and the totals for an order. Download the cost sheet, the CSV or the marker as DXF.

Shrinkage from your wash test enlarges every piece, so the garment is the right size after washing.

Downloads

  • PDF, A4 tiles: print at 100%, check the 5 cm square, tape the tiles together. Preview the pages before you print. Blank tiles are skipped.
  • PDF, full size: one page at true size for a plotter.
  • DXF: for Gerber, Lectra, Optitex, CLO, Illustrator or AutoCAD, on separate layers.
  • SVG and PNG: for editing, sharing and tech packs.
