The palette

Charcoal, midnight and chocolate tailoring, moss for outerwear, and a single crimson or cobalt piece.

CoreMidnightPantone 19-3923 TCX Navy Blazer#282D3CA near-black blue for coats and evening.
CoreChocolatePantone 19-1218 TCX Potting Soil#54392DThe new dark neutral. Coats, tailoring, leather and suede.
