The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Deep blue

Bright cobalt blue deepens for winter into cobalt and ink on knitwear, wool coats and leather.

Why now

Colours of the year usually carry through both seasons of their year in adapted shades.

Key pieces

Cobalt, Ink blue, Knitwear; funnel-neck knits.

Source: Based on Forecut's colour direction for the season. Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets

Cropped zip fleeces with stand collars and gathered drawcord hems, embroidered with tiny flowers; Uniqlo x Cecilie Bahnsen FW26 (announced 5 Nov 2026, Japan launch 20 Nov). The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Short, boxy down puffers covered in floral embroidery; The North Face x Cecilie Bahnsen 'Lucia Nuptse Short' in black and navy, launched in Copenhagen 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Floral-lined showerproof jackets

Showerproof field and quilted jackets with printed floral linings and reversible liners; Barbour x FARM Rio 'Tropical Greenhouse', on sale 30 Oct 2026. Lining details add a quality signal when the garment is open.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Choose a lining that is breathable and anti-static.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Sheer lattice capes

Open lattice or sheer capes layered over co-ords and bustiers; shown by Hertunba at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct) over a black-and-white woven peplum set. A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Make the cape detachable or attached at the yoke so the base garment sells on its own.
  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Shell-paillette jacket edging

Tweed and boxy jackets finished with shell-shaped paillettes along the fronts and hems; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 re-staged at Barangaroo, Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Wired peacoats

Double-breasted peacoats with wired edges that hold a curved, stand-away shape; OPEN YY Fall/Winter 2026, Seoul (2 Nov 2026). A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Sheer lattice capes
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Wired peacoats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Articulated sleeves
  • Lightweight technical trench

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cropped parkas over evening gowns
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Check-printed quilted puffers
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Faux-fur cuffs and hems

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cape backs and capelets
  • Dropped, rounded shoulders

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
