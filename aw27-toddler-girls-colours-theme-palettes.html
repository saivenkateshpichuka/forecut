How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the September 2026 fabric and leather fairs (Première Vision, Lineapelle, Milano Unica) and carried forward from the SS27 runways. The fairs put deep violet, plums, dusty pinks and mauves at the centre, with tinted off-blacks, greyish blues, sage and waxy ochre.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
