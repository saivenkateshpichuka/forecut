SS27 Men's Trim Innovations

Cooling and performance details move into menswear: fan-cooled garments, running-vest bottle holders and passport pockets, alongside chrome-free leather components.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Integrated cooling: Rick Owens showed inflatable tracksuits with integrated fans using Adidas's Climacool heat-regulation technology.
  • Performance details: Y-3 showed a running vest with bottle holders.
  • Travel details: Herno added passport-sized pockets to crease-proof travel pieces.
  • Chrome-free leather: Margaret Howell's Dr Martens 1461 uses chrome-free leather by C.F. Stead, designed to patina.

Readiness

Innovation Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Passport and utility pockets Ready
Bottle and gear holders Ready from sportswear suppliers
Chrome-free leather trims Ready; check supply
Integrated fans Experimental; showpieces only

Design implications

  • Add functional pockets to travel tailoring and outerwear.

Commercial implications

  • Functional details justify full price on core pieces.

Evidence and sources

  • Between heatwave and restraint: menswear trends from Paris, FashionUnited, 2 July 2026
  • Pitti Uomo SS 2027: menswear highlights, Wallpaper*, June 2026
