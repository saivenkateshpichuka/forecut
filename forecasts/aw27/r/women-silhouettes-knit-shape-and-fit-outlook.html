AW27 / Silhouettes / Women

AW27 Women's Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Knits for AW27 women: marl jumpers, fine merino under tailoring, kidult and whimsical knits, and tight, silky wool knits.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women
Category
Silhouettes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Boland sees the upper body widening (80s shoulders, broad chests) over narrower trousers or skirts; key items include the skirt suit, broad-shouldered and reversible double-faced coats, camouflage parka and marl jumper
  • Separable double-faced woollens return in more varied weights; ultrafine yarns and tight knits make wool lighter and softer, with thermal comfort without synthetics
  • Woolmark presented merino wool as "the technical fibre" for AW27 sourcing at Pitti Filati
  • FashionUnited notes "kidult" knitwear with whimsical faces and regressive animal motifs and a customer raised on Y2K who buys playful charms

Shapes and fit

  • Marl crew with a slightly broad shoulder.
  • Fine merino polo and turtleneck for layering.
  • Knit dress in tight, smooth wool.
  • Whimsical intarsia for a younger customer.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Ultrafine yarns for a silky hand.

Commercial implications

  • Merino basics are reorder lines.

Evidence and sources

  • Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  • Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited (Florence Julienne), 4 September 2026
  • Woolmark at Pitti Filati, June 2026, The Woolmark Company, 24 June 2026
