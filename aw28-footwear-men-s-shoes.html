Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Hiking boots with tailoring

Rugged soles under suits. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Rubber-soled Chelseas

Chelseas on lugged rubber soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Keep the sole light with an EVA or lightweight rubber unit.
  • Use strong elastic gussets and a pull tab for easy wear.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.

Fabric and trims

Lightweight rubber or EVA lug soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Slim tassel loafers

Tassel loafers on slim soles. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Footwear, Men's Shoes, peaking

Belgian loafers

Soft, slipper-like loafers. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.
  • Keep the last consistent across the style family.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Black oxfords

  • Cap-toe oxfords
  • Check-trimmed loafers
  • Crepe-sole desert boots
  • Derby boots

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
