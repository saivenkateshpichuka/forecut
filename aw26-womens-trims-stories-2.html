Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Belted wrap fronts

Self-fabric tie belts replacing buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Button-shoulder knits

Buttoned tabs along the shoulder. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless double-breasted jackets

Double-breasted fronts without lapels. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Double-breasted jackets

Test small

  • Halter-neck dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Peak-lapel coats
  • Shawl-lapel jackets
  • Velvet lapel facings

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Starry crystal velvet gowns

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage
