SS27 Kids' Material Innovations

Materials for SS27 kidswear: UPF-rated swim and performance knits, recycled fibres, organic and certified cotton, quick-dry and sand-free fabrics.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • UV filters in swimwear, tees and accessories[1].
  • Recycled fibres, organic cotton and certified materials; breathable, quick-dry fabrics[1].
  • Sustainable lycra (Piccoli Principi), UPF50+ performance fabrics and sand-free towels (Teeter Totter), recycled fine fabrics with UPF 50+ (Marie Raxevisky)[2].

Readiness

Material Readiness (Forecut assessment)
UPF swim and knit Ready; test and label
Recycled polyamide and polyester swim Ready
Organic cotton (certified) Ready
Sand-free microfibre towels Ready

Design implications

  • Test UPF to a recognised standard (EN 13758 or AS/NZS 4399).
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Verify every sustainability claim before using it.

Evidence and sources

  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
