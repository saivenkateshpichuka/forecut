SS27 Boys' Theme Colour Palettes

Working palettes for the SS27 boys' themes: Refined Sport, Workwear Denim and Harbour Club.

The evidence

  • At Playtime Paris, red "emerged as one of the defining colors of the season"; stripes ran horizontal, vertical and diagonal[1]
  • Sport influence was presented "through a more refined, creative lens"; denim felt "stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons"[1]

Design implications

  • Share navy and white across themes so pieces mix.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Build depth in lead colours; accents shallow.
