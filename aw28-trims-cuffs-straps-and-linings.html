Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Floral-lined showerproof jackets

Showerproof field and quilted jackets with printed floral linings and reversible liners; Barbour x FARM Rio 'Tropical Greenhouse', on sale 30 Oct 2026. Lining details add a quality signal when the garment is open.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Choose a lining that is breathable and anti-static.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, peaking

Leather slides

Wide-strap leather slides. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Shape the footbed so the foot stays secure without a back.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Mary Jane flats

Flats with a strap across the instep. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Place the strap at the instep and use a buckle or snap.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, peaking

Multi-strap ballet flats for men

Soft leather ballet flats for men with several buckled straps, as at Dries Van Noten SS27. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Add a cushioned insole and a flexible sole for comfort.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Round-toe slingback heels

Round toes on slim slingback heels. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Use a balanced last for comfort and a clean shape.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Test small

Emerging pieces

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Leather slides
  • Mary Jane flats
  • Multi-strap ballet flats for men

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Mary Jane sneakers
  • Shearling linings and cuffs
  • Shearling-lined mules
  • T-bar and T-strap pumps

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
