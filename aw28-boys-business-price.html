Overview

The short version.

Full price on what is new, value on what customers already know, and limited pricing for craft and new materials.

Price positioning

Where each kind of piece sits.

Good

Core shapes in core colours: carry depth and keep making simple.

  • Adjustable inside waist
  • Bandhgala jacket
  • Brushed-back fleece
  • Contrast elbow patches
  • Easy-grip zip pullers

Better

Seasonal pieces in new colours and shapes: the main margin of the range.

  • Clip-on bow ties
  • Contrast binding
  • Extendable hems
  • Sherwani set
  • Tencel twill

Best

Statement, craft and new materials: small buys at full price.

  • Corduroy dungarees
  • Sherpa fleece

Kidswear pricing notes

  • Price by size band: parents accept a step up for older sizes.
  • Multi-packs work for basics such as bodysuits, tees and leggings.
  • Occasion and festive pieces sell at full price close to the event; plan deliveries for the festival and wedding calendar.
  • Grow-with-me features justify a higher price when the label explains them.
