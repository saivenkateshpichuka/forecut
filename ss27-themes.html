Overview

The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and a bright luminous blue leads the palette.

The headline

A mix of fresh ideas and pieces carried on from last season.

Build into the range

Apron-toe derbies, Asymmetric wrap closures, Backless apron tops and Balloon trousers are rising and keep growing into AW27.

Test in small quantities

Band-collar shirts, Basque waist bodices, Boat shoes and Cinched peplum jackets are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Belted trench blazers, Cropped blazers and Denim trucker jackets are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Drop waist with pleated skirt, Hoodie under tailoring and Low-slung belted waists are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

Loewe: The return of the tie; Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"; SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats

17-6333 TCX and 12-5404 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

The waist returns

After years of oversized shapes the waist is back. Peplums, basque waists, balloon and bubble shapes and belted or wrapped fronts define the body again.

Why now

Forecasters see a return to elegance and shape. A defined waist refreshes existing silhouettes without new categories.

Seen at

Li Edelkoort, "Craft of Black": Wraps and belts the waist tightly; SS27 bridal and evening: Corseted bodices, basque waists and bubble skirts; Double belt: Returns as a waist accessory

Key pieces

Peplum, Basque waist, Bubble hem

18-4048 TCX and 13-3920 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, Spring/Summer 2027 trend report

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

Altuzarra: Deshabille looks; Christian Cowan: Undone layers; Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks; LII: Double-layer minis

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin

17-0610 TCX and 11-0608 TCX

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

The forecast

Select any piece to see its full detail.

The waist returns

After years of oversized shapes the waist is back. Peplums, basque waists, balloon and bubble shapes and belted or wrapped fronts define the body again.

Key pieces

Peplum, Basque waist, Bubble hem; basque waist bodices.

Actions

  • Add a peplum version of your best-selling blouse.
  • Cut a basque-waist seam into a core dress block.
  • Sell double belts as an add-on accessory.

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Actions

  • Add lace trims at cuffs and hems of core shirts.
  • Use machine smocking on bodices and cuffs.
  • Partner with a craft cluster for a premium capsule.

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Actions

  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing; fringed hems.

Actions

  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Actions

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.

Reworked denim

Denim reimagined: white cut-off shorts, cobalt-painted jeans, wax-coated bootcuts, embroidered and beaded denim, upcycled pieces and matching denim suits.

Key pieces

Cut-offs, Coated denim, Denim suits; cut-off denim shorts.

Actions

  • Add a coated finish to one core fit.
  • Sell denim suits as matching separates.
  • Shade-band jacket and jeans together to avoid mismatches.

Markets

How each region reads SS27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Best fit: Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Lace-trimmed slips over knits, Sheer organza overlays

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Best fit: Sheer lattice capes, Lace-trimmed slips over knits, Sheer organza overlays, Sheer-sleeve dresses

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Best fit: Cinched peplum jackets, Lace-trimmed jacket edges, Quilted liner jackets, Belted safari jackets

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Best fit: Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops, Crochet tops and cardigans, Long denim coats, Matching denim suits

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Best fit: Belted shirt dresses, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Striped patchwork shirt dresses, Gilded embroidered shirting

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Best fit: Sheer lattice capes, Sheer organza overlays, Sheer-sleeve dresses, Sheer mesh layering tops
