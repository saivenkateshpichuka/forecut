The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Commuter tailoring

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders; stretch commuter suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts

Pleated-front tuxedo shirts cut in light, crisp parachute-cotton poplin; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027 evening, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Taffeta jogger evening trousers

Featherweight taffeta joggers with elastic cuffs worn under a tuxedo blazer for evening; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Brushed wool-cashmere coats

Soft, brushed blends with a hazy surface. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Chambray work shirts

Chambray shirts with two flap pockets. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Check sports jackets

Checked jackets worn with odd trousers. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Chambray work shirts
  • Check sports jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Quilted and padded dresses
  • Seamless performance knits
  • Seamless technical knits
  • Slip-on knit sneakers
  • Technical wool suiting

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Broderie anglaise dresses
  • Crochet tops and cardigans
  • Featherweight trench windbreakers
  • Gingham and seersucker skirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fluid satin wide legs
  • Patent evening loafers
  • Patent knee boots
  • Patent leather
  • Patent trench with storm flaps
  • Rib-knit sweatshirts

Actions

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
