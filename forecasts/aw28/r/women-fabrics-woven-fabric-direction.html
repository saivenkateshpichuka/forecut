AW28 Women's Woven Fabric Direction

AW28 women's wovens: double-faced wool, flannel, suede and suede-look, voile and lace.

Season
Autumn Winter 2028
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

On this pageHow this forecast was builtThe evidenceFabric by fabricWhat to do
Research details

Research period AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • AW27 fairs made wool lighter and softer, brought back double-faced woollens and hid performance inside classic suitings
  • Deep classic brown in leather and leather-look pieces, sporty pieces and tailoring, suited to suede and canvas-like materials
