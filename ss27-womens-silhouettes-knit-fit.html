Proportions and fit

Shape, proportion and the fit point to check in the first fitting.

ShapeStageProportionHow it sitsFit point
Bishop-sleeve knitsRisingVolumeGenerous through the body or leg; balance with a neater piece on the other half.Add ease where the volume is, keep the shoulder or waist fitted so the shape stays intentional.
Cropped cardigansRisingShortShorter body or leg; the hem sits at waist, hip or knee.Check the rise and the hem line on the body; grade length carefully across sizes.
Knit fringe at hemsRisingRegularStandard proportions with the detail as the update.Keep the block you trust; change one point at a time.
Longline knit vestsRisingLongLong line: below the knee or to the ankle.Check stride and step; add a vent or split for movement.
Puff-shoulder cardigansRisingVolumeGenerous through the body or leg; balance with a neater piece on the other half.Add ease where the volume is, keep the shoulder or waist fitted so the shape stays intentional.
Tunic-length knitsRisingLongLong line: below the knee or to the ankle.Check stride and step; add a vent or split for movement.
Wrap cardigansEmergingDefined waistThe waist is marked: high, natural or dropped.Fit the waist first; set belt loops and darts after the first fitting.

Balancing the look

Rules of thumb for the season.

  • Pair one volume piece with one close piece.
  • Fit the shoulder and the waist first; everything else hangs from them.
  • Knit: choose the gauge before the shape; finer gauges hold closer fits, chunky gauges need room.
