Looks

Select any piece for its design notes.

Red-carpet arrival

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In royal blue with peach.

Pleated bib fronts with dark indigo tailoring, menswear three-piece suits and mary jane flats. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Airport off-duty

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In greyish mauve with straw.

Cropped boxy shirts with low-rise slip skirts with bra tops, waistcoat and trouser sets and slip-on canvas sneakers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Press-day tailoring

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In magnetic violet with bleached linen.

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses with high-waisted pleated trousers, wide-leg suit trousers and studded and ruffled mary jane clogs. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Front-row statement

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In peach with royal blue.

Belted shirt dresses with matching denim suits, slim cigarette trousers and bow-trim flats. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.
