Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, emerging

Concealed-placket shirts

Fly-front plackets that hide the buttons. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, emerging

Funnel-neck base layers

Fine ribbed funnel necks for layering. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, emerging

Funnel-neck knit dresses

Ribbed knit dresses with a high funnel neck. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, emerging

Funnel-neck knits

High funnel necks on fine and chunky knits. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Keep the knit light enough to avoid sagging.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Wool blends in 3–5 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
