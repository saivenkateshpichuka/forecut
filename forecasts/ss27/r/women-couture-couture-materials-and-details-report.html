SS27 Women's Couture Materials and Details

Couture materials moved beyond silk and lace in 2026: lab-grown silk, silicone, latex, glass beads and feathers. The details that translate are feathers, satin macramé, corsetry and pearl beading.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceDetails that translateWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

The evidence

  • Lab-grown silk: Balenciaga used AMSilk silk, which the house describes as "stronger than steel".
  • Silicone, latex, clay and paint: Schiaparelli used poured silicone, sculpted clay, latex and paint, and techniques from a workshop that makes lifelike silicone infants for films.
  • Feathers: goose and ostrich feathers at Balenciaga, ostrich feathers at Stéphane Rolland.
  • Satin macramé, agate and diamonds: Stéphane Rolland.
  • Beading: a prom dress beaded in putty-pink pearls at Schiaparelli and hand-blown glass beads on tulle at Iris van Herpen.
  • Velvet: velvet roses at Zuhair Murad and velvet corseted gowns at Elie Saab.

Details that translate

Couture detail Ready-to-wear translation Price tier
Feathers Feather trim at a hem or cuff Premium
Satin macramé Macramé belts and straps Mid to premium
Pearl beading Pearl buttons and beaded necklines Mid to premium
Corsetry Corset seaming and rubberised meshes instead of boning Mid
Moulded and coated surfaces Coated lace and pearlescent satins Mid
Design

Design implications

  • Source feathers and beads from certified, traceable suppliers and state care clearly.
  • Test coated and moulded materials for cracking and cleaning before committing.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Keep exotic materials to a few showpieces; translate the look into accessible trims for volume.
  • Lab-grown and bio-based materials carry a sustainability story: check claims before using them.
