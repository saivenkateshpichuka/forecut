Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Denim with technical details
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Stand-collar field coats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted safari jackets
  • Belted shirt dresses
  • Belted suit jackets
  • Carpenter jeans

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Belted trench blazers
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Denim shirt-jackets
  • Saucer-buckle wide belts
  • Welt-pocket cocoon coats

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
