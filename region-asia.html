Key markets

Climate, buying moments and preferences that shape how trends sell.

China
Climate
Four seasons in the north and east; subtropical south.
Key moments
Lunar New Year, 618 and Double 11 (11.11) shopping festivals
Buyers want
Fast adoption of runway ideas, strong local designer brands, heritage motifs, live-commerce driven drops.
Fashion week
Shanghai Fashion Week (SS27 opened 8 Oct 2026, theme "Renewed Radiance")
Japan & South Korea
Climate
Four distinct seasons with humid summers.
Key moments
Spring and autumn wardrobe changes, year-end gifting, Chuseok
Buyers want
Precise tailoring, layering, quality basics, technical outerwear, K-celebrity influence.
Fashion week
Rakuten Fashion Week Tokyo (31 Aug–5 Sep 2026) · Seoul Fashion Week (1–6 Sep 2026)
India
Climate
Tropical and subtropical; monsoon Jun–Sep; short north-Indian winter.
Key moments
Diwali and festive (Sep–Nov), wedding seasons (Nov–Feb, Apr–May), summer
Buyers want
Occasion and ethnic wear, handloom and embroidery, fusion for Gen Z, value-led western wear.
Fashion week
Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI (13–18 Oct 2026)

Climate & influences

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

  • Western runway trends filtered through local taste, often within one season
  • K-culture and celebrity-led demand from Seoul
  • Heritage revival: Chinese brocades and kesi, Indian handloom, Japanese craft
  • Modest and layered dressing across the Gulf, Malaysia and Indonesia

Runway & news from Asia

The latest fashion weeks and collections, summarised with links to the original coverage.

Oct 2026
Shanghai Fashion Week SS27: "Renewed Radiance"

Nearly 100 runway presentations and about 1,000 brands. HEMU opened with Dunhuang heritage techniques (yunjin and song brocade, kesi silk tapestry, embroidery); the season spotlights traditional Chinese motifs inspired by the Silk Road.

Source: China Daily, CGTN

Oct 2026
Dubai Fashion Week SS27 widens its map

Running 22–26 October at Dubai Design District, with designers from the UAE, Kuwait, India, Colombia, Vietnam, Ukraine, Azerbaijan, Jordan, France and Italy, and a buyers' programme for regional and international stores.

Source: Dubai PR Network

Sep 2026
Seoul SS27: tailoring as a system

Kwaeja-inspired overlapping forms, cropped jackets and windbreakers, ballooning trousers; earth tones with sheer fabrics and metallic silver; technical fabrics and denim-effect patchwork.

Source: METAL Magazine

Sep 2026
Tokyo SS27: translucency and reworked office wear

Yoshiokubo layered translucent fabrics with utility thermals; Pillings twisted shirts and pencil skirts with off-centre creases; Charinyeh used gossamer fabrics and coral-like cut jacquard.

Source: Tokyo Weekender
