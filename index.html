Featured detail · Womenswear topsRising in SS27

Band collar with long placket

Collarless shirts with an extended placket. The point collar steps back; a clean band and an extended placket carry the shirt from office to evening.

Construction
Interfaced band 2.5–3.5 cm high; placket extended to the hem or concealed as a fly front; 1–2 mm edge-stitching, no visible collar stand seam.
Fabric
Poplin and crepe de chine for SS27; brushed cotton and fine wool shirting as it moves into AW27.
Trims
Tonal shell or corozo buttons, 7–9 on the placket; hidden snaps on fly fronts.
Where
All six regions. Lightweight cottons for tropical Asia, Brazil and West Africa; layered under knits in Europe and North America.

How it works

From market signal to sample-ready insight.

Signal collection

We monitor runway coverage, street style, search interest and retail launches across Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and Oceania every week.

Detail-level analysis

AI-assisted analysis identifies construction details, trims and materials, and classifies each as emerging, rising, peaking or fading.

Actionable reports

Monthly detail cards with design notes, fabric guidance and pairing recommendations help your team sketch, specify and sample faster.

How statuses are set

Emerging: early signals in a few collections. Rising: spreading across designers and retail, add now. Peaking: at full adoption, use in core styles. Fading: phase out of new development. Statuses are reviewed monthly; news is checked daily.
