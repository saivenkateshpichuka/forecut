Runway & news from Europe

The latest fashion weeks and collections, summarised with links to the original coverage.

Nov 2026
Antonin Tron's first Balmain menswear: pre-spring 2027 with a 1970s California surf mood

One year after his appointment, Antonin Tron previewed his debut Balmain menswear (published 10 November), presented as the Pre-Spring/Resort 2027 men's lookbook with a campaign shot by Matthias Vriens. Inspired by California counterculture and 1970s surf culture, it has sharply tailored and double-breasted suits, velvet dinner jackets, gilded blazers, a double-breasted pony-hair coat, embroidered leather jackets, side-laced and heavy canvas trousers, leopard-print silk shirts and faded, riveted denim, with jacquard and fil coupé carried over from womenswear. It will sell in 18 of Balmain's 44 boutiques and at Saks, Neiman Marcus, Net-a-Porter and Mytheresa.

Source: Style Rave, The Impression

Nov 2026
Loewe x Tim Burton 'Nightmare Before Christmas' capsule; Veja x Marimekko trainers

Loewe's Christmas capsule with Tim Burton, inspired by the 1993 film, launches 12 November across bags, ready-to-wear, small leather goods, fragrance and candles, using leather marquetry, embroidery, printing and enamelling, with limited Flamenco, Puzzle and Amazona 180 bags. Veja and Marimekko release a limited capsule the same day: the Salar trainer for adults and the Small Canary for children in Marimekko's archive 'Popkorni' floral by Antti Kekki.

Source: FashionUnited: Loewe x Tim Burton, FashionUnited: Veja x Marimekko

Nov 2026
Gucci opens a four-floor Avenue Montaigne flagship; Chloé's Festive 2026 campaign with Claudia Schiffer

Gucci opened a new Paris flagship at 56 Avenue Montaigne on 30 October, spread over four floors and 1,480 square metres. Chloé's Festive 2026 campaign, shot by David Sims under creative director Chemena Kamali and starring Claudia Schiffer, features tailoring, pleated eveningwear, ruffles and embellished looks, arriving in boutiques and online from early November.

Source: RETAILBOSS

Nov 2026
Cecilie Bahnsen goes technical: The North Face collaboration and a second Uniqlo collection

The Copenhagen designer launched a seven-piece The North Face collection on 5 November, led by the 'Lucia Nuptse Short', a cropped Nuptse puffer with floral 'Superpose' embroidery, plus an insulated crew-neck top, a dot-knit top, Verto Alpine Mid boots and an Angela ThermoBall mule in black and navy. Her second Uniqlo collection, announced the same day for late November (Japan 20 November), has cropped stand-collar zip fleeces with drawcord hems and tiny embroidered flowers, a boa-fleece vest, matching fleece skirts, ditsy-floral tees and a curved-hem navy midi skirt.

Source: Les Façons, DSCENE

Nov 2026
Hoka x Marni 'Clifton Marni'; Reserved x Dawid Woliński capsule in Milan

Marni's take on the Hoka Clifton 11 (orange, white or black; $275) has an engineered-mesh upper with reflective and 3D silicone details, metal lace dubraes and a plaid sockliner on a rocker sole, on sale 12–13 November. Polish retailer Reserved launched a capsule with designer Dawid Woliński on 5 November, including at its Milan store: structured shoulders, long jackets, wide trousers and soft coats in wool, cashmere, heavy coating and corduroy, in black with fuchsia as the only accent.

Source: DSCENE, FashionUnited

Nov 2026
Martine Rose named Balenciaga creative director; Coperni closes after eight years

British designer Martine Rose succeeds Pierpaolo Piccioli at Balenciaga, the first woman to lead the house, taking over on 16 November. In Paris, Coperni co-founders Sébastien Meyer and Arnaud Vaillant announced on 4 November that Coperni SAS is ceasing operations after eight years, saying no solution could be found in a conflict with majority shareholder and distributor Tomorrow Ltd; the label had skipped Paris Fashion Week in February and entered receivership in June.

Source: Wallpaper*, Fashionista, Mille World

Nov 2026
Max Mara Atelier coats; Loro Piana autumn palette; RIXO x CLÒIMH merino hood

Max Mara Atelier's fall/winter 2026 outerwear, 'The Architecture of a Coat', draws on brutalist and Bauhaus design in greys lifted by brick red and Klein blue. Loro Piana's 'Back to School' edit uses rust, burnt orange and moss green. Burberry's new campaign centres on lightweight outerwear and trench coats for transitional weather, and Chloé spring 2027 tailoring looks back to late-1990s Edward Sexton influence. In the UK, RIXO and Scottish knitwear label CLÒIMH released a limited 100% merino tied hood.

Source: Harper's Bazaar Singapore, Country & Town House

Nov 2026
Circular outerwear: Armedangels windbreaker named a TIME 2026 best invention; E.L.V. Denim x Sanderson

German label Armedangels' windbreaker, made without fossil-fuel-derived fibres, was named one of TIME's best inventions of 2026. London's E.L.V. Denim turned Sanderson fabric offcuts into a 12-piece collection of jeans, dresses and other pieces.

Source: Good On You

Nov 2026
Dior Alps by Jonathan Anderson; Margiela x Gentle Monster; Burberry x Smythson

Jonathan Anderson's debut alpine line for Dior, the Dior Alps 2026 capsule, is built for everyday mountain wear: puffer jackets, face masks and shearling bags, plus bag charms, throws and cushions. Maison Margiela and Gentle Monster released their fourth eyewear collection, 'Deconstruction' (68 sunglasses and optical frames, from oversized shapes to slim frames with stitching that floats over the edges). Burberry's first collaboration with Smythson brings leather notebooks and travel games (cards, chess, backgammon) in Panama crossgrain leather lined in House Check, timed for the festive season.

Source: Robb Report Singapore

Oct 2026
Balenciaga 2026 Skiwear; Atelier Versace couture returns in January

Pierpaolo Piccioli's Balenciaga Skiwear collection brings oversized parkas, cropped puffers, reversible jackets, technical snow pants, animal-inspired prints, faux fur and reflective details in polar blue, storm grey, optic white, rose quartz and caramel, with Le City, Rodeo and Le 7 bags in shearling and suede, chunky Alaska boots, ski masks and earmuffs. Versace confirmed its first dedicated Atelier Versace couture show since July 2016, Pieter Mulier's couture debut, for Paris Couture Week (25–28 January 2027). Yohji Yamamoto will receive the Outstanding Achievement Award at the Fashion Awards in London on 30 November.

Source: Mille World

Oct 2026
Uniqlo x F.RISSO: knitwear in warm 1970s colours

Francesco Risso's knitwear-focused Uniqlo collaboration offers thin knits and accessories in warm, '70s-inspired colours. Miu Miu's holiday campaign features headbands and sparkly outerwear; Prada's 'Drawing Prada' holiday campaign uses hand-drawn animation by John Pomeroy.

Source: Nylon

Oct 2026
Louis Vuitton Men's SS27 pre-collection: 'Whatever the Weather'

Pharrell Williams' pre-collection, released worldwide on 29 October, is built on technical outerwear: reversible jackets, nylon puffers with monogram leather shoulders, suede-trimmed denim, fisherman-inspired raincoats and rugged boots, with weathered and rain-washed bags and umbrellas.

Source: Mille World

Oct 2026
Jil Sander x Puma goes on sale

After its preview on the Milan SS27 runway, the collaboration reworks Puma's low-profile Speedcat in all-black and all-white leather and a white suede version, and recuts the T7 tracksuit as a fluid track jacket and tapered track pant.

Source: Fashionista

Oct 2026
H&M x Elie Saab lands in stores

The collaboration launched on 22 October in selected H&M stores worldwide and online, covering womenswear, menswear (Elie Saab's first) and accessories including footwear, with embellished dresses, shimmering finishes, tailoring, lace and the house's dégradé sequins. Earlier in October, Acne Studios released a denim, jersey and bag collaboration with Hussein Chalayan.

Source: FashionUnited, The Zoe Report

Oct 2026
Luxury Q3: Kering flat, LVMH fashion down

Kering's Q3 2026 revenue was €3.35bn, flat reported and down 1% comparable; Gucci fell 6% comparable, softest in Asia Pacific, mainland China and Japan, with North America most resilient. Saint Laurent, Bottega Veneta and Brioni grew, and Kering confirmed Anthony Vaccarello (Saint Laurent) and Seán McGirr (McQueen) have left. Earlier in October, LVMH reported fashion and leather goods down 5% organic in Q3, citing slower demand in China, with growth in the US, Europe and Japan.

Source: Kering via GlobeNewswire, Luxury Tribune

Oct 2026
Paris SS27: the featherlight season

Ultrafine ripstop nylon at Issey Miyake, sheer organza sets at Chanel, sheer fabrics with soft tailoring at Dior.

Source: Monocle

Sep 2026
Milan SS27: sheer meets gloss

Latex-effect rubber and PVC at Ferragamo, glossy nylon at MSGM, taffeta and crinkled nylon at Bottega Veneta.

Source: nss magazine

Aug 2026
Copenhagen SS27: Nordic design meets ethical fashion

Collina Strada, Stine Goya and By Malene Birger led social coverage of a week built on heritage and sustainability.

Source: Onclusive

May 2026
Chanel Métiers d'art 2027 set for Rome on 2 December

Chanel will show Matthieu Blazy's Métiers d'art 2027 collection in Rome on 2 December 2026. Métiers d'art collections arrive in stores as pre-fall, so the show is a key early read for Pre-Fall 27 embroidery, tweed and craft.

Source: The Impression
