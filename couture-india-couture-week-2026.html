India Couture Week 2026: the trends to know

Headpieces, sculptural corsets, jewelled garments and the return of bridal red: Forecut's summary of India Couture Week 2026.

By Forecut Editorial · 14 October 2026 · 5 min read

India Couture Week 2026 ran from 23 to 29 July, organised by FDCI, with the opening show held at the Taj Falaknuma Palace in Hyderabad. These were the trends that defined it, summarised from published coverage.

The key trends

  • Ceremonial headpieces as the season's statement accessory.
  • Sculptural corsets giving lehengas and gowns a strong line.
  • Jewellery built into garments rather than worn separately.
  • Bridal reds returning alongside blush and ivory.
  • Sculptural veils used as design elements.
  • Artisan credit: several collections put their craftspeople at the centre.

Collections noted

Coverage highlighted crystal corsets and feathers at Falguni Shane Peacock, steel-wire textiles inspired by Mughal inlay at Rimzim Dadu, an artisan-focused tribute at the House of Rohit Bal, and Masaba Gupta's bridal debut in saffron, crimson and fuchsia.

What it means for brands

  • Plan bridal ranges with red as a lead colour again.
  • Offer structured bodices and corset options.
  • Develop headpieces and veils as add-on products.
  • Tell the artisan story in product content.

Sources: ETV Bharat, t2 Online, Wedding Affair.
