Climate & influences

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

  • International runway trends, adapted for a warm, outdoor lifestyle
  • Surf and beach culture
  • Australian Fashion Week Resort collections, shown in May

Runway & news from Oceania

The latest fashion weeks and collections, summarised with links to the original coverage.

Oct 2026
Melbourne Fashion Week 2026: street style, bridal and circularity

Melbourne Fashion Week runs 19–25 October with Cult Status (emerging and independent labels), The Edit (Bianca Spender, Third Form, Asiyam), City Square Street Style and After Dark runways, a courtside street-style runway with the Australian Open, a luxury bridal runway, a Kowtow clothes swap and a recycled-beading workshop.

Source: Fashion Journal

Oct 2026
Christchurch Spring Fashion Festival 2026

The second edition, curated by New Zealand Fashion Week, ran 15–18 October at Te Pae Christchurch Convention Centre: The New Guard showcase of contemporary New Zealand designers (curated by NZFW creative director Dan Ahwa), The Spring Edit with NZFW runway highlights from Caitlin Crisp, Kathryn Wilson, Juliette Hogan and Taylor, and a Fashion for a Cure fundraising runway with 22 designers including Trelise Cooper, Storm and Ruby.

Source: NZ Fashion Week, FashioNZ

May 2026
Australian Fashion Week Resort 27

Five directions: bi-coastal utility (nylon, raincoat fabrics), ethereal sheers (silk organza), high shine (liquid sequin and georgette), "surfer perfect" (wraps, crochet, raffia, sunset prints) and berry reds.

Source: Style Arcade
