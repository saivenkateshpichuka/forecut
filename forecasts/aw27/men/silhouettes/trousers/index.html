The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Soft boots

Flat riding boots, slim soles and soft leather replace chunky lugs.

Why now

Footwear follows the move to elegance; slimmer soles suit refined trousers and skirts.

Key pieces

Riding boots, Slim soles, Soft leather; soft knee-high riding boots.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Barrel trousers

Curved, tapered barrel legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Corduroy wide trousers

Wide trousers in corduroy. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Choose wale size for the garment: fine wale for shirts, wider wale for trousers and jackets.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton corduroy in 8–14 wale.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Deep turn-ups

Deep cuffed hems. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Double-knee work trousers

Reinforced knees on workwear trousers. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Drawcord linen trousers

Linen trousers with drawcord waists. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Pre-wash linen to control shrinkage and soften the handle.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Barrel trousers
  • Corduroy wide trousers
  • Deep turn-ups
  • Double-knee work trousers
  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Drawstring tailored trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Skirts for men
  • Technical stretch chinos

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted high waists
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Shorts with turn-ups
  • Single and double pleats
  • Utility cargo trousers
  • Wide-wale corduroy

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
