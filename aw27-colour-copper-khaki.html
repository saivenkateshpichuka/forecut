The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets

Cropped zip fleeces with stand collars and gathered drawcord hems, embroidered with tiny flowers; Uniqlo x Cecilie Bahnsen FW26 (announced 5 Nov 2026, Japan launch 20 Nov). The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers

Very full trousers with a low crotch gathered into a narrow ankle, shown at ALUF, Lilly Sarti and Uó at São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Elongated evening jumpsuits

Long, lean jumpsuits in black with glossy or metallic finishes and gold embroidery, as in Namrata Joshipura's OBSIDIAN, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Allow for less give in coated fabrics; adjust ease and avoid tight seams.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Fabric and trims

Polyurethane-coated cotton or recycled polyester with a glossy finish.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Short, boxy down puffers covered in floral embroidery; The North Face x Cecilie Bahnsen 'Lucia Nuptse Short' in black and navy, launched in Copenhagen 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fringed macramé tops

Hand-knotted macramé and crochet tops finished with long fringe, at Apartamento 03, Catarina Mina and Mercado Livre, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). A focused detail update for knitwear.

Design notes

  • Back crochet panels or use them as overlays to keep shape.
  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton crochet panels or crochet-look knit.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers
  • Elongated evening jumpsuits
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Fringed macramé tops

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted suit jackets
  • Clean column dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Balloon trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long denim coats
  • Sheer fine-gauge layers

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
