SS28 Women's Themes.

Cushioned sport soles under refined flats, loafers and derbies: elegant shoes with sneaker comfort.

Why now

SS27 moved from sneakers to elegance; SS28 keeps the look but brings back comfort.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Hybrid sport-smart boots
  • Sport-sole smart flats
  • Trail-sole hybrids
  • Comfort block heels
  • Flat espadrille sneakers
  • Flat strappy sandals

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Slip-on knit sneakers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Hiking sandals
  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Retro court sneakers
  • Reworked racing sneakers
  • Skinny thong sandals
  • Slim, low-profile sneakers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Flatform sneakers
  • Mary Jane sneakers

Actions

  • Develop one sport sole and use it across three uppers.
  • Market comfort features with clear claims.
  • Keep uppers refined to stay smart.
