Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Leather-look trousers

Straight trousers in faux leather. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Quilted and padded dresses

Quilted dresses for cold-weather layering. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Satin slip skirts

Fluid satin skirts cut on the bias. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, peaking

Black and grey denim

Washed black and grey denim. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
