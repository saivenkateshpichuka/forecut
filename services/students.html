Questions

Straight answers.

Is it free for students?

You can join free and read the full reports. Tools are available to members until the end of the current offer; write to us if your school wants a batch code.

Can I cite Forecut in my project?

Yes. Credit Forecut and the season, for example "Forecut SS27 forecast". Do not present forecast images as photographs of real products.

Where do I start?

Open the SS27 forecast, read the themes, then pick one and build a board with Trend to Style Brief.
