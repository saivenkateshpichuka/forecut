The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Deep blue

Bright cobalt blue deepens for winter into cobalt and ink on knitwear, wool coats and leather.

Why now

Colours of the year usually carry through both seasons of their year in adapted shades.

Key pieces

Cobalt, Ink blue, Knitwear; funnel-neck knits.

Source: Based on Forecut's colour direction for the season. Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Off-shoulder ruffle dresses

Dresses and corsets cut off the shoulder, with ruffles and gathers along the neckline, at Amir Slama, Ammi and Hisha, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Cut ruffles on the bias or curve for a soft drape; keep volume controlled.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses

Fitted midi sheaths in metallic tweed with an off-shoulder, sculpted neckline; Rebecca Vallance's custom tweed dress for Helena Christensen at Victoria Derby Day, Melbourne (31 Oct 2026), in line with the VRC's sleeveless midi sheath forecast for the carnival. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts

Pinstripe shirts with one exaggerated collar point, tucked into soft skirts, as at Dior SS27. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Band-collar shirts

Collarless shirts with a band neck. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Band-collar shirts with long placket

Collarless shirts with an extended placket. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless tailored jackets

Jackets with clean V-necklines and no lapels. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Band-collar shirts
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Collarless tailored jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Fly-front placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Chunky shawl-collar cardigans
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Fur and shearling collars
  • Heavy shawl-collar cardigans
  • Knitted polo collars

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Backless apron tops
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Rugby stripes, short plackets

Actions

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
