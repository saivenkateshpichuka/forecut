The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

The waist returns

After years of oversized shapes the waist is back. Peplums, basque waists, balloon and bubble shapes and belted or wrapped fronts define the body again.

Why now

Forecasters see a return to elegance and shape. A defined waist refreshes existing silhouettes without new categories.

Seen at

  • Li Edelkoort, "Craft of Black": Wraps and belts the waist tightly
  • SS27 bridal and evening: Corseted bodices, basque waists and bubble skirts
  • Double belt: Returns as a waist accessory

Key pieces

Peplum, Basque waist, Bubble hem; basque waist bodices.

Source: FashionUnited, Spring/Summer 2027 trend report

Reworked denim

Denim reimagined: white cut-off shorts, cobalt-painted jeans, wax-coated bootcuts, embroidered and beaded denim, upcycled pieces and matching denim suits.

Why now

Denim is a core volume category, and new finishes refresh it without new fits.

Seen at

  • Area: Cut-off white shorts and trousers
  • Simkhai: Wax-coated bootcut jeans
  • PatBo: Embroidered and beaded denim
  • 7 For All Mankind: Skinny jeans
  • Miu Miu × Wrangler: Upcycled denim edition

Key pieces

Cut-offs, Coated denim, Denim suits; cut-off denim shorts.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons

Nylon twill blousons in black, navy or khaki where thick contrast topstitching is the design accent; sacai Holiday 2026, Tokyo, from 6 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Fisherman-style raincoats

Long, roomy raincoats modelled on fishermen's oilskins with storm flaps and hoods; part of Louis Vuitton's weather-themed Men's SS27 pre-collection (Oct 2026). A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Piped satin track jackets

Zip-up satin track jackets with contrast piping, as in Born x Raised's Pre-Holiday 2026 drop; sporty polish for evening. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Chore and work jackets

Workwear jackets with large patch pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Round or square patch pockets should be topstitched evenly and reinforced at the corners.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Clean leather bombers

Brown, navy and burgundy leather bombers. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Belted field coats
  • Chore and work jackets
  • Clean leather bombers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Coach jackets
  • Field jackets with bellows pockets
  • Funnel-neck puffers
  • Packable technical shells

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Denim trucker jackets
  • Shearling-collar aviators
  • Tech fleece jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add a peplum version of your best-selling blouse.
  • Cut a basque-waist seam into a core dress block.
  • Sell double belts as an add-on accessory.
  • Add a coated finish to one core fit.
  • Sell denim suits as matching separates.
  • Shade-band jacket and jeans together to avoid mismatches.
