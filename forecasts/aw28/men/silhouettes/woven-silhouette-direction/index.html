How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • AW27 forecasters expected familiar codes made personal, eveningwear in daywear and protected, light volume in menswear[1]
  • AW27 fairs made wool lighter and softer, brought back double-faced woollens and hid performance inside classic suitings[2]
  • Pink becomes a neutral base ("Pink standard"), from soft blush to greyish dusty rose, no longer limited to womenswear; rosehip is a standout pink; suggested for blazers, skirt suits and voile dresses with ruffles or lace[3]

Shapes to lead with

  • Light down.
  • Suede jacket.
  • Relaxed suit.
  • Waistcoat.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Develop blocks early; confirm proportions after the shows.

Commercial implications

  • Keep new shapes to tested quantities.
