AW27 Kids' Forever Pieces

Permanent, durable kidswear: organic and merino basics in calm colour, made to be handed down.

Audience

Themes

How this forecast was built

Pre-fair forecast. The AW27 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime Paris) take place in January 2027. This report applies the 2026 adult yarn, fabric and leather fair evidence and the AW26 kidswear fair record to kidswear, and will be updated after the fairs.

The story

At Playtime in January 2026, CSR was "no longer a peripheral narrative": brands such as Gray Label, Minimalisma and MarMar Copenhagen build on organic cotton, merino, modal, permanent ranges and take-back [1].

Colours

Oat, greyed blue, sage.

Silhouettes

  • Unisex basics.
  • Merino base layers.
  • Durable trousers.

Fabrics

Organic cotton, merino, modal blends.

Trims and details

Minimal branding, reinforced knees.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Products and customers

  • Basics, base layers, outerwear.

Design implications

  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Evidence and sources

  1. CSR drives desirability in kidswear market: Playtime 2026 insights, FashionUnited, 28 January 2026
