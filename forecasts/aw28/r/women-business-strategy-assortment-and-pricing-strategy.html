AW28 Women's Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Assortment and pricing for AW28 womenswear.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Fashion entered 2027 expecting low single-digit growth, with markdown risk and faster growth in resale[1]

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Tailoring, knits in ink, oat, cocoa
Seasonal (about a third) Mulberry, pink suits, suede
Statement (the rest) Blocked brights, lace

Pricing

  • Full price on coats and suede.
  • Value on merino.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Fewer styles, more combinations.

Commercial implications

  • Track sales-to-stock weekly; plan resale and rework routes.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
