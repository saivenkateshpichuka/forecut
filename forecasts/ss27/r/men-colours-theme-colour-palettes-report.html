SS27 Men's Theme Colour Palettes

A working palette for each SS27 men's theme: Short Story, Ventilated Tailoring, Tender Masculinity and Pyjama Polish.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pagePalettes by themeWhy these palettesWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

Palettes by theme

Theme Lead Accent Range colours Carried by
Short Story Tobacco 18-1028 TCX Rust 18-1250 TCX Sand 13-1009 TCX Cropped leather and suede blousons, shorts
Ventilated Tailoring Sand 13-1009 TCX Ice blue 12-4805 TCX White Summer suits, open shirts
Tender Masculinity Salmon pink 15-1523 TCX Aquamarine (sea blue family) Butter 12-0711 TCX Satin shirts, soft knits
Pyjama Polish Ice blue 12-4805 TCX Black (piping) Sand, khaki Two-piece sets, tunic shirts

Why these palettes

They follow the runway colour: monochrome sets at Celine and IM Men, khaki, blue and beige sleepwear sets with black piping at Dior, nude and aquamarine at Dries Van Noten, and Forecut's SS27 menswear palette.

Design

Design implications

  • Lead colour on most of the look; accent on one piece or the piping.
  • Share sand across themes so pieces cross-sell.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Build depth in sand and ice blue; accents as small buys.
