SS27 Men's Woven Silhouette Direction

Lighter, closer and shorter: soft summer suits, long trousers cut closer to the body, cropped jackets and shorts, from tailored Bermudas to boxer shorts.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading
Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2027. Closer trousers and cropped jackets are rising; Forecut expects the closer line to continue into AW27.

On this pageThe evidenceShapes to lead withShapes to watchWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

Milan showed lighter, less oversized tailoring with open necklines and more airflow, long trousers cut closer to the body, cropped leather jackets and five-pocket slim trousers. In Paris, collections kept to cleaner, more minimal, straight-edged silhouettes; classic suiting was loosened, and black suits sat alongside smart shorts and athletic shapes at Dries Van Noten, Louis Gabriel Nouchi, Egonlab and Ami. Micro jackets (shrunken peacoats, collarless truckers, bombers and blousons) and boxer shorts were among the season's defining shapes. Thom Browne showed pleated skirts for men.

Shapes to lead with

  • Soft summer suit: unlined, softer shoulder, lighter double-breasted versions.
  • Closer straight trouser: long, closer to the leg, with a single pleat or flat front.
  • Cropped jacket: blouson, trucker or micro peacoat ending at the waist.
  • Shorts: tailored Bermudas, suit shorts and smart shorts.
  • Camp and tunic shirts: for sets and pyjama-inspired looks.

Shapes to watch

Boxer shorts as outerwear and skirts for men are early, fashion-customer ideas. Very oversized tailoring is giving way to lighter, closer cuts.

Make

Products and customers

  • Summer suits, straight trousers, Bermudas and suit shorts, cropped blousons, camp and tunic shirts.
Design

Design implications

  • Slim trouser blocks slightly from SS26 relaxed fits, keeping room at the seat.
  • Shorten jacket blocks; adjust pocket placement for cropped lengths.
  • Develop suit shorts with the same cloth as jackets.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Shift part of the wide-trouser buy into straighter, closer fits.
  • Sell suit shorts as part of a suit offer for hot markets.
