Cuffs, straps and linings

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Reworked denim

Denim reimagined: white cut-off shorts, cobalt-painted jeans, wax-coated bootcuts, embroidered and beaded denim, upcycled pieces and matching denim suits.

Why now

Denim is a core volume category, and new finishes refresh it without new fits.

Seen at

  • Area: Cut-off white shorts and trousers
  • Simkhai: Wax-coated bootcut jeans
  • PatBo: Embroidered and beaded denim
  • 7 For All Mankind: Skinny jeans
  • Miu Miu × Wrangler: Upcycled denim edition

Key pieces

Cut-offs, Coated denim, Denim suits; cut-off denim shorts.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Block-heel slingbacks

Slingbacks on mid block heels. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Elongated rib cuffs

Long ribbed cuffs that bunch at the wrist. A cuff detail is visible in every pose and costs little to add.

Design notes

  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Interface cuffs and check opening size for hand pass-through.
  • Use the cuff to repeat a trim from the front.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Fisherman sandals

Closed-toe sandals with cut slots. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Hiking sandals

Webbing sandals for trails. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Leather, suede, mesh or knit uppers, rubber cupsoles or EVA midsoles, recycled laces.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Leather slides

Wide-strap leather slides. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Shape the footbed so the foot stays secure without a back.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Elongated rib cuffs
  • Fisherman sandals
  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Hiking sandals
  • Leather slides

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Multi-strap ballet flats for men
  • Sport sandals

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Contrast cuffs

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.
  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
