The story

AW27 Women's Themes.

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cinched peplum jackets
  • Double-belted waistbands
  • Double-belted waists
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Cinched one-button jackets
  • Peplum blouses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted suit jackets
  • Belted knee-high boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted trench blazers
  • Contrast sash waists
  • Cropped blazers
  • Cummerbunds over suits
  • Denim waistcoats
  • Double-breasted jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long denim coats

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
