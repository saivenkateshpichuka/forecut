The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Hidden closures

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Why now

Supports the minimal, structured look and is easier to fasten.

Key pieces

Magnets, Concealed plackets, Hidden zips; hidden magnetic closures.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Hybrid boots

Smart boots on cushioned sport soles: Chelseas, hikers and weatherproof derbies.

Why now

Winter version of SS28 sport-meets-smart footwear.

Key pieces

Sport soles, Chelseas, Comfort; hybrid sport-smart boots.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Cuffed jean hems

Turned-up hems on straight jeans. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Denim with technical details

Denim jackets with zips and bellows pockets. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Hidden-rivet minimal jeans

Jeans with concealed rivets and minimal trims. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Raw-hem jeans

Cut, unfinished hems. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Stop fraying with a stitch line set in from the edge so it stays controlled after washing.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, peaking

Barrel-leg cargo jeans

Curved legs with cargo pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, peaking

Black and grey denim

Washed black and grey denim. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Denim with technical details
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Raw-hem jeans

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded denim outerwear

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Black and grey denim
  • Carpenter jeans
  • Clean wide-leg trouser jeans
  • Dark indigo tailoring
  • Denim chore coats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Button-fly denim shorts
  • Dark rinse, clean finish
  • Denim shirt-jackets
  • Denim waistcoats
  • Mid-rise relaxed jeans

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
