SS27 Men's Formalwear Styling

Formal SS27 menswear: the lighter suit with an open neck, monochrome black suits, leather suiting and tuxedos cut for both men and women.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

Designers broadly agreed that a well-dressed man still wears a suit, but lighter, with open necklines and unbuttoned or transparent shirts[1]. Paris showed black suits with matching trousers and monochrome dressing at Celine, IM Men and Valette Studio[2]. Saint Laurent used beige leather for suits[3]. At couture, Elie Saab cut tuxedos and capes for women as well as men[4].

Formulas

  • Light suit + open-neck shirt + loafers.
  • Black suit + black shirt, no tie.
  • Beige or camel leather jacket + matching trousers.
  • Seersucker suit + knit polo.
  • Tuxedo jacket + satin shirt for evening.

Design implications

  • Shirts designed to be worn open: collar that holds its shape without a tie.

Commercial implications

  • Offer suits as separates; summer weights lead.
