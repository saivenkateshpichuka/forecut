This is Forecut's SS27 menswear palette[1]. Confirm codes against a physical Pantone book before dyeing.

The evidence

Two strong colour signals came from the June shows. Aquatic blues, from turquoise and sky blue to electric blue, ran through leather jackets, shorts, linen shirts and loafers at Shinyakozuka, Auralee, Zegna and others[2]. Orange, from blood orange and rust to tangerine, appeared at Dries Van Noten, Willy Chavarria, Saint Laurent and Jacquemus[2]. Dries Van Noten also showed nude and aquamarine[3]. Paris collections leaned on monochrome palettes, notably at Celine and IM Men[4]. Leather came in camel and beige at Dior and Saint Laurent[3].

Colour groups

  • Core: sand and tobacco, with white and black for tailoring.
  • Seasonal: ice blue, butter and salmon pink for shirts, knits and summer suits.
  • Accent: rust and orange, and aquatic blue, for one piece per look (a bag, cap, jacket or shorts).

Combinations

  • Ice blue shirt with sand trousers and tobacco suede.
  • Head-to-toe sand or beige, including leather.
  • Turquoise or sky blue shorts with a white shirt.
  • Orange accessory or blouson over tobacco and sand.

Timing

Ice blue and sand are rising for SS27. Aquatic blues and orange are statement colours for high summer.

Products and customers

  • Ice blue: shirts. Sand and beige: suits, trousers, leather. Aquatic blue: shorts, linen shirts, leather jackets. Orange: blousons, knits, bags.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip ice blue across poplin, linen and knit; it shifts between fibres.
  • Use orange as one accessory or garment per look, as runways suggested.

Commercial implications

  • Lead shirts with ice blue; keep sand and tobacco deep.
  • Buy aquatic blue and orange as smaller statement buys.
