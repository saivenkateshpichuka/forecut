The story

What is behind it.

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Bishop-sleeve knits
  • Brushed mohair
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Cable-front cardigans

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Smocked knit details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Cricket cables with tipping
  • Embroidered denim
  • Gilded embroidered shirting
  • Knit fringe at hems
  • Knitted polo collars

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Sheer fine-gauge layers

Actions

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
