Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Concealed-button blazers

Blazers with hidden buttons for a clean front. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Denim with technical details

Denim jackets with zips and bellows pockets. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Half-zip knits

Short zips on funnel-neck knits. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Hidden magnetic closures

Fronts that close with concealed magnets instead of buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Hidden-rivet minimal jeans

Jeans with concealed rivets and minimal trims. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Hidden-zip blouses

Blouses with a concealed front zip. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Denim with technical details
  • Half-zip knits
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Hidden-zip blouses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Fine-gauge zip polos
  • Fly-front placket shirts
  • Funnel neck with concealed zip
  • Funnel-neck zip knits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Detachable hoods
  • Quarter-zip tops
  • Tech fleece jackets
  • Zip-neck polos
  • Zip-through knit jackets

Actions

  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
