Looks

Red-carpet arrival

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In tinted black with dusky violet.

Cardigans worn as shirts with drawstring tailored trousers, generous 1980s double-breasted suits and belgian loafers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Airport off-duty

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In greyed blue with tannic bordeaux.

Boxy short-sleeve shirts with relaxed pleated chinos, matching waistcoat three-pieces and black oxfords. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Press-day tailoring

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In copper khaki with sage.

Check shackets with tailored joggers, puffed pleated suit trousers and chelsea boots for tailoring. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Front-row statement

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In dusky violet with tinted black.

Elbow-seam jumpers with tailored track pants, single-pleat suit trousers and derby boots. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.
