AW27 / Business strategy / Women

AW27 Women's Category Opportunities

Growth for AW27 women: coats (double-faced and technical), skirt suits, velvet and lace evening-to-day, marl knits and fine merino.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Boland sees the upper body widening (80s shoulders, broad chests) over narrower trousers or skirts; key items include the skirt suit, broad-shouldered and reversible double-faced coats, camouflage parka and marl jumper[1]
  • Citywear suitings hide waterproof, breathable or windproof membranes, three-layer constructions and bi-stretch, so a jacket looks like a classic suit while performing like technical wear[2]
  • The Première Vision selection came out "silkier and more influenced by eveningwear codes" than expected: satins, crushed velvets and wool blends[2]
  • Première Vision links demand for softness, flexibility and ease to an ageing customer who still wants elegance[2]

Opportunities

  1. Coats.
  2. Skirt suits and blazers.
  3. Velvet and lace day-to-evening.
  4. Fine merino layering.
  5. Comfort-led elegance for older customers.

Reduce

Pure black basics; heavy synthetic outerwear.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Shift development to coats and suits.

Commercial implications

  • Re-weight open-to-buy to outerwear.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  2. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited (Florence Julienne), 4 September 2026
