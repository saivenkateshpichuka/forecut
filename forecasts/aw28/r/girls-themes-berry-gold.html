AW28 Girls' Berry and Gold

Festive kidswear in mulberry and antique gold: velvet, knits and occasion pieces.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Girls
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews
Market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 kidswear fairs take place in January 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fairs, kidswear brands' long-term practices and market drivers, and will be updated as evidence arrives.

The story

Forecut's "Berry and gold" story pairs mulberry and antique gold .

Colours

Mulberry, antique gold.

Silhouettes

  • Velvet dress or blazer.
  • Knit.

Fabrics

Velvet, knit.

Trims and details

Gold-tone buttons (secured).

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Products and customers

  • Occasion, knits.

Design implications

  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Evidence and sources

  • Forecut AW28 colour forecast, Forecut, November 2026. Long-range Forecut projection carrying the AW27 move away from black and the SS28 return of strong blues and violets
