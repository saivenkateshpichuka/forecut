SS27 Kids' Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Plan SS27 kidswear for a slower market: durable core, sets that sell together and apart, shallow buys on novelty, and full price for UV and organic value.

Spring/Summer 2027
Audience

The market

In Italy, childrenswear exports fell 9.3% and domestic sales 2.6% in 2025 (Confindustria Moda via Fortune Italia)[1]. Pitti Bimbo, which once had more than 500 exhibitors, now runs a smaller, more selective format[2]. Fashion overall is expected to grow in low single digits in 2027; resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion through 2027[3].

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Organic tees, stripes, denim, shorts, pyjamas in navy, sand, white
Seasonal (about a third) Swim and UV, sets, dresses, sailor pieces, brights
Statement (the rest) Character and story pieces, party, reversible and novelty

Pricing

  • Full price on UPF and certified-organic pieces; show the benefit.
  • Value on basics.
  • Sets priced to reward buying both pieces.
  • Premium on artist prints and hand craft.

Design implications

  • Durable construction supports hand-me-down and resale value.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Track sell-through weekly; chase swim before holidays.
