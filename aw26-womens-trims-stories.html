Trim stories

One story per theme.

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Power tailoring

Sharp, slick suiting in pinstripes and slate grey, with pointed shoulders, peak lapels and double-breasted fronts. Oxblood accents and polished heels finish the look.

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Fully loaded

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Sequinned and beaded jeans
  • Velvet lapel facings
  • Western yokes and pearl snaps
  • Halter-neck dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Peak-lapel coats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Wide ruffle collars
  • Large flap patch pockets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Raw-edge blanket coats
