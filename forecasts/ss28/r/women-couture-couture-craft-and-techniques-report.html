SS28 Women's Couture Craft and Techniques

Craft for SS28 women: crochet, raffia, hand embroidery and hand-finished linen.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Milano Unica's SS28 theme "Metamorphosis" rests on Lightness, Enrichment ("the rediscovery of handcrafted techniques") and Adaptability, with LIGHTWEAR, HANDWEAR and TECHWEAR themes[1]

Translating to ready-to-wear

  • Crochet.
  • Raffia and straw.
  • Hand embroidery on linen.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Hand work on hero pieces only.

Commercial implications

  • Tell the craft story; price as limited.

Evidence and sources

  1. MU Metamorphosis: Spring-Summer 2028 collections, Milano Unica, 2026
