How to use this report

  • Rising: commit to development and broader ranges.
  • Emerging: test in small capsules.
  • Peaking: keep in core, avoid over-buying.
  • Fading: clear stock and move on.

Statuses are Forecut's forecast, built from public runway, retail and trade signals and refined monthly. AW27, SS28 and AW28 are long-range outlooks.
