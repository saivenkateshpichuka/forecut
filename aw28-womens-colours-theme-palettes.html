How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

A long-range Forecut projection. It carries the AW27 move away from black and the SS28 return of strong blues and violets into winter. No AW28 fabric fair has taken place yet; it will be refined after the September 2027 fairs.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
