The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Soft boots

Flat riding boots, slim soles and soft leather replace chunky lugs.

Why now

Footwear follows the move to elegance; slimmer soles suit refined trousers and skirts.

Key pieces

Riding boots, Slim soles, Soft leather; soft knee-high riding boots.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers

Very full trousers with a low crotch gathered into a narrow ankle, shown at ALUF, Lilly Sarti and Uó at São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Fringed minis with back trains

Mini skirts cut in layered fringe with a longer train at the back; Oshobor showed an ombré blue version at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 Day 2. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops

A satin dress worn pulled down into a floor-length low-rise skirt with lace at waist and hem and a matching bra top; Simone Rocha SS27 on Camila Cabello at the Latin Grammys, Las Vegas, 12 Nov 2026. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Shirred-waist fleece skirts

Soft fleece skirts with a shirred, adjustable waist and frills in a contrast material; Uniqlo x Cecilie Bahnsen AW26 'winter walk', on sale in Japan from 20 Nov 2026. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Cut ruffles on the bias or curve for a soft drape; keep volume controlled.
  • Use elastic thread or shirring with enough stretch to pull on easily.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lightweight cotton, seersucker or viscose.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Barrel-leg trousers

Curved legs that taper at the hem. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops
  • Shirred-waist fleece skirts
  • Barrel-leg trousers
  • Bubble hems

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Tailored stretch trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Balloon trousers
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Double-layer sheer skirts
  • Flat-front straight trousers
  • Fluid satin wide legs
  • Lace-trimmed hems

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Mesh midi skirts

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
