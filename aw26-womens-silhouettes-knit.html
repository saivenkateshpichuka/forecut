Design notes

How to put the leading shapes into a range.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Bishop-sleeve knits

Full sleeves gathered into long cuffs. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Longline knit vests

Long sleeveless knits over shirts. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Adjust the back with a strap and buckle for fit.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Puff-shoulder cardigans

Raised, gathered shoulders on cardigans. The shoulder line sets the silhouette and how the garment drapes.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Adjust shoulder pads or remove them to match the silhouette.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Tunic-length knits

Long knits worn over trousers. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.
