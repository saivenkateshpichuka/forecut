SS27 Men's Knit Fabric Direction

Cool wools and extra-fine merino, elastic ribs, retro-sport piqués and sun-faded cotton cables for SS27 menswear knits.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Fabrics
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

Première Vision highlighted flat, Derby and Richelieu ribs, more elastic and elongated than jersey, and retro-sport bindings, honeycomb piqués, plated knits, diamond and wheat stitches in slightly yellowed, sepia-like tones[1]; cool wools and extra-fine merino[2]. Brunello Cucinelli's cable knits were dyed with resin for a sun-faded, one-of-a-kind finish[3].

Fabric by fabric

Knit Hand Uses
Extra-fine merino Smooth, cool Polos, V-necks, knit shirts
Cool wool Dry, crisp Knit jackets
Cotton cable, garment-faded Textured, soft Crewnecks
Honeycomb and piqué Structured, breathable Polos
Derby and Richelieu rib Elastic, close Tanks, fitted knits

Timing

In stores February to July 2027.

Products and customers

  • Knit polos and shirts, crewnecks, rib tanks, knit jackets.

Design implications

  • Specify recovery and pilling tests on merino and rib.
  • Garment-dye cotton knits for the sun-faded look.

Commercial implications

  • Merino at mid to premium; cotton cables at entry to mid.

Evidence and sources

  1. Spring-Summer 27 Decodings: Fabrics, Premiere Vision, 13 February 2026
  2. Decoding Spring-Summer 27 key transversal highlights, Premiere Vision, 5 February 2026
  3. Pitti Uomo SS 2027: menswear highlights, Wallpaper*, June 2026
