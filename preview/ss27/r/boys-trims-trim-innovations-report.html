SS27 Boys' Trim Innovations

Functional details for boys: UPF-rated components, reflective trims, scented and novelty finishes tested for skin safety.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Poco Blush, designed "almost exclusively for girls", showed bold hues and a loose, printed, reversible dress, and a T-shirt that releases a minty scent when crumpled[1]
  • Sticky Lemon showed backpacks in key shades from past collections and a mini digital camera that attaches to a bag[1]
  • Teeter Totter showed UPF50+ swim and sportswear, backpacks and ultra-light, sand-free beach towels[2]

Readiness

Innovation Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Reflective trims Ready
Sand-free towel fabric Ready
Scented finishes Test skin safety first
Bag-attached gadgets Accessory category; check toy rules

Design implications

  • Skin-safety evidence for any finish.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Novelty as small gift buys.

Evidence and sources

  1. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
