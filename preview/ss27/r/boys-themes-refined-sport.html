SS27 Boys' Refined Sport

Sport made smarter for boys: mesh and piqué polos, track sets in clean colour, UV tees and swim shorts that move from pitch to pool.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Themes

2026-11-25

Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The story

Playtime Paris showed sport "through a more refined, creative lens" [1]. Pitti Bimbo's pool theme [2] and the move to UV-protective, quick-dry pieces [3] make performance a summer basic. Teeter Totter's UPF50+ swim and sportswear [4] show the product. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to boyswear, roughly ages 4 to 14.

Colours

Navy, red and white, with aqua and grass green. Red is the season's newness [1].

Silhouettes

  • Piqué and mesh polos.
  • Track jacket and shorts set.
  • Swim shorts with a matching UV tee.
  • Pull-on shorts with a flat drawcord-free waistband.

Fabrics

  • Micro-mesh and piqué [5].
  • UPF-rated swim and jersey.
  • Recycled polyester for track pieces [3].

Trims and details

  • Contrast piping and side stripes.
  • Rubberised badges instead of metal.
  • Flat, covered elastic waists.

Timing

In stores March to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Polos, track sets, UV tees, swim shorts, caps, sneakers.
  • Customer: boys who choose their own clothes; parents buying for camp and holiday.

Design implications

  • No drawstrings at the waist of swim shorts for younger sizes; use elastic and a flat tie only where permitted for the age.
  • Test UPF claims to EN 13758 or AS/NZS 4399.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Track sets and polos are reorder lines; buy depth in navy and red.
