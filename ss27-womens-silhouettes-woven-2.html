Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, emerging

Ankle-strap balloon trousers

Loose balloon-leg trousers gathered at the ankle by a cinchable strap; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027, 6 Nov 2026. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, emerging

Armour-structured vests

Structured tailored vests with pronounced shoulders and a protective, armour-like shape over fluid dresses; De Goeye 10th-anniversary show, Brazil (29 Oct 2026). A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Adjust the back with a strap and buckle for fit.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, emerging

Basque waist bodices

A pointed waist seam that dips below the natural waist. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, emerging

Cinched peplum jackets

Short jackets flared below a defined waist seam. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
