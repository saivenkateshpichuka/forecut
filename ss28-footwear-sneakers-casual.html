The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Sport meets smart

Cushioned sport soles under refined flats, loafers and derbies: elegant shoes with sneaker comfort.

Why now

SS27 moved from sneakers to elegance; SS28 keeps the look but brings back comfort.

Key pieces

Sport soles, Hybrid shoes, Comfort heels; sport-sole smart flats.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Flat espadrille sneakers

Canvas shoes on jute soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Use a rubber-backed jute sole for durability.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Jute rope soles with rubber backing.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Leather court sneakers

Clean leather court shoes. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.
  • Keep the last consistent across the style family.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Mesh running sneakers

Retro runners with mesh uppers. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Perforated leather sneakers

Perforated toes on leather sneakers. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Use perforations as decoration and for breathability.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Running-inspired sneakers

Mesh runners with gum soles. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Satin ballet-trainers

Satin ballet shapes on trainer soles. A hybrid that borrows comfort from one category and style from another.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Add a cushioned insole and a flexible sole for comfort.
  • Wear-test for comfort over a full day.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Flat espadrille sneakers
  • Leather court sneakers
  • Mesh running sneakers
  • Perforated leather sneakers
  • Running-inspired sneakers
  • Satin ballet-trainers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Slip-on knit sneakers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Reworked racing sneakers
  • Sculpted rocker-sole trainers
  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Espadrilles

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Flatform sneakers
  • Mary Jane sneakers
  • Soft suede moccasins
  • Split-toe trainers
  • Suede skate shoes

Actions

  • Develop one sport sole and use it across three uppers.
  • Market comfort features with clear claims.
  • Keep uppers refined to stay smart.
  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
