AW27 Women's Woven Fabric Direction

AW27 women's wovens: double-faced wool and cashmere, flannels, satins and crushed velvets, membrane suitings and heritage checks.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Separable double-faced woollens return in more varied weights; ultrafine yarns and tight knits make wool lighter and softer, with thermal comfort without synthetics
  • The Première Vision selection came out "silkier and more influenced by eveningwear codes" than expected: satins, crushed velvets and wool blends
  • Citywear suitings hide waterproof, breathable or windproof membranes, three-layer constructions and bi-stretch, so a jacket looks like a classic suit while performing like technical wear
  • Milano Unica: double-faced wool and cashmere, flannels and cashmere fleece ("Classic"); ripstops and ultralight nylon ("Freestyle"); lurex jacquards, sequin tulle, metallic velvets and lamé tweeds ("Romance")
  • Checks lead prints: Boland suggests four checks in one garment; Milano Unica shows pinstripes, herringbone, glen checks and houndstooth at micro and macro scale
