How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams[1]
  • Performance, everyday and occasion wear merge: "technical blazers, adaptable skirts, and tailored baselayers", in sun-warmed neutrals and soft gradients[1]
  • Lace, corsetry and dramatic silhouettes return, reinterpreted with lighter materials[2]
  • SS27 was shaped by heat: Hypebeast called the sun "the true main character of SS27"[3]

Shapes to lead with

  • Technical blazer.
  • Ultrastretch trouser.
  • Linen suit.
  • Camp shirt.
  • Tailored shorts.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Develop blocks early; confirm proportions after the shows.

Commercial implications

  • Keep new shapes to tested quantities.

Evidence and sources

  1. SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
  2. After Dusk: why Spring/Summer 2028 is going dark, FashionUnited (Joanne Thomas, Director of Color, Future Snoops), 30 October 2026
  3. The SS27 trends: what comes after fashion's hottest season ever?, Hypebeast, July 2026
