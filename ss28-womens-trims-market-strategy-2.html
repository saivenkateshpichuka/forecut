              Design notes

              How to use the trims.

              Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, peaking

              Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts

              Pinstripe shirts with one exaggerated collar point, tucked into soft skirts, as at Dior SS27. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

              Design notes

              • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
              • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
              • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

              Fabric and trims

              Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

              Where to use it

              Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Womenswear, Dresses, peaking

              Beaded-fringe canvas dresses

              Structured canvas dresses finished with long beaded fringe; Hertunba opened Lagos Fashion Week 2026 Day 2 (29 Oct) with a black canvas dress and red beaded fringe. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

              Design notes

              • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
              • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
              • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

              Fabric and trims

              Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

              Where to use it

              Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

              Belted wrap fronts

              Self-fabric tie belts replacing buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

              Design notes

              • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
              • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
              • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

              Fabric and trims

              Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

              Where to use it

              Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

              Button-shoulder knits

              Buttoned tabs along the shoulder. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

              Design notes

              • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
              • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
              • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

              Fabric and trims

              Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

              Where to use it

              Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Strategy

              What to build, test, sell through and drop.

              Build depth

              Rising pieces

              • Nothing at this stage

              Test small

              Emerging pieces

              • Nothing at this stage

              Sell through

              Peaking pieces

              • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
              • Belted wrap fronts
              • Button-shoulder knits
              • Cargo midi skirts
              • Chunky rib turtlenecks

              Exit or rework

              • Brooch-fastened cardigans
              • Collarless cropped jackets

              • Double-breasted jackets
              • Elongated rib cuffs
