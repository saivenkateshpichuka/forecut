AW27 Women's Trending Trims Report

AW27 women's trims: horn, corozo and mother-of-pearl buttons, jewelled buckles, oxidised metals, lace insets and matte gold.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Milano Unica trims: horn, corozo, mother-of-pearl and bio-based polyester buttons, oxidised metal pulls, chains and recycled leather labels[1]
  • APLF trims include patinated brass buckles, hammered studs, horn closures, magnetic closures, invisible zips, jewelled buckles, decorative chains, oversized resin buckles and rubberised webbing[2]
  • Cited looks include sculptural pleats, funnel necks, round-cut waists (Jil Sander), padded shoulders (Carven), oversized fur coats (Saint Laurent), teddy-bear coats (Loewe) and all-over lace (Saint Laurent)[1]
