Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, peaking

Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs

Mary Janes, Boston clogs and Arizona sandals reworked with gold studs, leather ruffles and shearling in black, lilac, sandstone and chutney; Birkenstock 1774 Pont des Arts (Nov 2026). The upper material and shape carry the trend message.

Design notes

  • Use faux fur or recycled shearling-look pile; make fur parts detachable where you can so the garment is easier to care for.
  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test the upper for flex cracking and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Faux fur, recycled shearling-look pile or bonded borg, backed with a stable knit.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
