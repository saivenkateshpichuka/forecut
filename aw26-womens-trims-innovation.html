Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Double-breasted jackets
  • Hidden-placket overcoats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Button-front midi skirts
  • Half-zip knits
  • Henley plackets
  • Western yokes and pearl snaps
  • Belted safari jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Long exposed button fly
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Lace-edged bias slips
  • Large flap patch pockets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Raw-edge blanket coats
  • Visible corset boning
