The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Sport meets smart

Cushioned sport soles under refined flats, loafers and derbies: elegant shoes with sneaker comfort.

Why now

SS27 moved from sneakers to elegance; SS28 keeps the look but brings back comfort.

Key pieces

Sport soles, Hybrid shoes, Comfort heels; sport-sole smart flats.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts

Pleated-front tuxedo shirts cut in light, crisp parachute-cotton poplin; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027 evening, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Taffeta jogger evening trousers

Featherweight taffeta joggers with elastic cuffs worn under a tuxedo blazer for evening; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Articulated sleeves

Shaped elbows for easier movement in outerwear. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Clean column dresses

Straight, unadorned column shapes. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Clean minimal T-shirts

Plain, well-cut tees as a base piece. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Articulated sleeves
  • Clean column dresses
  • Clean minimal T-shirts
  • Clean straight-leg trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Denim with technical details
  • Elastic-back tailored waists
  • Hidden magnetic closures

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Reworked racing sneakers
  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Broderie anglaise dresses

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Flat-front straight trousers

Actions

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
