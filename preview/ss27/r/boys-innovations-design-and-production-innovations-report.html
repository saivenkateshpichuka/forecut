SS27 Boys' Design and Production Innovations

Design and production for boyswear: durable construction, unisex blocks and product data ready for the EU Digital Product Passport.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • True Artist makes unisex outfits for children and pre-teens[1]
  • Newer lines emphasise breathability, quick drying and comfort; childrenswear is moving toward functionality, material quality and durability[2]
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026 large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel[3]

Actions

  • Shared unisex blocks for tees and shorts.
  • Reinforced knees and seats.
  • Record fibre, origin, care and safety tests per style.

Design implications

  • Grade for growth.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Plan resale and donation routes for unsold stock.
