The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Harrods, AW26 menswear trends; Who What Wear

Fully loaded

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Why now

Social media rewards texture and shine, and embellishment makes basics feel special.

Key pieces

Sequins, Beadwork, Sheer layers; sequin and chainmail minis.

Source: Who What Wear, AW26 trends (the source describes the trend without naming brands)

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Buckled ankle boots

Ankle boots with a buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Chelsea ankle boots

Elastic-sided ankle boots. Shaft height and fit decide how the boot works with trousers and skirts.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Use strong elastic gussets and a pull tab for easy wear.
  • Check calf width across sizes; offer wide-calf fits.

Fabric and trims

Footwear, Boots, rising

Flat suede knee boots

Flat knee boots in suede. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Hiker styles

Hiking boots worn with tailoring. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Kitten-heel boots

Low kitten heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Flat suede knee boots
  • Hiker styles
  • Kitten-heel boots
  • Knee-high block-heel boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Embroidered and silk boots
  • Harness buckle straps
  • Kitten-heel knee boots
  • Pointed-toe ankle boots
  • Square-toe knee boots
  • Western knee boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Slouchy mid-calf boots
  • Stretch knee boots on block heels
  • Western stitching

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Lug-sole Chelseas

Actions

  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
