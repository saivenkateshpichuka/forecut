SS27 Kids' Couture Craft and Techniques

Craft in premium kidswear: artist-designed prints, handcrafted embroidery, handmade costumes and luxury "princess" dressing.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • Halcyon Nights builds several graphic universes each season with patterns designed by a panel of artists[1].
  • Bobo Choses publishes illustrated books alongside its collections[1].
  • Selini Action makes handmade costumes decorated with shells, pebbles, seaweed and embroidery[2].
  • Speranza and Junona show luxury "princess" style[1].
  • Fatamadrina's "Wildflowers Bloom" uses delicate handcrafted embroidery[3].

Translating to ready-to-wear

  • Artist collaborations on prints.
  • Hand embroidery on one placement per garment.
  • Smocking and pleating for occasion dresses.

Design implications

  • Budget hand work into hero pieces.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Tell the craft and artist story; price as limited.

Evidence and sources

  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  3. Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026. Design studio forecast; used for season-wide fabric, silhouette and print notes
