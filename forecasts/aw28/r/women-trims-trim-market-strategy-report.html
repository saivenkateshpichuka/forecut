AW28 Women's Trim Market Strategy

Trim strategy for AW28 womenswear: durable, verifiable and easy to change late.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Fashion entered 2027 expecting low single-digit growth, with markdown risk and faster growth in resale

Build, test, sell through

Action Trims
Build Horn, corozo and leather buttons; tonal piping
Test Antique-gold hardware, embroidery
Sell through Logo hardware

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Document trim materials for any environmental claim.

Commercial implications

  • Late-added trims (embroidery, piping) let you react to sales.

Evidence and sources

  • Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
