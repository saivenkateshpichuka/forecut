The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Key pieces

Stretch wool, Water-resistant, Travel suits; technical wool suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Earth deepens

Warm earth orange deepens into rust and brick, often on quilted and padded pieces.

Why now

Colours of the year carry into winter in deeper shades.

Key pieces

Rust, Brick, Quilting; quilted and padded dresses.

Source: Based on Forecut's colour direction for the season. Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Argyle knits

Diamond argyle patterns on knits. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Textured stripe knits

Striped knits with texture. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

Fair Isle yoke cardigans

Patterned yokes on button cardigans. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, peaking

Floral print midis

Small and painterly florals on midi dresses. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Striped poplin shirts

Bold and candy stripes on poplin. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Textured stripe knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Fair Isle yoke cardigans
  • Floral print midis
  • Striped poplin shirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Fair Isle yokes
  • Striped Breton tops
  • Tartan and check jacquards

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
