SS27 Women's Trim Stories

The trims that carry each SS27 women's theme: ribbon and rolled hems for One-Layer Summer, lace and satin bows for Lingerie as Outerwear, belts and peplum seams for The Waist Returns, and lace, smocking and embroidery for Handcraft.

Women

Market
Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

One-Layer Summer

Narrow rolled hems and French seams keep sheer cloth clean[1]; ribbon binding replaces facings; organza petals, as at Dior, give decoration without weight[2]. Hardware stays minimal so a look still reads as one layer.

Lingerie as Outerwear

Lace trims, satin bows and adjustable straps, with hook-and-eye and visible lingerie fastenings. Fendi showed lace-trimmed bras with a fur stole[2]; Première Vision highlighted dark Leavers lace and lace with a melted-film sheen[3]. Bra tops need lingerie components: firm elastic top edges and adjustable sliders.

The Waist Returns

Belts carry the waist story: Anteprima, Ferragamo, Alberta Ferretti and Ermanno Scervino cinched fluid pieces, Bottega Veneta and Max Mara used leather belts, and Ferragamo enlarged belts and covered snap buttons in sequins[4]. Add peplum seams, basque-waist panels and belt loops.

Handcraft

Lace flowers, smocking and embroidery on poplin and knits. Première Vision's "Drawn" embroidery direction, with scattered, childlike florals and grass stems on tulle and gauze[3], and Chanel's corsages[2] point the way.

In Motion

Fringe and tassels: Roksanda showed tasselled evening dresses and Ahluwalia fringed skirts, and fringe was a fast-rising detail in Copenhagen[1]. Use fringe at hems and on scarves where movement shows it.

Design implications

  • Choose one trim family per theme and use it consistently across the theme's pieces.
  • Lab-dip trims to the garment colour; lace and ribbon in particular vary by dye lot.
  • Test attachment strength and washing for petals, beads and fringe.

Commercial implications

  • Trims are the cheapest way to make core shapes read as a theme; plan one core skirt or shirt in several theme trims.
  • Sell belts, corsages and bows separately as add-ons.
