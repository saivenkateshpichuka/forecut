SS27 Kids' Lull

Elevated basics set to the rhythm of the sea: calm, coastal, intentional pieces where design serves well-being rather than novelty.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Themes

Trend status
Rising

The story

Future Snoops named Lull one of its two kids' trends for SS27, presented at Playtime Paris and New York. Lull is "set to the rhythm of the sea" and celebrates "the quiet elegance of elevated basics"; it "favors emotional resonance over novelty and mindfulness over momentum", with design as "a tool for well-being, presence, and lasting impact"[1]. Its inspirations include coastal comfort, California casual design and seaside living[1].

The trade fairs back the mood. Fortune Italia concludes that childrenswear is moving towards more attention to functionality, material quality and garment durability, with less short-lived seasonal fashion[2]. Gray Label shows a minimalist range with no patterns except stripes, in pieces described as "exceptionally soft"[3]. Cadhan showed soft organic cotton pyjamas[4].

Colours

Sand, aqua, soft navy and white: the calmer half of the SS27 kids' palette.

Silhouettes

  • Relaxed shapes, oversized T-shirts, dungarees and lightweight jackets[5].
  • Coordinated sets; easy pull-on trousers and shorts.

Fabrics

Lightweight linen, voile, organic cotton and terry[5]; soft jersey.

Trims and details

Subtle details and clever contrasts: tonal stitching, stripe trims, wooden or corozo buttons, minimal branding.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027. A long-running direction that supports year-round core ranges.

Products and customers

  • Core tees, stripe tops, linen shirts and shorts, dungarees, sets, pyjamas.
  • Customers: parents buying fewer, better pieces; gift buyers.

Design implications

  • Spend on fabric hand and fit, not on decoration.
  • Build pieces that pass between siblings: neutral colour, durable seams.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Core lines suit replenishment and longer selling periods.
  • Price on quality; avoid early markdowns.
