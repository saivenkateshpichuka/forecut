Why? Gen Z makes up 40–45% of India's online shoppers and drove almost half of new orders in 2025. They discover styles on social video and buy fast, often through quick commerce.

What? Gen Z mixes ethnic and street: short kurtas over jeans, sarees with sneakers, jackets over lehengas, oversized tees, baggy denim, athleisure co-ords and gender-fluid boxy tailoring.

Who's showing it?

  • Gully Labs: Everyday leather sneakers with Indian colour stories
  • Payal Pratap and Jubinav: Embroidered jackets and sporty knitwear

How to build the capsule

  • Style every ethnic hero piece with sneakers and denim in imagery.
  • Launch small, fast drops and watch social signals weekly.
  • Make sure products are listed on quick-commerce and marketplace apps.
