SS27 Men's Tender Masculinity

Softness, sensuality and decoration in menswear: nude and aquamarine tones, lingerie references, organza feathers and floral satins.

The story

Menswear embraced softness and sensuality in Paris: Dries Van Noten showed sensual silhouettes in nude and aquamarine tones and Egonlab lingerie-inspired ensembles[1]. Patrick Clark, fashion editor at GQ France, called the season "a confirmation of the crossover between the men's and women's wardrobes"[1]. At Pitti Uomo, Simone Rocha's first men's show used boas of organza feathers, cornflower-blue embellishment, aprons and pinafores, under the theme "tender masculinity"[2]. Willy Chavarria showed floral satin sets[3], and Thom Browne put pleated skirts on men in Milan[4].

Colours

Salmon pink, butter and ice blue, with nude and aquamarine tones. See SS27 Men's Season Colours.

Silhouettes

  • Fluid shirts and trousers; floral satin sets.
  • Skirts and aprons layered over trousers (fashion customers).
  • Soft, unstructured jackets.

Fabrics

Satin, including floral satin[3]; organza; sheer cottons; fine knits.

Trims and details

Embroidered florals, organza feathers[2], brooches, beading.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. An emerging direction: test with fashion customers; Forecut expects softer colour and fabric to reach the mainstream before skirts do.

Products and customers

  • Satin shirts and sets; embroidered shirts; soft-coloured knitwear; skirts and aprons for fashion customers.

Design implications

  • Introduce softness through colour and fabric first (satin, pastel knits).
  • Keep construction masculine where the customer needs it: shoulders, collars, trouser blocks.

Commercial implications

  • Small buys for skirts and aprons; build depth in satin shirts and soft-coloured knits.
  • Merchandise by mood, not by gender, for fashion stores and online.
