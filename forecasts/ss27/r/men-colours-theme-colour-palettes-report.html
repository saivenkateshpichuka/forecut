Why these palettes

They follow the runway colour: monochrome sets at Celine and IM Men, khaki, blue and beige sleepwear sets with black piping at Dior, nude and aquamarine at Dries Van Noten, and Forecut's SS27 menswear palette.

Design

Design implications

  • Lead colour on most of the look; accent on one piece or the piping.
  • Share sand across themes so pieces cross-sell.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Build depth in sand and ice blue; accents as small buys.
