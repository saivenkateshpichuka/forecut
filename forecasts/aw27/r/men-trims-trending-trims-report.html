How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Menswear forecaster Edwin van den Hoek: jackets, trousers and down grow bigger without weight ("It looks very big, but it is very light to wear"); layering at the neck; fur or hairy collars; leather as a refined detail
  • Milano Unica trims: horn, corozo, mother-of-pearl and bio-based polyester buttons, oxidised metal pulls, chains and recycled leather labels
  • APLF trims include patinated brass buckles, hammered studs, horn closures, magnetic closures, invisible zips, jewelled buckles, decorative chains, oversized resin buckles and rubberised webbing
