SS27 Boys' Workwear Denim

Denim leads boyswear: carpenter shorts, chore jackets, overalls and coordinated denim sets, washed light and mid blue.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The story

Denim's "presence felt stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons" at Playtime Paris, across everyday, workwear-inspired and coordinated styles [1]. Dungarees and lightweight jackets are on Fatamadrina's SS27 silhouette list [2]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to boyswear, roughly ages 4 to 14.

Colours

Light and mid indigo with sand, red and white.

Silhouettes

  • Carpenter and relaxed denim shorts.
  • Chore jacket and overshirt.
  • Short overalls.
  • Denim shirt and short set.

Fabrics

  • Lightweight cotton denim and chambray.
  • Twill for workwear shorts.
  • Recycled or organic cotton denim where certified [3].

Trims and details

  • Hammer loops and patch pockets (bar-tacked, no sharp edges).
  • Corozo or metal shank buttons, pull-tested.
  • Contrast topstitching.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027; carries into autumn.

Products and customers

  • Denim shorts, chore jackets, overalls, denim shirts.

Design implications

  • Adjustable waists for growth.
  • Pre-wash to control shrinkage; check abrasion on knees and seat.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Denim is a volume category for boys; keep the wash palette tight.

Evidence and sources
