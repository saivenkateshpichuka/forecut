Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Forecut collects when you use forecut.in, how it is used, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little personal data as possible and to handle it in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us, including through the early-access or feedback form (which opens your own email app), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your name, role and feedback.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider and font service automatically receive standard technical data when you load the website, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not use this data to identify you.

We do not ask for passwords or payment details, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

2. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Forecut does not use advertising, tracking cookies or third-party analytics. The website does not require an account. If this changes, we will update this policy before doing so.

3. How we use information

  • to reply to your messages and enquiries;
  • to inform you about the launch, if you asked us to;
  • to improve Forecut using your feedback; and
  • to keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

4. Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to run the website and email:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), website hosting
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC), web fonts
  • Zoho Mail (Zoho Corporation), email
  • GoDaddy, domain registration

These providers process data under their own privacy policies, and some may process it outside India.

5. Retention

We keep emails and feedback only as long as needed to respond and to improve the Service, and delete them on request.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information you have shared with us, or withdraw consent to receive launch updates. Email hello@forecut.in and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, including grievances: hello@forecut.in
