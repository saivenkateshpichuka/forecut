AW27 Women's Remastered Classics

Familiar pieces made personal: blazers, skirts, marl jumpers and checks remixed with one colour that pops.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading
Season
Autumn Winter 2027
Audience
Women

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The story

Boland's "Individual Familiarity" expects the blazer, skirt, trousers and suit to dominate through personal combinations; pure retro becomes nostalgia unless one hue pops . Checks lead, from pinstripe to houndstooth .

Colours

Mushroom, creamy white and tinted black with one accent: mustard or chartreuse .

Silhouettes

  • Blazer with a court skirt.
  • Marl jumper over a shirt.
  • Tapered trousers.

Fabrics

Flannel, glen check, herringbone, marl knit.

Trims and details

Contrast piping, horn and corozo buttons .

Make

Products and customers

  • Blazers, skirts, jumpers, check trousers.
Design

Design implications

  • Change linings, piping or colour rather than shape.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Core volume theme; buy deep in neutrals.
