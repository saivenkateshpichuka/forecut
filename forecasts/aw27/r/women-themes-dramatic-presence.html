AW27 Women's Dramatic Presence

Beauty as an answer to hard times: eveningwear codes in daywear, lace, crushed velvet, satin and broad shoulders worn without waiting for a party.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The story

Christine Boland's "Dramatic Antidote" and "Unapologetic Presence" seek beauty, seduction and wonder [1]. Première Vision's fabric selection came out "silkier and more influenced by eveningwear codes than the teams had anticipated", with satins and crushed velvets [2]. Milano Unica's "Romance" adds lurex jacquards, sequin tulle, metallic velvets and lamé tweeds [1].

Colours

Deep violet and tannic bordeaux with "sensual black" [3] and waxy ochre light.

Silhouettes

  • Broad-shouldered jackets over slim skirts.
  • Bias satin slips layered over knits.
  • All-over lace dresses.
  • Skirt suits with ruffled blazers.

Fabrics

Satin, crushed and panne velvet, lace, lurex jacquard, lamé tweed.

Trims and details

Jewelled buttons, ruffles, lace insets, matte gold and antique silver hardware.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Products and customers

  • Evening-to-day dresses, velvet blazers, lace tops, satin skirts.
  • Customer: women who dress up "without an occasion".

Design implications

  • Back velvet and satin with comfortable linings.

Commercial implications

  • Price the hero velvet and lace pieces at premium; keep satin skirts at mid.
