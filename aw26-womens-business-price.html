Overview

The short version.

Full price on what is new, value on what customers already know, and limited pricing for craft and new materials.

Price positioning

Where each kind of piece sits.

Good

Core shapes in core colours: carry depth and keep making simple.

  • Ballet flats
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Contrast-piped edges

Better

Seasonal pieces in new colours and shapes: the main margin of the range.

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Balloon trousers
  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Barrel-leg trousers

Best

Statement, craft and new materials: small buys at full price.

  • Argyle knits
  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Belted safari jackets
  • Black and grey denim
  • Bow-trim flats

Positioning notes

  • Hold full price on rising pieces; they are what is new in store.
  • Use peaking pieces for value and promotion: customers already know them.
  • Price emerging and craft pieces as limited, with the story on the label.
