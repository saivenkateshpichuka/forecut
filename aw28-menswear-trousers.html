Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Skirts for men

Skirts worn as menswear on the runway; Cynthia Abila sent male models out in skirts at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct). A focused detail update for trousers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Articulated-knee trousers

Shaped knees for movement. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Flat-front slim trousers

Clean flat fronts on slim legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Tailored wool shorts

Pleated shorts in wool. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.
  • Check length on petite and tall fits.

Menswear, Trousers, peaking

Barrel trousers

Curved, tapered barrel legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Menswear, Trousers, peaking

Corduroy wide trousers

Wide trousers in corduroy. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Choose wale size for the garment: fine wale for shirts, wider wale for trousers and jackets.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton corduroy in 8–14 wale.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Skirts for men
  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Flat-front slim trousers
  • Tailored wool shorts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Technical wool cargo trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel trousers
  • Corduroy wide trousers
  • Double-knee work trousers
  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Drawstring tailored trousers
  • Painter trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Deep turn-ups
  • Full straight legs
  • Gurkha-buckle waists
  • Relaxed pleated chinos
  • Selvedge chinos
  • Single and double pleats

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
