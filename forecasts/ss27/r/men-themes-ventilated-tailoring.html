SS27 Men's Ventilated Tailoring

The suit adapts to heat: lighter, open-necked and in places transparent, in silk, gauze, seersucker and perforated leather.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading
Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2027; strongest for wedding season and summer events. Forecut expects lighter tailoring to carry into SS28.

On this pageThe storyColoursSilhouettesFabricsTrims and detailsImagesWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The story

Milan designers "went lighter" in silhouette and broadly agreed that a well-dressed man still wears a suit, but the suit is adapting: lighter, less oversized tailoring with open necklines and more airflow, dress shirts left unbuttoned or rendered transparent. Thom Browne showed layered seersucker suiting. In Paris, Jonathan Anderson's Dior collection had fluid suits in very light, almost sheer silk and gauze, and Hypebeast tracked sheer double-breasted suits at Dior and sheer trousers at Hed Mayner. At Pitti Uomo, Herno launched crease-proof travel pieces in stretch wool and technical fabrics.

Colours

Ice blue, sand and white, with tobacco for depth. See SS27 Men's Season Colours.

Silhouettes

  • Unlined, soft-shoulder jackets with open-necked shirts.
  • Lighter double-breasted suits.
  • Long trousers cut closer to the body.

Fabrics

Silk and gauze, seersucker, perforated and woven leather, fine lightweight wools blended with silk or linen, crease-proof stretch wool.

Trims and details

Minimal canvas and interlining, unlined backs, mother-of-pearl buttons, open-weave and perforated panels.

Images

Make

Products and customers

  • Unlined summer suits; seersucker suits; linen travel suits; sheer or semi-sheer shirts.
  • Customers: working men who still wear tailoring in hot climates; wedding guests.
Design

Design implications

  • Remove weight: half-canvas or unconstructed fronts, quarter lining.
  • Test semi-sheer shirting for modesty and comfort before committing.
  • Use crease-recovery and stretch wool for travel pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Lead tailoring with summer weights rather than four-season cloth.
  • Price sheer and silk pieces at premium; seersucker and linen at mid.
