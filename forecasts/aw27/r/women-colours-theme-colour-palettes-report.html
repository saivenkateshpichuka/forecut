How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • At Première Vision, deep violet, plums, dusty pinks, mauves and tinted off-blacks set the tone; violet is "centre stage", and plum and deep red become alternatives to black[1]
  • Boland expects mustard and chartreuse, creamy white and "sensual black", with bold red, yellow, blue, green and purple[2]
