Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Slim-sole Chelseas
  • Soft knee-high riding boots
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Embroidered and silk boots
  • Flat suede knee boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Hybrid sport-smart boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Over-the-knee boots
  • Patent knee boots
  • Square-toe ankle boots
  • Stretch knee boots on block heels
  • Balloon trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Reduce lug-sole depth in new styles.
  • Add a flat knee-high riding boot.
  • Offer wide-calf fits.
