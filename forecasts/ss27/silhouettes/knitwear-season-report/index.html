Overview

The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and colour comes back in full: purple, true red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites and sand.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Argyle knits, Bishop-sleeve knits, Brooch-fastened cardigans and Brushed cardigan coats are rising and keep growing into AW27.

Test in small quantities

Fringed macramé tops, Cardigans worn as shirts, Crochet-panel knits and Elbow-seam jumpers are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Chunky rib turtlenecks, Colour-block knits and Shawl-collar pullovers are at their height. Do not add depth.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe; DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts; Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing

16-1448 TCX and 12-1009 TCX

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets; Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship; Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery

17-1147 TCX and 11-1306 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

The waist returns

After years of oversized shapes the waist is back. Peplums, basque waists, balloon and bubble shapes and belted or wrapped fronts define the body again.

Why now

Forecasters see a return to elegance and shape. A defined waist refreshes existing silhouettes without new categories.

Seen at

Li Edelkoort, "Craft of Black": Wraps and belts the waist tightly; SS27 bridal and evening: Corseted bodices, basque waists and bubble skirts; Double belt: Returns as a waist accessory

Key pieces

Peplum, Basque waist, Bubble hem

18-4048 TCX and 13-3920 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, Spring/Summer 2027 trend report

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Bishop-sleeve knits
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Cable fisherman jumpers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Cardigans worn as shirts
  • Crochet-panel knits
  • Elbow-seam jumpers
  • Fine-gauge zip polos
  • Half-zip knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Colour-block knits
  • Shawl-collar pullovers
  • Sheer fine-gauge layers
  • Square-neck knits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions for the range

  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.
  • Add lace trims at cuffs and hems of core shirts.
  • Use machine smocking on bodices and cuffs.
  • Partner with a craft cluster for a premium capsule.

Markets

How each region reads SS27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Best fit: Knit fringe at hems, Sheer fine-gauge layers, Knitted polo collars, Openwork and pointelle knits

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Best fit: Knit fringe at hems, Sheer fine-gauge layers, Knitted polo collars, Openwork and pointelle knits

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Best fit: Knitted polo collars, Fine-gauge zip polos, Ribbed polo knits, Rib-knit sweatshirts

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Best fit: Crochet tops and cardigans, Striped cotton crewnecks, Crochet-panel knits

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Best fit: Rib-knit sweatshirts, Cardigans worn as shirts, Colour-block knits, Raglan sweatshirts

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Best fit: Sheer fine-gauge layers, Crochet tops and cardigans, Crochet-panel knits
