SS28 Women's Partywear Styling

Party SS28 womenswear.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 colour shift "After Dusk": "Deep aubergines, inky blues and murky greens are bringing a darker, more atmospheric mood to warm-weather dressing"[1]
  • Matte finishes ground dark colour, satin and gloss intensify it, and sheer fabrics and open knits lighten it; extreme heat is moving socialising into the evening[1]
  • Lace, corsetry and dramatic silhouettes return, reinterpreted with lighter materials[1]

Formulas

  • Inky slip dress + acid-lemon bag.
  • Lace top + satin skirt.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Line sheer, metallic and embellished pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Party buys sell close to events; buy shallow.

Evidence and sources

  1. After Dusk: why Spring/Summer 2028 is going dark, FashionUnited (Joanne Thomas, Director of Color, Future Snoops), 30 October 2026
