SS27 Men's Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Plan SS27 menswear for low growth: separates and sets that combine, summer tailoring sold as separates, shallow fashion buys and full price on what is new.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The market

Fashion is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027; sales up 4% with inventory up 10% is a weaker position than steady stock; mid-market brands face promotional pressure from luxury and value players[1]. Resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion through 2027[1].

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Shirts in ice blue and white, straight trousers, knit polos, sand and tobacco
Seasonal (about a third) Summer suits, shorts, cropped blousons, sets, satin shirts
Statement (the rest) Studded denim, sheer shirts, skirts and aprons, orange and aquatic pieces

Pricing

  • Full price on rising pieces (cropped jackets, summer suits, sets).
  • Value on core reorder lines.
  • Premium for leather and hand-finished pieces.
  • Sets priced to reward buying both pieces.

Design implications

  • Fewer styles, more combinations; shared fabrics across shirts and sets.

Commercial implications

  • Weekly sales-to-stock tracking; chase winners.
  • In the EU, plan rework and resale routes for unsold stock.

Evidence and sources
