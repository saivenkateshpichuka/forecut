The palette

AccentAcid lemonPantone 12-0646 TCX Celandine#EBDF67A sharp yellow to light up the dark mood.
AccentPeachPantone 13-1125 TCX Peach Quartz#F5B895A soft warm accent for knits and dresses.

Where it works

Rising and peaking kidswear pieces to colour this season.

Blue hour

Royal blue, sonar turquoise and inky blue: summer blues with real pigment.

For kids: shirting, denim, swim, tailoring.

Atmosphere

Greyish mauve and magnetic violet, soft and hazy against bleached linen.

For kids: dresses, knits, evening.

Vegetal

Straw, khaki green and warm beige for natural fibres and crafted surfaces.

For kids: linen, crochet, menswear, accessories.

After dusk

Summer goes dark: inky blue and murky green with an acid lemon spark.

For kids: evening, statement pieces, footwear.
