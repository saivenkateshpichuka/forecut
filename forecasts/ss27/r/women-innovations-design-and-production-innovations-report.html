SS27 Women's Design and Production Innovations

Body scanning and 3D development, product data ready for the EU's Digital Product Passport, and production planned around new EU rules on unsold stock and low-value imports.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageDesign: scanning and 3DProduct data and regulationProduction planningWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

Design: scanning and 3D

Balenciaga used 3D body scans to build new mannequins for its July 2026 couture collection. The same approach is available to ready-to-wear: scanning fit models and developing first samples in 3D reduces sampling rounds. Forecut's Garment Studio supports drafting and 3D checks before a physical sample.

Product data and regulation

  • Digital Product Passport: it will not apply to all fashion from the start of 2027; the European Commission plans to adopt the textiles delegated act in the fourth quarter of 2027. Brands that collect fibre, origin and care data now will be ready.
  • Unsold goods: since 19 July 2026, large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel, accessories and footwear in the EU.
  • Low-value imports: since 1 July 2026, the EU applies a temporary €3 customs duty per item on consignments worth up to €150.
  • Ultra-fast fashion: a French law adopted on 8 July 2026 allows penalties of up to 50% of a product's price.

Production planning

  • Sourcing: brands are diversifying towards Vietnam, Bangladesh, India, Turkey and Morocco while keeping China relationships.
  • Calendar: Lunar New Year falls on 6 February 2027; Vietnamese and Chinese factories slow from late January and usually return to full capacity in early March. Plan bulk for later spring drops after the holiday.
  • Climate risk: the World Meteorological Organization warns that El Niño could bring drought, flooding and extreme heat to key sourcing regions through early 2027.
Design

Design implications

  • Develop first samples in 3D and keep physical sampling for final fit and fabric approval.
  • Record fibre composition, origin and care for every style from the start.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Buy shallower in the EU and plan rework, resale or donation routes for unsold stock.
  • Build buffer time around Lunar New Year and climate disruption into the SS27 critical path.
