The story

What is behind it.

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

  • Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe
  • DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts
  • Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Broderie anglaise dresses
  • Contrast-tipped knits
  • Cricket cables with tipping
  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Knit fringe at hems
  • Pinstripe suiting

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses
  • Reworked racing sneakers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Fringed hems
  • Rugby stripes, short plackets
  • Striped Breton tops
  • Wide ruffle collars

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.
