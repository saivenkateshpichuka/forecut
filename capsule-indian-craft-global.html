Why? The Kolhapuri debate made Indian shoppers proud of the original crafts. GI-tagged and artisan-made products now carry real brand value.

What? Indian crafts are in the global spotlight. Prada showed Kolhapuri-inspired sandals in 7 of 56 Spring/Summer 2026 looks, Rabari embroidery reached the Indian runway, and kantha is moving onto sneakers.

Who's showing it?

  • Prada (Spring/Summer 2026): Kolhapuri-inspired sandals in 7 of 56 looks
  • Kartik Research: Rabari embroidery on handspun khadi
  • Gully Labs: Sneakers with kantha embroidery and Indian colour stories

How to build the capsule

  • Source one GI-tagged hero product and credit the craft cluster.
  • Add mirror or kantha accents to everyday basics.
  • Pair Kolhapuris and juttis with western looks in imagery.
