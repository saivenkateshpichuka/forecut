Design notes

How to put the leading shapes into a range.

Kidswear, Bottoms, rising

Extendable hems

Deep hems with a let-down line so trousers last longer. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on bottoms. Works in kidswear bottoms as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Bottoms, rising

Wide-leg linen trousers

Relaxed wide legs with an elastic back waist in linen-cotton. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Kidswear, Bottoms, peaking

Adjustable inside waist

Buttonhole elastic inside the waistband so trousers fit for longer. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Kidswear, Dresses, peaking

Dropped-waist pleated dress

The adult waist story for girls: a dropped seam with a knife-pleated skirt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • Sequins, beads and small hardware suit ages 5 and up only; sew them securely and test them.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
