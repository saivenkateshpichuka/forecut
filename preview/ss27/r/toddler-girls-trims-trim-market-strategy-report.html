SS27 / Trims / Toddler Girls

SS27 Toddler Girls' Trim Market Strategy

Safety-first trims are the commercial choice for toddler girls.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Girls
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • In Italy, childrenswear exports fell 9.3% and domestic sales 2.6% in 2025 (Confindustria Moda via Fortune Italia)[1]
  • Newer lines emphasise breathability, quick drying and comfort; childrenswear is moving toward functionality, material quality and durability[1]

Build, test, sell through

Action Trims
Build Snaps, printed labels, embroidery
Test Appliqué
Avoid Beads, sequins, shells

Design implications

  • Avoid small parts.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Safety builds trust.

Evidence and sources

  1. Pitti, la moda bambino punta su sostenibilita e protezione UV: cosi i brand preparano l'estate 2027, Fortune Italia, 24 June 2026
