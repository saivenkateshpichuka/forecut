The story

Whites, sand, tobacco and ice blue, as if bleached by summer. Linen, cotton and soft tailoring.

Colour comes back in full: purple and lilac, head-to-toe red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites, sand and tobacco.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

CoreOptic whitePantone 11-0602 TCX Snow White#F2F0EBCrisp white for shirting, linen and tailoring; the biggest riser on the runways.
CoreSandPantone 13-1009 TCX Biscotti#DAC7ABSun-faded neutral for linen suits, knits and outerwear.
CoreTobaccoPantone 18-1028 TCX Emperador#684832Warm brown that replaces black in summer tailoring and leather.
Ice bluePantone 12-4805 TCX Wan Blue#CBDCDF

How to use it

Use for: Tailoring, shirting, linen, menswear.

Proportions

Lead with optic white as the main colour, sand as the second, and keep ice blue to trims, linings or one piece.

Departments

Womenswear, Menswear, Kidswear carry colours from this story.
