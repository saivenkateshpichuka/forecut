Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts

Pleated-front tuxedo shirts cut in light, crisp parachute-cotton poplin; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027 evening, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Drawcord linen trousers

Linen trousers with drawcord waists. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Pre-wash linen to control shrinkage and soften the handle.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Linen or linen-cotton blends.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Grandad collar linen shirts

Band collars on washed linen. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Pre-wash linen to control shrinkage and soften the handle.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Linen or linen-cotton blends.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Henley plackets

Short button plackets on jersey tops. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Lightweight technical trench

Trenches in light technical cotton with taped seams. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Mesh and fabric ankle boots

Breathable mesh and fabric uppers. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Grandad collar linen shirts
  • Henley plackets
  • Lightweight technical trench
  • Mesh and fabric ankle boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Performance polo tops
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Seamless performance knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Broderie anglaise dresses
  • Gingham and seersucker skirts
  • Linen and seersucker shirts
  • Linen and seersucker suiting
  • Linen travel suits
  • Linen waistcoats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Unfinished raw hems

Actions

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
