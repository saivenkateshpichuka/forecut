SS27 Kids' Knit Fabric Direction

Organic cotton jersey and interlock, terry, micro-mesh, and UPF-rated performance and swim fabrics for SS27 kidswear.

The evidence

Terry and micro-mesh are among Fatamadrina's SS27 fabrics[1]; MilK chose a terry top[2]. Salt and Pepper describes its SS27 fabrics as sustainable organic cotton, "exceptionally soft and gentle on the skin"[3]. Cadhan uses soft organic cotton for pyjamas[4]. Teeter Totter uses performance fabrics with responsible materials and UPF50+ protection; Piccoli Principi uses technical fabrics including sustainable lycra[4]. Newer lines emphasise breathability and quick drying[5].

Fabric by fabric

Knit Hand Uses
Organic cotton jersey Soft Tees, sets
Interlock Smooth, stable Baby, pyjamas
Terry Soft, absorbent Tops, shorts, cover-ups
Micro-mesh Light, airy Sport, layers
UPF swim and performance knits Stretch, quick-dry Swim, rash guards

Design implications

  • Test UPF after stretch and wet, when protection can drop.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Organic cotton basics support repeat purchase; UPF supports full price.
