By market

Regional direction for the season, with the trims that fit each market.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Fits: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Open Johnny-collar knit polos

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Fits: Utility trench details, Raglan-sleeve overcoats, Mac coats

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Fits: Utility trench details, Lightweight harrington jackets, Chore and work jackets

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Fits: Denim trucker jackets, Waxed cotton jackets, Linen and seersucker shirts

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Fits: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Striped rugby shirts

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Fits: Utility trench details, Denim trucker jackets, Linen and seersucker shirts

Design notes

How to use the trims.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Contrast cuffs

White or contrast cuffs. A cuff detail is visible in every pose and costs little to add.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Interface cuffs and check opening size for hand pass-through.
  • Use the cuff to repeat a trim from the front.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Oxford button-downs

The classic button-down collar. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Popover shirts

Half-placket shirts pulled over the head. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Shawl-collar pullovers

Pullovers with shawl collars. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
