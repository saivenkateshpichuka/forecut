SS27 Kids' Season Colour Analysis

Bright, saturated summer colour returns to kidswear, led by red, aqua and lemon, balanced by soft pastels and calm neutrals.

The evidence

  • Red: "one of the defining colors of the season" at Playtime Paris, from bright statement shades to deeper tones[2].
  • Navy and nautical: sailor collars and navy blues throughout Playtime Paris[2].
  • Colour overall: Pitti Bimbo called its June edition "the most colorful summer edition yet"[3]; Pisamonas' SS27 shoes arrive "full of color"[4]; Poco Blush showed bold hues for girls[5].
  • Soft pastels: Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy[4].
  • Neutrals: Fatamadrina notes "neutrals enhanced with terracotta and sage green" alongside bright, saturated colours[6].

Colour groups

  • Core: navy, sand, white.
  • Seasonal brights: lemon, aqua, coral, grass green.
  • Seasonal soft: lavender, vintage pink, pastel green.
  • Accent: red.

By age

  • Baby and toddler: soft pastels and sand, with one bright accent.
  • Kids: brights with navy and white.
  • Older kids and teens: red, navy and stripes; lavender for girls who want softer colour.

Timing

Red is rising for SS27; brights return after several pastel-led seasons. Soft pastels stay core for baby.

Products and customers

  • Red: tees, sandals, accessories. Aqua and lemon: swim and play. Lavender: dresses and knits. Navy and sand: bottoms and basics.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip brights on both jersey and swim fabric; they shift between substrates.
  • Check colour fastness to chlorinated and salt water for swim.

Commercial implications

  • Navy and sand carry volume; brights and red carry newness.
