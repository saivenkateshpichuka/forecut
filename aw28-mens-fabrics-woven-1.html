Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, peaking

Chambray work shirts

Chambray shirts with two flap pockets. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, peaking

Check sports jackets

Checked jackets worn with odd trousers. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, peaking

Corduroy wide trousers

Wide trousers in corduroy. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Choose wale size for the garment: fine wale for shirts, wider wale for trousers and jackets.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton corduroy in 8–14 wale.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
