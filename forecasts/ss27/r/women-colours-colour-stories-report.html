SS27 Women's Colour Stories

Four colour stories for the SS27 womenswear range: Purple Reign for dresses and knits, Full Colour for statement tailoring, Sun-Faded for linen and shirting, and Living Green for swim and summer knits.

Purple Reign

Lilac 15-3720 TCX, violet 19-3642 TCX, butter 12-0711 TCX, optic white 11-0602 TCX.

The lead story for women. Lilac appeared at Chanel, royal purple at Miu Miu and violet across Milan[1]; on the red carpet, Demi Moore wore custom red and lilac Gucci at Cannes[2]. Use lilac for day dresses, knitwear and shirts, violet for satin and evening, and butter as a soft companion rather than a contrast.

Full Colour

True red 18-1763 TCX, Klein blue 18-3949 TCX, lemon 12-0642 TCX, midnight 19-3933 TCX.

Chanel, Balenciaga and Celine paired canary yellow, true red and cobalt in strong blocks[1], and Céline, Akris and Stella McCartney dressed head to toe in a single colour[1]. "Colour confidence" was also one of the trends of the Cannes red carpet, with Simone Ashley and Isabelle Huppert in red[2]. Use this story on tailoring, coats and statement dresses, one colour per look or two colours in large blocks.

Sun-Faded

Optic white 11-0602 TCX, sand 13-1009 TCX, tobacco 18-1028 TCX, ice blue 12-4805 TCX.

White is the rising neutral of SS27, up from 23.7% to 28.4% of looks, with ecru also gaining[3]. "'90s minimalism" in pristine white gowns was another Cannes trend[2]. This story carries linen tailoring, shirting and the sheer layers of the One-Layer Summer theme.

Living Green

Grass green 16-0237 TCX, teal 17-5117 TCX, sand 13-1009 TCX, butter 12-0711 TCX.

A smaller story for swim, summer knits and prints. It suits holiday and resort drops and markets with long, hot summers.

Where the stories come from

These four stories are Forecut's SS27 colour stories[4], applied here to womenswear products. The same stories are used across audiences with different products; this page sets out how they work for women.

Design implications

  • Give each story its own delivery or capsule, so colours do not fight on the shop floor.
  • Purple Reign and Sun-Faded mix well (lilac with white); Full Colour works best on its own.
  • Match trims to the story: tonal buttons and lace for Purple Reign, contrast piping for Full Colour, natural horn and shell for Sun-Faded.

Commercial implications

  • Lead the season with Purple Reign and Sun-Faded; they carry most of the volume.
  • Buy Full Colour as statements: more styles, fewer units each.
  • Use Living Green for the high-summer and holiday drop.
