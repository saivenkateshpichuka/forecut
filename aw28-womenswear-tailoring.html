The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Concealed-button blazers

Blazers with hidden buttons for a clean front. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Peak-lapel coats

Tailored coats with peak lapels. Lapel shape sets the attitude of a jacket before anything else is noticed.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Keep the roll line soft; pad-stitch or use a light canvas on premium styles.
  • Check gorge height against the season proportion.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Technical wool suiting

Wool suiting with stretch and water resistance. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Wide-leg suit trousers

Wide legs with a single pleat. Leg shape is the key fit signal in bottoms each season.

Design notes

  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Check rise and hip ease when the leg width changes.

Womenswear, Tailoring, peaking

Armour-structured vests

Structured tailored vests with pronounced shoulders and a protective, armour-like shape over fluid dresses; De Goeye 10th-anniversary show, Brazil (29 Oct 2026). A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Adjust the back with a strap and buckle for fit.

Womenswear, Tailoring, peaking

Elongated evening jumpsuits

Long, lean jumpsuits in black with glossy or metallic finishes and gold embroidery, as in Namrata Joshipura's OBSIDIAN, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Allow for less give in coated fabrics; adjust ease and avoid tight seams.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Peak-lapel coats
  • Technical wool suiting
  • Wide-leg suit trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Quilted suit jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Armour-structured vests
  • Elongated evening jumpsuits
  • Belted suit jackets
  • Check tailoring
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Collarless tailored jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Belted trench blazers
  • Cinched one-button jackets
  • Cropped blazers
  • Longline blazers
  • Pinstripe waistcoats
  • Single-button tuxedo jackets

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
