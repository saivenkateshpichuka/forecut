The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos

Black smoking suits embroidered all over in black, worn as the alternative to a gown; Marian Saud for Débora Plager at the Martín Fierro de Cable awards, Buenos Aires, 12 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Belgian loafers

Soft, slipper-like loafers. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.
  • Keep the last consistent across the style family.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Bishop-sleeve knits

Full sleeves gathered into long cuffs. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable fisherman jumpers

Heavy cables on chunky jumpers. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Keep the knit light enough to avoid sagging.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable-front cardigans

Cables running down cardigan fronts. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Check shackets

Heavy check shirt-jackets. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Belgian loafers
  • Bishop-sleeve knits
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Check shackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Check-printed quilted puffers
  • Check-trimmed loafers
  • Contrast-collar polos
  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Double-pocket camp shirts

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Overshirt-style blazers
  • Oxford button-downs
  • Penny loafers
  • Shawl-collar pullovers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cuban camp collars

Actions

  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
