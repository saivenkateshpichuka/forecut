SS27 / Couture and craft / Toddler Boys

SS27 Toddler Boys' Couture Craft and Techniques

Craft for toddler boys: smocking, hand embroidery and artist prints.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Halcyon Nights builds several graphic universes each season with patterns from a panel of artists; Bobo Choses publishes illustrated books alongside its collections[1]
  • Fatamadrina lists oversized florals, checks with fruit prints, paisley, vintage geometrics, nautical themes and gingham, and "Wildflowers Bloom" with delicate handcrafted embroidery[2]

Translating

  • Smocking.
  • Embroidery with soft backing.
  • Artist prints.

Design implications

  • Hand work on safe placements.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Heirloom gifts.

Evidence and sources

  1. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
  2. Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026. Design studio forecast; used for season-wide fabric, silhouette and print notes
