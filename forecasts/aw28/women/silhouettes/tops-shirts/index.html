Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Double-pocket camp shirts

Camp-collar shirts with two chest pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Funnel-neck base layers

Fine ribbed funnel necks for layering. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Henley plackets

Short button plackets on jersey tops. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Hidden-zip blouses

Blouses with a concealed front zip. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Performance polo tops

Polo collars on breathable mesh jersey. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, peaking

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts

Pleated-front tuxedo shirts cut in light, crisp parachute-cotton poplin; Ralph Lauren Pre-Fall 2027 evening, 6 Nov 2026. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Henley plackets
  • Hidden-zip blouses
  • Performance polo tops

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Thermal-knit base layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Clean minimal T-shirts
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Lace-cami layering tops
  • Mesh-panel tops

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Velvet cape tops
  • Cropped boxy shirts
  • Oversized boyfriend shirts
  • Raglan baseball tees
  • Smocked necklines and cuffs
  • Striped Breton tops

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
