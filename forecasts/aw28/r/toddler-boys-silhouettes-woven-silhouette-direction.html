How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 kidswear fairs take place in January 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fairs, kidswear brands' long-term practices and market drivers, and will be updated as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • AW27 fairs made wool lighter and softer, brought back double-faced woollens and hid performance inside classic suitings[1]
  • Kidswear brands build on organic cotton, merino, modal, permanent ranges and take-back schemes[2]

Shapes to lead with

  • Pramsuit.
  • Cord dungaree.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Develop blocks early; confirm proportions after the shows.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Keep new shapes to tested quantities.

Evidence and sources

  1. Première Vision Paris: what fabrics reveal about AW27/28 trends, FashionUnited, 4 September 2026
  2. CSR drives desirability in kidswear market: Playtime 2026 insights, FashionUnited, 28 January 2026
