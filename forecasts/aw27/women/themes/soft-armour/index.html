How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The story

Performance is now hidden inside the material: waterproof, breathable or windproof membranes and bi-stretch in citywear suitings [1]. Double-faced wool returns in more weights and wool becomes lighter and softer [1]. FashionUnited's "Urban Retreater" wants a coat that performs on the commute and looks like tailoring at dinner [2].

Colours

Greyed blue, mushroom and tinted black.

Silhouettes

  • Broad-shoulder double-faced coat.
  • Technical blazer and tapered trouser.
  • Light quilted liners.

Fabrics

Double-faced wool and cashmere, membrane-backed suiting, bi-stretch twill.

Trims and details

Concealed zips, magnetic closures, bonded seams.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Products and customers

  • Coats, technical suits, liners.

Design implications

  • Bonded seams to keep the tailored look.

Commercial implications

  • Coats at premium; technical suits as a commuter capsule.

Evidence and sources
