SS28 Men's Season Colour Analysis

SS28 menswear colour is projected to centre on inky blue and khaki green tailoring, deeper sky-blue shirting and sonar turquoise for summer pieces.

Season
Spring Summer 2028
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2028.

Research details

Research period SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Forecut's SS28 colour forecast: royal blue and a new turquoise beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge
  • Future Snoops' SS28 colour shift "After Dusk": "Deep aubergines, inky blues and murky greens are bringing a darker, more atmospheric mood to warm-weather dressing"
  • Its named colours include Aubergine Skin, Solar Midnight, Smoky Lavender and Swamp Green, with Cloud Bloom as a luminous counterpoint and Fruit Punch for "juicy intensity"; dark shades work best with contrast, not head to toe
  • Copenhagen SS27 data showed colour returning and neutrals fading; Jacquemus built its SS27 show around turquoise and orange

Colour groups

  • Core: inky blue, khaki green, bleached linen.
  • Seasonal: deep sky, sonar turquoise.
  • Accent: acid lemon on one piece.

By product

  • Inky blue: tailoring.
  • Deep sky: shirts.
  • Turquoise: shorts, knits.
  • Khaki green: utility.
Design

Design implications

  • Lab-dip key colours on each main fabric before committing.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Keep the core deep and the accents shallow until the fairs confirm them.
