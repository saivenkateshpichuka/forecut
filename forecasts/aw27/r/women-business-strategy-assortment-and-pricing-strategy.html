AW27 Women's Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Plan AW27 womenswear for low growth: depth in coats and suiting, shallow statement buys, full price on what is new.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Women
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Fashion is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027, with markdown risk from excess stock[1]
  • Resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion through 2027[1]
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026 large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel[1]

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Tailoring, knits in mushroom, tinted black, greyed blue
Seasonal (about a third) Violet and bordeaux coats, velvet, skirt suits
Statement (the rest) Lace, lurex, teddy coats

Pricing

  • Full price on double-faced and technical coats.
  • Value on merino basics.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Fewer styles, more combinations.

Commercial implications

  • Plan resale and rework routes in the EU.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
