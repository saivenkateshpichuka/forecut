The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Deep blue

Bright cobalt blue deepens for winter into cobalt and ink on knitwear, wool coats and leather.

Why now

Colours of the year usually carry through both seasons of their year in adapted shades.

Key pieces

Cobalt, Ink blue, Knitwear; funnel-neck knits.

Source: Based on Forecut's colour direction for the season. Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable fisherman jumpers

Heavy cables on chunky jumpers. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Keep the knit light enough to avoid sagging.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cardigans worn as shirts

Collared cardigans buttoned up as shirts. It changes the whole front view, which is what customers see first online.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Check balance at fit so the front hangs straight.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Elbow-seam jumpers

Reinforced elbow seams. Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.
  • Show the sleeve clearly in flat-lay and on-body imagery.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Fair Isle yokes

Patterned yokes on crewnecks. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Use the yoke to add shape and structure.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Lambswool or merino blends in 7–12 gauge jacquard.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Funnel-neck zip knits

Full zips on funnel-neck knits. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cardigans worn as shirts
  • Elbow-seam jumpers
  • Fair Isle yokes
  • Funnel-neck zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Knitted overshirts
  • Mock-neck fine knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Brushed alpaca
  • Colour-block knits
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Heavy shawl-collar cardigans
  • Jewel-tone cashmere
  • Long-sleeve knitted polos

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Lab-dip cobalt and ink shades across knit, woven and leather.
  • Use deep blue as a core neutral, not just an accent.
  • Pair with cognac and cream.
