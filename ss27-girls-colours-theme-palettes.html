How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

Read from the spring/summer 2027 shows (June menswear and Pitti Uomo, September womenswear) and the Première Vision February fabric fair. White rose the most on the runways; purple was the most reported colour in New York; red returned head to toe; menswear moved to butter yellow, lavender, pink and ice blue over earth tones.

Pantone TCX codes are the nearest published match; confirm with a physical Pantone book before dyeing.
