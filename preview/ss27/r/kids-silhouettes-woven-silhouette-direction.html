SS27 Kids' Woven Silhouette Direction

Relaxed, easy-moving woven shapes for SS27 kidswear: dungarees, pleated and reversible dresses, sailor-collar tops, lightweight jackets and denim.

Rising

The evidence

Fatamadrina lists relaxed shapes, dungarees, oversized T-shirts, pleated dresses and lightweight jackets as SS27 silhouettes[1]. At Playtime Paris, denim's "presence felt stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons", across everyday, workwear-inspired and coordinated styles; sailor collars ran through the show[2]. Poco Blush showed a loose, printed, reversible dress[3]. Future Snoops' Happenstance calls for "exaggerated silhouettes"[4].

Shapes to lead with

  • Dungarees and short overalls in denim and cotton twill.
  • Pleated and loose dresses, including reversible styles.
  • Sailor-collar tops and dresses.
  • Lightweight jackets: denim, chore and overshirt shapes.
  • Relaxed shorts with elastic backs and real pockets.

Shapes to watch

Exaggerated collars and balloon shapes from Happenstance; use on one hero piece per drop.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Dungarees, dresses, sailor tops, denim jackets and shorts.

Design implications

  • Easy dressing: wide neck openings, front fastenings for younger ages, adjustable straps on dungarees.
  • Allow room for movement and growth.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Denim and dungarees are strong core buys; reversible dresses offer two looks for one price.
