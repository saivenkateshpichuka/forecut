Colour Forecast · India 2026–28

India Colour Forecast 2026–28

Nine colours for Indian fashion from Festive 26 to Festive 28, in three families: festive jewels, calm handloom, and earth and indigo.

Forecut Editorial · 11 October 2026

Download PDF ↓

Key colours

  • Indigo (Pantone 19-3864 TCX · #2f3f7a): Blue, from deep indigo to blue-and-white, was the most-seen colour story at Lakmé Fashion Week 2026. Use it on denim, handloom kurtas and printed co-ords.
  • Emerald (Pantone 18-5845 TCX · #0f6b4f): Jewel tones still lead festive wear, but in richer, more refined shades: emerald, sapphire and deep wine, often with champagne or antique gold.

How to use the palette

  • Build festive capsules from one jewel tone plus champagne gold and ivory.
  • Use sage, ivory and dusty rose for handloom and day-function ranges.
  • Run indigo across every category, from denim to sarees and footwear, and warm it with rust.
  • Pantone TCX (cotton) codes are the nearest match to each screen colour. Confirm with a physical Pantone book and lab dips before bulk dyeing.
