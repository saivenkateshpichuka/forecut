SS27 Kids' Sailor Stripes

Nautical classics return with energy: sailor collars, navy and red, and stripes in every direction.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen
Trend status
Rising

The story

At Playtime Paris, "red emerged as one of the defining colors of the season", "stripes were another dominant trend" in horizontal, vertical and diagonal runs, and "sailor collars, navy blues, nautical details" appeared throughout, tied to summer travel and the seaside[1]. MilK's Dressing Idéal included a sailor top by Loir Paris[2]. At Pitti Bimbo, Cozmo showed striped T-shirts and coordinated sets[3] and Gray Label kept to stripes alone[4]. Fatamadrina lists nautical themes and a "Nostalgic Nautical" board[5].

Silhouettes

  • Sailor-collar tops and dresses; Breton tees.
  • Shorts and dungarees with button fronts.
  • Coordinated stripe sets.

Fabrics

Cotton jersey and interlock for stripes; poplin and twill for collared pieces; denim.

Trims and details

Sailor collars with contrast tape, anchor or metal-look buttons, ric-rac.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. A classic with renewed energy; red is the newness.

Products and customers

  • Breton tees, sailor tops, stripe sets, dungarees, red accessories and sandals.

Design implications

  • Vary stripe direction and width across the range.
  • Engineer stripes to match at seams.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Stripes are reorder lines; red and sailor collars are the seasonal buys.
