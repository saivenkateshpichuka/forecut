AW27 Women's Couture Craft and Techniques

Craft for AW27 women: rediscovered weaving, double-faced finishing, tapestries, jacquards and hand-patinated leather.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Separable double-faced woollens return in more varied weights; ultrafine yarns and tight knits make wool lighter and softer, with thermal comfort without synthetics[1]
  • Brushed woollens, fur effects, mosses and wool sponges add touch; tapestries, jacquards and nomadic influences form the pattern repertoire[1]
  • APLF's AW27 leather directions run from raw, vegetable-tanned and waxed pull-up leathers to ultra-supple nappas and cashmere-hand suedes, glazed and moiré luxury leathers, and rubbery, holographic and laser-engraved experimental leathers[2]
  • Première Vision's AW27 theme "Living Matter" puts materials "at the center", with materials "infused with memory"[3]

Translating to ready-to-wear

  • Double-faced coats with hand-finished edges.
  • Tapestry and jacquard jackets.
  • Hand-patinated leather bags.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Hand work on hero pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Tell the mill and atelier story.
