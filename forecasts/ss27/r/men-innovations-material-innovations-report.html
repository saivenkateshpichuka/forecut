SS27 Men's Material Innovations

Materials for SS27 menswear: crease-proof stretch wool, technical cottons and water-repellent linen, chrome-free leather, softshell and heat-regulating sportswear fabrics.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceReadinessWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Herno's travel collection uses crease-proof fabrics, stretch wool and technical materials.
  • Première Vision highlights technical cottons and water-repellent linens, and compact milanos and softshells.
  • Margaret Howell's Dr Martens 1461 uses chrome-free leather by C.F. Stead.
  • Rick Owens used Adidas's Climacool heat-regulation technology.
  • Balenciaga used lab-grown AMSilk silk in its July 2026 couture.

Readiness

Material Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Stretch and crease-proof wool Ready
Water-repellent linen, technical cotton Ready from specialist mills
Chrome-free leather Ready; limited supply
Heat-regulating sport fabrics Ready in sportswear
Lab-grown silk Early; showpieces only
Design

Design implications

  • Use technical wools in travel tailoring; water-repellent linen for unlined outerwear.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Verify every sustainability claim before using it.
