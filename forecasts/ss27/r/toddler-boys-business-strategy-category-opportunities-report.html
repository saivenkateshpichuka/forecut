SS27 / Business strategy / Toddler Boys

SS27 Toddler Boys' Category Opportunities

Growth for toddler boys: organic basics, sleepwear, first shoes, UV swim, gift sets.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Salt and Pepper describes its SS27 fabrics as sustainable organic cotton, "exceptionally soft and gentle on the skin"[1]
  • Cadhan showed soft organic cotton pyjamas, with a "Sogni d'oro" line for sensitive skin[2]
  • Pisamonas showed strap ballerinas, leather sandals, barefoot models and first-summer shoes for babies[2]
  • Swimwear, T-shirts and accessories with built-in UV filters were prominent and are becoming common, linked to family concern for health and to changing climate[3]

Opportunities

  1. Organic basics.
  2. Sleepwear.
  3. First-walker shoes.
  4. UV swim and hats.
  5. Gift sets.

Design implications

  • Comfort first.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Multipacks and gift sets.

Evidence and sources

  1. Salt and Pepper: Spring/Summer 2027 brand profile, Pitti Immagine Bimbo, June 2026
  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  3. Pitti, la moda bambino punta su sostenibilita e protezione UV: cosi i brand preparano l'estate 2027, Fortune Italia, 24 June 2026
