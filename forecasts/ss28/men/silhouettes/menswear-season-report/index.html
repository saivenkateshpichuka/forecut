Overview

Long-range outlook. Structured clarity and easy movement: fewer, better pieces with performance comfort for commuting, in strong blues, greyish mauve and straw.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Skirts for men, Band-collar shirts, Barrel trousers and Belted field coats are rising and keep growing into AW28.

Test in small quantities

Articulated-knee trousers, Seamless technical knits and Technical wool travel suits are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons, Fisherman-style raincoats and Generous 1980s double-breasted suits are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Belted high waists, Buckle side adjusters and Clean leather bombers are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable

18-1340 TCX and 12-1110 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims

19-4916 TCX and 12-4604 TCX

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres

18-0920 TCX and 11-0105 TCX

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Skirts for men
  • Band-collar shirts
  • Barrel trousers
  • Belted field coats
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Cardigans worn as shirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Seamless technical knits
  • Technical wool travel suits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Boxy short-sleeve shirts
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Chore and work jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Belted high waists
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Denim trucker jackets
  • Double-breasted two-piece

Actions for the range

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.

Markets

How each region reads SS28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Open Johnny-collar knit polos, Overshirt chest flap pockets

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Utility trench details, Raglan-sleeve overcoats, Mac coats, Belted field coats

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Utility trench details, Lightweight harrington jackets, Chore and work jackets, Packable technical shells

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Denim trucker jackets, Waxed cotton jackets, Linen and seersucker shirts, Printed resort shirts

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Overshirt jackets, Leather overshirts, Overshirt chest flap pockets, Linen and seersucker shirts

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility trench details, Denim trucker jackets, Linen and seersucker shirts, Utility overshirts with snaps
