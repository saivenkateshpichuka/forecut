Overview

Early outlook. SS27 signals carried into winter: waisted outerwear, handcraft on knits and quiet structure, with deep violet, plum and tannic bordeaux taking the place of black.

The headline

A mix of fresh ideas and pieces carried on from last season.

Build into the range

Fringed macramé tops, Laser-cut coral-relief dresses, Raffia wedge sandals and Sequin-flecked Fair Isle jumpers are rising and keep growing into SS28.

Test in small quantities

Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts, Taffeta jogger evening trousers, Lightweight technical trench and Raw selvedge denim are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Piped satin track jackets, Suede equestrian jackets and Brushed alpaca are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Clear PVC heels, Mesh midi skirts and Plastic clogs and jellies are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet

Soft boots

Flat riding boots, slim soles and soft leather replace chunky lugs.

Why now

Footwear follows the move to elegance; slimmer soles suit refined trousers and skirts.

Key pieces

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Sequin-flecked Fair Isle jumpers
  • Sheer-panel check shirts
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Lightweight technical trench
  • Raw selvedge denim
  • Stretch commuter suiting
  • Stretch performance denim

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Brushed alpaca
  • Check-printed quilted puffers
  • Double-face wool overcoats
  • Double-layer sheer skirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Clear PVC heels
  • Mesh midi skirts
  • Plastic clogs and jellies
  • Sheer fine-gauge layers
  • Sheer mesh layering tops

Actions for the range

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Reduce lug-sole depth in new styles.
  • Add a flat knee-high riding boot.
  • Offer wide-calf fits.

Markets

How each region reads AW27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Waisted outerwear for East Asia; lightweight layering pieces for tropical markets
  • Craft on knits and festive wear for India and the Gulf winter season
  • Structured tailoring in soft wools for Japan, Korea and northern China

Best fit: Sheer organza overlays, Column knit dresses, Sheer mesh layering tops, Slogan T-shirts

Europe

  • Waisted and belted outerwear, soft-focus brushed surfaces
  • Irregular, "living" textures from Première Vision AW27-28
  • Quiet tailoring in technical wools

Best fit: Check-printed quilted puffers, Patent trench with storm flaps, Lightweight technical trench, Brushed wool-cashmere coats

North America

  • Technical outerwear and waisted coats
  • Elevated denim and leather
  • Sneakers step back for loafers and refined shoes

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Quilted liner jackets, Suede equestrian jackets, Slogan T-shirts

South America

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028, in lighter weights for the tropical north
  • Leather and knitwear for Argentina and southern Brazil

Best fit: Check-printed quilted puffers, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis, Seersucker sundresses

Africa

  • West Africa: occasion and festive (Detty December) rather than cold-weather wear
  • South Africa: AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • South Africa AW27 runway (SAFW, Oct 2026): kaftans, capes and blanket wraps, A-line shapes, handcrafted and unisex denim, ochre, azure and orange-blue palettes

Best fit: Check-printed quilted puffers, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis, Slogan T-shirts

Oceania

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • Merino knitwear and practical outerwear

Best fit: Check-printed quilted puffers, Sheer organza overlays, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis
