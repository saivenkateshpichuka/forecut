Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, fading

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons

Nylon twill blousons in black, navy or khaki where thick contrast topstitching is the design accent; sacai Holiday 2026, Tokyo, from 6 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, fading

Deep turn-ups

Deep cuffed hems. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, fading

Fisherman-style raincoats

Long, roomy raincoats modelled on fishermen's oilskins with storm flaps and hoods; part of Louis Vuitton's weather-themed Men's SS27 pre-collection (Oct 2026). A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, fading

Full straight legs

Wide, straight trouser legs. Leg shape is the key fit signal in bottoms each season.

Design notes

  • Check rise and hip ease when the leg width changes.
  • Show the leg shape full-length in product imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
