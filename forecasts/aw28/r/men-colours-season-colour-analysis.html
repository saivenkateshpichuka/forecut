AW28 Men's Season Colour Analysis

AW28 menswear colour is projected around ink, slate and bottle green tailoring, cocoa knits and petrol teal outerwear, with deep brown and cobalt from the catwalk forecasts.

Research details

Research period AW28/29 colour previews (October 2026), AW27 fairs (September 2026), SS28 previews

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Forecut's AW28 projection: ink, mulberry and bottle green over oat and slate, with petrol teal and vermilion as accents
  • Future Snoops' AW28/29 "Blocked brights": "Bold colour becomes an act of defiance, a refusal to disappear into the algorithmic mass"; blue leads, with red and purple rising, and cornflower as a new "anchor colour"
  • Pink becomes a neutral base ("Pink standard"), from soft blush to greyish dusty rose, no longer limited to womenswear; rosehip is a standout pink; suggested for blazers, skirt suits and voile dresses with ruffles or lace
  • Vibrant cobalt with a violet undertone, paired with white; deep wine red with cream and black; bright matcha green with chartreuse and ecru, in menswear and womenswear
  • Deep classic brown in leather and leather-look pieces, sporty pieces and tailoring, suited to suede and canvas-like materials

Colour groups

  • Core: ink, slate, cocoa, oat.
  • Seasonal: bottle green, deep brown.
  • Accent: petrol teal, cobalt, matcha.

By product

  • Ink and slate: tailoring.
  • Cocoa: knits.
  • Bottle green: coats.
  • Petrol teal: outerwear.
