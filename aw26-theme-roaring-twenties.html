The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Why now

Party-season demand meets nostalgia for glamour. Drop waists are easier to wear than bodycon, which helps them sell beyond evening.

Seen at

  • Chanel: Fringed silks and beaded sequins
  • Fendi: Drop-waist dresses and 1920s evening codes
  • Rabanne: Chainmail and sequin minis

Key pieces

Drop waist, Fringe, T-bar heels; drop waist with pleated skirt.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Ombré sequin gowns

Long gowns with sequins graded from dense to sparse or light to dark; Elie Saab's dégradé sequins reached the high street in the H&M collaboration launched 22 Oct 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Velvet cape tops

Evening tops with a short cape layer in place of sleeves, in burgundy velvet; Mint Velvet holiday 2026, with Sézane x Lucia Zolea's Angeline cape in the same round-up (18 Nov 2026). Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Make the cape detachable or attached at the yoke so the base garment sells on its own.
  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Beads and sequins on knits

Embellishment moving onto everyday jumpers. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bias-cut velvet

Fluid bias-cut velvet in jewel tones. A cut change that updates the fit without changing the fabric story.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Fit-test on multiple sizes before bulk.
  • Keep the cut consistent across colourways.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Embellished flats

Beaded and studded flats. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Sequin and chainmail minis

Allover sequins or metal mesh for evening. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Velvet cape tops
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Bias-cut velvet
  • Embellished flats
  • Sequin and chainmail minis

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Cropped parkas over evening gowns
  • Feathers on satin
  • Knit fringe at hems
  • Sequinned and beaded jeans
  • Snap-front western shirts
  • Velvet dinner jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Sequin-flecked Fair Isle jumpers
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Drop waist with pleated skirt
  • Fringed hems

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Lead evening with one drop-waist dress in two lengths.
  • Add fringe to hems and sleeves rather than the whole garment.
  • Pair with T-bar heels and long pearls in visual merchandising.
