Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Button-front midi skirts
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Fine-gauge zip polos
  • Half-zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Hidden-zip blouses
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Cargo pockets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Double-breasted jackets
  • Western yokes and pearl snaps
  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Denim with chain and patch trims
