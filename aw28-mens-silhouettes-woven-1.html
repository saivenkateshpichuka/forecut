Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Flat-front slim trousers

Clean flat fronts on slim legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Single-pleat suit trousers

One forward pleat on suit trousers. Pleats add volume and movement while keeping a tailored finish.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Press and tack pleats so they hold through wear.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Skirts for men

Skirts worn as menswear on the runway; Cynthia Abila sent male models out in skirts at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct). A focused detail update for trousers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Technical parkas

Gorpcore parkas with taped seams. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
