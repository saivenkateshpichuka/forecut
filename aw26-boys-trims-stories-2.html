Design notes

How to use the trims.

Kidswear, Occasion, rising

Mandarin-collar kurta set

Short kurta with mandarin collar and pyjama for festive and wedding seasons. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on occasion. Works in kidswear occasion as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Outerwear, emerging

Wooden toggles

Toggles on duffle coats, fixed with loops rather than cords. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use horn-effect or wooden toggles with cord or leather loops; check that the loops hold.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on outerwear. Works in kidswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Outerwear, emerging

Two-way zips

Two-way zips on coats so children can sit and climb comfortably. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on outerwear. Works in kidswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Knitwear, emerging

Shawl-collar cardigan

Chunky shawl collar on a button cardigan; the grandad cardigan for small boys. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on knitwear. Works in kidswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • Sequins, beads and small hardware suit ages 5 and up only; sew them securely and test them.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
