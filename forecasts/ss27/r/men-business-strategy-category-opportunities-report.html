SS27 Men's Category Opportunities

Growth categories for SS27 menswear: summer tailoring, shorts, cropped jackets, soft leather, knit polos and shirts, and two-piece sets.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceOpportunitiesCategories to reduceWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Tailoring is adapting, not disappearing: designers broadly agreed that a well-dressed man still wears a suit, now lighter.
  • Shorts: microshorts in Milan, smart shorts in Paris, boxer shorts as outerwear.
  • Cropped jackets: the micro jacket.
  • Leather: across Louis Vuitton, Dior, Saint Laurent and Givenchy.
  • Sets: pyjama-inspired sets at Dior and Givenchy.
  • Accessories: Louis Vuitton offered Speedy bags in many colours; jewellery and leather goods are projected to grow 4 to 6% a year from 2025 to 2027.

Opportunities

  1. Summer suits and suit shorts.
  2. Cropped blousons, especially leather and suede.
  3. Knit polos and knit shirts.
  4. Two-piece sets.
  5. Bags and accessories in season colours.

Categories to reduce

Very oversized tailoring and heavy four-season suits.

Design

Design implications

  • Shift development time to summer-weight tailoring and sets.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Re-weight open-to-buy towards shorts, sets and cropped jackets.
