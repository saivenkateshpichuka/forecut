SS27 Men's Woven Fabric Direction

Sheer shirting, satin, seersucker, soft leather and light tailoring wools lead SS27 menswear fabrics, with crease-proof stretch for travel.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Fabrics
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear
Trend status
Rising

The evidence

  • Sheer shirting: translucent fabrics from subtle to full transparency, at Kiko Kostadinov, Feng Chen Wang, Dior, Prada, Dries Van Noten, Jacquemus and Saint Laurent[1].
  • Satin: synthetic and silk satin, floral and white, with a billowing drape, at Willy Chavarria, Celine, GmbH and AMI[1].
  • Silk and gauze tailoring: fluid, almost sheer suits at Dior[2].
  • Seersucker: layered seersucker suiting at Thom Browne[3].
  • Leather: camel at Dior, beige suits at Saint Laurent, brown leather Bermudas at Sonia Carrasco and leather tracksuits at Givenchy; leather is becoming "more malleable", with "flexibility, fluidity, lightness, almost like butter", according to Alice Feillard of Galeries Lafayette[2]. Milan used woven and perforated leather for breathability[3].
  • Tailoring cloth: fine, lightweight wools blended with viscose, silk or linen; heavier double crepes with a dry, cool hand[4].
  • Travel fabrics: crease-proof stretch wool and technical materials at Herno[5].

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. Soft leather and seersucker continue into SS28 (Forecut assessment).

Products and customers

  • Sheer shirts, satin shirts and sets, seersucker suits, leather blousons and shorts, linen and travel suits.

Design implications

  • Sheer shirts need French seams and clean facings.
  • Test leather for colour fastness and perforation strength.
  • Choose crease-recovery finishes for travel suits.

Commercial implications

  • Leather at premium; seersucker and linen at mid; satin across tiers depending on fibre.
  • State fibre content clearly on satins.
