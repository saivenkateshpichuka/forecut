AW27 / Silhouettes / Men

AW27 Men's Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Men's AW27 knits: turtlenecks, fine merino layers, brushed and hairy collars, marl and equestrian stripes, pink sweatshirts.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Silhouettes
Published

Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Menswear forecaster Edwin van den Hoek: jackets, trousers and down grow bigger without weight ("It looks very big, but it is very light to wear"); layering at the neck; fur or hairy collars; leather as a refined detail[1]
  • Woolmark presented merino wool as "the technical fibre" for AW27 sourcing at Pitti Filati[2]
  • Pitti Filati's AW27 research theme "Discipline" draws on equestrian codes: dressage (braided manes, saddle pads), Western reining (Quarter Horse spots, quilt geometry, denim, suede, flannel) and show jumping (stripes, colour blocking, technical knits)[3]
  • Pink and mauve are becoming gender-neutral, reaching menswear and even junior sweatshirts[4]

Shapes

  • Fine merino turtleneck.
  • Brushed crew.
  • Knit waistcoat.
  • Striped rugby knit.
  • Sweatshirt.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Neck heights for layering.

Commercial implications

  • Merino turtlenecks are reorder lines.
