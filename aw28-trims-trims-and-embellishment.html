The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Key pieces

Stretch wool, Water-resistant, Travel suits; technical wool suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Hidden closures

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Why now

Supports the minimal, structured look and is easier to fasten.

Key pieces

Magnets, Concealed plackets, Hidden zips; hidden magnetic closures.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Bow-trim flats

Flats finished with a small bow. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Cut ties on the bias for a soft drape.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Fluid georgette, crepe de chine or satin.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Denim with technical details

Denim jackets with zips and bellows pockets. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Slim tassel loafers

Tassel loafers on slim soles. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, peaking

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, peaking

Braided officer coats

Officer-style coats and jackets with exaggerated proportions trimmed in braids and cords, reworked from French military dress uniforms; Songzio x Egonlab 'The Art of Dark Poetry', Seoul (Korea Herald, 15 Nov 2026). Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Short, boxy down puffers covered in floral embroidery; The North Face x Cecilie Bahnsen 'Lucia Nuptse Short' in black and navy, launched in Copenhagen 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bow-trim flats
  • Denim with technical details
  • Slim tassel loafers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Braided officer coats
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Embroidered pocket shirts
  • Lace-cami layering tops

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brogue perforations
  • Check-trimmed loafers

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
