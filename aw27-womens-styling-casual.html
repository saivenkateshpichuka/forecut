Looks

Weekend

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In deep violet with silicone pink.

Raglan baseball tees with button-fly denim shorts, boxy cropped jackets and mesh running sneakers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.

City errands

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In tannic bordeaux with waxy ochre.

Bishop-sleeve knits with denim shirt-jackets, cinched peplum jackets and running-inspired sneakers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.

Travel day

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In dusty mauve with mushroom.

Bubble-hem dresses with matching denim suits, drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets and sock-knit trainers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.

Café afternoon

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In silicone pink with deep violet.

Cropped boxy shirts with bubble hems, bermuda suit shorts and sun-bleached distressed sneakers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.
