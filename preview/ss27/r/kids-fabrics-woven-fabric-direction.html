SS27 Kids' Woven Fabric Direction

Lightweight linen, voile, organic cotton poplin, gingham and diverse denim lead SS27 kidswear wovens, with recycled and certified materials.

The evidence

Collections increasingly use recycled fibres, organic cotton and certified materials[1]. Fatamadrina lists lightweight linen, voile and organic cotton, with gingham among SS27 prints[2]. Denim was "stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons" at Playtime Paris[3]. Marie Raxevisky uses fine Italian fabrics and recycled materials[4].

Design implications

  • Pre-wash linen and denim to control shrinkage.
  • Certify organic claims (for example GOTS) before using them.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Organic cotton and linen support a premium; denim is volume.
