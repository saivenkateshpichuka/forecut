Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, with radiant earth deepening into rust and brick.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Contrast-collar polos, Elbow-seam jumpers, Flat-front slim trousers and Funnel-neck puffers are rising.

Test in small quantities

Hybrid quilted bombers, Insulated overshirts, Quilted dinner jackets and Technical merino layers are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Band-collar shirts, Barrel trousers and Belted field coats are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Brushed alpaca, Cable fisherman jumpers and Chore and work jackets are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Key pieces

Hidden closures

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Why now

Supports the minimal, structured look and is easier to fasten.

Key pieces

Magnets, Concealed plackets, Hidden zips

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Contrast-collar polos
  • Elbow-seam jumpers
  • Flat-front slim trousers
  • Funnel-neck puffers
  • Satin side-stripe trousers
  • Single-pleat suit trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated overshirts
  • Quilted dinner jackets
  • Technical merino layers
  • Technical wool cargo trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Band-collar shirts
  • Barrel trousers
  • Belted field coats
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Cardigans worn as shirts
  • Chambray work shirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brushed alpaca
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Chore and work jackets
  • Colour-block knits
  • Corduroy suits
  • Deep turn-ups

Actions for the range

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Overshirt jackets, Velvet shirts, Overshirt chest flap pockets, Tucked shirts with slim ties

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Double-face wool overcoats, Raglan-sleeve overcoats, Mac coats, Belted field coats

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Technical parkas, Chore and work jackets, Packable technical shells, Taped-seam commuter jackets

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Waxed cotton jackets, Printed resort shirts, Grandad collar linen shirts, Seersucker suits

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Overshirt jackets, Velvet shirts, Overshirt chest flap pockets, Tucked shirts with slim ties

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility overshirts with snaps, Printed resort shirts, Grandad collar linen shirts, Sheer-panel check shirts

Festive and summer seasons, handloom, craft clusters and the colours that sell in India, read against AW28.
