The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Natural tech

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Belted knee-high boots

Knee-highs with a single buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Cutout summer boots

Ankle boots with cutout panels. Shaft height and fit decide how the boot works with trousers and skirts.

Design notes

  • Bind cut-out edges cleanly and check placement on several body types.
  • Check calf width across sizes; offer wide-calf fits.
  • Use zips or elastic for easy on and off.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Flat suede knee boots

Flat knee boots in suede. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Hybrid sport-smart boots

Smart boots on cushioned sport soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Kitten-heel knee boots

Knee boots on low kitten heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Lug-sole combat boots

Lace-up boots on deep lugs. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Keep the sole light with an EVA or lightweight rubber unit.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Cutout summer boots
  • Flat suede knee boots
  • Hybrid sport-smart boots
  • Kitten-heel knee boots
  • Lug-sole combat boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Knee-high block-heel boots
  • Lace-up ankle boots
  • Low ankle sock boots
  • Low-heel slouch ankle boots

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Kitten-heel boots
  • Patent knee boots
  • Slouchy mid-calf boots
  • Square-toe ankle boots
  • Western stitching

Actions

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
