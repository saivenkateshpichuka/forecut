AW27 Men's Design and Production Innovations

Design and production for AW27 menswear: design for repair, light volume and product data for the EU passport.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Footwear forecasters (AEC and Trendstop) describe "style without sacrifice", durability and repair with replaceable parts, sensory quilted surfaces and 70s and 90s micro-heels
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026 large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel
  • EU Directive 2024/825 bans greenwashing and misleading environmental claims; Italy's transposing decree entered into force on 27 September 2026

Actions

  • Replaceable parts.
  • Record fibre and origin.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Repair.

Commercial implications

  • Plan around Lunar New Year 2028.

Evidence and sources

  • Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  • Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
  • Events at Lineapelle September 2026 (EU Directive 2024/825 on greenwashing), Lineapelle, 6 August 2026
