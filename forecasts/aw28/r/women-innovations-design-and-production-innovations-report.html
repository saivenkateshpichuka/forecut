AW28 Women's Design and Production Innovations

Design and production for AW28: the first winter planned under the EU passport timeline; repair and data by design.

Audience

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027, so AW28 production will be planned under it[1]

Actions

  • Product data from the first sample.
  • Repairable construction.
  • Fibre and origin records.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Record fibre, origin, care and test results for every style.

Commercial implications

  • Plan production around Lunar New Year and EU product-data rules.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
