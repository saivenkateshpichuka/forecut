The story

Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy, with UV protection ; UV kidswear is becoming common . Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to toddler girls, roughly 1 to 4 years.

Colours

Vintage pink, pastel green, soft navy.

Silhouettes

  • One-piece swimsuits with legs.
  • Sun suits.
  • Cover-up dresses.

Fabrics

  • UPF swim, organic cotton.

Trims and details

  • Ruffles secured, snap crotches.
Make

Products and customers

  • Rompers, sets, sun hats, sandals.
Design

Design implications

  • Easy dressing: snaps at the crotch, wide neck openings.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Gift buyers matter; package sets.
