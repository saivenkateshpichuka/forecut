Soft boots

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers

Very full trousers with a low crotch gathered into a narrow ankle, shown at ALUF, Lilly Sarti and Uó at São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Woven-stripe longline overlayers

Ankle-length open overlayers in hand-woven stripes running in several directions; DARRA reworked Javanese lurik, ikat and songket this way at Jakarta Fashion Week 2027 (27 Oct 2026). A focused detail update for coats and jackets.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.

Fabric and trims

Engineered mesh or knit uppers.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Buckled ankle boots

Ankle boots with a buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Chelsea ankle boots

Elastic-sided ankle boots. Shaft height and fit decide how the boot works with trousers and skirts.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Use strong elastic gussets and a pull tab for easy wear.
  • Check calf width across sizes; offer wide-calf fits.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Chelsea boots for tailoring

Sleek Chelseas worn with suits. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Cropped ankle trousers

Slim trousers cut above the ankle. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.
  • Check length on petite and tall fits.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Dropped-crotch Aladdin trousers
  • Woven-stripe longline overlayers
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Chelsea boots for tailoring
  • Cropped ankle trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Hybrid sport-smart boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Balloon trousers
  • Crepe-sole desert boots
  • Hiker styles
  • Hiking boots with tailoring

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Reduce lug-sole depth in new styles.
  • Add a flat knee-high riding boot.
  • Offer wide-calf fits.
