The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Deep blue

Bright cobalt blue deepens for winter into cobalt and ink on knitwear, wool coats and leather.

Why now

Colours of the year usually carry through both seasons of their year in adapted shades.

Key pieces

Cobalt, Ink blue, Knitwear; funnel-neck knits.

Source: Based on Forecut's colour direction for the season. Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Braided officer coats

Officer-style coats and jackets with exaggerated proportions trimmed in braids and cords, reworked from French military dress uniforms; Songzio x Egonlab 'The Art of Dark Poetry', Seoul (Korea Herald, 15 Nov 2026). Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons

Nylon twill blousons in black, navy or khaki where thick contrast topstitching is the design accent; sacai Holiday 2026, Tokyo, from 6 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Convertible backpack puffers

Oversized reversible puffers with underarm slits that fold and strap into a backpack; Melitta Baumeister x Nike, New York, 5 Nov 2026. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Leather-yoke nylon puffers

Nylon puffers with leather shoulder yokes; Louis Vuitton's Men's SS27 pre-collection 'Whatever the Weather' (launched 29 Oct 2026) puts monogram leather on the shoulders. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.

Fabric and trims

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Chore and work jackets

Workwear jackets with large patch pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Round or square patch pockets should be topstitched evenly and reinforced at the corners.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Braided officer coats
  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Leather-yoke nylon puffers
  • Belted field coats
  • Chore and work jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Taped-seam commuter jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Double-face wool overcoats
  • Fur and shearling collars
  • Leather and wool varsity

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Technical parkas

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
