Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fair Isle yoke cardigans

Patterned yokes on button cardigans. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Openwork and pointelle knits

Open stitches and pointelle for light knits. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Check that open stitches do not snag; use a firm yarn.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton or viscose blends in fine gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Pointelle cardigans

Openwork pointelle on fine cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Check that open stitches do not snag; use a firm yarn.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Cotton or viscose blends in fine gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

Sheer fine-gauge layers

Transparent fine knits under tailoring. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Organza, chiffon, tulle or recycled mesh.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
