AW27 Men's Elegant Layers

Layering at the neck and refined detail: turtleneck, shirt, waistcoat and jacket, fur or hairy collars, leather as a detail.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading

Autumn Winter 2027

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The story

Van den Hoek sees layering at the neck (turtleneck, shirt, waistcoat, jacket), fur or hairy collars and leather moving into details: a collar, a shirt, a patch pocket . Agelink describes "layering by construction", such as Prada's shirt under jacket .

Colours

Mushroom, copper khaki, tannic bordeaux.

Silhouettes

  • Waistcoat with jacket.
  • Turtleneck under a shirt.
  • Leather-collared coat.

Fabrics

Flannel, cashmere, brushed wool, nappa.

Trims and details

Leather collars and patch pockets, horn buttons.
