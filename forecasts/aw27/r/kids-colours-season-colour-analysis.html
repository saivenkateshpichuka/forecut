AW27 Kids' Season Colour Analysis

AW27 kidswear colour: plum and dusty mauve with waxy ochre and sage, grounded in greyed blue and oat.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Colour
Published
2026-11-25
Research period

Emerging

How this forecast was built

Pre-fair forecast. The AW27 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime Paris) take place in January 2027. This report applies the 2026 adult yarn, fabric and leather fair evidence and the AW26 kidswear fair record to kidswear, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • At Première Vision, deep violet, plums, dusty pinks, mauves and tinted off-blacks set the tone; violet is "centre stage", and plum and deep red become alternatives to black[1]
  • Pink and mauve are becoming gender-neutral, reaching menswear and even junior sweatshirts[1]
  • Forecut's AW27 colour forecast: violet, plum and tannic bordeaux replace black over tinted neutrals[2]

Colour groups

  • Core: greyed blue, oat.
  • Seasonal: plum, dusty mauve.
  • Accent: waxy ochre, sage.

Why

Adult fairs put violet, plum and dusty pinks at the centre and note pink and mauve reaching junior sweatshirts; Forecut's kidswear palette applies that with brighter ochre and sage for play.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Test plum and mauve on cotton jersey and fleece; check colour fastness to washing.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Greyed blue and oat carry volume; plum and mauve are the newness.
