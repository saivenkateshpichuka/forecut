The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Waisted outerwear

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Beaded-fringe canvas dresses

Structured canvas dresses finished with long beaded fringe; Hertunba opened Lagos Fashion Week 2026 Day 2 (29 Oct) with a black canvas dress and red beaded fringe. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Braided officer coats

Officer-style coats and jackets with exaggerated proportions trimmed in braids and cords, reworked from French military dress uniforms; Songzio x Egonlab 'The Art of Dark Poetry', Seoul (Korea Herald, 15 Nov 2026). Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Floral-embroidered cropped puffers

Short, boxy down puffers covered in floral embroidery; The North Face x Cecilie Bahnsen 'Lucia Nuptse Short' in black and navy, launched in Copenhagen 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Shell-paillette jacket edging

Tweed and boxy jackets finished with shell-shaped paillettes along the fronts and hems; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 re-staged at Barangaroo, Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Starry crystal velvet gowns

Black velvet bustier gowns scattered with crystals laid out like a night sky; Louis Vuitton on HoYeon Jung at the Governors Awards, Los Angeles, 15 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Braided officer coats
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Smocked knit details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brogue perforations
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Cricket cables with tipping

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Feathers on satin

Actions

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
