SS27 Kids' Trim Stories

Trims that carry each SS27 kids' theme: flat seams for Pool Days, minimal details for Lull, characters for Happenstance and tape for Sailor Stripes.

Pool Days

Flat seams, covered elastics, zip garages, printed labels.

Lull

Tonal stitching, stripe trims, natural buttons; subtle details and clever contrasts[1].

Happenstance

Character embroidery, appliqué, novelty buttons and playful embellishment[1].

Sailor Stripes

Sailor collars, contrast tape, anchor-look buttons[2].

Design implications

  • One trim family per theme.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Trims refresh carry-over blocks.
