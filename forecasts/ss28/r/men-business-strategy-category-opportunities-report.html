SS28 / Business strategy / Men

SS28 Men's Category Opportunities

Projected growth for SS28 menswear: technical tailoring, linen suits, knit polos, evening shirts.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Men
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Performance, everyday and occasion wear merge: "technical blazers, adaptable skirts, and tailored baselayers", in sun-warmed neutrals and soft gradients[1]
  • Future Snoops' SS28 colour shift "After Dusk": "Deep aubergines, inky blues and murky greens are bringing a darker, more atmospheric mood to warm-weather dressing"[2]
  • SS27 was shaped by heat: Hypebeast called the sun "the true main character of SS27"[3]
  • C2 Fashion Studio calls SS28 "Structured Clarity": consumers "are no longer asking for more. They're asking for better"[4]

Opportunities

  1. Technical tailoring.
  2. Linen suits.
  3. Knit and crochet polos.
  4. Satin evening shirts.
  5. Tailored shorts.

Reduce

Styles that did not sell through in the previous comparable season.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Shift development time to the opportunities.

Commercial implications

  • Re-weight open-to-buy accordingly.

Evidence and sources

  1. SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
  2. After Dusk: why Spring/Summer 2028 is going dark, FashionUnited (Joanne Thomas, Director of Color, Future Snoops), 30 October 2026
  3. The SS27 trends: what comes after fashion's hottest season ever?, Hypebeast, July 2026
  4. Spring Summer 2028 trend intelligence: the rise of structured clarity, FashionUnited (partner content, C2 Fashion Studio), June 2026
