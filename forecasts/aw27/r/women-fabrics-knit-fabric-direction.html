AW27 Women's Knit Fabric Direction

AW27 women's knits: extra-fine merino, tight knitted wools close to silk, brushed and fur-effect yarns, and equestrian-inspired stripes.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Separable double-faced woollens return in more varied weights; ultrafine yarns and tight knits make wool lighter and softer, with thermal comfort without synthetics[1]
  • Woolmark presented merino wool as "the technical fibre" for AW27 sourcing at Pitti Filati[2]
  • Brushed woollens, fur effects, mosses and wool sponges add touch; tapestries, jacquards and nomadic influences form the pattern repertoire[1]
  • Pitti Filati's AW27 research theme "Discipline" draws on equestrian codes: dressage (braided manes, saddle pads), Western reining (Quarter Horse spots, quilt geometry, denim, suede, flannel) and show jumping (stripes, colour blocking, technical knits)[3]
