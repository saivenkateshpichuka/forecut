Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Barrel trousers

Curved, tapered barrel legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Boxy short-sleeve shirts

Wide, boxy short-sleeve shirts. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Check shackets

Heavy check shirt-jackets. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
