The story

SS28 Women's Themes.

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Stretch commuter suiting
  • Articulated sleeves
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Soft unstructured tailoring

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Hidden-zip blouses
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Soft drape jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Soft suede moccasins

Actions

  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.
