Overview

Long-range outlook. Structured clarity and easy movement: fewer, better pieces with performance comfort for commuting, anchored by a warm earth orange.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Argyle knits, Black and grey denim, Brushed cardigan coats and Brushed wool-cashmere coats are rising and keep growing into AW28.

Test in small quantities

Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Seamless technical knits, Slip-on knit sneakers and Technical wool travel suits are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Cable fisherman jumpers, Cable-front cardigans and Crochet tops and cardigans are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Fluid satin wide legs, Patent evening loafers and Patent knee boots are moving out.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Black and grey denim
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Chambray work shirts
  • Check sports jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Seamless technical knits
  • Slip-on knit sneakers
  • Technical wool travel suits
  • Quilted and padded dresses
  • Seamless performance knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Crochet tops and cardigans
  • Dark rinse, clean finish
  • Gingham and seersucker skirts
  • Leather overshirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fluid satin wide legs
  • Patent evening loafers
  • Patent knee boots
  • Patent leather
  • Patent trench with storm flaps
  • Photographic and art prints

Actions for the range

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.

Markets

How each region reads SS28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Sheer organza overlays, Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Column knit dresses, Slogan T-shirts

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Patent trench with storm flaps, Lightweight technical trench, Brushed wool-cashmere coats, Sheer organza overlays

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Lightweight technical trench, Seersucker and linen jackets, Quilted liner jackets, Performance-jersey shirt dresses

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis, Seersucker sundresses

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Performance-jersey shirt dresses, Floral print midis

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Seersucker and linen jackets, Sheer organza overlays, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis
