Where it works

Rising and peaking kidswear pieces to colour this season.

Sun-faded

Whites, sand, tobacco and ice blue, as if bleached by summer. Linen, cotton and soft tailoring.

For kids: tailoring, shirting, linen, menswear.

Purple reign

From lilac to violet, the season's most talked-about colour, paired with butter for an unexpected summer mix.

For kids: dresses, knits, leather, evening.

Full colour

Red, Klein blue and lemon worn head to toe. Primary colour without prints.

For kids: statement dresses, outerwear, shoes.

Living green

Grass green and teal from nature and the pool: summer knits, swim and prints.

For kids: swim, knits, prints, kidswear.
