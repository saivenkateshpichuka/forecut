SS27 Toddler Boys' Woven Silhouette Direction

Woven shapes for toddler boys: short rompers, pull-on shorts, camp shirts and short dungarees.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceShapesWhat to do
Research details

Research period Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Fatamadrina lists relaxed shapes, dungarees, oversized T-shirts, pleated dresses and lightweight jackets as SS27 silhouettes
  • Fatamadrina lists lightweight linen, voile, organic cotton, terry and micro-mesh as SS27 fabrics

Shapes

  • Short romper.
  • Pull-on shorts.
  • Short dungarees with snap legs.
  • Camp shirt.
Design

Design implications

  • Snap legs for nappy changes; no back buttons.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Sets sell best.
Evidence and sources
  • Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026
