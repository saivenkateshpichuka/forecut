The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Commuter tailoring

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders; stretch commuter suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Belted knee-high boots

Knee-highs with a single buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Button-front midi skirts

Buttons running down the front of the skirt. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless double-breasted jackets

Double-breasted fronts without lapels. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fine-gauge zip polos

Collared knits with a short zip. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Funnel-neck zip knits

Full zips on funnel-neck knits. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Button-front midi skirts
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Fine-gauge zip polos
  • Funnel-neck zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Hidden-zip blouses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Button-shoulder knits
  • Double-breasted overcoats
  • Horsebit mules
  • Lace-up ankle boots

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Double-breasted jackets
  • Double-breasted two-piece
  • Horsebit vamp hardware

Actions

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
  • Launch a machine-washable suit.
  • Use soft, unlined construction.
  • Market comfort and care, not just looks.
