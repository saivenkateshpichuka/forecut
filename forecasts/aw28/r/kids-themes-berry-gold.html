How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 kidswear fairs take place in January 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fairs, kidswear brands' long-term practices and market drivers, and will be updated as evidence arrives.

Design implications

  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
