SS27 Men's Colour Stories

Three colour stories for SS27 menswear: Sun-Faded for tailoring and linen, Aquatic for summer shirts and shorts, and Orange Energy for statements.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageSun-FadedAquaticOrange EnergyWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

Sun-Faded

Optic white, sand, tobacco and ice blue. Tailoring, shirting, linen. Brunello Cucinelli's cable knits with a sun-faded finish, achieved with resin in the dyeing process, show the look in knitwear.

Aquatic

Turquoise, sky blue and electric blue on leather jackets, shorts, linen shirts, socks and boat shoes. Pair with white.

Orange Energy

Blood orange, rust, tangerine and true orange, on a sunset-gradient overcoat, a cropped blouson or a V-neck sweater, or as a single accessory such as a bag or cap.

Design

Design implications

  • Give each story its own delivery or capsule.
  • Sun-Faded mixes with both other stories; Aquatic and Orange are best kept apart.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Sun-Faded carries volume; Aquatic and Orange Energy carry newness.
