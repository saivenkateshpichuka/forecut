Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Textured waffle knits
  • Chambray work shirts
  • Crochet-panel knits
  • Funnel-neck zip knits
  • Grandad collar linen shirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Seamless technical knits
  • Slip-on knit sneakers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Striped cotton crewnecks
  • Textured knit polos
  • Colour-block knits
  • Knitted short-sleeve polos
  • Knitted tank vests
  • Linen and seersucker shirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long-sleeve knitted polos
  • Open Johnny-collar knit polos
  • Rib-knit sweatshirts
