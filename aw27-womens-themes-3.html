The story

What is behind it.

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts.

Key pieces

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Clean straight-leg trousers
  • Clean wide-leg trouser jeans
  • Collarless tailored jackets
  • Column knit dresses
  • Cropped boxy shirts
  • Cuffed jean hems

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Clean column dresses
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck base layers
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Lacquered funnel-neck trenches

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Collarless cropped jackets
  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Flat-front straight trousers
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Photographic and art prints
  • Pleated tartan minis

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
