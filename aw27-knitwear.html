Overview

Early outlook. SS27 signals carried into winter: waisted outerwear, handcraft on knits, quiet structure and warmer earth tones, with luminous blue deepening for the cold season.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Fringed macramé tops, Argyle knits, Bishop-sleeve knits and Brushed cardigan coats are rising and keep growing into SS28.

Test in small quantities

Funnel-neck knits, Knitted overshirts, Long-line ribbed cardigans and Mock-neck fine knits are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Beads and sequins on knits, Brooch-fastened cardigans and Brushed alpaca are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Sheer fine-gauge layers are moving out.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Argyle knits
  • Bishop-sleeve knits
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Brushed mohair
  • Button-shoulder knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Funnel-neck knits
  • Knitted overshirts
  • Long-line ribbed cardigans
  • Mock-neck fine knits
  • Smocked knit details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Brushed alpaca
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Chunky shawl-collar cardigans
  • Colour-block knits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Sheer fine-gauge layers

Actions for the range

  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
  • Lab-dip cobalt and ink shades across knit, woven and leather.
  • Use deep blue as a core neutral, not just an accent.
  • Pair with cognac and cream.

Markets

How each region reads AW27, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

  • Waisted outerwear for East Asia; lightweight layering pieces for tropical markets
  • Craft on knits and festive wear for India and the Gulf winter season
  • Structured tailoring in soft wools for Japan, Korea and northern China

Best fit: Knit fringe at hems, Beads and sequins on knits, Sheer fine-gauge layers, Knitted polo collars

Europe

  • Waisted and belted outerwear, soft-focus brushed surfaces
  • Irregular, "living" textures from Première Vision AW27-28
  • Quiet tailoring in technical wools

Best fit: Knit fringe at hems, Beads and sequins on knits, Tartan and check jacquards, Sheer fine-gauge layers

North America

  • Technical outerwear and waisted coats
  • Elevated denim and leather
  • Sneakers step back for loafers and refined shoes

Best fit: Knitted polo collars, Fine-gauge zip polos, Ribbed polo knits, Rib-knit sweatshirts

South America

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028, in lighter weights for the tropical north
  • Leather and knitwear for Argentina and southern Brazil

Africa

  • West Africa: occasion and festive (Detty December) rather than cold-weather wear
  • South Africa: AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • South Africa AW27 runway (SAFW, Oct 2026): kaftans, capes and blanket wraps, A-line shapes, handcrafted and unisex denim, ochre, azure and orange-blue palettes

Best fit: Rib-knit sweatshirts, Knitted overshirts, Cardigans worn as shirts, Colour-block knits

Oceania

  • International AW27 trends sell in local winter, Mar–Aug 2028
  • Merino knitwear and practical outerwear

Best fit: Sheer fine-gauge layers
