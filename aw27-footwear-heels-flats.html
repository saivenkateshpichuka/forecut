The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Barefoot sole-less sandals

Strap-only sandals that leave the sole of the foot bare, for a barefoot look on the beach and runway; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 in Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for heels and flats.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Raffia wedge sandals

Woven raffia wedges and heels in metallic gold, chocolate, cinnamon and natural; Monday Swimwear x Billini launched 16 raffia styles in Oct 2026 for the southern summer. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Block-heel slingbacks

Slingbacks on mid block heels. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Bow-trim flats

Flats finished with a small bow. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Cut ties on the bias for a soft drape.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Fluid georgette, crepe de chine or satin.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Cap-toe kitten slingbacks

Contrast cap toes on kitten slingbacks. Toe shape is the first thing that dates a shoe, so it signals newness fast.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Develop a new last for the toe shape; check fit across widths.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Cap-toe kitten slingbacks
  • Flat strappy sandals

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Comfort block heels
  • Sport-sole smart flats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos
  • Curved sculptural heels
  • Embellished flats
  • Horsebit vamp hardware
  • Lace-up court shoes
  • Patent leather

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet flats
  • Clear PVC heels
  • Feathers on satin

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
