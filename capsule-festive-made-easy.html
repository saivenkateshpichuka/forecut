Why? Busy schedules and many functions per wedding mean shoppers want looks they can put on in minutes and wear more than once.

What? Festive dressing gets easier: ready-to-wear sarees, embroidered kurta co-ords, fusion kaftans, cape lehengas and lighter organza for sangeet, plus fit-and-flare Anarkalis that suit most occasions.

Who's showing it?

  • Satya Paul: Fluid sarees styled for the office
  • Abraham & Thakore: Sari-inspired silhouettes, printed co-ords and kaftans
  • Verandah: Breezy, resort-style separates

Cost to make: Medium. Stitched drapes and capes add pattern work. Keep embroidery to placement areas and reuse one base silhouette across colours.

How to build the capsule

  • Offer the hero festive look in a pre-draped or co-ord version.
  • Add capes and organza dupattas as add-ons to sell up.
  • Show three ways to wear each piece across wedding functions.
