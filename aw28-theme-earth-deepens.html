Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Quilted and padded dresses

Quilted dresses for cold-weather layering. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Floral-lined showerproof jackets

Showerproof field and quilted jackets with printed floral linings and reversible liners; Barbour x FARM Rio 'Tropical Greenhouse', on sale 30 Oct 2026. Lining details add a quality signal when the garment is open.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Choose a lining that is breathable and anti-static.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, peaking

Flat suede knee boots

Flat knee boots in suede. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Quilted liner jackets

Diamond-quilted jackets worn as outer layers. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, peaking

Suede ankle boots

Soft suede ankle boots. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, emerging

Hybrid quilted bombers

Bombers with quilted bodies and rib trims. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Quilted and padded dresses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated overshirts
  • Insulated winter sneakers
  • Packable down liners
  • Quilted dinner jackets
  • Quilted panel skirts

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Flat suede knee boots
  • Quilted liner jackets
  • Suede ankle boots

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Padded leather
  • Quilted gilets
  • Soft suede moccasins
  • Suede T-toe runners

Actions

  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
