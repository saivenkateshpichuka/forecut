AW28 Women's Season Colour Analysis

AW28 womenswear colour is projected to lead with mulberry and ink, with antique gold, lavender grey and a vermilion statement; pink becomes a neutral and blue a new anchor.

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 fabric fairs take place in September 2027 and the shows in early 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fabric fairs, SS28 previews and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Forecut's AW28 projection: ink, mulberry and bottle green over oat and slate, with petrol teal and vermilion as accents[1]
  • Future Snoops' AW28/29 "Blocked brights": "Bold colour becomes an act of defiance, a refusal to disappear into the algorithmic mass"; blue leads, with red and purple rising, and cornflower as a new "anchor colour"[2]
  • Pink becomes a neutral base ("Pink standard"), from soft blush to greyish dusty rose, no longer limited to womenswear; rosehip is a standout pink; suggested for blazers, skirt suits and voile dresses with ruffles or lace[2]
  • Vibrant cobalt with a violet undertone, paired with white; deep wine red with cream and black; bright matcha green with chartreuse and ecru, in menswear and womenswear[2]
  • Deep classic brown in leather and leather-look pieces, sporty pieces and tailoring, suited to suede and canvas-like materials[2]

Colour groups

  • Core: ink, oat, cocoa.
  • Seasonal: mulberry, antique gold, cornflower-type blues.
  • Accent: vermilion, lavender grey, dusty pink as a neutral.

By product

  • Mulberry: coats, knits, velvet.
  • Ink: tailoring.
  • Antique gold: knits, trims.
  • Dusty pink: blazers, skirt suits.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip key colours on each main fabric before committing.

Commercial implications

  • Keep the core deep and the accents shallow until the fairs confirm them.
