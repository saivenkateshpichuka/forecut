AW28 Toddler Girls' Assortment and Pricing Strategy

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. AW28 kidswear fairs take place in January 2028. This report projects from the first AW28/29 colour forecasts, the AW27 fairs, kidswear brands' long-term practices and market drivers, and will be updated as evidence arrives.

The evidence

  • Fashion entered 2027 expecting low single-digit growth, with markdown risk and faster growth in resale[1]

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core Basics, cords, fleece
Seasonal Coats, velvet
Statement Festive

Pricing

  • Full price on outerwear and merino.

Timing

In stores August 2028 to January 2029.

Design implications

  • Fewer styles, more combinations.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Track sales-to-stock weekly; plan resale and rework routes.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
