SS27 Men's Casualwear Styling

Casual SS27 menswear: oversized tees and polos, shirts tied at the waist, "I ♥" graphics, shorts with sporty jackets and flip-flops with smart looks.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

At Pitti Uomo, street style favoured oversized T-shirts, polos and colourful shirts tied around the waist[1]. In Paris, "I ♥" T-shirts were reinterpreted by 3.Paradis, Ami and Doublet; sporty jackets were layered over classic menswear; and lace-up ballet flats, boots and flip-flops were worn with both smart and athletic looks[2]. Dior styled a fluid check blazer with a loosely buttoned shirt, ripped jeans and a scarf-tie[2].

Formulas

  • Oversized tee + tailored shorts + loafers.
  • Polo + straight jeans + shirt tied at the waist.
  • Graphic tee + check blazer + ripped jeans.
  • Sporty jacket over shirt and tailored trousers.
  • Camp shirt set + flip-flops or ballet flats.

Design implications

  • Design casual pieces in the season palette so they mix with tailoring.

Commercial implications

  • Graphic tees and polos are reorder lines; keep them in core colours.

Evidence and sources

  1. Pitti Uomo SS 2027: menswear highlights, Wallpaper*, June 2026
  2. Between heatwave and restraint: menswear trends from Paris, FashionUnited, 2 July 2026
