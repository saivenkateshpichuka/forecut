The story

What is behind it.

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Belted field coats
  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Clean leather bombers
  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Double-breasted overcoats
  • Double-face wool overcoats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Braided officer coats
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Funnel-neck puffers
  • Leather-yoke nylon puffers
  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Shearling-collar aviators
  • Technical parkas

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • High-shine puffers

Actions

  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
