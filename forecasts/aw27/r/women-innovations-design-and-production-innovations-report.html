AW27 Women's Design and Production Innovations

Design and production for AW27: rediscovered techniques on new machines, design for repair and product data for the EU passport.

Audience

Innovations

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Première Vision's AW27 theme "Living Matter" puts materials "at the center", with materials "infused with memory"[1]
  • Footwear forecasters (AEC and Trendstop) describe "style without sacrifice", durability and repair with replaceable parts, sensory quilted surfaces and 70s and 90s micro-heels[2]
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026 large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel[3]
  • EU Directive 2024/825 bans greenwashing and misleading environmental claims; Italy's transposing decree entered into force on 27 September 2026[4]

Actions

  • Revive weaving and finishing techniques with mills.
  • Replaceable and repairable parts in coats and bags.
  • Record fibre, origin and care for every style.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Design for repair.

Commercial implications

  • Lunar New Year 2028 falls on 26 January; plan reorders around it.

Evidence and sources

  1. Autumn-Winter 27-28: "Living Matter", Première Vision, 21 May 2026
  2. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  3. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
  4. Events at Lineapelle September 2026 (EU Directive 2024/825 on greenwashing), Lineapelle, 6 August 2026
