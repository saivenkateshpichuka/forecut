The story

Single saturated pieces against dark neutrals: a tomato-red leather coat, a cobalt shoe, a bubblegum blazer.

Browns and near-blacks take over from black, lit by plum, moss and hot accents of tomato red, cobalt and mustard.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

AccentTomato redPantone 18-1564 TCX Poinciana#CA3422Leather coats, single statement pieces and shoes.
AccentCobaltPantone 18-3949 TCX Dazzling Blue#3850A0Saturated blue for outerwear and shoes.
AccentBubblegumPantone 14-2311 TCX Prism Pink#F0A1BFSoft pink tailoring and knits against browns.
CoreCharcoalPantone 19-0201 TCX Asphalt#434447Softer than black for tailoring and knits; the fastest-growing dark on the runways.

How to use it

Use for: Outerwear, shoes, bags, statement pieces.

Proportions

Lead with tomato red as the main colour, cobalt as the second, and keep charcoal to trims, linings or one piece.

Womenswear, Menswear, Footwear carry colours from this story.
