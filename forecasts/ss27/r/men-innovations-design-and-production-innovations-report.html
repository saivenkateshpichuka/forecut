SS27 Men's Design and Production Innovations

Body scanning and 3D sampling, product data ready for the EU Digital Product Passport, and SS27 production planned around new EU rules and the Lunar New Year.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Balenciaga used 3D body scans to build mannequins for its couture.
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026, large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel; since 1 July 2026, a temporary €3 duty applies per item on consignments up to €150.
  • Brands are diversifying sourcing towards Vietnam, Bangladesh, India, Turkey and Morocco.
  • Lunar New Year falls on 6 February 2027; factories slow from late January.
Design

Design implications

  • Develop first samples in 3D; record fibre, origin and care for every style.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Build buffer time around Lunar New Year; buy shallower in the EU.
