The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Commuter tailoring

Suits built for city days: stretch suiting, soft shoulders, concealed buttons and easy-care fabrics.

Why now

Hybrid working needs tailoring that is comfortable on a commute and smart at a desk.

Key pieces

Stretch suits, Concealed buttons, Soft shoulders; stretch commuter suiting.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Barrel-leg cargo jeans

Curved legs with cargo pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Black and grey denim

Washed black and grey denim. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Carpenter jeans

Hammer loops and utility pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Clean wide-leg trouser jeans

Wide jeans cut like trousers, with creases. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Cuffed jean hems

Turned-up hems on straight jeans. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Dark indigo tailoring

Pleated, creased trousers in dark indigo. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.
  • Build two price tiers of the idea.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Black and grey denim
  • Carpenter jeans
  • Clean wide-leg trouser jeans
  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Dark indigo tailoring

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Denim with technical details
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Button-fly denim shorts
  • Cut-off denim shorts
  • Dark rinse, clean finish
  • Denim shirt-jackets
  • Denim waistcoats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Denim with chain and patch trims
  • Embroidered denim
  • Mid-rise relaxed jeans
  • Pressed front seam
  • Wax-coated denim
  • Western yokes and pearl snaps

Actions

  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
