SS27 Women's Partywear Styling

Party SS27 is gold, bias-cut and jewelled: Saint Laurent's gold, Chanel's 1930s gowns, Louis Vuitton's reflective sixties dresses and Miu Miu's cocktail dresses worn with swimwear.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe runway looksFormulasWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

The runway looks

Gold was the season's evening colour: 49 of 59 Saint Laurent looks were gold, from brass to rose, in lamé, organza, lace, brocade, jacquard and gold-leaf latex. Chanel included bias-cut 1930s gowns; Louis Vuitton showed sixties dresses with reflective diamonds in grey, silver and yellow. Miu Miu paired jewelled cocktail dresses with swimwear, and Roksanda showed tasselled evening dresses. Early-2000s glamour returned at Etro, Roberto Cavalli and Gucci with leopard print, glitter, micro hems and stilettos over five inches.

Formulas

  • Gold head to toe. A gold lamé or metallic jacquard dress, or a gold skirt with a black top.
  • Bias-cut slip. A satin bias-cut dress in violet, black or optic white, with a fine knit or jacket over the shoulders.
  • Sixties shift. A short, straight dress with reflective embellishment and flat shoes.
  • Jewelled dress + swim layer. A beaded dress over a bandeau or swimsuit for holiday evenings.
  • Fringe in motion. A tasselled dress or skirt for dancing.
Design

Design implications

  • Line metallic and beaded fabrics to protect skin and seams.
  • Bias-cut dresses need to hang before hemming and careful grading.
  • Keep one statement element per look: gold, fringe or beading.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Partywear sells close to events; plan the main delivery for the late-spring wedding and party season and keep quantities tight.
  • Price gold and beaded pieces at premium; offer a satin slip at mid price as the accessible version.
