SS27 Men's Celebrity Influence

Red-carpet arrival

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In sand with butter.

Striped rugby shirts with pleated bermuda shorts, generous 1980s double-breasted suits and belgian loafers. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Airport off-duty

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In tobacco with salmon pink.

Boxy short-sleeve shirts with relaxed pleated chinos, puffed pleated suit trousers and black oxfords. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Press-day tailoring

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In ice blue with rust.

Check shackets with single and double pleats, relaxed 1930s drape and chelsea boots for tailoring. Keep one statement piece and let the rest stay quiet.

Front-row statement
