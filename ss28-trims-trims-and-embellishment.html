Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Shell-paillette jacket edging

Tweed and boxy jackets finished with shell-shaped paillettes along the fronts and hems; Chanel Cruise 2026/27 re-staged at Barangaroo, Sydney, 5 Nov 2026. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Bow-trim flats

Flats finished with a small bow. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Cut ties on the bias for a soft drape.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Fluid georgette, crepe de chine or satin.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Embroidered pocket shirts

Small embroidery on chest pockets. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Lace-cami layering tops

Lace-edged camisoles for layering. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Slim tassel loafers

Tassel loafers on slim soles. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Secure fringe at the base and check for tangling after washing.
  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Smocked knit details

Smocking moves onto knit cuffs and hems. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Use elastic thread or shirring with enough stretch to pull on easily.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.
  • Cost the detail early so it survives range review.

Fabric and trims

Lightweight cotton, seersucker or viscose.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Embroidered pocket shirts
  • Lace-cami layering tops
  • Slim tassel loafers
  • Smocked knit details

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Denim with technical details

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Contrast-tipped knits
  • Smocked necklines and cuffs

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Denim with chain and patch trims
  • Embellished flats
  • Embroidered denim

Actions

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
