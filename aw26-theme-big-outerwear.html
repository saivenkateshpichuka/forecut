The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Harrods, AW26 menswear trends; Who What Wear

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons

Nylon twill blousons in black, navy or khaki where thick contrast topstitching is the design accent; sacai Holiday 2026, Tokyo, from 6 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for outerwear.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses

Strapless corset bodices over a crinoline-lined petticoat skirt cut to tea length, softened with tulle draped over the shoulders; Vivienne Westwood custom black Chantilly-lace dress for Ariana Grande at the London 'Focker-In-Law' premiere, 18 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for dresses.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Suede equestrian jackets

Structured riding-style jackets in suede with shearling trims, as in Cara Cara's Equestrian capsule (Oct 2026). The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use faux fur or recycled shearling-look pile; make fur parts detachable where you can so the garment is easier to care for.
  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Faux fur, recycled shearling-look pile or bonded borg, backed with a stable knit.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Velvet cape tops

Evening tops with a short cape layer in place of sleeves, in burgundy velvet; Mint Velvet holiday 2026, with Sézane x Lucia Zolea's Angeline cape in the same round-up (18 Nov 2026). Sleeve volume changes the silhouette more than almost any other single detail.

Design notes

  • Make the cape detachable or attached at the yoke so the base garment sells on its own.
  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Check armhole depth and movement at fit; volume needs ease.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Belted field coats

Field coats with a waist belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool melton, waxed or brushed cotton, recycled nylon ripstop, with matte metal zips and snaps.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons
  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Velvet cape tops
  • Belted field coats
  • Brushed cardigan coats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Braided officer coats
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Cropped parkas over evening gowns
  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Floral-embroidered cropped puffers
  • Leather-yoke nylon puffers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Belted trench blazers
  • Denim waistcoats
  • Large flap patch pockets
  • Shearling-collar aviators
  • Technical parkas

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • High-shine puffers
  • Raw-edge blanket coats

Actions

  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
