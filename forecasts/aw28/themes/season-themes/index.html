Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, in ink blues, mulberry and bottle green.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Cargo pockets, Concealed-button blazers, Denim with technical details and Double-pocket camp shirts are rising.

Test in small quantities

Hybrid quilted bombers, Insulated hybrid hikers, Insulated overshirts and Insulated winter sneakers are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets, Floral-lined showerproof jackets and Barrel-leg cargo jeans are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Low-rise bootcut jeans, Piped satin track jackets and Suede equestrian jackets are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Key pieces

Stretch wool, Water-resistant, Travel suits; technical wool suiting.

Actions

  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.

Read the full story

Earth deepens

Warm earth orange deepens into rust and brick, often on quilted and padded pieces.

Key pieces

Rust, Brick, Quilting; quilted and padded dresses.

Actions

  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Denim with technical details
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Half-zip knits
  • Hidden magnetic closures

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated hybrid hikers
  • Insulated overshirts
  • Insulated winter sneakers
  • Packable down liners
  • Quilted dinner jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted field coats
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted safari jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Belted wrap fronts

Actions for the range

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Belted shirt dresses, Concealed-placket shirts, Utility shirt-jackets

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Single-breasted car coats, Stand-collar field coats, Hidden-placket overcoats

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Quilted liner jackets, Belted safari jackets, Suede equestrian jackets

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Denim shirt-jackets, Denim with technical details, Patch-pocket denim jackets, Denim chore coats

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Belted shirt dresses, Concealed-placket shirts, Utility shirt-jackets, Double-pocket camp shirts

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Utility shirt-jackets, Denim shirt-jackets, Denim with technical details

India in depth

Festive and summer seasons, handloom, craft clusters and the colours that sell in India, read against AW28.
