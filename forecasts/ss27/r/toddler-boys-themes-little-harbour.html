SS27 Toddler Boys' Little Harbour

Nautical for toddler boys: stripes, sailor collars and navy with red, in soft jersey.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Toddler Boys
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The story

Sailor collars, navy and red were season-defining at Playtime Paris [1]; Loir Paris's sailor top was in MilK's selection [2]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to toddler boys, roughly 1 to 4 years.

Colours

Navy, red, white.

Silhouettes

  • Stripe rompers.
  • Sailor-collar shirts.
  • Shorts.

Fabrics

  • Jersey, interlock, light poplin.

Trims and details

  • Sailor tape, printed anchors.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Rompers, sets, sun hats, sandals.

Design implications

  • Easy dressing: snaps at the crotch, wide neck openings.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Gift buyers matter; package sets.

Evidence and sources

  1. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
  2. MilK's Spring/Summer 2027 Dressing Ideal, Playtime, July 2026
