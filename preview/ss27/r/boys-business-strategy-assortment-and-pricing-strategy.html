SS27 Boys' Assortment and Pricing Strategy

Assortment and pricing for SS27 boyswear in a slower market.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Business strategy
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • In Italy, childrenswear exports fell 9.3% and domestic sales 2.6% in 2025 (Confindustria Moda via Fortune Italia)[1]
  • Fashion is expected to grow in low single digits in 2027; resale is expected to grow two to three times faster than first-hand fashion through 2027[2]

Assortment

Tier What goes in it
Core (about half) Tees, shorts, denim, polos in navy, sand, white
Seasonal (about a third) Swim and UV, sets, red pieces, track
Statement (the rest) Prints, character, occasion shirts

Pricing

  • Full price on UPF and certified organic.
  • Value on basics.
  • Sets priced to reward both pieces.

Design implications

  • Durable construction.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Weekly sell-through tracking.

Evidence and sources

  2. Fashion: 2027, the year growth will finally have to be profitable, FashionUnited, 18 November 2026
