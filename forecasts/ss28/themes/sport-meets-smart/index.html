Key pieces

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Comfort block heels

Lower, wider block heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Cupsole derbies

Derbies on sneaker cupsoles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Flat espadrille sneakers

Canvas shoes on jute soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Use a rubber-backed jute sole for durability.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Jute rope soles with rubber backing.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Hybrid sport-smart boots

Smart boots on cushioned sport soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Hybrid sport-sole derbies

Derbies on lightweight sport soles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Comfort block heels
  • Cupsole derbies
  • Flat espadrille sneakers
  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Hybrid sport-smart boots
  • Hybrid sport-sole derbies

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Slip-on knit sneakers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Reworked racing sneakers
  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Sun-bleached distressed sneakers
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Fisherman sandals

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Ballet-sneaker hybrids
  • Flatform sneakers
  • Mary Jane sneakers

Actions

  • Develop one sport sole and use it across three uppers.
  • Market comfort features with clear claims.
  • Keep uppers refined to stay smart.
