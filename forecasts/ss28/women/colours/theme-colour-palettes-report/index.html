How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Its named colours include Aubergine Skin, Solar Midnight, Smoky Lavender and Swamp Green, with Cloud Bloom as a luminous counterpoint and Fruit Punch for "juicy intensity"; dark shades work best with contrast, not head to toe[1]
  • Forecut's SS28 colour forecast: royal blue and a new turquoise beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge[2]
