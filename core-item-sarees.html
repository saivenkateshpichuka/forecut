Core Item Update: Sarees 2026–28

The saree is being redesigned for speed.

Forecut Editorial · 11 October 2026

Download PDF ↓

Overview

The saree is being redesigned for speed. Pre-draped and stitched versions are rising into Festive 26, handloom and tissue add quiet shine, and office-ready fluid drapes are an early signal worth testing. Heavier scalloped organza is at its peak and starts to fade by Festive 27.

How to update your sarees range

  • Offer every hero saree in a classic and a pre-draped version.
  • Add belts and jackets as styling add-ons to sell the look, not just the drape.
  • Use pastel handloom and champagne tissue for festive capsules that can be worn again.
  • Plan the exit for heavy scalloped organza after Summer 27.
