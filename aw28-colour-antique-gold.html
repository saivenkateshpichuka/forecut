The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Technical wool

Wool that stretches, sheds rain and resists creases, for suits and coats that travel well.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Key pieces

Stretch wool, Water-resistant, Travel suits; technical wool suiting.

Functional tailoring

Why now

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Key pieces

Belted jackets, Utility pockets, Clean seams; belted suit jackets.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Earth deepens

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Tailored wool shorts

Pleated shorts in wool. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.
  • Check length on petite and tall fits.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Technical wool suiting

Wool suiting with stretch and water resistance. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Technical wool travel suits

Crease-resistant wool with hidden closures. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, peaking

Elongated evening jumpsuits

Long, lean jumpsuits in black with glossy or metallic finishes and gold embroidery, as in Namrata Joshipura's OBSIDIAN, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Allow for less give in coated fabrics; adjust ease and avoid tight seams.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Fabric and trims

Polyurethane-coated cotton or recycled polyester with a glossy finish.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Raffia wedge sandals

Woven raffia wedges and heels in metallic gold, chocolate, cinnamon and natural; Monday Swimwear x Billini launched 16 raffia styles in Oct 2026 for the southern summer. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Brushed wool-cashmere coats

Soft, brushed blends with a hazy surface. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Tailored wool shorts
  • Technical wool suiting
  • Technical wool travel suits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Quilted suit jackets
  • Technical merino layers
  • Technical wool cargo trousers
  • Wool-felt flats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Elongated evening jumpsuits
  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Brushed wool-cashmere coats
  • Flannel overshirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Studded and ruffled Mary Jane clogs
  • Double-face wool overcoats
  • Grey melange flannel
  • Leather and wool varsity
  • Leather-trimmed collars
  • Merino crewnecks

Actions

  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
