SS27 Women's Knit Fabric Direction

Fine, cool and elastic: extra-fine merino, cool wools and combed mohair for single-layer knits; flat, Derby and Richelieu ribs for close fits; retro-sport stitches and crochet for texture.

The evidence

Première Vision's SS27 knit selection set out two families. "Iconic Sweaterwear" uses flat, Derby and Richelieu ribs, which are more elastic and elongated than jersey[1]. "Retro Sport" revives bindings, weighted and reverse stitches, honeycomb piqués, plated knits, diamond stitch and wheat stitch, in slightly yellowed, sepia-like tones[1]. Its season overview adds cool wools, extra-fine merino and combed mohairs, and compact milanos for structured pieces[2].

On the runway, the finest knits did the most work. Louis Vuitton cut tailoring from the lightest knits to be worn as one layer, Hermès showed sheer knits[3], and Chanel's collection included sheer knits[4]. Bottega Veneta and Brunello Cucinelli showed crochet and open-stitch knits in oversized cuts[5]. Jersey held its share of looks at 12.1%[6].

Why it matters

Single-layer dressing for heat puts knit in place of the light jacket and shirt. The fibre and gauge decide whether a knit can be worn alone: fine merino and silk blends can, open crochet needs a slip underneath. Rib structures with good recovery also let knits replace darts and seams on fitted tops, which simplifies production.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. Fine merino and silk-blend knits continue into AW27 as transitional layers. Crochet is at its height and suits the high-summer drop only.

Products and customers

  • Sheer fine-gauge tops and tunics; knit jackets and cardigan-jackets; long rib tops and column dresses; knit polos; crochet tops and dresses.
  • Customers: women who want one piece that works alone in heat; younger customers for crochet and sheer layers.

Design implications

  • Choose fibre and gauge before the shape; test sheerness on the body under shop lighting.
  • Stabilise shoulders and necklines on open stitches with tape.
  • Specify recovery tests for rib (length growth after wear) in the tech pack.
  • Design sheer knits with a matching under-layer.

Commercial implications

  • Price fine merino and silk blends at the premium tier; crochet works at entry and mid price in cotton.
  • Plan reorders for rib tops and knit polos, which repeat across seasons; keep crochet as a short run.
  • Sheer knits need clear product photography of what is worn underneath to limit returns.
