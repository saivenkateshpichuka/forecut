Methodology

Forecast track record.

Every time the forecast changes, a dated snapshot is kept. When a season is shown and assessed, we compare what we projected with what we then assessed, and publish the result here, good or bad.

Back-test

For each season that has moved from projection to assessed: the share of details whose assessed stage is the same as the last projected stage, and the share within one stage (for example projected Rising, assessed Peaking).

No back-test result yet. Records start on 2026-10-26. Since then no season has moved from projection to assessed, so there is nothing honest to score. The first test will be AW27, once its runway shows have been reviewed: its last projected stages will be compared, detail by detail, with the assessed ones and published here.
