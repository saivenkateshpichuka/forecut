The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Argyle knits

Diamond argyle patterns on knits. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Fair Isle yoke cardigans

Patterned yokes on button cardigans. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Limit colours per row to control float length and cost.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Floral print midis

Small and painterly florals on midi dresses. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Striped poplin shirts

Bold and candy stripes on poplin. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Textured stripe knits

Striped knits with texture. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.
  • Plan extra fabric for pattern matching.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, peaking

Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops

Sweatshirts, tees and dresses printed with the image of a shirt layered on top, as at À La Garçonne, São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026). A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Argyle knits
  • Fair Isle yoke cardigans
  • Floral print midis
  • Striped poplin shirts
  • Textured stripe knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Trompe-l'oeil shirt-print tops
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cable-front cardigans
  • Striped Breton tops
  • Striped cotton crewnecks

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Photographic and art prints
  • Slogan T-shirts
  • Vintage poster-print tops

Actions

  • Specify finishes (quick-dry, UV) with mills early.
  • Use certified fibres where possible.
  • Label care benefits clearly.
  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
