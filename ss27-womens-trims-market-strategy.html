By market

Regional direction for the season, with the pieces that fit each market.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Fits: Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Lace-trimmed slips over knits

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Fits: Patent trench with storm flaps, Raglan sleeve coats, Welt-pocket cocoon coats

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Fits: Cinched peplum jackets, Collarless cropped jackets, Lace-trimmed jacket edges

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Drop-shoulder shirt dresses

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Sheer lattice capes, Sheer organza overlays

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Backless apron tops
  • Band-collar shirts with long placket
  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted safari jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Asymmetric-collar pinstripe shirts
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Collarless tailored jackets
  • Denim with chain and patch trims
  • Fine-gauge zip polos

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Lace-edged bias slips

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Large flap patch pockets
