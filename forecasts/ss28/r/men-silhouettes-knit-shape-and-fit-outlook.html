SS28 Men's Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

SS28 men's knits: open-knit and crochet polos, cooling baselayers, fine cardigans.

Season
Spring Summer 2028
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2028.

On this pageHow this forecast was builtThe evidenceShapes and fitWhat to do
Research details

Research period SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record

Markets Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Matte finishes ground dark colour, satin and gloss intensify it, and sheer fabrics and open knits lighten it; extreme heat is moving socialising into the evening
  • Milano Unica sees wool moving toward "ultra-fine qualities, airy surfaces and ultralight constructions"
  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams

Shapes and fit

  • Open-knit polo.
  • Crochet polo.
  • Cooling baselayer.
  • Fine cardigan.
