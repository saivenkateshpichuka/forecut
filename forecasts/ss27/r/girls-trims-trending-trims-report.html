SS27 Girls' Trending Trims Report

Girls' trims: hand embroidery, ruffles, smocking, lined sequins, shell and bead details for older girls.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Fatamadrina lists oversized florals, checks with fruit prints, paisley, vintage geometrics, nautical themes and gingham, and "Wildflowers Bloom" with delicate handcrafted embroidery[1]
  • Selini Action makes handmade costumes decorated with shells, pebbles, seaweed and embroidery[2]
  • Pitti Bimbo 103 took "The Pitti Pool" as its theme and called it "the most colorful summer edition yet"[3]

Trim Application Safety note

Design implications

  • No small parts for younger sizes.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Embroidery adds value.
