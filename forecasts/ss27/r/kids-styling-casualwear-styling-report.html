SS27 Kids' Casualwear Styling

Casual SS27 kidswear: coordinated sets, stripes with denim, layered accessories and sport pieces styled with a refined, creative lens.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

Cozmo built coordinated sets into a total look[1]; MilK chose matching sets by Catimini and Semon ApartMent, shoes from Tnin and Affenzahn[2]. Playtime Paris showed "layered styling", character-driven accessories, refined sport and diverse denim[3].

Formulas

  • Striped tee + denim dungarees + sandals.
  • Matching printed set + canvas shoes.
  • Oversized tee + relaxed shorts + backpack.
  • Sport jacket over a terry set.
  • Sailor top + shorts + hat.

Design implications

  • Design sets so pieces also work as separates.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Sell sets together and apart; show full looks online.

Evidence and sources

  1. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  2. MilK's Spring/Summer 2027 Dressing Ideal, Playtime, July 2026
  3. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
