SS27 Girls' Wildflower

Romantic summer for girls: wildflower prints, handcrafted embroidery, airy voile and linen dresses in soft pastels.

Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls

Rising

The story

Fatamadrina's "Wildflowers Bloom" is a dreamy, romantic aesthetic with airy fabrics, delicate handcrafted embroidery and a meadow pastel palette; oversized florals and pleated dresses are on its SS27 lists [1]. Future Snoops' Lull favours elevated basics [2]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to girlswear, roughly ages 4 to 14.

Colours

Lavender, vintage pink and pastel green over sand and white.

Silhouettes

  • Pleated and tiered dresses.
  • Puff-sleeve blouses.
  • Wide shorts and skirts.

Fabrics

  • Voile, lightweight linen and organic cotton [1].

Trims and details

  • Hand embroidery, smocking, ruffles (secured).

Timing

In stores February to July 2027.

Products and customers

  • Dresses, blouses, skirts, sandals.

Design implications

  • Line voile where needed; no long ribbons at the neck.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Dresses lead the girls' buy.

Evidence and sources

  1. Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026. Design studio forecast; used for season-wide fabric, silhouette and print notes
  2. Kids spring/summer 2027 trends outlined by Future Snoops, Playtime, June 2026
