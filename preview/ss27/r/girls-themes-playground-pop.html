SS27 Girls' Playground Pop

Bold, joyful colour for girls: bright prints, reversible dresses, sparkle and character pieces that girls choose themselves.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Themes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail
Trend status
Rising

The story

Poco Blush showed bold hues, a reversible printed dress and a scented T-shirt [1]. The Pirouette Disco Pool Party Runway promised "bold looks, sparkling details, and positive vibes" [2]. Junior Style saw a growing whimsical trend [3]. Most SS27 kidswear sources describe collections across ages and genders. The evidence below is the fair record; the shapes, products and sizes are Forecut's application of it to girlswear, roughly ages 4 to 14.

Colours

Lemon, coral, aqua and red.

Silhouettes

  • Reversible and loose dresses.
  • Bike shorts and oversized tees.
  • Sets.

Fabrics

  • Jersey, terry and poplin.

Trims and details

  • Sequins (lined), appliqué, embroidered characters.

Timing

In stores March to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Printed dresses, sets, party tops, accessories.

Design implications

  • Line sequins; check edges.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Smaller, sharper buys.

Evidence and sources

  2. Pitti Bimbo 103: the energy of summer ignites kidswear, Pitti Immagine, 13 May 2026
  3. Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
