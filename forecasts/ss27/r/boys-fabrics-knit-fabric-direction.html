SS27 Boys' Knit Fabric Direction

Boys' knits: cotton jersey, piqué, micro-mesh, terry and UPF-rated swim and performance fabrics.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Fabrics
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Fatamadrina lists lightweight linen, voile, organic cotton, terry and micro-mesh as SS27 fabrics[1]
  • Swimwear, T-shirts and accessories with built-in UV filters were prominent and are becoming common, linked to family concern for health and to changing climate[2]
  • Teeter Totter showed UPF50+ swim and sportswear, backpacks and ultra-light, sand-free beach towels[3]
  • Newer lines emphasise breathability, quick drying and comfort; childrenswear is moving toward functionality, material quality and durability[2]

Fabric by fabric

Knit Hand Uses
Organic cotton jersey Soft Tees
Piqué Structured Polos
Micro-mesh Airy Sport tops, linings
Terry Absorbent Shorts, cover-ups
UPF swim and performance Stretch, quick-dry Swim, rash guards

Design implications

  • Test UPF after stretching and wetting.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • UPF supports full price.

Evidence and sources

  3. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
