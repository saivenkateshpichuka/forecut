Looks

Select any piece for its design notes.

Evening out

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In tinted black with dusky violet.

Elbow-seam jumpers with scarf-tied waistbands, oversized lapel flower brooches and slip-on canvas sneakers. Let one shine or texture lead, and keep the shoes comfortable enough to last the night.

Cocktail

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In greyed blue with tannic bordeaux.

Boxy short-sleeve shirts with barrel trousers, relaxed 1930s drape and belgian loafers. Let one shine or texture lead, and keep the shoes comfortable enough to last the night.

Festive

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In copper khaki with sage.

Cardigans worn as shirts with deep turn-ups, belted field coats and black oxfords. Let one shine or texture lead, and keep the shoes comfortable enough to last the night.

Celebration

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In dusky violet with tinted black.

Check shackets with double-knee work trousers, contrast-stitch nylon twill blousons and derby boots. Let one shine or texture lead, and keep the shoes comfortable enough to last the night.

The palette to style in.

Tinted black, greyed blue and copper khaki for tailoring and outerwear, with dusky violet for a younger customer.
