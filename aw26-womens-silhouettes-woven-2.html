Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, emerging

Boxy cropped jackets

Short, square jackets with dropped shoulders. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Check the crop length against high-rise bottoms.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, emerging

Bubble hems

Puffed hems on short skirts. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, emerging

Cinched one-button jackets

Nipped waists with a single button. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, emerging

Contrast sash waists

Cummerbunds and sashes tied at the waist. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
