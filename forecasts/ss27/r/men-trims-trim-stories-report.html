SS27 Men's Trim Stories

Trims that carry each SS27 men's theme: flat pockets and raw edges for Short Story, light construction for Ventilated Tailoring, embroidery and feathers for Tender Masculinity, piping for Pyjama Polish.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageShort StoryVentilated TailoringTender MasculinityPyjama PolishWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

Short Story

Flat patch pockets, raw edges on collarless truckers, button-fly and elastic waists on boxer shorts.

Ventilated Tailoring

Perforated and woven leather panels, unlined construction, light horn and shell buttons.

Tender Masculinity

Organza feathers and cornflower-blue embellishment at Simone Rocha; embroidered florals; brooches.

Pyjama Polish

Black collars and piping on khaki, blue and beige sets at Dior; covered buttons; drawstrings.

Design

Design implications

  • One trim family per theme, used consistently.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Trims refresh core shirts and jackets without new patterns.
