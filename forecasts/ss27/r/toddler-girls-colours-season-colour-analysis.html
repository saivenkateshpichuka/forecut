SS27 Toddler Girls' Season Colour Analysis

Soft pastels with coral and lemon for SS27 toddler girls.

The evidence

  • Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" swimwear runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy, in technical fabrics including sustainable lycra, with UV protection[1]
  • At Playtime Paris, red "emerged as one of the defining colors of the season"; stripes ran horizontal, vertical and diagonal[2]

Colour groups

  • Core: sand, white.
  • Seasonal: lavender, coral, aqua.
  • Playful colour remains important for this age; keep brights as accents.

Design implications

  • Low-impact, skin-friendly dyes; check colour fastness to saliva and sweat where required.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Depth in sand and white basics.
