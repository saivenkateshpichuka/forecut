Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, emerging

Elastic-back tailored waists

Flat fronts with elastic at the back waist. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, fading

Balloon trousers

Volume gathered into the ankle. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

Fluid crepe, jersey or satin.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, fading

Bermuda suit shorts

Tailored shorts worn with blazers. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, fading

Black tailoring with fuchsia accents

Black wool and corduroy suiting with fuchsia kept as the only accent colour, never mixed in one outfit; Reserved x Dawid Woliński capsule, Europe, 5 Nov 2026. A colour-led detail that is easy to roll across many styles.

Design notes

  • Choose wale size for the garment: fine wale for shirts, wider wale for trousers and jackets.
  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Lab-dip early and approve under daylight and store lighting.

Fabric and trims

Cotton corduroy in 8–14 wale.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
