AW27 men's colour stories: New Neutrals, Natural Dye and Pink for All.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Neutrals shift: grey toward greyish blue, khaki toward taupe, green or copper, navy toward turquoise or purplish tones[1]
  • Van den Hoek describes natural dyes from tea, coffee, corn and tree bark, whose limits give richer colour gradations[2]
  • Pink and mauve are becoming gender-neutral, reaching menswear and even junior sweatshirts[1]

New Neutrals

Tinted black, greyed blue, copper khaki.

Natural Dye

Tea, coffee, corn and bark tones with rich gradations.

Pink for All

Dusty mauve and dusky violet.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Commercial implications

  • New Neutrals carries volume.
