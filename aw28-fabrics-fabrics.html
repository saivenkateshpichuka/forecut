The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Earth deepens

Colours of the year carry into winter in deeper shades.

Why now

Natural fibre with performance benefits fits the "fewer, better" shift.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Leather-look trousers

Straight trousers in faux leather. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Performance-jersey shirt dresses

Shirt dresses in stretch, easy-care jersey. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Quilted and padded dresses

Quilted dresses for cold-weather layering. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Satin slip skirts

Fluid satin skirts cut on the bias. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Seamless performance knits

Seam-free knits with stretch and support. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Seamless technical knits

Seam-free quarter-zips with stretch. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Use a matte zip and a stand collar that sits flat.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Leather-look trousers
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Quilted and padded dresses
  • Satin slip skirts
  • Seamless performance knits
  • Seamless technical knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated overshirts
  • Insulated winter sneakers
  • Quilted dinner jackets
  • Quilted panel skirts
  • Quilted suit jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Parachute-poplin tuxedo shirts
  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Sheer-panel check shirts
  • Taffeta jogger evening trousers
  • Brushed cardigan coats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Suede equestrian jackets
  • Tonal suede low-profile sneakers
  • Brushed alpaca
  • Corduroy suits
  • Double-face wool overcoats

Actions

  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
