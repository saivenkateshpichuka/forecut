Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Seamless technical knits
  • Snap-front western shirts
  • Storm-collar jackets
  • Technical wool travel suits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Fly-front placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck zip knits
  • Knitted bomber jackets
  • Overshirt jackets
  • Utility overshirts with snaps

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fisherman-style raincoats
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Quarter-zip tops
  • Tech fleece jackets
  • Zip-neck polos
  • Zip-through knit jackets
