How we forecast
1

Signal collection

Runway coverage, street style, search interest and retail launches across Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa and Oceania, every week.

2

Detail-level analysis

Each construction detail, trim and material is identified and placed at a stage: emerging, rising, peaking or fading.

3

Reports you can use

Design notes, fabric guidance, colour codes and range strategy that help your team sketch, specify and sample faster.

Emerging early signals in a few collections. Test in small quantities.

Rising spreading across designers and retail. Add it now.

Peaking at full adoption. Use in core styles, do not over-buy.

Fading demand is slowing. Phase it out of new development.

Stages are reviewed monthly; news is checked daily.

Before you start

Anything else? Write to hello@forecut.in.

What is free?

Joining is free. Every report opens with a free preview, and signed-in members read the full reports, download the PDFs and use every Forecut tool.

How do you make your forecasts?

From public runway, trade and retail research, AI-assisted and reviewed by an editor, written in our own words. Each detail is placed at a stage and reviewed every month.

Is Forecut only for India?

No. Every season starts from the global runways and is read for six regions. India is covered in more depth, with festive and summer seasons, handloom and craft.

Can my college or team get access?

Yes. Colleges, alumni groups and teams can get an access code for their members. Write to hello@forecut.in.

What is in a report PDF?

Each report has three PDFs: the full report, inspiration and design, and strategy, pricing and markets. Share only the part a team needs.

How do I contact Forecut?

Email hello@forecut.in. We reply within a working day.
