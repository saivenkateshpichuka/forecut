Collections · Resort 27

Resort / Cruise 2027

Resort (also called Cruise) collections are shown in May and June and arrive in stores from November, for holidays, travel and warm-weather markets. For the southern hemisphere they land in peak summer.

Global forecast · Updated 23 Oct 2026

The Cruise 2027 shows

Four houses, four cities: spectacle on the runway, wearable clothes underneath.

ChanelMatthieu Blazy's first Cruise collection · Biarritz, France

Sailor stripes, sporty pieces, robe coats, scarf dresses, relaxed tailoring; fluid knits, Basque-inspired linens and retro swimwear; turquoise and an explosion of colour.

DiorJonathan Anderson · LACMA, Los Angeles

Floral dresses inspired by Californian poppies, softened Bar jackets, elongated tailoring; denim embroidered with silver chains; sculptural headpieces by Philip Treacy.

GucciDemna's Cruise debut · Times Square, New York

Banker tailoring, pencil skirts, technical coats, cropped leather separates and giant carryalls: grounded "Gucci Core" essentials.

Louis VuittonNicolas Ghesquière · The Frick Collection, New York

Structured leather coats, leather minidresses, pleated high-neck blouses with denim, colourful patchwork evening coats.

Australian Fashion Week: Resort 27

Shown in Sydney in May 2026: the southern hemisphere's own resort calendar.

  • Bi-coastal utility: nylon and raincoat fabrics, funnel-neck jackets, frayed edges
  • Ethereal sheers: silk and silk organza, micro polka dots, slip dresses under blazers
  • High shine: liquid sequin and georgette, iridescent green, ice blue, gold
  • Surfer perfect: wraps, sarong ties, crochet sets, raffia hems, sunset prints
  • Berry reds: cherry, raspberry, wine, coral, ballet pink
