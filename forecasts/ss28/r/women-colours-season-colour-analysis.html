SS28 Women's Season Colour Analysis

SS28 womenswear colour is projected to bring royal blue and greyish mauve forward, over straw and bleached linen, with darker after-dusk tones for evening.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Women

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Forecut's SS28 colour forecast: royal blue and a new turquoise beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge[1]
  • Future Snoops' SS28 colour shift "After Dusk": "Deep aubergines, inky blues and murky greens are bringing a darker, more atmospheric mood to warm-weather dressing"[2]
  • Its named colours include Aubergine Skin, Solar Midnight, Smoky Lavender and Swamp Green, with Cloud Bloom as a luminous counterpoint and Fruit Punch for "juicy intensity"; dark shades work best with contrast, not head to toe[2]
  • Copenhagen SS27 data showed colour returning and neutrals fading; Jacquemus built its SS27 show around turquoise and orange[3]

Colour groups

  • Core: bleached linen, straw, inky blue.
  • Seasonal: royal blue, greyish mauve.
  • Accent: magnetic violet, peach.

By product

  • Royal blue: shirts, dresses, tailoring.
  • Greyish mauve: sheer layers, knits.
  • Inky blue: evening, after-dusk dressing.
  • Straw and linen: summer bases, raffia accessories.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip key colours on each main fabric before committing.

Commercial implications

  • Keep the core deep and the accents shallow until the fairs confirm them.
