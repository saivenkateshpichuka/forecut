The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Power tailoring

Sharp, slick suiting in pinstripes and slate grey, with pointed shoulders, peak lapels and double-breasted fronts. Oxblood accents and polished heels finish the look.

Why now

Return-to-office dressing and a move away from very oversized blazers toward sharper fits.

Seen at

  • Gucci: Horsebit pumps with sharp suiting
  • Classic-fit suits (market): Up 44% in market adoption
  • Double-breasted suits (market): Up 4% year on year; pointed shoulders up 12%

Key pieces

Pinstripe, Peak lapels, Pointed shoulders; sharp pointed shoulders.

Source: Who What Wear (AW26 trends); Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Why now

Party-season demand meets nostalgia for glamour. Drop waists are easier to wear than bodycon, which helps them sell beyond evening.

Seen at

  • Chanel: Fringed silks and beaded sequins
  • Fendi: Drop-waist dresses and 1920s evening codes
  • Rabanne: Chainmail and sequin minis

Key pieces

Drop waist, Fringe, T-bar heels; drop waist with pleated skirt.

Source: Who What Wear, Autumn/Winter 2026 trends

Fully loaded

Embellishment moves from evening onto everyday pieces: sequinned jeans, beaded knits, feathers and sheer layers over heavier fabrics.

Why now

Social media rewards texture and shine, and embellishment makes basics feel special.

Key pieces

Sequins, Beadwork, Sheer layers; sequin and chainmail minis.

Source: Who What Wear, AW26 trends (the source describes the trend without naming brands)

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses

Strapless corset bodices over a crinoline-lined petticoat skirt cut to tea length, softened with tulle draped over the shoulders; Vivienne Westwood custom black Chantilly-lace dress for Ariana Grande at the London 'Focker-In-Law' premiere, 18 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for dresses.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Ombré sequin gowns

Long gowns with sequins graded from dense to sparse or light to dark; Elie Saab's dégradé sequins reached the high street in the H&M collaboration launched 22 Oct 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Belted shirt dresses

Classic shirt dresses with a self-belt. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Bias-cut velvet

Fluid bias-cut velvet in jewel tones. A cut change that updates the fit without changing the fabric story.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Fit-test on multiple sizes before bulk.
  • Keep the cut consistent across colourways.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Column knit dresses

Long ribbed knit dresses in column shapes. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Floral print midis

Small and painterly florals on midi dresses. A print-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Scale the print for the garment size; small prints for shirts, larger prints for dresses.
  • Scale the print for small and large sizes.
  • Check print placement at seams.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Belted shirt dresses
  • Bias-cut velvet
  • Column knit dresses
  • Floral print midis

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Fishtail gowns with attached drapes
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Contrast sash waists
  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Halter-neck dresses
  • Photographic and art prints

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Drop waist with pleated skirt
  • Drop-shoulder shirt dresses
  • Fringed hems

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Big puff sleeves

Actions

  • Offer the pinstripe suit as separates so customers can mix sizes.
  • Use pointed shoulders on jackets, but keep trousers relaxed.
  • Add a waistcoat that works alone as a top.
  • Lead evening with one drop-waist dress in two lengths.
  • Add fringe to hems and sleeves rather than the whole garment.
  • Pair with T-bar heels and long pearls in visual merchandising.
