Trims and embellishment

Build around Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos, Brogue perforations and Contrast-tipped knits. The waist returns and handcraft moves into everyday clothes. Shapes get defined again, sneakers step back for elegant shoes, and a bright luminous blue leads the palette.

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

  • Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets
  • Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship
  • Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

  • Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe
  • DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts
  • Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing; fringed hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos

Black smoking suits embroidered all over in black, worn as the alternative to a gown; Marian Saud for Débora Plager at the Martín Fierro de Cable awards, Buenos Aires, 12 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Brogue perforations

Broguing returns. Perforation adds breathability and a crafted look.

Design notes

  • Use perforations as decoration and for breathability.
  • Back perforations with a breathable lining.
  • Keep perforation away from flex points.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Contrast-tipped knits

Contrast stripes at necks and cuffs. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Cricket cables with tipping

Cable jumpers with striped V-neck tipping. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cricket jumpers

Cables with striped tipping. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Embellished flats

Beaded and studded flats. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Brogue perforations
  • Contrast-tipped knits
  • Cricket cables with tipping
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Embellished flats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Shell-paillette jacket edging
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Denim with chain and patch trims
  • Slim tassel loafers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Contrast-piped edges
  • Lace-edged bias slips

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Carpenter hammer loops

Actions

  • Add lace trims at cuffs and hems of core shirts.
  • Use machine smocking on bodices and cuffs.
  • Partner with a craft cluster for a premium capsule.
  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.
