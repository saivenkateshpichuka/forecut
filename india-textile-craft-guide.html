India Textile and Craft Sourcing Guide

Where India's key weaves and hand-crafts come from, what they are like, what they suit, and where each one sits in the trend cycle.

Fabrics and weaves

Handloom and silk lead the quiet-luxury direction. True handloom has longer lead times and minimums: use it for hero pieces.

Sourcing checklist

  • Order handloom early and confirm minimums with the cluster.
  • Ask for GI certification where it applies, such as Chanderi, Banarasi, Kanjeevaram and Kolhapuri.
  • Approve strike-offs and lab dips before bulk.
  • Credit the craft cluster or artisan group on labels and product pages.
