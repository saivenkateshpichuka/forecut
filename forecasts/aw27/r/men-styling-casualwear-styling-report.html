AW27 Men's Casualwear Styling

Casual AW27 menswear: oversized light down, camouflage as a neutral, preppy layering, lived-in mess.

Season
Autumn/Winter 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Styling
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Filati, June 2026; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica, September 2026; forecaster reports to November 2026
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Menswear forecaster Edwin van den Hoek: jackets, trousers and down grow bigger without weight ("It looks very big, but it is very light to wear"); layering at the neck; fur or hairy collars; leather as a refined detail[1]
  • Woodland camouflage now works almost like a neutral, distorted and recoloured[1]
  • Jan Agelink's "Age of Agency": a messy, lived-in aesthetic, layering by construction, pixelated "glitch meets nature" checks, and watches used as styling devices[2]

Formulas

  • Down jacket + wide trouser + knit.
  • Camo overshirt + grey knit.
  • Rugby knit over shirt.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Layering.

Commercial implications

  • Camo and down for volume.

Evidence and sources

  1. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
  2. Watch parties, glitch and layering: AW27/28 according to trend forecaster Jan Agelink, FashionUnited, 30 July 2026
