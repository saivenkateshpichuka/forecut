The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

  • Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets
  • Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship
  • Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses

Strapless corset bodices over a crinoline-lined petticoat skirt cut to tea length, softened with tulle draped over the shoulders; Vivienne Westwood custom black Chantilly-lace dress for Ariana Grande at the London 'Focker-In-Law' premiere, 18 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for dresses.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns

Fully sequinned column gowns closed with corset lacing down the back; Tamara Ralph on Lily James at the Governors Awards, Los Angeles, 15 Nov 2026, with Karol G's lace-up corset bodice (Celia Kritharioti couture) at the Latin Grammys, 12 Nov. A back detail adds interest for the second look and for styling shots.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Make sure the back is shot in imagery so customers see the detail.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos

Black smoking suits embroidered all over in black, worn as the alternative to a gown; Marian Saud for Débora Plager at the Martín Fierro de Cable awards, Buenos Aires, 12 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Black oxfords

Classic black lace-ups. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.
  • Keep the last consistent across the style family.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Broderie anglaise dresses

White eyelet-embroidered cotton dresses with puff sleeves and tiers, as at Chloé SS27. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Crochet tops and cardigans

Granny-square and crochet textures. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Back crochet panels or use them as overlays to keep shape.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Cotton crochet panels or crochet-look knit.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Black oxfords
  • Broderie anglaise dresses
  • Crochet tops and cardigans

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Low-rise slip skirts with bra tops
  • Boat shoes
  • Crochet-panel knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Lace-edged bias slips

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add lace trims at cuffs and hems of core shirts.
  • Use machine smocking on bodices and cuffs.
  • Partner with a craft cluster for a premium capsule.
