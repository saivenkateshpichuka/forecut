AW27 Women's Season Colour Analysis

Violet leads AW27 womenswear, with tannic bordeaux replacing black and dusty pinks softening the range; waxy ochre is the light.

Audience

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • At Première Vision, deep violet, plums, dusty pinks, mauves and tinted off-blacks set the tone; violet is "centre stage", and plum and deep red become alternatives to black[1]
  • Première Vision's colour preview opens with "grayed mid-tones, as if patinated by time", with purples "taking center stage"[2]
  • Boland expects mustard and chartreuse, creamy white and "sensual black", with bold red, yellow, blue, green and purple[3]
  • Forecut's AW27 colour forecast: violet, plum and tannic bordeaux replace black over tinted neutrals[4]

Colour groups

  • Core: mushroom, tinted black, creamy white.
  • Seasonal: deep violet, tannic bordeaux, dusty mauve.
  • Accent: waxy ochre, silicone pink, and one bright: mustard or chartreuse.

By product

  • Violet: coats, knits, velvet.
  • Bordeaux: leather, tailoring, boots.
  • Dusty mauve and silicone pink: knits, satin, accessories.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip violet on wool, satin and leather; it shifts strongly between substrates.

Commercial implications

  • Replace part of the black buy with tinted black and bordeaux.
