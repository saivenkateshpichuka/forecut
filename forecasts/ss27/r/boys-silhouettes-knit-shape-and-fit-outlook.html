SS27 Boys' Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Boys' knit shapes: oversized tees, piqué and mesh polos, track sets, rash guards and swim shorts with liners.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to August 2027.

On this pageThe evidenceShapes and fitWhat to do
Research details

Research period Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Cozmo showed striped T-shirts and coordinated sets forming a total look, for newborns to 14 years
  • Sport influence was presented "through a more refined, creative lens"; denim felt "stronger and more diverse than in recent seasons"
  • Teeter Totter showed UPF50+ swim and sportswear, backpacks and ultra-light, sand-free beach towels
  • Fatamadrina lists relaxed shapes, dungarees, oversized T-shirts, pleated dresses and lightweight jackets as SS27 silhouettes

Shapes and fit

  • Oversized tee: dropped shoulder.
  • Polo: straight fit, piqué or mesh.
  • Track set: zip jacket and shorts.
  • Rash guard and swim short.
  • Stripe set.
Make

Products and customers

  • Tees, polos, track sets, swim.
Design

Design implications

  • Flat seams on rash guards.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Tees and polos are reorder lines.

  • Playtime Paris SS27: where fashion met the spirit of a summer music festival, Junior Style (Aleksandra Ataca), 30 June 2026
  • Childrenswear fashion trends and forecast S/S 2027, Fatamadrina (surface design studio for childrenswear), 2026
