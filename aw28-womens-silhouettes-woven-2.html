Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, emerging

Packable down liners

Light quilted liners worn under or as coats. Layered construction adds depth and styling value in one piece.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Keep layers light so the garment is not bulky.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, fading

Barrel-leg trousers

Curved legs that taper at the hem. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, fading

Basque waist bodices

A pointed waist seam that dips below the natural waist. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Place the peplum seam at the natural waist for the most flattering line.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, fading

Belted trench blazers

Trench details on belted blazers. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Keep classic trench details (storm flap, epaulettes, belt) but simplify proportions for a modern look.
  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Cotton gabardine, coated cotton or water-repellent twill.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
