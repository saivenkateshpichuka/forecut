SS27 Women's Formalwear Styling

Formal SS27 means the skirt suit, the cinched jacket and tailoring made light: knee-length skirts, peplum jackets, gold with a trench and organza suits for heat.

The runway looks

Chanel's narrow, knee-length skirts in smocked tweed and sheer organza[1] and its new tweed suit[2] set the tone for formal dressing. Givenchy showed cinched peplum tailoring, trench riffs and woollen overcoats[1]; Armani, Gucci and Jil Sander cinched jackets at the waist, and Jil Sander, Tod's and Marni shortened them[2]. Saint Laurent paired gold pencil skirts with trench coats[3]. For heat, Dior made organza tailoring printed to look like wool suiting[1].

Sarah Burton described her Givenchy wardrobe as one "to bring precision and simplicity to dressing"[1].

Formulas

  • Skirt suit. A cropped, waisted jacket with a matching knee-length straight skirt.
  • Peplum jacket + slim trouser. For the office; the waist is defined, the trouser stays straight.
  • Trench + pencil skirt. A light trench over a satin or metallic pencil skirt.
  • Organza suit. A summer suit in printed or plain organza over a slip, for weddings and day events in heat.
  • Shirt + full midi skirt + belt. A poplin shirt tucked into a layered midi skirt, cinched with a leather belt.

Design implications

  • Cut skirt suits as separates sold together, so customers can buy different sizes top and bottom.
  • Use cool wool, double crepe and organza to keep formalwear wearable in summer.
  • Specify linings and finishing carefully: formal pieces are judged on the inside as much as the outside.

Commercial implications

  • Skirt suits replace part of the trouser-suit buy this season.
  • Offer alteration-friendly seam allowances on skirts and jackets for a better fit at full price.
