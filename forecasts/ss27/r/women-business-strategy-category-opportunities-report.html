SS27 Women's Category Opportunities

Skirts, short and waisted jackets, satin pieces, floral prints and accessories-style trims are the SS27 womenswear growth categories. Loose trousers, chinos and mini dresses are shrinking.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageThe evidenceOpportunitiesCategories to reduceWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

The evidence

Runway share of looks, SS27 against SS26:

Category SS26 SS27 Change
Skirts 19.3% 27.9% +8.6 points
Straight skirts 5.1% 10.3% +5.2 points
Dropped-waist dress 0.6% 4.9% +4.3 points
Jackets 20.9% 23.1% +2.2 points
Satin 17.8% (derived) 20.8% +3.0 points
Floral prints 11.4% 14.2% +2.8 points
Dresses 37.0% 33.3% -3.7 points
Loose trousers 11.5% 3.0% -8.5 points
Chinos 6.7% 1.5% -5.2 points
Mini dresses 9.8% 4.8% -5.0 points

Retail buyers at Le Bon Marché and Galeries Lafayette Paris highlighted skirts and separates. Outside apparel, jewellery and leather goods are projected to grow 4 to 6% a year between 2025 and 2027.

Opportunities

  1. Skirts: the biggest shift of the season; build straight and midi skirts as a core category.
  2. Jackets: short, waisted jackets and skirt suits.
  3. Satin: slip skirts and dresses, blouses and wide trousers.
  4. Prints: florals in particular, on dresses and skirts.
  5. Add-on accessories: belts, corsages, brooches and bows, which follow the runway trims and the strength of accessories.

Categories to reduce

Loose trousers, chinos and mini dresses fell sharply in runway share. Reduce depth, move customers to straight tailored trousers and midi lengths, and clear remaining stock before the SS27 drop.

Design

Design implications

  • Shift design time from trousers to skirts and jackets.
  • Plan skirt and jacket pairs in shared fabrics and colours.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Re-weight the SS27 open-to-buy towards skirts and jackets.
  • Test accessories-style trims as add-ons at the till and online checkout.
  • Watch early sell-through on dropped-waist dresses before adding depth.
