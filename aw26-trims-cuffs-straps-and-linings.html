The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Big outerwear

Oversized, protective outerwear: shearling, big fur collars, leather bombers and sculptural cocoon coats.

Why now

Outerwear is a high-value hero category, and leather bombers are growing fast.

Seen at

  • Acne Studios: Fur accents on outerwear
  • Leather bombers (market): Up 28% year on year
  • Harrods menswear edit: Leather jacket as the hero piece

Key pieces

Shearling, Fur trims, Leather; fur and shearling collars.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Harrods, AW26 menswear trends; Who What Wear

Roaring Twenties

1920s flapper glamour returns. Drop waists, swinging fringe, beading and long pearls lead evening, and the same codes cross into daywear through T-bar heels, velvet and pleated skirts.

Why now

Party-season demand meets nostalgia for glamour. Drop waists are easier to wear than bodycon, which helps them sell beyond evening.

Seen at

  • Chanel: Fringed silks and beaded sequins
  • Fendi: Drop-waist dresses and 1920s evening codes
  • Rabanne: Chainmail and sequin minis

Key pieces

Drop waist, Fringe, T-bar heels; drop waist with pleated skirt.

Source: Who What Wear, Autumn/Winter 2026 trends

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Block-heel slingbacks

Slingbacks on mid block heels. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Use elastic in the slingback strap so it stays on.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Contrast cuffs

White or contrast cuffs. A cuff detail is visible in every pose and costs little to add.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Interface cuffs and check opening size for hand pass-through.
  • Use the cuff to repeat a trim from the front.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, rising

Elongated rib cuffs

Long ribbed cuffs that bunch at the wrist. A cuff detail is visible in every pose and costs little to add.

Design notes

  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Interface cuffs and check opening size for hand pass-through.
  • Use the cuff to repeat a trim from the front.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Sneakers & Casual, rising

Hiking sandals

Webbing sandals for trails. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Leather, suede, mesh or knit uppers, rubber cupsoles or EVA midsoles, recycled laces.

Where to use it

Best on court sneakers, runners, slides and clogs. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Leather slides

Wide-strap leather slides. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Shape the footbed so the foot stays secure without a back.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Mary Jane flats

Flats with a strap across the instep. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Place the strap at the instep and use a buckle or snap.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Contrast cuffs
  • Elongated rib cuffs
  • Hiking sandals
  • Leather slides
  • Mary Jane flats

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Sherpa-lined trucker jackets

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
  • Pick one hero coat per range and build marketing around it.
  • Offer detachable fur collars to stretch one coat into two looks.
  • Pair bombers with knitwear in outfit-led merchandising.
