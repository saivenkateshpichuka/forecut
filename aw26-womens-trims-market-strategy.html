              Strategy

              What to build, test, sell through and drop.

              Build depth

              Rising pieces

              • Asymmetric wrap closures
              • Barrel leg with curved outseam
              • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
              • Beads and sequins on knits
              • Belted wrap fronts
              • Button-shoulder knits

              Test small

              Emerging pieces

              • Belted safari jackets
              • Brooch-fastened cardigans
              • Button-front midi skirts
              • Corset-seamed dresses
              • Double-pocket camp shirts
              • Flap-pocket blazers

              Sell through

              Peaking pieces

              • Cargo midi skirts
              • Chunky rib turtlenecks
              • Contrast-piped edges
              • Lace-edged bias slips
              • Large flap patch pockets
              • Leather-trimmed collars

              Exit or rework

              Fading pieces

              • Cargo pockets
              • Raw-edge blanket coats
              • Visible corset boning
