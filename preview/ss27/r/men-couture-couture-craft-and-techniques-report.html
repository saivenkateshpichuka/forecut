SS27 / Couture and craft / Men

SS27 Men's Couture Craft and Techniques

Hand craft in menswear: days of handwork on crystal denim, resin-dyed knits, hand-illustrated motifs and tailoring cut for both men and women.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Philipp Plein's crystal-encrusted denim takes days of handwork[1].
  • Brunello Cucinelli dyed cable knits with resin so each sun-faded piece is unique[2].
  • Yohji Yamamoto showed a hand-illustrated bird tee[3]; Sacai wove a vintage photo into a two-piece suit[3].
  • Martin Quad debuted in Milan with unusual tailoring techniques[1].
  • At couture, Elie Saab cut tuxedos and capes for both men and women and is expanding its menswear line[4].

Translating to ready-to-wear

  • One hand technique per piece (a hand-painted motif, a crystal seam line).
  • Garment-dyed or resin-dyed knits for one-of-a-kind colour.
  • Woven or engineered photographic prints for statement suits.

Design implications

  • Budget hand labour into hero pieces only.

Commercial implications

  • Tell the craft story on the label; price as limited.
