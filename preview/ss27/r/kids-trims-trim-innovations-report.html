SS27 Kids' Trim Innovations

Functional and sensory details in kidswear: scented finishes, UPF-rated components, reversible construction and gadgets attached to bags.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • Poco Blush's T-shirt releases a minty scent when crumpled; its printed dress is reversible[1].
  • Sticky Lemon showed a mini digital camera that attaches to a bag[1].
  • Cadhan uses dermatologically tested fragrances in its sleep line, with a "Sogni d'oro" line for sensitive skin[2].
  • Teeter Totter's ultra-light, sand-free beach towels[2].

Readiness

Innovation Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Reversible construction Ready
Sand-free towel fabrics Ready
Scented finishes Test skin safety and wash durability first
Bag-attached gadgets Accessory category; check toy and battery rules

Design implications

  • Any finish on skin-contact garments needs skin-safety evidence.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Novelty sells as gift and story; keep buys small.
