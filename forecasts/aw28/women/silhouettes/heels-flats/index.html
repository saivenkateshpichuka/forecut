Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Bow-trim flats

Flats finished with a small bow. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Cut ties on the bias for a soft drape.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Fluid georgette, crepe de chine or satin.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, rising

Stiletto court shoes

Classic pointed stiletto courts. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Raffia wedge sandals

Woven raffia wedges and heels in metallic gold, chocolate, cinnamon and natural; Monday Swimwear x Billini launched 16 raffia styles in Oct 2026 for the southern summer. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on pumps, slingbacks, ballet flats, mules and sandals. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Comfort block heels

Lower, wider block heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Check heel width for stability and comfort.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Nappa or patent leather, suede or satin uppers, leather or TPU soles, slim metal buckles.

Where to use it

Footwear, Heels & Flats, peaking

Flat strappy sandals

Thin-strap flat sandals. Strap placement changes fit, security and the look on foot.

Design notes

  • Check strap placement for comfort and rubbing.
  • Make straps adjustable for different foot shapes.
  • Check strap edges for rubbing.

Fabric and trims

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Bow-trim flats
  • Stiletto court shoes

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Wool-felt flats

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Raffia wedge sandals
  • Comfort block heels
  • Flat strappy sandals
  • Low block sandals
  • Mary Jane flats
  • Patent ballet flats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Block-heel slingbacks
  • Cap-toe kitten slingbacks
  • Horsebit vamp hardware
  • Kitten-heel pumps
  • Patent leather
  • Satin evening pumps

Actions

  • Source technical wool samples early.
  • Test water resistance and stretch recovery.
  • Position as investment pieces.
  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
