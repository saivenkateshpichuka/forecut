SS27 Girls' Couture Materials and Details

Premium girls' materials: fine Italian and recycled fabrics, sustainable lycra, organic cotton voile.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Girls
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Marie Raxevisky's "Beach Couture" offers UPF 50+ swimwear, sarongs and skirts in fine Italian and recycled fabrics, with mother-daughter matching[1]
  • Piccoli Principi's "Pearl & Tides" swimwear runs from vintage pink to pastel green and soft navy, in technical fabrics including sustainable lycra, with UV protection[1]
  • Salt and Pepper describes its SS27 fabrics as sustainable organic cotton, "exceptionally soft and gentle on the skin"[2]

Materials

  • Fine Italian cottons.
  • Recycled swim fabrics.
  • Organic voile.

Design implications

  • Check certifications.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Mother-daughter capsules at premium.

Evidence and sources

  1. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  2. Salt and Pepper: Spring/Summer 2027 brand profile, Pitti Immagine Bimbo, June 2026
