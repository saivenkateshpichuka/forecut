Design notes

How to use the leading trims.

Kidswear, Tops, rising

Name-label panels

Printed panels inside the neck to write a child's name; useful for school. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.
  • Cost the detail early so it survives range review.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Occasion, rising

Nehru waistcoat

Band-collar waistcoat over a kurta or shirt for festive dressing. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Adjust the back with a strap and buckle for fit.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

Where to use it

Best on occasion. Works in kidswear occasion as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

Polo-collar knit

Knitted polo with a Johnny collar; the adult knit polo for kids. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

Where to use it

Best on knitwear. Works in kidswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

Short-sleeve knit polo

Fine cotton knit polo with a Johnny or button collar for summer. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • Sequins, beads and small hardware suit ages 5 and up only; sew them securely and test them.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
