SS27 Men's Trim Stories

Short Story

Flat patch pockets, raw edges on collarless truckers[1], button-fly and elastic waists on boxer shorts.

Ventilated Tailoring

Perforated and woven leather panels[2], unlined construction, light horn and shell buttons.

Tender Masculinity

Organza feathers and cornflower-blue embellishment at Simone Rocha[3]; embroidered florals; brooches.

Pyjama Polish

Black collars and piping on khaki, blue and beige sets at Dior[4]; covered buttons; drawstrings.

Design implications

  • One trim family per theme, used consistently.

Commercial implications

  • Trims refresh core shirts and jackets without new patterns.
