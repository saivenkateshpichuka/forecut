How the pattern maker works

Start from a Forecut detail, such as a band-collar kurta, Cuban camp collar shirt, barrel-leg jeans, peplum top or fit-and-flare Anarkali, and the matching garment and options load automatically. Or pick a category (womenswear, menswear, kidswear or unisex) and one of 12 garments: fitted bodice, kurta, shirt, T-shirt, hoodie, saree blouse, top, dress or frock, Nehru jacket or waistcoat, skirt, lehenga and trousers.

Measurements start from a size chart for each category and can be changed to your own in centimetres, millimetres or inches. Style options cover fit ease, length, neckline, collar (band, shirt collar with band, camp, Peter Pan), sleeve (set-in, shirt with cuff, T-shirt, puff, bell, cap), pockets, yoke, hem, slits, flare, dropped shoulders, skirt type and leg shape.

Every piece is drafted from the measurements, so pieces that are sewn together fit together: sleeve heads are matched to the armhole, collars and bands to the neckline, and cuffs and waistbands to the wrist and waist. Each piece gets an automatic name, like “Men's shirt · Collar band”, which you can edit. Seam and hem allowances can be set for the whole pattern or for each piece, and fold edges never get an allowance.

Downloads

  • PDF, A4 tiles: print at 100% or actual size, check the 5 cm square, and tape the tiles together (A1, A2 across, then B1…). Blank tiles are skipped.
  • PDF, full size: one page at true size for a plotter or copy shop.
  • DXF: for Gerber, Lectra, Optitex, CLO, Illustrator or AutoCAD, with cutting lines, sewing lines, marks, grain lines and text on separate layers.
  • SVG and PNG: for editing, sharing and tech packs.

Every file carries the Forecut label on each piece and on the sheet.

Before you cut

These are block patterns for samples, learning and first toiles. They do not include grading across sizes, and every body and fabric behaves differently. Make a toile in calico or muslin, check the fit, and transfer any changes before cutting good fabric. Advanced pattern editing is planned for a later version.
