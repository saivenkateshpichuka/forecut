SS27 Women's Trim Innovations

Ribbon built into the cloth, coated and pearlescent lace, rubberised corsetry meshes and fastenings used as decoration: the newest trim ideas for SS27.

Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Published
2026-11-25
On this pageRibbon as structureCoated and pearlescent laceCorsetry componentsFastenings as decorationReadinessWhat to do
Research details

Research period Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026

Markets Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

Ribbon as structure

Première Vision describes ribbon acting as material, ornament and structure: finishing ribbons, highly decorative ribbons and ribbons integrated into fabric construction, inlaid into jacquards, embroidered as backgrounds or applied onto tulle and lace. For production, this means trims ordered as part of the fabric rather than added at the end.

Coated and pearlescent lace

The "Wet Sheen" direction brings plastic-coated laces and pearlescent satins. Première Vision's decoration report adds Leavers lace with a melted-film coating for a latex-like sheen. On the runway, Saint Laurent coated latex in gold leaf.

Corsetry components

Membrane-backed draperies, spacer fabrics and rubberised meshes borrowed from corsetry appear among Première Vision's structural fabrics. They let a bodice hold its shape without boning.

Fastenings as decoration

Prada used coloured Velcro strips with embroidered florals on the fastenings; Ferragamo covered snap buttons in sequins; Miu Miu laced skirts with cross-lace fastenings; Givenchy made belt buckles that double as powder compacts.

Readiness

Innovation Readiness for production (Forecut assessment)
Ribbon-inlaid jacquards Available from specialist mills; long lead times
Coated and pearlescent lace Available; test cleaning and abrasion
Rubberised and spacer meshes Available from lingerie and sportswear suppliers
Decorative fastenings Ready; mainly a design and sourcing decision
Design

Design implications

  • Bring trim suppliers into fabric development early when ribbon is part of the cloth.
  • Test coated laces for cracking, peeling and dry-cleaning.
  • Use corsetry meshes to shape bodices and bra tops without rigid boning.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Innovative trims justify a premium on a few hero pieces; do not spread them across the range.
  • Long lead times for jacquard ribbons mean early commitment: order SS27 quantities now and limit reorders.
