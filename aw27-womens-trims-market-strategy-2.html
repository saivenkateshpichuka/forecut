Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Denim, peaking

Barrel leg with curved outseam

Curved seams shaping the leg. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

Beads and sequins on knits

Embellishment moving onto everyday jumpers. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Recycled sequins, glass beads or heat-set stones.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

Brooch-fastened cardigans

Brooches replacing buttons. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, peaking

Cargo midi skirts

Utility patch pockets on midi skirts. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Round or square patch pockets should be topstitched evenly and reinforced at the corners.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Beads and sequins on knits
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Cargo midi skirts
  • Chunky rib turtlenecks
  • Chunky shawl-collar cardigans

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Backless apron tops
  • Contrast collars and cuffs
  • Floppy oversized collars
  • Long exposed button fly
  • Ruched side seams
  • Rugby stripes, short plackets
