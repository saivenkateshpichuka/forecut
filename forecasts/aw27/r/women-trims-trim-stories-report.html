How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • APLF trims include patinated brass buckles, hammered studs, horn closures, magnetic closures, invisible zips, jewelled buckles, decorative chains, oversized resin buckles and rubberised webbing[1]
  • Milano Unica trims: horn, corozo, mother-of-pearl and bio-based polyester buttons, oxidised metal pulls, chains and recycled leather labels[2]

Dramatic Presence

Jewelled buttons, ruffles, lace, matte gold.

Soft Armour

Concealed zips, magnetic closures, bonded seams.

Remastered Classics

Contrast piping, horn buttons.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • One trim family per theme.

Commercial implications

  • Trims refresh carry-over blocks.

Evidence and sources

  2. Fall/Winter 2027/28 trend report: controlled audacity in an age of agency, FashionUnited, 3 November 2026. Compiled by FashionUnited from forecaster and fair documents and checked by an editor; only non-WGSN material is used here
