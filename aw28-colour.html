Overview

Long-range Forecut projection.

Winter deepens the SS28 blues and violets: ink, mulberry and bottle green over oat and slate, with petrol teal and vermilion as accents.

Core

The base of the range: carry these across most styles.

Ink, Oat, Slate, Cocoa

Seasonal

What makes the season new: give these real depth.

Mulberry, Bottle green, Antique gold

Accent

Small, high-impact doses: one piece, one trim.

Petrol teal, Vermilion, Lavender grey

How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

A long-range Forecut projection. It carries the AW27 move away from black and the SS28 return of strong blues and violets into winter. No AW28 fabric fair has taken place yet; it will be refined after the September 2027 fairs.
