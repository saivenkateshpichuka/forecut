Weekend

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In inky blue with sonar turquoise.

Knitted overshirts with tailored wool shorts, cropped work jackets and mesh running sneakers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.

City errands

top + bottom + layer + shoes. In khaki green with straw.

Crochet-panel knits with pleated bermuda shorts, knitted ties and running-inspired sneakers. Keep the colours close and vary the texture.

Travel day
