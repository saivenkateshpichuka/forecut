The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Soft boots

Flat riding boots, slim soles and soft leather replace chunky lugs.

Why now

Footwear follows the move to elegance; slimmer soles suit refined trousers and skirts.

Key pieces

Riding boots, Slim soles, Soft leather; soft knee-high riding boots.

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Buckled ankle boots

Ankle boots with a buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Chelsea ankle boots

Elastic-sided ankle boots. Shaft height and fit decide how the boot works with trousers and skirts.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Use strong elastic gussets and a pull tab for easy wear.
  • Check calf width across sizes; offer wide-calf fits.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Embroidered and silk boots

Decorated knee-highs. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Flat suede knee boots

Flat knee boots in suede. Material choice drives both look and price point for this detail.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Test material for wear, flex and colour fastness.
  • Check certifications for leather and synthetics.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Kitten-heel knee boots

Knee boots on low kitten heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Chelsea ankle boots
  • Embroidered and silk boots
  • Flat suede knee boots
  • Harness buckle straps
  • Kitten-heel knee boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Hybrid sport-smart boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Hiker styles
  • Kitten-heel boots
  • Knee-high slouch
  • Over-the-knee boots
  • Patent knee boots
  • Second-skin stretch shafts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Reduce lug-sole depth in new styles.
  • Add a flat knee-high riding boot.
  • Offer wide-calf fits.
  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
