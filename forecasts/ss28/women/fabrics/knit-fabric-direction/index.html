How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Milano Unica sees wool moving toward "ultra-fine qualities, airy surfaces and ultralight constructions"[1]
  • Matte finishes ground dark colour, satin and gloss intensify it, and sheer fabrics and open knits lighten it; extreme heat is moving socialising into the evening[2]
  • Everyday basics gain "integrated wellness properties" and materials that "intuitively adjust for temperature and activity"[3]

  • Core knits carry volume.
