SS27 Kids' Design and Production Innovations

Storytelling-led design, artist collaborations, durable construction and product data ready for the EU Digital Product Passport.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

  • Collections designed as worlds and characters (Playtime Paris)[1]; Bobo Choses and Halcyon Nights build collections around books and artist panels[2].
  • Durability and functionality over short-lived seasonality[3].
  • The EU textiles Digital Product Passport delegated act is planned for the fourth quarter of 2027; since 19 July 2026, large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel[4].

Design implications

  • Record fibre, origin, care and safety test results for every style.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Plan rework, resale or donation routes for unsold stock in the EU.
