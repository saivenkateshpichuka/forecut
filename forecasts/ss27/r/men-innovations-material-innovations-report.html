SS27 Men's Material Innovations

Materials for SS27 menswear: crease-proof stretch wool, technical cottons and water-repellent linen, chrome-free leather, softshell and heat-regulating sportswear fabrics.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Herno's travel collection uses crease-proof fabrics, stretch wool and technical materials[1].
  • Première Vision highlights technical cottons and water-repellent linens, and compact milanos and softshells[2].
  • Margaret Howell's Dr Martens 1461 uses chrome-free leather by C.F. Stead[1].
  • Rick Owens used Adidas's Climacool heat-regulation technology[3].
  • Balenciaga used lab-grown AMSilk silk in its July 2026 couture[4].

Readiness

Material Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Stretch and crease-proof wool Ready
Water-repellent linen, technical cotton Ready from specialist mills
Chrome-free leather Ready; limited supply
Heat-regulating sport fabrics Ready in sportswear
Lab-grown silk Early; showpieces only

Design implications

  • Use technical wools in travel tailoring; water-repellent linen for unlined outerwear.

Commercial implications

  • Verify every sustainability claim before using it.
