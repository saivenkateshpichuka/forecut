              Design notes

              How to use the trims.

              Kidswear, Tops, rising

              Chenille lettering

              Varsity-style chenille names and numbers on sweats and jackets. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

              Design notes

              • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
              • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

              Fabric and trims

              Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

              Where to use it

              Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Kidswear, Knitwear, rising

              Contrast elbow patches

              Patches that add wear on knit elbows and trouser knees. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

              Design notes

              • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
              • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.
              • Cost the detail early so it survives range review.

              Fabric and trims

              Soft jersey, fleece or fine knit with stretch for movement; flat seams against the skin.

              Where to use it

              Best on knitwear. Works in kidswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Kidswear, Tops, rising

              Contrast piping

              Piping on collars, pockets and pyjama edges in an accent colour. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

              Design notes

              • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
              • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
              • Order trims early to avoid delays.

              Fabric and trims

              Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

              Where to use it

              Best on tops. Works in kidswear tops as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Kidswear, Outerwear, rising

              Easy-grip zip pullers

              Large, rounded pullers small hands can hold. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

              Design notes

              • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
              • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
              • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

              Fabric and trims

              Soft, washable cottons and blends; pre-shrunk, colour-fast and tested for children's wear.

              Best on outerwear. Works in kidswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

              Safety notes

              Check these before sampling.

              • Sequins, beads and small hardware suit ages 5 and up only; sew them securely and test them.
              • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
              • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
              • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
