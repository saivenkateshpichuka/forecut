Functional tailoring

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Cargo pockets

Utility cargos cooling in womenswear. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Denim with technical details

Denim jackets with zips and bellows pockets. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Double-pocket camp shirts

Camp-collar shirts with two chest pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Hiker styles

Hiking boots worn with tailoring. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Hiking boots with tailoring

Rugged soles under suits. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Keep the jacket and trousers in the same cloth so they sell as a set.
  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Kitten-heel knee boots

Knee boots on low kitten heels. Heel height and shape set the occasion and comfort level of the shoe.

Design notes

  • Keep heels 3–5 cm and reinforce the heel tip for stability.
  • Test heel stability and attachment strength.
  • Offer two heel heights where possible.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Cargo pockets
  • Denim with technical details
  • Double-pocket camp shirts
  • Hiker styles
  • Hiking boots with tailoring
  • Kitten-heel knee boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Hybrid quilted bombers
  • Insulated hybrid hikers
  • Technical wool cargo trousers
  • Utility vest layers
  • Weatherproof derbies

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Belted field coats
  • Belted knee-high boots
  • Belted safari jackets
  • Belted shirt dresses

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Piped satin track jackets
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Cargo midi skirts

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
