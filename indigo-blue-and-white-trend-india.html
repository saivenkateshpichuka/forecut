Indigo, blue and white: the colour story of Indian fashion in 2026

Blue was the most-seen colour story at Lakmé Fashion Week 2026. How indigo denim, handloom and blue-and-white prints are being used, and how to bring blue into every category.

By Forecut Editorial · 11 October 2026 · 5 min read

If one colour defined the Indian runway in 2026, it was blue. Coverage of Lakmé Fashion Week x FDCI 2026 described indigo denim with crisp tailoring, blue-and-white eveningwear with fine surface work, and whole shows built around indigo.

Why blue, and why now

Blue is easy to wear across age groups and works for both festive and everyday wear. Indigo also links directly to India's dyeing heritage, so it sits naturally on handloom as well as on denim. That makes it one of the lowest-risk colour bets of the year.

Who showed it

  • Payal Pratap: indigo denim and structured tailoring.
  • AFEW by Rahul Mishra: blue-and-white eveningwear with surface work.
  • Saim: silk Chanderi paired with denim.

How to use it

Think of indigo as a category-wide colour rather than a single product. It works on kurtas and shirts, denim co-ords and jackets, printed sarees and even footwear. For festive, pair indigo with ivory and champagne gold; for a warmer palette, add rust.

Cost and sourcing

Indigo dyeing and blue prints are widely available in India, so cost risk is low. Use natural indigo for premium lines, where the story adds value, and synthetic indigo for value ranges.

What to do this season

  • Add indigo to every category: kurtas, denim, sarees and footwear.
  • Pair indigo with ivory and champagne gold for festive capsules.
  • Try one denim-meets-handloom fusion piece as a hero.
