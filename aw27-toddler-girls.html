Toddler girls

Kidswear follows adult direction a season or two later, adapted for comfort, safety and growth. Early outlook. SS27 signals carried into winter: waisted outerwear, handcraft on knits and quiet structure, with deep violet, plum and tannic bordeaux taking the place of black.

Safety notes

Check these before sampling.

  • For under-3s, avoid small parts: no beads, sequins or loose pom-poms. Use snaps, sewn-down appliqué and embroidery instead.
  • No drawstrings at the hood or neck; waist cords must not hang loose. Follow EN 14682 and your market's own rules.
  • Test snaps, buttons and trims for pull strength, and check flammability rules for nightwear, costumes and party wear.
  • Use flat seams and printed care labels where skin is sensitive; choose certified dyes and finishes.
