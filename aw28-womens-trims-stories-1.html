Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Cargo pockets

Utility cargos cooling in womenswear. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Denim with technical details

Denim jackets with zips and bellows pockets. A small detail that gives a core style a reason to be bought again.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Keep the detail in the same place across colourways for consistent imagery.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Double-pocket camp shirts

Camp-collar shirts with two chest pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Stand-collar field coats

Short stand collars on zip-front field coats. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
