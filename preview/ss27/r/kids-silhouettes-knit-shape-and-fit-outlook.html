SS27 Kids' Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Knit shapes for SS27 kidswear: oversized T-shirts, striped tees, coordinated jersey sets, terry tops, swimwear and soft pyjamas.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Kids
Category
Silhouettes
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The evidence

Cozmo showed striped T-shirts and coordinated sets forming a total look, for newborns to 14 years[1]; Piupiuchick's terry top was in MilK's Dressing Idéal, with pyjamas from Oda London[2]; Cadhan showed organic cotton pyjamas[1]. Oversized T-shirts are among Fatamadrina's SS27 silhouettes[3]. Sport influence was "presented through a more refined, creative lens" at Playtime Paris[4].

Shapes and fit

  • Oversized tee: dropped shoulder, longer body.
  • Striped tee: classic or boxy fit.
  • Jersey set: tee or sweatshirt with shorts.
  • Terry top and shorts.
  • Pyjamas: close, comfortable fit for sleep.
  • Swim: one-piece, two-piece, rash guard.

Timing

In stores February to August 2027.

Products and customers

  • Tees, sets, terry pieces, pyjamas, swimwear.

Design implications

  • In many markets, children's nightwear has its own flammability rules; check them before choosing loose-fitting pyjama shapes and fabrics.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Tees and sets are high-volume reorder lines.
