The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Performance ease

Designs that feel intuitive and move easily: articulated sleeves and knees, stretch and breathable fabrics for sport and commuting.

Why now

Early SS28 previews point to performance design that removes restriction.

Key pieces

Articulation, Stretch, Breathable; articulated sleeves.

Source: FashionUnited, "SS28 Preview: The modern flow of performance design". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Structured clarity

Consumers move away from constant novelty toward fewer, better pieces: clean column shapes, clear lines and minimal trims.

Why now

Early SS28 trade intelligence describes a demand for "better" fashion rather than more fashion.

Key pieces

Column shapes, Clean fronts, Minimal trims; clean column dresses.

Source: FashionUnited, "Spring Summer 2028 Trend Intelligence: The Rise of Structured Clarity". Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Natural tech

Natural fibres finished to perform: quick-dry linen, UV-resistant cotton and easy-care jersey.

Why now

Customers want natural fibres without the creasing and care trouble.

Key pieces

Quick-dry, Easy-care, Natural fibres; performance-jersey shirt dresses.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Skirts for men

Skirts worn as menswear on the runway; Cynthia Abila sent male models out in skirts at Lagos Fashion Week 2026 (29 Oct). A focused detail update for trousers.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Barrel trousers

Curved, tapered barrel legs. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.
  • Fit on at least two sizes before committing to bulk.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Corduroy wide trousers

Wide trousers in corduroy. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Choose wale size for the garment: fine wale for shirts, wider wale for trousers and jackets.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Cotton corduroy in 8–14 wale.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Double-knee work trousers

Reinforced knees on workwear trousers. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Drawcord linen trousers

Linen trousers with drawcord waists. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Use flat cord or elastic with branded tips for a premium look.
  • Pre-wash linen to control shrinkage and soften the handle.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.

Fabric and trims

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Skirts for men
  • Barrel trousers
  • Corduroy wide trousers
  • Double-knee work trousers
  • Drawcord linen trousers
  • Drawstring tailored trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Deep turn-ups
  • Full straight legs
  • Gurkha-buckle waists
  • Loop-free waistbands
  • Pleated Bermuda shorts
  • Relaxed pleated chinos

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Belted high waists
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Shorts with turn-ups
  • Single and double pleats
  • Utility cargo trousers

Actions

  • Add stretch to core tailoring fabrics.
  • Introduce articulated elbows on outerwear.
  • Test breathability claims before marketing them.
  • Edit ranges down and deepen buys on proven fits.
  • Upgrade fabrics on core styles.
  • Tell a "made to last" story.
