SS27 Men's Knit Shape and Fit Outlook

Knitwear goes light and textured: knitted polos and short-sleeve knit shirts, open-stitch summer knits, sun-faded cables and V-necks, cut closer to the body.

The evidence

Brunello Cucinelli showed rainbow cable-knit sweaters with a sun-faded finish[1]; Hypebeast pointed to the V-neck sweater in orange[2]. Première Vision's SS27 knit selection highlights flat, Derby and Richelieu ribs that are more elastic and elongated than jersey, and retro-sport stitches such as honeycomb piqué, diamond and wheat stitch[3]. Its overview adds cool wools and extra-fine merino[4]. Milan used knits within lighter, closer silhouettes[5].

Shapes and fit

  • Knitted polo and short-sleeve knit shirt: the summer shirt replacement; fit close at the shoulder, straight body.
  • V-neck sweater: close fit, worn alone or over a T-shirt.
  • Open-stitch summer knit: relaxed, for layering over shirts.
  • Cable crewneck in a light yarn: classic shape, sun-faded colour.
  • Rib tank: close, for layering under open shirts and jackets.

Timing

In stores February to July 2027. Knit polos and short-sleeve knit shirts are a long-running growth line; open-stitch knits are a summer idea.

Products and customers

  • Knitted polos, knit shirts, V-necks, open-stitch knits, light cables, rib tanks.

Design implications

  • Use 14 to 16 gauge for polos and knit shirts; check sheerness in light colours.
  • Retro-sport stitches (piqué, honeycomb) add texture without weight.

Commercial implications

  • Knit polos and knit shirts are reorder lines: buy core colours deep.
  • Keep open-stitch and statement cables to smaller buys.
