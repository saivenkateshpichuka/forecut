Footwear

SS27 shoe trends explained: square toes, bows, lace-ups and woven leather

The shoe ideas from the SS27 shows, how Forecut rates each one, and how to adapt them for buyers in Europe, the Americas, Asia, the Gulf, Africa and Oceania.

By Forecut Editorial · 5 November 2026 · 6 min read

The SS27 shows in New York, London, Milan and Paris closed in early October, and the footwear story is clear: shoes are getting more decorated, more grown-up and more tailored. Who What Wear summed up the season as "more opulent, maximalist, and, well, fun", with academic lace-ups beginning to crowd out the plain ballet flat. This guide checks each SS27 shoe idea against Forecut's lifecycle tracking and explains how to adapt it by region.

Eight ideas from the SS27 runways

Who What Wear named eight shoe trends from the season. Hypebae's round-up of favourite runway shoes, and Out's menswear report, add detail. Together they give this list:

  • Embellishment: crystal and floral appliqué and gemstones on heels and sandals, cited at Prada, Chanel, Carven and Dries Van Noten.
  • Square toes: square-toe pumps with a crisp, architectural line at Michael Kors, Altuzarra, Calvin Klein and Chanel.
  • Bows: on flats and heels at Miu Miu, Dior, Prada and Chanel.
  • Contrast cap toes: on pumps, slingbacks and flats at Chanel, Celine, Valentino and Max Mara.
  • Derbies, oxfords and brogues: menswear lace-ups for women at Altuzarra, Celine, Ralph Lauren, Bottega Veneta and Erdem.
  • Exotic-effect finishes: croc, snake, lizard and ostrich textures at Tory Burch, Ferragamo, Gucci and Dries Van Noten.
  • Metallics: silver, gold and iridescent finishes at Saint Laurent, Chanel and Chloé.
  • Woven leather: woven shoes at Bottega Veneta, Calvin Klein and Tory Burch.

Hypebae adds sculptural heels, such as Prada's curved heel on an open-toe sandal, and Mary Janes, which Marni showed in 1970s earth tones.

How Forecut rates each one

A runway idea is not the same as a buying decision. Here is where each direction sits in Forecut's SS27 footwear data, which records whether a detail is emerging, rising, peaking or fading:

  • Square toes and square-toe ankle boots are rising, and we expect square toes to peak in AW27. This is a safe core shape. Note that square-toe ballet flats are already peaking, so put the newness into pumps rather than flats.
  • Two-tone cap toes and cap-toe kitten slingbacks are rising. A contrast toe updates an existing last without new tooling.
  • Brogue perforations, plain derbies, wingtip brogues and lace-up court shoes are all rising. The lace-up is the strongest story for women's flats.
  • Bow-trim flats are emerging in SS27 and rise from there. Test now, build depth later.
  • Embellished flats are rising, while brooch-and-chain stilettos are only emerging. Keep heavy embellishment to occasion edits.
  • Woven leather mules and woven leather slides are rising, and woven leather flats are emerging.
  • Curved sculptural heels and Mary Jane flats are rising; sculpted wedge heels are emerging.
  • Ballet flats are peaking in SS27 and turn to fading in AW27. Keep them as a carry-over, not a growth line.

Menswear: open toes and the men's ballet flat

Out called open-toed sandals "the top trend of spring/summer 2027" for men, listing flip-flops, T-straps, slides, huaraches, mules, espadrilles and gladiators. It singled out very thin thong sandals at Celine and ankle-length gladiators at Yohji Yamamoto. It also reported men's ballet flats, including multi-strap leather pairs at Dries Van Noten. Forecut tracks skinny thong sandals, fisherman sandals and leather slides as rising for SS27, and multi-strap ballet flats for men as emerging.

Adapting the trends by region

The same runway shoe sells differently around the world. Forecut's regional data points to these adjustments:

  • Europe: France, Italy and the UK favour tailoring and quality materials, so lace-ups, cap toes and square-toe pumps fit best. Southern Europe's sandal and craft market suits woven leather.
  • North America: the US prefers wearable versions of runway ideas, sneakers and size range. Offer square toes and cap toes on comfortable block or kitten heels and in wide fittings.
  • Asia: China adopts runway ideas fast, so bows and embellishment can work early. Japan and South Korea favour precise design and quality basics, where a well-made derby or cap-toe flat fits. Southeast Asia's heat favours open and woven styles.
  • Middle East (GCC): modest luxury and eveningwear make embellished, metallic and bow-trimmed heels and flats the strongest fit for the October–March season and Ramadan 2027.
  • Africa and South America: West African occasion dressing suits statement embellishment and metallics, while Brazil's colour-led market can take brighter versions of the season's shapes.
  • Oceania and the southern hemisphere: SS27 reaches stores from September 2027, a full season after Europe, so buyers there can watch northern sell-through before committing.

What to do now

  • Brands: build the core around square toes, cap toes and lace-ups; add bows and embellishment as smaller test buys.
  • Manufacturers: prepare square-toe and cap-toe lasts, perforation and broguing capacity, and woven-leather sampling. Exotic-effect looks can be made with embossed leathers rather than real exotic skins.
  • Everyone: plan the ballet flat as a fading carry-over after SS27.
