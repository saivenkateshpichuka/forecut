The palette

Lilac and violet lead, with true red and Klein blue for statement pieces and butter for softness.

SeasonalLilacPantone 15-3720 TCX Lilac Breeze#B3A0C9Soft purple for dresses, knits and relaxed workwear.
SeasonalVioletPantone 19-3642 TCX Royal Purple#603F83Saturated purple for statement pieces, leather and evening.
SeasonalTrue redPantone 18-1763 TCX High Risk Red#C71F2DWorn head to toe, not just as a pop.
AccentKlein bluePantone 18-3949 TCX Dazzling Blue#3850A0Intense ultramarine for single statement pieces.
SeasonalButterPantone 12-0711 TCX Lemon Meringue#F6E199Soft yellow for knits, shirting and menswear polos.
CoreOptic whitePantone 11-0602 TCX Snow White#F2F0EBCrisp white for shirting, linen and tailoring; the biggest riser on the runways.
