How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The story

Future Snoops' "After Dusk" brings deep aubergines, inky blues and murky greens to warm-weather dressing, as extreme heat moves socialising into the evening . Lace, corsetry and dramatic silhouettes return in lighter materials .

Colours

Inky blue and magnetic violet with acid lemon or peach as the flash.

Silhouettes

  • Slip and corset-line dresses in light fabrics.
  • Sheer layers over dark bases.

Fabrics

Satin, lace, voile, open knits.

Trims and details

Lace insets, fine straps, satin piping.

Make

Products and customers

  • Evening dresses, sheer tops, satin skirts.
