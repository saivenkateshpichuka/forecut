The avatar meshes in this folder are built from the MakeHuman base mesh, body targets (macro and measurement targets)
and the game-engine skeleton with its skin weights (https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman and MPFB 2),
all released under CC0 1.0 Universal. The MPFB 2 copies used are the ones shipped inside Anny (github.com/naver/anny,
Apache 2.0); no SMPL or other non-commercial data is used.
Built by build/avatar_build.py; fitted to measurements and posed in the browser by tools/drape-sim.js.
