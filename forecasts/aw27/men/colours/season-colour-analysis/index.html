How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Neutrals shift: grey toward greyish blue, khaki toward taupe, green or copper, navy toward turquoise or purplish tones[1]
  • At Première Vision, deep violet, plums, dusty pinks, mauves and tinted off-blacks set the tone; violet is "centre stage", and plum and deep red become alternatives to black[1]
  • Van den Hoek's menswear colour: sand, beige, brown and cognac, army to bright greens, grey as a base, and one bold accent[2]
  • Forecut's AW27 colour forecast: violet, plum and tannic bordeaux replace black over tinted neutrals[3]

Colour groups

  • Core: tinted black, greyed blue, copper khaki, mushroom.
  • Seasonal: dusky violet, tannic bordeaux.
  • Accent: sage, and one bold accent on a subdued base.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip tinted black against true black.

Commercial implications

  • Move part of the black and navy buy to tinted black and greyed blue.
