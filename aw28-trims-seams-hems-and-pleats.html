Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Articulated-knee trousers

Shaped knees for movement. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Bonded, raw-cut seams

Bonded panels with taped or raw-cut edges. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Stop fraying with a stitch line set in from the edge so it stays controlled after washing.
  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corset-seamed dresses

Visible corset-style seams on day dresses. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Cuffed jean hems

Turned-up hems on straight jeans. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Raw-hem jeans

Cut, unfinished hems. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Stop fraying with a stitch line set in from the edge so it stays controlled after washing.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Satin side-stripe trousers

Evening trousers with satin stripes. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Cut with care; pile and sheen change with direction, so lay all pieces one way.
  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.

Fabric and trims

Viscose velvet, satin-back crepe or washable silk-feel polyester.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Cuffed jean hems
  • Raw-hem jeans
  • Satin side-stripe trousers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded denim outerwear

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Scale-beaded mermaid hems
  • Bubble-hem dresses
  • Cuffed wide trousers
  • Pleated midi dresses

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Bubble hems
  • Deep turn-ups
  • Double front pleats, sharp creases
  • Moody tiered ruffle maxis
  • Pleated tartan minis

Actions

  • Carry SS28 sole units into boots.
  • Add weatherproofing for monsoon and winter markets.
  • Keep uppers sleek.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
