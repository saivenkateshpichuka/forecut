Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Trousers, rising

Tailored wool shorts

Pleated shorts in wool. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.
  • Check length on petite and tall fits.

Fabric and trims

Cotton twill, wool-blend flannel, ripstop or denim, with corozo or metal shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on chinos, pleated trousers, cargo pants and jeans. Works in menswear trousers as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Colour-block knits

Knits pieced in contrasting colours. Panelling allows colour and fabric blocking from existing materials.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Use panels to block colour from existing fabrics.
  • Check shade matching across panels in bulk.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, peaking

Jewel-tone cashmere

Fine knits in aubergine, mustard and emerald. A colour-led detail that is easy to roll across many styles.

Design notes

  • Lab-dip early and approve under daylight and store lighting.
  • Roll the colour across tops, bottoms and accessories.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, peaking

Leather and wool varsity

Varsity jackets with rib trims. This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Use leather or a high-quality alternative; keep seams minimal to show off the surface.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Fabric and trims

Lamb or cow nappa, split suede, or bio-based and recycled leather alternatives.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
