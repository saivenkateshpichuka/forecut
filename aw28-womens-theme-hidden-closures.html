The story

AW28 Women's Themes.

Magnetic and concealed fastenings give clean, uninterrupted fronts on coats, shirts and blazers.

Why now

Supports the minimal, structured look and is easier to fasten.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Hidden magnetic closures
  • Hidden-zip blouses
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Hidden-rivet minimal jeans
  • Denim with technical details
  • Half-zip knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Funnel neck with concealed zip
  • Concealed-placket shirts
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets
  • Fine-gauge zip polos
  • Hidden-placket overcoats

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Detachable hoods

Actions

  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.
