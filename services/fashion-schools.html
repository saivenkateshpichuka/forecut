Questions

Straight answers.

How does the school access code work?

You email hello@forecut.in with the school name and batch size. We set up a code that students enter when they sign up.

Can students use the work in portfolios?

Students can use their own designs and exported tech packs. Forecut forecast text and images belong to Forecut, so credit them when you show them.

Is there a cost?

Write to us with your batch size. We will tell you what applies for your school.
