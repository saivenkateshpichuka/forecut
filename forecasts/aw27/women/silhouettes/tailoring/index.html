The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Armour-structured vests

Structured tailored vests with pronounced shoulders and a protective, armour-like shape over fluid dresses; De Goeye 10th-anniversary show, Brazil (29 Oct 2026). A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Adjust the back with a strap and buckle for fit.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Elongated evening jumpsuits

Long, lean jumpsuits in black with glossy or metallic finishes and gold embroidery, as in Namrata Joshipura's OBSIDIAN, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). This is a garment-level idea that can anchor a capsule.

Design notes

  • Allow for less give in coated fabrics; adjust ease and avoid tight seams.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Start with a small capsule and track sell-through.

Fabric and trims

Polyurethane-coated cotton or recycled polyester with a glossy finish.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Check tailoring

Prince of Wales and windowpane checks. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Match checks at centre front, pockets and side seams; plan for the extra fabric.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Yarn-dyed wool or brushed cotton checks; matching adds about 10–15% fabric.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Cinched one-button jackets

Nipped waists with a single button. Waist treatment controls the silhouette and how the garment flatters.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Mark waist position on the block and check on several body shapes.
  • Offer adjustable or elastic options for size inclusivity.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless double-breasted jackets

Double-breasted fronts without lapels. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Where to use it

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Collarless tailored jackets

Jackets with clean V-necklines and no lapels. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Balance lapel width with shoulder width and garment length.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Armour-structured vests
  • Elongated evening jumpsuits
  • Check tailoring
  • Cinched one-button jackets
  • Collarless double-breasted jackets
  • Collarless tailored jackets

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted suit jackets
  • Soft unstructured tailoring
  • Stretch commuter suiting

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Black tailoring with fuchsia accents
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Belted trench blazers
  • Bermuda suit shorts
  • Cropped blazers
  • Cummerbunds over suits

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Sharp pointed shoulders

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
