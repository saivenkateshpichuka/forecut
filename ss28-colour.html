Overview

Long-range forecast from early fair previews.

Blues come back strong, royal and a new turquoise, beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge of inky blue and murky green.

Core

The base of the range: carry these across most styles.

Bleached linen, Straw, Inky blue, Khaki green

Seasonal

What makes the season new: give these real depth.

Royal blue, Sonar turquoise, Greyish mauve

Accent

Small, high-impact doses: one piece, one trim.

Magnetic violet, Peach, Acid lemon

Colour stories

How the colours combine, story by story.

Blue hour

Royal blue, sonar turquoise and inky blue: summer blues with real pigment.

Use for: Shirting, denim, swim, tailoring

Royal blue, Sonar turquoise, Inky blue, Bleached linen

Atmosphere

Greyish mauve and magnetic violet, soft and hazy against bleached linen.

Use for: Dresses, knits, evening

Greyish mauve, Magnetic violet, Bleached linen, Peach

Vegetal

Straw, khaki green and warm beige for natural fibres and crafted surfaces.

Use for: Linen, crochet, menswear, accessories

Straw, Khaki green, Bleached linen, Warm beige

After dusk

Summer goes dark: inky blue and murky green with an acid lemon spark.

Use for: Evening, statement pieces, footwear

Inky blue, Murky green, Acid lemon, Magnetic violet

How we built it

Forecut's own reading of public evidence.

A long-range Forecut forecast from the first SS28 previews: Première Vision's October 2026 colour preview (royal blue, deeper sky blue, a new turquoise, greyish mauve and a dense violet), fabric-fair womenswear previews (straw, khaki green, warm beige, peach) and a darker after-dusk summer mood. It will be updated after the February 2027 fairs.
