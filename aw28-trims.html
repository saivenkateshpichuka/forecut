Overview

Long-range outlook. Functional tailoring and technical wool for winter, hidden closures and hybrid boots, with radiant earth deepening into rust and brick.

The headline

A season of development: most pieces carry on from last season and grow.

Build into the range

Articulated-knee trousers, Bonded, raw-cut seams, Bow-trim flats and Cargo pockets are rising.

Test in small quantities

Bonded denim outerwear, Knitted hoods and snoods, Technical wool cargo trousers and Utility vest layers are new ideas to try.

Sell through

Angrakha-overlap tunics, Ankle-strap balloon trousers and Bead-droplet embroidered dresses are at their height. Do not add depth.

Reduce or rework

Beaded-fringe canvas dresses, Fringed minis with back trains and Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses are moving out.

Inspiration

The stories behind this direction and where they were seen.

Functional tailoring

Tailoring that works hard: belted jackets, useful pockets, clean seams and cargo details in wool.

Continues SS28 performance thinking into winter suiting.

Strategy

How to plan the range from this forecast.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers
  • Bonded, raw-cut seams
  • Bow-trim flats
  • Cargo pockets
  • Concealed-button blazers
  • Contrast-collar polos

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Bonded denim outerwear
  • Knitted hoods and snoods
  • Technical wool cargo trousers
  • Utility vest layers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Angrakha-overlap tunics
  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Braided officer coats
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Fringed minis with back trains
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Beads and sequins on knits

Actions for the range

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Test magnets for washing and safety.
  • Use fly-front plackets on core shirts.
  • Market ease of fastening.

Markets

How each region reads AW28, and the pieces from this report that fit it best.

Asia

From temperate four-season East Asia to tropical Southeast Asia and India, and the hot, dry Gulf. One collection rarely fits every market: weight, sleeve length and coverage change by country.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Funnel-neck knit dresses, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Europe

Temperate four-season climate: cold, wet winters in the north, hot dry summers around the Mediterranean.

Best fit: Welt-pocket cocoon coats, Stand-collar field coats, Hidden-placket overcoats, Lacquered funnel-neck trenches

North America

From cold Canadian winters to hot, humid southern US summers and Mexico's warm highlands and coasts.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Belted safari jackets, Floral-lined showerproof jackets, Drawcord-hem cropped fleece jackets

South America

Tropical in the north and Brazil's coast; temperate and four-season in Argentina, Chile and southern Brazil. Seasons are opposite to Europe.

Best fit: Denim with technical details, Bonded denim outerwear, Patch-pocket denim jackets, Printed resort shirts

Africa

Mostly hot: tropical West and Central Africa, highland East Africa, Mediterranean north, and a temperate, southern-hemisphere South Africa.

Best fit: Floral-embroidered cropped puffers, Bead-droplet embroidered dresses, Band-collar shirts with long placket, Concealed-placket shirts

Oceania

Southern-hemisphere seasons: summer Dec–Feb, winter Jun–Aug. Mostly temperate, tropical in the north of Australia.

Best fit: Utility vest layers, Utility shirt-jackets, Denim with technical details, Bonded denim outerwear
