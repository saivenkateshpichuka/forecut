SS27 / Couture and craft / Boys

SS27 Boys' Couture Materials and Details

Premium materials for boyswear: certified organic cotton, fine linen, soft leather sandals and recycled performance fabrics.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Boys
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Pitti Bimbo 103, Florence, 24-25 June 2026; Playtime Paris, 28-30 June 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Premium and mid-market childrenswear, Europe-led fairs, global retail

The evidence

  • Collections increasingly use recycled fibres, organic cotton and certified materials[1]
  • Pisamonas showed strap ballerinas, leather sandals, barefoot models and first-summer shoes for babies[2]
  • Gray Label keeps to a minimalist range with no patterns except stripes, in "exceptionally soft" pieces made in Portugal[3]

Materials

  • Organic cotton jersey.
  • Linen and linen-cotton shirting.
  • Leather sandals.
  • Recycled swim and track fabrics.

Design implications

  • Fewer, better materials.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Premium on certified and leather pieces.

Evidence and sources

  2. Slow living: run, dive, play, the summer wardrobe (Pitti Bimbo 103 exhibitors), Pitti Immagine, 29 May 2026
  3. Pitti Bimbo in Florence proves the merits of an intimate format, FashionUnited, 26 June 2026
