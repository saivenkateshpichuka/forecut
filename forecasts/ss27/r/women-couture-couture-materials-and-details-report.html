SS27 Women's Couture Materials and Details

Couture materials moved beyond silk and lace in 2026: lab-grown silk, silicone, latex, glass beads and feathers. The details that translate are feathers, satin macramé, corsetry and pearl beading.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Women
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Shows 10 September to 6 October 2026; Haute Couture, July 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis October to November 2026
Market
Global: New York, London, Milan, Paris and Copenhagen runways; premium and mid-market womenswear

The evidence

  • Lab-grown silk: Balenciaga used AMSilk silk, which the house describes as "stronger than steel"[1].
  • Silicone, latex, clay and paint: Schiaparelli used poured silicone, sculpted clay, latex and paint[2], and techniques from a workshop that makes lifelike silicone infants for films[1].
  • Feathers: goose and ostrich feathers at Balenciaga[2], ostrich feathers at Stéphane Rolland[1].
  • Satin macramé, agate and diamonds: Stéphane Rolland[1].
  • Beading: a prom dress beaded in putty-pink pearls at Schiaparelli and hand-blown glass beads on tulle at Iris van Herpen[1].
  • Velvet: velvet roses at Zuhair Murad and velvet corseted gowns at Elie Saab[1].

Details that translate

Couture detail Ready-to-wear translation Price tier
Feathers Feather trim at a hem or cuff Premium
Satin macramé Macramé belts and straps Mid to premium
Pearl beading Pearl buttons and beaded necklines Mid to premium
Corsetry Corset seaming and rubberised meshes[3] instead of boning Mid
Moulded and coated surfaces Coated lace and pearlescent satins[3] Mid

Design implications

  • Source feathers and beads from certified, traceable suppliers and state care clearly.
  • Test coated and moulded materials for cracking and cleaning before committing.

Commercial implications

  • Keep exotic materials to a few showpieces; translate the look into accessible trims for volume.
  • Lab-grown and bio-based materials carry a sustainability story: check claims before using them.
