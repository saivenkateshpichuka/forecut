SS27 Kids' Trim Market Strategy

In a slowing childrenswear market, use trims to add value to fewer, longer-lasting pieces; avoid fragile decoration that shortens a garment's life.

Spring/Summer 2027
Audience

Europe-led kidswear trade fairs; premium and mid-market childrenswear, newborn to teen

The market context

In Italy, childrenswear exports fell 9.3% and domestic sales 2.6% in 2025, according to Confindustria Moda data reported by Fortune Italia; families are focused on sustainability and well-being, and the sector is moving toward functionality, material quality and durability[1]. Fashion overall is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027[2].

Build, test, sell through

Action Trims
Build Embroidery, appliqué, sailor collars and tape
Test Shell and bead decoration (older kids only), sparkle for party
Sell through Fragile glued decoration

Design implications

  • Choose trims that survive repeated washing and hand-me-downs.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist of young children's clothing, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, beads, shells and other decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Durable trims support resale and hand-me-down value, which parents increasingly consider.
