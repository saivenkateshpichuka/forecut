AW27 Women's Colour Stories

Four AW27 women's colour stories: Painted by Time, New Neutrals, Pink for All and Living Green.

How this forecast was built

Pre-runway forecast. The AW27 shows run from January to March 2027. This report is built from the 2026 yarn, fabric and leather fairs (Pitti Filati in June; Première Vision, Lineapelle and Milano Unica in September), published forecaster views and the SS27 runways. Forecut will update it after the shows.

The evidence

  • Première Vision's colour preview opens with "grayed mid-tones, as if patinated by time", with purples "taking center stage"[1]
  • Neutrals shift: grey toward greyish blue, khaki toward taupe, green or copper, navy toward turquoise or purplish tones[2]
  • Pink and mauve are becoming gender-neutral, reaching menswear and even junior sweatshirts[2]
  • Première Vision's AW27 theme "Living Matter" puts materials "at the center", with materials "infused with memory"[3]

Painted by Time

Deep violet, tannic bordeaux, waxy ochre and tinted black, softened as if by old varnish, for velvet and patinated leather.

New Neutrals

Tinted black, mushroom, greyed blue and copper khaki: neutrals with colour inside.

Pink for All

Dusty mauve and silicone pink in tailoring and knits.

Living Green

Sage and moss with copper khaki for natural fibres.

Timing

In stores August 2027 to January 2028.

Design implications

  • One story per delivery.

Commercial implications

  • New Neutrals carries volume.
