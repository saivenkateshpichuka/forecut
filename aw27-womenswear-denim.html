Denim

The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Barrel-leg cargo jeans

Curved legs with cargo pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Shape the volume through curved side seams and darts; check on several body types.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Black and grey denim

Washed black and grey denim. Wash and finish decide how fresh the denim looks on shelf.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Approve wash standards on bulk fabric, not only on samples.
  • Choose lower-impact wash methods such as laser and ozone.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Button-fly denim shorts

Short jeans with exposed button flies. Length shifts are simple to apply across existing blocks.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Update length on existing blocks rather than new patterns.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Carpenter jeans

Hammer loops and utility pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Clean wide-leg trouser jeans

Wide jeans cut like trousers, with creases. This is a silhouette change, so it needs new blocks and fit sessions.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Use a fabric with enough weight and drape so the leg falls cleanly.
  • Develop the new block on a core style first, then roll out.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Cuffed jean hems

Turned-up hems on straight jeans. The hem finish changes the mood of a piece with minimal pattern work.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Test hem finish after washing for twisting or fraying.
  • Keep hem treatment consistent within a capsule.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Barrel-leg cargo jeans
  • Black and grey denim
  • Button-fly denim shorts
  • Carpenter jeans
  • Clean wide-leg trouser jeans
  • Cuffed jean hems

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Raw selvedge denim
  • Stretch performance denim

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Low-rise bootcut jeans
  • Barrel leg with curved outseam
  • Denim waistcoats
  • Embroidered denim
  • Mid-rise relaxed jeans
  • Pressed front seam

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long denim coats
  • Long exposed button fly
  • Thick contrast topstitching

Actions

  • Invest in fabric quality over trims.
  • Build a capsule of five clean core pieces.
  • Use concealed plackets to update shirts.
  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
