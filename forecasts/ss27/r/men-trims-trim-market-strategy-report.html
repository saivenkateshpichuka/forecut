SS27 Men's Trim Market Strategy

In a low-growth 2027, use piping, embroidery and pocket details to update core menswear; keep hand-applied studs and beading for premium pieces.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Trims
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The market context

Fashion is expected to remain in low single-digit growth in 2027, with high freight, energy and financing costs, and markdown risk from excess stock[1]. In the EU, large companies may not destroy certain unsold apparel since 19 July 2026[1].

Build, test, sell through

Action Trims
Build Contrast piping, flat pockets, embroidered motifs
Test Studs and stones[2], bird embroidery[2], beading
Sell through Heavy distressing and logo hardware

By market

  • India and the Gulf: embroidery and piping suit festive and resort dressing; India's GDP grew 7.8% year on year in the second quarter of 2026[1].
  • Europe: keep trims modular so unsold stock can be reworked.
  • United States: use trims to differentiate core shirts at full price.

Design implications

  • Design trims that can be added late (embroidery, piping) to respond to sales.

Commercial implications

  • Track sell-through by trim variant and chase winners.

Evidence and sources
