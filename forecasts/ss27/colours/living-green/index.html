The story

Grass green and teal from nature and the pool: summer knits, swim and prints.

Colour comes back in full: purple and lilac, head-to-toe red and Klein blue over sun-faded whites, sand and tobacco.

Palette

In order of how much to use.

AccentGrass greenPantone 16-0237 TCX Foliage#75A14FA living green for knits, prints and swim.
TealPantone 17-5117 TCX Green Blue Slate#358082
CoreSandPantone 13-1009 TCX Biscotti#DAC7ABSun-faded neutral for linen suits, knits and outerwear.
SeasonalButterPantone 12-0711 TCX Lemon Meringue#F6E199Soft yellow for knits, shirting and menswear polos.

How to use it

Use for: Swim, knits, prints, kidswear.

Proportions

Lead with grass green as the main colour, teal as the second, and keep butter to trims, linings or one piece.

Departments

Womenswear, Menswear, Kidswear carry colours from this story.
