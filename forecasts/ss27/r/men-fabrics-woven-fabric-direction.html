SS27 Men's Woven Fabric Direction

Sheer shirting, satin, seersucker, soft leather and light tailoring wools lead SS27 menswear fabrics, with crease-proof stretch for travel.

EmergingRisingPeakingFading
Season
Spring Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Published
2026-11-25
When it lands

In stores February to July 2027. Soft leather and seersucker continue into SS28 (Forecut assessment).

On this pageThe evidenceFabric by fabricWhat to do
Research details

Research period Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026

Markets Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Sheer shirting: translucent fabrics from subtle to full transparency, at Kiko Kostadinov, Feng Chen Wang, Dior, Prada, Dries Van Noten, Jacquemus and Saint Laurent.
  • Satin: synthetic and silk satin, floral and white, with a billowing drape, at Willy Chavarria, Celine, GmbH and AMI.
  • Silk and gauze tailoring: fluid, almost sheer suits at Dior.
  • Seersucker: layered seersucker suiting at Thom Browne.
  • Leather: camel at Dior, beige suits at Saint Laurent, brown leather Bermudas at Sonia Carrasco and leather tracksuits at Givenchy; leather is becoming "more malleable", with "flexibility, fluidity, lightness, almost like butter", according to Alice Feillard of Galeries Lafayette. Milan used woven and perforated leather for breathability.
  • Tailoring cloth: fine, lightweight wools blended with viscose, silk or linen; heavier double crepes with a dry, cool hand.
  • Travel fabrics: crease-proof stretch wool and technical materials at Herno.

Make

Products and customers

  • Sheer shirts, satin shirts and sets, seersucker suits, leather blousons and shorts, linen and travel suits.
Design

Design implications

  • Sheer shirts need French seams and clean facings.
  • Test leather for colour fastness and perforation strength.
  • Choose crease-recovery finishes for travel suits.
Buy

Commercial implications

  • Leather at premium; seersucker and linen at mid; satin across tiers depending on fibre.
  • State fibre content clearly on satins.
