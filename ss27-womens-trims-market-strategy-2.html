By market

Regional direction for the season, with the trims that fit each market.

Asia

  • Heritage textiles and motifs (brocade, kesi, handloom) on modern silhouettes
  • Sheer and translucent layers worn over opaque bases for modesty and heat
  • Tailoring with technical and utility details, cropped jackets and windbreakers

Fits: Sheer lattice capes, Woven-stripe longline overlayers, Lace-trimmed slips over knits

Europe

  • Featherlight, see-through fabrics: organza, voile, ripstop
  • The waist returns: peplums, basques, defined jackets
  • Skirts as the key piece, often at "the Chanel length" below the knee

Fits: Patent trench with storm flaps, Raglan sleeve coats, Welt-pocket cocoon coats

North America

  • Sheer skirts and overlays on opaque bases: the wearable version
  • Primary colours with cobalt blue; marigold as the breakout shade
  • Knits and shirts tied low at the waist

Fits: Cinched peplum jackets, Collarless cropped jackets, Lace-trimmed jacket edges

South America

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Featherlight and sheer fabrics translated into beach and resort wear
  • Bold colour: Cobalt blue, marigold, berry reds

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Floral print midis

Africa

  • Cinched waists and volume: puff, bubble and peplum shapes
  • Bold colour and print; chocolate brown as a neutral
  • Craft and heritage textiles in contemporary tailoring

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Photographic and art prints, Drop-shoulder shirt dresses

Oceania

  • International SS27 trends reach local stores for spring/summer, Sep 2027–Feb 2028
  • Sheer layers and silk organza (Resort 27 confirms it)
  • Surf-inflected tailoring, wraps and sarong ties

Fits: Seersucker and linen jackets, Sheer lattice capes, Sheer organza overlays

Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, peaking

Cargo midi skirts

Utility patch pockets on midi skirts. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Round or square patch pockets should be topstitched evenly and reinforced at the corners.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Knitwear, peaking

Chunky rib turtlenecks

Deep ribbed roll necks. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on crew-neck jumpers, cardigans, knit polos and vests. Works in womenswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, peaking

Contrast collars and cuffs

White collars and cuffs on coloured shirting. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton poplin, viscose twill, georgette or cotton jersey, with shell or resin buttons.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, peaking

Contrast-piped edges

Fine piping outlining fronts and collars. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
