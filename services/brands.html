Questions

Straight answers.

Do you sell sales data?

No. Forecut reads runways, retail and fabric fairs and turns them into direction. We do not publish retailer sales figures, and we do not claim to know any brand's sales.

Can my whole team use it?

Each person signs up with their own account. If your team is larger, write to hello@forecut.in and we will arrange access.

How do I know how reliable a forecast is?

Each trend carries a stage and each season is rechecked after the fairs. Our track record page lists what we called and what happened.
