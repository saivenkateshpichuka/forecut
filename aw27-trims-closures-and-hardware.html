The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Waisted outerwear

The defined waist moves into winter outerwear: belted and double-belted coats, wrap fronts and cinched jackets.

Why now

SS27 brought the waist back; outerwear is the natural next step for autumn.

Key pieces

Belted coats, Double belts, Wrap fronts; double-belted waists.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Winter handcraft

Craft continues into the cold season: smocking on knit cuffs and hems, tonal embroidery on wool and crochet textures.

Why now

Handcraft from SS27 carries on, adapted to heavier fabrics.

Key pieces

Smocked knits, Tonal embroidery, Crochet; smocked knit details.

Source: Runway shows for this season have not happened yet. Forecut will add the brands showing this theme once collections are presented.

Quiet structure

Clean lines: pleated wool trousers, funnel necks, concealed closures and straight shapes without decoration.

Why now

A calmer reaction to heavy embellishment, and pieces that last more than one season.

Key pieces

Pleats, Funnel necks, Clean fronts; high-waisted pleated trousers.

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Angrakha-overlap tunics

Tunics with an overlapping angrakha-style front worn under a bandhgala, as at Tasva by Tarun Tahiliani, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026); a festive idea for India and the Gulf. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.
  • Keep the closure consistent across a capsule for a clear story.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Convertible backpack puffers

Oversized reversible puffers with underarm slits that fold and strap into a backpack; Melitta Baumeister x Nike, New York, 5 Nov 2026. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Grade extra ease carefully so smaller sizes do not swamp.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Asymmetric wrap closures

Off-centre wrap fronts held with a tie. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Coats & Jackets, rising

Belted wrap fronts

Self-fabric tie belts replacing buttons. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Add a hidden inner tie or snap so the wrap stays closed when worn.
  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend melton, double-face wool, cotton gabardine or recycled-nylon shells, with horn-effect or matte metal trims. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wool overcoats, cropped jackets, trenches and quilted puffers. Works in womenswear coats and jackets as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Boots, rising

Buckled ankle boots

Ankle boots with a buckled strap. Trims carry a lot of perceived value for a small cost increase.

Design notes

  • Size buckles to the strap width and test for durability.
  • Specify hardware finish and test for corrosion and nickel content.
  • Order trims early; hardware often sets the critical path.

Fabric and trims

Full-grain or suede leather, elastic gussets or YKK zips, rubber commando or leather soles.

Where to use it

Best on ankle boots, knee-highs, Chelseas and lace-up boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Trousers & Skirts, rising

Button-front midi skirts

Buttons running down the front of the skirt. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Use engineered mesh or knit for breathability.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Wool-blend suiting, cotton twill, crepe or satin, with hook-and-bar closures and concealed zips. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on wide-leg trousers, tailored pants, midi and mini skirts. Works in womenswear trousers and skirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Angrakha-overlap tunics
  • Convertible backpack puffers
  • Asymmetric wrap closures
  • Belted wrap fronts
  • Buckled ankle boots
  • Button-front midi skirts

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Belted knee-high boots

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Brooch-and-chain stilettos
  • Brooch-fastened cardigans
  • Buckle side adjusters
  • Double-breasted jackets
  • Double-breasted two-piece
  • Horsebit loafers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Long exposed button fly

Actions

  • Add a self-belt to your core coat.
  • Test a double-belt version in one colour.
  • Carry SS27 peplum jackets into wool.
  • Move SS27 smocking details onto knit cuffs.
  • Use tonal embroidery to keep it subtle.
  • Offer mohair blends at mid price.
