Pantone TCX (cotton) codes are the nearest match to the screen colour; confirm against a physical Pantone book and lab dips before production.

Why? Blue is easy to wear across age groups and works for both festive and everyday. Indigo also links to India's dyeing heritage, so it suits handloom and denim alike.

What? Blue ran through Lakmé Fashion Week 2026: indigo denim and crisp tailoring, blue-and-white evening gowns with fine surface work, and whole shows built on indigo.

Who's showing it?

  • Payal Pratap: Indigo denim and structured tailoring
  • AFEW by Rahul Mishra: Blue-and-white eveningwear with surface work
  • Saim: Silk Chanderi paired with denim

Cost to make: Low. Indigo dyeing and prints are widely available. Use natural indigo for premium and synthetic indigo for value.

How to build the capsule

  • Add indigo to every category: kurtas, denim, sarees and footwear.
  • Pair indigo with ivory and champagne gold for festive.
  • Try one denim-meets-handloom fusion piece.
