Design notes

How to use the trims.

Womenswear, Denim, rising

Embroidered denim

Floral embroidery on denim. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Approve wash and finish on bulk fabric; set clear shade bands.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

11–14 oz cotton or cotton-lyocell denim, with copper or antique-brass rivets and shank buttons.

Where to use it

Best on jeans, denim jackets, skirts and shirts. Works in womenswear denim as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Gilded embroidered shirting

Metallic embroidery on crisp poplin. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Lace yoke insets

Lace panels inset at yoke or sleeve. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use the yoke to add shape and structure.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tops & Shirts, rising

Lace-cami layering tops

Lace-edged camisoles for layering. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.
  • Order trims early to avoid delays.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on blouses, poplin shirts, jersey tops and going-out tops. Works in womenswear tops and shirts as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.
