Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Heritage rugby polos
  • Relaxed pleated chinos
  • Striped and repp ties
  • Belgian loafers
  • Black oxfords

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Boat shoes
  • Contrast-collar polos
  • Scarf-tied waistbands
  • Single-pleat suit trousers
  • Slim tassel loafers
  • Unlined soft loafers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Striped rugby shirts
  • Cap-toe oxfords
  • Oxford button-downs
  • Penny loafers
  • Textured knit polos
  • Wool flannel trousers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage
