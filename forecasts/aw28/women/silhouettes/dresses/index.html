Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corset-seamed dresses

Visible corset-style seams on day dresses. Seam placement adds structure and visual lines without extra trims.

Design notes

  • Use topstitching to make seam lines visible.
  • Check seam strength at stress points.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Funnel-neck knit dresses

Ribbed knit dresses with a high funnel neck. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use a rib with good recovery so it holds its shape.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.

Fabric and trims

Cotton-elastane or merino rib.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Halter-neck dresses

Necklines tied at the back of the neck. Changing the neckline is one of the cheapest ways to update a proven block.

Design notes

  • Add silicone tape or internal stays so the garment stays in place.
  • Check neck opening against head circumference on pull-on styles.
  • Stay-stitch or tape the neckline so it does not stretch out.

Fabric and trims

Viscose crepe, cotton poplin, satin-back crepe or jersey, with self-covered buttons and invisible zips.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Performance-jersey shirt dresses

Shirt dresses in stretch, easy-care jersey. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Quilted and padded dresses

Quilted dresses for cold-weather layering. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Plan channel width with the silhouette: wider channels look bolder, narrow channels look more refined.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.
  • Lock fabric minimums early; specialist fabrics have longer lead times.

Fabric and trims

Recycled nylon or matte peached shells with recycled polyester wadding.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, peaking

Bead-droplet embroidered dresses

Dense glass-bead embroidery falling like raindrops over sheer or lace bases, as at Catarina Mina (São Paulo Fashion Week N61 (Oct 2026)); glass-bead layering also at Anushree Reddy, Lakmē Fashion Week x FDCI, Delhi (Oct 2026). Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Use French seams and finish edges cleanly; seams show through sheer fabric.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Corset-seamed dresses
  • Funnel-neck knit dresses
  • Halter-neck dresses
  • Performance-jersey shirt dresses
  • Quilted and padded dresses

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Knitted pinafore dresses

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Fishtail gowns with attached drapes
  • Laser-cut coral-relief dresses
  • Off-shoulder ruffle dresses
  • Scale-beaded mermaid hems
  • Striped patchwork shirt dresses

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Beaded-fringe canvas dresses
  • Off-shoulder tweed sheath dresses
  • Ombré sequin gowns
  • Starry crystal velvet gowns
  • Basque waist bodices
  • Bias-cut velvet

Actions

  • Add one useful pocket to core tailoring.
  • Belt a suit jacket.
  • Offer a utility vest as a layering piece.
  • Lab-dip rust and brick shades early.
  • Add quilting to dresses and skirts.
  • Pair with charcoal and oatmeal.
