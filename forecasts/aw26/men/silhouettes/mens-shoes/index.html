The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Preppy sport

School and playing-field references: rugby shirts, cricket cables, varsity jackets and oversized statement collars and cuffs.

Why now

Heritage sportswear feels familiar and gift-friendly, and collars and cuffs update basics at low cost.

Seen at

  • Polo Ralph Lauren: Sporty prep and timeless knitwear
  • Willy Chavarria: Sports prep on the runway
  • Statement collars and cuffs: Named a top menswear runway trend

Key pieces

Rugby stripes, Cable knits, Big collars; rugby stripes, short plackets.

Source: Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26; Who What Wear, AW26 trends

Power tailoring

Sharp, slick suiting in pinstripes and slate grey, with pointed shoulders, peak lapels and double-breasted fronts. Oxblood accents and polished heels finish the look.

Why now

Return-to-office dressing and a move away from very oversized blazers toward sharper fits.

Seen at

  • Gucci: Horsebit pumps with sharp suiting
  • Classic-fit suits (market): Up 44% in market adoption
  • Double-breasted suits (market): Up 4% year on year; pointed shoulders up 12%

Key pieces

Pinstripe, Peak lapels, Pointed shoulders; sharp pointed shoulders.

Source: Who What Wear (AW26 trends); Trendalytics, top menswear runway trends FW26

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Apron-toe derbies

Derbies with stitched apron toes. Toe shape is the first thing that dates a shoe, so it signals newness fast.

Design notes

  • Develop a new last for the toe shape; check fit across widths.
  • Check toe spring and comfort on fit trials.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Belgian loafers

Soft, slipper-like loafers. A style-level trend that can lead a footwear capsule.

Design notes

  • Use a cushioned footbed and check heel slip on fit trials.
  • Launch in core colours first, then add seasonal shades.
  • Keep the last consistent across the style family.

Black oxfords

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Brogue perforations

Broguing returns. Perforation adds breathability and a crafted look.

Design notes

  • Use perforations as decoration and for breathability.
  • Back perforations with a breathable lining.
  • Keep perforation away from flex points.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Cupsole derbies

Derbies on sneaker cupsoles. Sole units drive both comfort and trend; check lead times with suppliers.

Design notes

  • Check sole weight and flexibility.
  • Confirm sole mould availability and lead times.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Apron-toe derbies
  • Belgian loafers
  • Black oxfords
  • Brogue perforations
  • Cupsole derbies
  • Derby boots

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Check-trimmed loafers
  • Chelsea boots for tailoring
  • Lug-sole loafers
  • Rubber-soled Chelseas
  • Slim tassel loafers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Cap-toe oxfords
  • Crepe-sole desert boots
  • Penny loafers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Chunky sneakers with suits

Actions

  • Add white contrast collars to existing shirt blocks.
  • Build one striped rugby shirt in three colourways.
  • Use tipping on knit V-necks and cuffs.
  • Offer the pinstripe suit as separates so customers can mix sizes.
  • Use pointed shoulders on jackets, but keep trousers relaxed.
  • Add a waistcoat that works alone as a top.
