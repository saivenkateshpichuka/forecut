SS27 Men's Couture Materials and Details

Elevated menswear materials and details: butter-soft leather, organza feathers, beading, satin and chrome-free leather.

Season
Spring/Summer 2027
Audience
Men
Category
Couture and craft
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
Milan, Paris and Pitti Uomo menswear shows, June 2026; Premiere Vision Paris, February 2026; analysis July to November 2026
Market
Global: Milan, Paris and Florence runways; premium and mid-market menswear

The evidence

  • Butter-soft leather: "flexibility, fluidity, lightness, almost like butter" (Alice Feillard, Galeries Lafayette)[1].
  • Organza feathers and embellishment: Simone Rocha's men's show[2].
  • Beading: coral-like beads at Dolce & Gabbana[3].
  • Chrome-free leather: Margaret Howell x Dr Martens, by C.F. Stead[2].
  • Satin: silk and synthetic, floral and white[4].

Design implications

  • Source leather and feathers with traceability; check chrome-free claims.

Commercial implications

  • Premium materials on a few hero pieces.
