How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 kidswear fairs (Pitti Bimbo and Playtime) take place in June 2027. This report projects from SS28 adult previews, the SS27 kidswear fair record and long-term drivers such as heat and UV protection, and will be updated after the fairs.

The evidence

  • Forecut's SS28 colour forecast: royal blue and a new turquoise beside greyish mauve and violet, over straw and bleached linen, with an after-dark edge[1]
  • Copenhagen SS27 data showed colour returning and neutrals fading; Jacquemus built its SS27 show around turquoise and orange[2]
  • Its named colours include Aubergine Skin, Solar Midnight, Smoky Lavender and Swamp Green, with Cloud Bloom as a luminous counterpoint and Fruit Punch for "juicy intensity"; dark shades work best with contrast, not head to toe[3]

Confirm codes against a physical Pantone book before dyeing.

Colour groups

  • Core: bleached linen, inky blue.
  • Seasonal: sonar turquoise, deep sky, greyish mauve.
  • Accent: acid lemon, peach.

By product

  • Turquoise and sky: swim and play.
  • Lemon: tees and accessories.
  • Peach and mauve: dresses and baby.

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Lab-dip key colours on each main fabric before committing.
  • Safety: no drawstrings or long cords at the hood, neck or waist, in line with EN 14682; test the attachment strength of buttons, snaps and decorations, and keep small detachable parts off baby and toddler pieces.

Commercial implications

  • Keep the core deep and the accents shallow until the fairs confirm them.
