The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

In motion

Long fringe that moves with every step, and bold head-to-toe stripes, from rainbow to tonal.

Why now

Movement and stripes photograph well for video-led social media.

Seen at

  • Luar, Kim Shui, Ulla Johnson, Heirlome, Monse: Long, figure-covering fringe
  • DVF: Rainbow stripes with long shorts
  • Proenza Schouler, Tommy Hilfiger: Head-to-toe stripes

Key pieces

Fringe, Stripes, Swing; fringed hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

  • Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets
  • Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship
  • Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Brushed cardigan coats

Long brushed cardigans. The fabric story carries this detail, so sampling and handle are key.

Design notes

  • Brush after knitting for a soft halo; check for shedding.
  • Use a stable button band so the cardigan does not gape.
  • Request hand-feel swatches and test for pilling, shrinkage and colour fastness.

Fabric and trims

Mohair, alpaca or brushed wool blends.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cable fisherman jumpers

Heavy cables on chunky jumpers. A pattern-led update that refreshes existing silhouettes.

Design notes

  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Keep the knit light enough to avoid sagging.
  • Match pattern at centre front and key seams.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Cricket jumpers

Cables with striped tipping. Trims give an instant seasonal update to proven styles.

Design notes

  • Match stripes at seams and centre front.
  • Place cables symmetrically from the centre front.
  • Check trim care is compatible with the main fabric.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Funnel-neck zip knits

Full zips on funnel-neck knits. The closure changes how the garment opens, fits and is styled.

Design notes

  • Keep the funnel high but soft at the front so it is comfortable under the chin.
  • Use matte or two-way zips; check that the tape does not ripple.
  • Test closures for ease of use and stress points at fit stage.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: yKK or equivalent metal and coil zips in matte finishes.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Half-button polo jumpers

Jumpers with polo collars and buttons. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Merino, lambswool, cotton or recycled wool blends in 7–12 gauge, with self-yarn or horn buttons. Trims: horn-effect, corozo, metal or mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on crew necks, quarter-zips, cardigans and knit polos. Works in menswear knitwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Knitwear, rising

Henley knits

Buttoned plackets on knits. A placket update freshens the front without touching the pattern block.

Design notes

  • Size buttons to the garment weight; use shanks on thick fabrics.
  • Interface plackets so they lie flat after laundering.
  • Match buttonhole spacing to the bust or chest line to avoid gaping.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Brushed cardigan coats
  • Cable fisherman jumpers
  • Cricket jumpers
  • Funnel-neck zip knits
  • Half-button polo jumpers
  • Henley knits

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Cardigans worn as shirts
  • Crochet-panel knits
  • Elbow-seam jumpers
  • Textured waffle knits

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Colour-block knits
  • Shawl-collar pullovers

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Nothing at this stage

Actions

  • Use fringe on hems only to control cost.
  • Offer stripes as co-ord sets.
  • Shoot in motion for e-commerce and reels.
  • Add lace trims at cuffs and hems of core shirts.
  • Use machine smocking on bodices and cuffs.
  • Partner with a craft cluster for a premium capsule.
