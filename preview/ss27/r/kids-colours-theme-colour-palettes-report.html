Why these palettes

They follow the trade-fair evidence: Piccoli Principi's vintage pink, pastel green and soft navy swim[1]; red, navy and nautical at Playtime Paris[2]; Future Snoops' calm, coastal Lull and playful Happenstance[3].

Design implications

  • Lead colour on most pieces; accent on one item or trim per outfit.
  • Share navy and white across themes so pieces mix.

Commercial implications

  • Depth in lead colours; accents as small buys.
