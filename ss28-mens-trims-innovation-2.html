Design notes

How to use the trims.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Band-collar shirts

Collarless shirts with a band neck. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.
  • Use a firm fusible or interlining so the collar holds its shape after washing.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Contrast-collar polos

Polos with contrast collars. It frames the face, so it reads instantly on the hanger and in product imagery.

Design notes

  • Keep the collar flat with a firm rib or interfacing.
  • Use contrast to highlight the shape; check colour fastness between shades.
  • Grade collar size with the body so it stays in proportion across sizes.

Fabric and trims

Piqué, fine-gauge knit or jersey.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Shirts & Polos, rising

Embroidered pocket shirts

Small embroidery on chest pockets. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.
  • Keep embellishment away from friction areas like underarms.

Fabric and trims

Oxford cotton, poplin, linen blends, piqué or fine-gauge knit, with mother-of-pearl effect buttons.

Where to use it

Best on Oxford shirts, camp-collar shirts, knit and piqué polos. Works in menswear shirts and polos as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Outerwear, rising

Field jackets with bellows pockets

Field jackets with expanding pockets. Pockets add function and visual interest, and are easy to vary across a range.

Design notes

  • Keep utility pockets flat and tidy for city wear; avoid too much bulk.
  • Reinforce pocket corners with bar tacks.
  • Check pocket placement against hand position on the fit model.

Fabric and trims

Cotton ripstop, gabardine or nylon twill with snap or Velcro closures.

Where to use it

Best on overcoats, bombers, chore jackets, puffers and overshirts. Works in menswear outerwear as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy

What to build, test, sell through and drop.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Band-collar shirts
  • Contrast-collar polos
  • Embroidered pocket shirts
  • Field jackets with bellows pockets
  • Fly-front placket shirts
  • Funnel-neck puffers

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Articulated-knee trousers

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Chore and work jackets
  • Cuban camp collars
  • Knitted short-sleeve polos
  • Patch pockets on soft jackets
  • Polo-collar cardigans
  • Printed resort shirts

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Cricket jumpers
  • Long-sleeve knitted polos
  • Open Johnny-collar knit polos
  • Oxford button-downs
  • Popover shirts
  • Shawl-collar pullovers
