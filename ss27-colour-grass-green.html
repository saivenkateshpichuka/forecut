The story

What is behind it, and where it was seen.

Elegant ease

Dressing gets refined again. The tie returns, shirts are tucked in, trousers are pleated and slightly puffed, and elegant mules and pointed flats replace sneakers.

Why now

Fabric forecasters say street style and sneakers are giving way to elegance.

Seen at

  • Loewe: The return of the tie
  • Munich Fabric Start forecasters: "Everything is becoming elegant"
  • SS27 menswear: Slimmer ties, pleated and puffed trousers

Key pieces

Slim ties, Mules, Pointed flats; tucked shirts with slim ties.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Undone

A deliberately unfinished look: straps falling off the shoulder, exposed or unhooked undergarments, top layers left unsewn and raw, frayed hems on muslin-like cottons.

Why now

A relaxed, personal take on elegance, and raw edges suit natural fabrics.

Seen at

  • Altuzarra: Deshabille looks
  • Christian Cowan: Undone layers
  • Coach and Prabal Gurung: Muslin looks
  • LII: Double-layer minis
  • Paloma Wool: Dropped linings

Key pieces

Raw hems, Slip straps, Muslin; unfinished raw hems.

Source: The Zoe Report, NYFW SS27 trends; FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage

Handcraft

Lace flowers, embroidery and smocking move onto crisp, everyday shirting. Craft techniques such as wrapping, gathering, pleating and openwork feel personal and slow.

Why now

Lace is named a growth category, and craft adds perceived value to simple poplin and knits.

Seen at

  • Chloé: Embroidery and waxed jackets
  • Gabriela Hearst: Mirrorwork inspired by Afghan craftsmanship
  • Lace (category): Forecasters call it a growth category

Key pieces

Lace, Smocking, Embroidery; lace-embroidered poplin shirts.

Source: FashionUnited, SS27 Paris coverage and SS27 trend report

Design notes

How to put the leading pieces into a range.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses

Strapless corset bodices over a crinoline-lined petticoat skirt cut to tea length, softened with tulle draped over the shoulders; Vivienne Westwood custom black Chantilly-lace dress for Ariana Grande at the London 'Focker-In-Law' premiere, 18 Nov 2026. A focused detail update for dresses.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Cut on the bias for drape and pin-test gathers on the stand.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Cup-bodice gold column gowns

Long metallic-gold gowns with a defined cup or bustier bodice, all-over shine and a high slit; several stars at the 47th Cairo International Film Festival, 11 Nov 2026. Bodice shaping defines the fit of the whole dress.

Design notes

  • Test metallic finishes for scratching and cracking.
  • Reinforce the top of the slit with a bar tack.
  • Fit bodice on a live model for movement.

Fabric and trims

Metallic leather or foil-coated fabric.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Menswear, Tailoring, rising

Generous 1980s double-breasted suits

Roomy double-breasted suits with pleated, higher-waisted trousers in a late-80s/early-90s line; the VRC's Melbourne Cup Carnival 2026 forecast names them for race-day menswear, alongside the return of the tie. A focused detail update for tailoring.

Design notes

  • Use pressed pleats for tailoring and soft pleats for relaxed styles.
  • Space buttons evenly and balance the wrap width.

Fabric and trims

Tropical wool, wool-viscose suiting or linen blends, with half-canvas fronts and horn buttons.

Where to use it

Best on suits, blazers, waistcoats and smart trousers. Works in menswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Dresses, rising

Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns

Fully sequinned column gowns closed with corset lacing down the back; Tamara Ralph on Lily James at the Governors Awards, Los Angeles, 15 Nov 2026, with Karol G's lace-up corset bodice (Celia Kritharioti couture) at the Latin Grammys, 12 Nov. A back detail adds interest for the second look and for styling shots.

Design notes

  • Line lace where needed and place scalloped edges at hems and cuffs.
  • Keep embellishment away from underarms and seams; check washability.
  • Make sure the back is shot in imagery so customers see the detail.

Fabric and trims

Cotton lace, broderie anglaise or recycled polyester lace.

Where to use it

Best on midi day dresses, shirt dresses, slip dresses and occasion styles. Works in womenswear dresses as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Womenswear, Tailoring, rising

Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos

Black smoking suits embroidered all over in black, worn as the alternative to a gown; Marian Saud for Débora Plager at the Martín Fierro de Cable awards, Buenos Aires, 12 Nov 2026. Embellishment lifts price perception and suits occasion and gifting drops.

Design notes

  • Use laser finishing for consistent, lower-impact distressing.
  • Place embroidery on flat areas; use backing to avoid puckering.
  • Test embellishment for snagging and washing.

Fabric and trims

Merino or lambswool in 5–7 gauge.

Where to use it

Best on blazers, waistcoats, suit trousers and co-ord sets. Works in womenswear tailoring as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Footwear, Men's Shoes, rising

Apron-toe derbies

Derbies with stitched apron toes. Toe shape is the first thing that dates a shoe, so it signals newness fast.

Design notes

  • Develop a new last for the toe shape; check fit across widths.
  • Check toe spring and comfort on fit trials.

Fabric and trims

Calf, suede or polished leather uppers, Goodyear-welted leather or rubber soles.

Where to use it

Best on loafers, derbies, oxfords and Chelsea boots. Use it as a capsule hero or as a quiet update to core styles.

Strategy and price

What to build, test, sell through and drop, with price bands.

Build depth

Rising pieces

  • Corseted tea-length crinoline dresses
  • Cup-bodice gold column gowns
  • Generous 1980s double-breasted suits
  • Lace-up corset-back sequin gowns
  • Tone-on-tone embroidered tuxedos
  • Apron-toe derbies

Test small

Emerging pieces

  • Ankle-strap balloon trousers
  • Barefoot sole-less sandals
  • Bead-droplet embroidered dresses
  • Floral-lined showerproof jackets
  • Fringed macramé tops
  • Fringed minis with back trains

Sell through

Peaking pieces

  • Denim waistcoats
  • Lace-edged bias slips
  • Mesh midi skirts
  • Penny loafers
  • Pointed shoulders
  • Sharp pointed shoulders

Exit or rework

Fading pieces

  • Drop waist with pleated skirt
  • Hoodie under tailoring

Actions

  • Add slim ties and pocket squares as add-on sales.
  • Style shirts tucked into pleated trousers in imagery.
  • Shift footwear mix from sneakers toward mules and flats.
  • Test raw hems in wash trials before bulk.
  • Layer a longer slip under existing dresses.
  • Style straps off-shoulder in imagery, not just in the garment.
