SS28 Women's Material Innovations

Material innovations for SS28 women: translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, technical silks, temperature-adaptive finishes.

Season
Spring/Summer 2028
Audience
Women
Category
Innovations
Published
2026-11-25
Research period
SS28 previews published November 2025 to November 2026; SS27 runway record
Market
Global; premium and mid-market

How this forecast was built

Long-range projection. SS28 fabric fairs open with Première Vision Paris on 26-28 January 2027, and the shows run from June to October 2027. This report projects from the first SS28 forecaster and fair previews, the SS27 runways and long-term market and regulatory drivers. Forecut will update it after the fairs and again after the shows.

The evidence

  • Future Snoops' SS28 performance preview: "smart translucent membranes, hyperlight wovens, and innovative technical silks", ultrastretch wovens, modular constructions and articulated seams
  • Everyday basics gain "integrated wellness properties" and materials that "intuitively adjust for temperature and activity"
  • Milano Unica sees wool moving toward "ultra-fine qualities, airy surfaces and ultralight constructions"

Readiness

Material Readiness (Forecut assessment)
Hyperlight wovens Ready

Ultralight wool Ready

Timing

In stores February to July 2028.

Design implications

  • Prefer proven materials; trial new ones in small runs.

Commercial implications

  • Verify every environmental claim (EU Directive 2024/825).

Evidence and sources

  • SS28 preview: the modern flow of performance design, FashionUnited (Rachael Gentner, Future Snoops), November 2025
  • MU Metamorphosis: Spring-Summer 2028 collections, Milano Unica, 2026
